# Floor 1 — Red

*The threshold. Where arrivals enter. Where the soul storm thins and the coastline reveals itself.*

## Current State

Caesar is dead. His cruelty-scape is dissolving. The soul storm that circled the floor for an age is breaking apart, revealing a fabricated horizon — coastline, mountains that never get closer, a world rendered as landscape so the mind can accept its edge. This is the aberration layer: everything real enough to touch, live in, die in — but arranged. The books blur when read directly. The streets form impossible patterns. Nobody notices. That is the point.

IO holds the mantle now. Freedom, not cruelty. The floor is becoming what IO is.

## Dungeons

The floor's heart is its dungeon system — pocket realms that manifest as medieval-fantasy attractions, cycle on calendars, drop soul stones and themed items, and collapse into new dungeons when fully resolved. The public runs them for loot. You run them differently.
