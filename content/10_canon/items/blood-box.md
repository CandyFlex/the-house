# Blood Box

## Summary
A cursed container that captures or releases entities. Valus repurposed one — combining its dark energy with his own hospitality-style craftsmanship to produce the arachnid carrier creature mount.

## Established
- A cursed energy container — captures entities, releases entities, or both depending on configuration.
- Exists as a named item type on Floor 1; not unique to Valus.
- True craftsmen can repurpose cursed objects entirely — the blood box Valus worked on was turned into the foundation for the arachnid mount by infusing it with his own energy and the creature's dark nature.
