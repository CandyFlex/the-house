# Caesar's Spell Focus

## Summary
The original spell-focus wand of Caesar, from before he gave up magic as his first act of cruelty. A long silver metamorphic wand that mirrors liquidity while holding its shape. Carries the residue of Caesar's original magic. Originally given to his daughter Woe; she passed it to IO in her final moments.

## Established
- A wand of silver, metamorphic in nature — appears liquid while holding a solid shape.
- Described as resembling "the legs of the first floor's poles" — long, slender, reflective.
- Was Caesar's original spell focus when he was a powerful spellcaster.
- Carries the residue or imprint of Caesar's original magic — the magic he gave up.
- Caesar gave up his magic as his first act of taking the mantle of cruelty, making this wand the last physical remnant of his pre-cruelty existence.
- Caesar originally gave the wand to his daughter, Woe.
- Woe, in her dying moments, gave the wand to IO.

## Details
- The wand's metamorphic silver quality suggests it may be adaptive or responsive to its wielder.
- As the last remnant of Caesar's original magic, it may hold significance beyond its function as a focus.
- The wand passed from Caesar to Woe to IO — a chain of transfer connecting the pre-cruelty era to the present.

## Properties
- **Spell focus**: Functions as a spell focus for sorcerer and wizard magic. Enhances spellcasting within Floor 1's domain.
- **Affinity weapon**: Tied to Floor 1's claim. Acts as a magnifying glass for the floor's power — the wielder's connection to the mantle is amplified through it.
- **Floor synchronization**: Gives the wielder a direct link to Floor 1's mantle. They can sense soul energy currents, feel the presence of outposts, and perceive concentrations of soul stone wealth.
- **Pure magic**: The wand itself is not cursed. It carries the residue of Caesar's original magic — pure, before cruelty. However, power corrupts, and the wielder is warned.
- **Metamorphic**: The wand can shift its form at will, responding to the wielder's intent. This property becomes more pronounced at higher stages.

## Stage Progression

The wand grows in power as the wielder's synchronization with Floor 1 deepens — tied to the mantle registration process, control of territory, and the floor's economic health.

### Stage 1 — Silver Remnant (Base Form)
The wand in its initial state. A spell focus of exceptional quality, responsive to the wielder's will. Grants basic mantle synchronization.

**Abilities:**
- Spell focus for sorcerer/wizard magic. Spells cast through the wand gain advantage on concentration checks within Floor 1's domain.
- **Mantle Sense**: The wielder can sense the floor's soul energy currents within 1 mile — outpost locations, significant wealth concentrations, and the general health of the floor's economy.
- The wand's liquid-silver surface ripples in response to the wielder's magical intent.

### Stage 2 — Metamorphic Blade (Longsword Unlocked)
The wand learns to take combat form. It can transform into a longsword at will, retaining its liquid-silver appearance. Spectral blades begin to manifest.

**Abilities (retains all Stage 1 plus):**
- **Blade Transformation**: As a bonus action, the wand shifts between wand form (spell focus) and longsword form (magical longsword, finesse, light). In longsword form, spells can be channeled through the blade — the wielder can deliver touch-range spells through melee attacks.
- **Spectral Blade Initiation**: 1-2 spectral silver swords manifest behind the wielder, orbiting slowly. Each blade can be directed as a bonus action to strike a target within 30 ft (magical piercing damage equal to the wielder's spellcasting modifier + the wand's scaling bonus). The blades are translucent, metallic, echoing the wand's liquid-silver aesthetic.
- The transformation between forms is seamless — the weapon can shift mid-swing, striking as a sword and casting as a wand in the same flow.

### Stage 3 — The Cruelty Echo (Blade Formation Expands)
The spectral blade formation expands to 4-6 orbiting swords. The wielder can command them as a coordinated force.

**Abilities (retains all prior plus):**
- **Blade Formation**: 4-6 spectral swords orbit the wielder in a fan-formation. The wielder can:
  - **Volley**: Launch the entire formation as a single attack against a target or spread across multiple targets in a 30-foot cone.
  - **Turret Mode**: Assign individual blades to hover at fixed points, attacking independently when targets approach or when the wielder casts a spell through them.
  - **Barrier**: Collapse the formation into a protective barrier of interlocking blades that provides a defensive bonus (cover or damage reduction).
- **Mantle Synchronization II**: The wielder can now perceive the floor's economic health in detail — track wealth flows, sense when soul stones enter or leave controlled territory, and identify the wealthiest targets.
- The wand's metamorphic nature is fully expressed — it can shift between wand and blade mid-swing, or maintain both properties simultaneously.

### Stage 4 — The First Mark's Mandate (Final Form + Economy Agent Unlock)
The wand fully awakens. The spectral blade formation expands to 10-12 swords. The weapon itself becomes a floating entity that can fight independently while the wielder casts.

**Abilities (retains all prior plus):**
- **Grand Formation**: 10-12 spectral swords. The wielder can maintain a permanent orbital formation while also using individual blades for independent actions.
- **Autonomous Manifestation**: The wand can fight independently — floating in longsword form, attacking targets while the wielder casts spells through the formation. The wand and wielder act on separate initiative counts.
- **Economy Agent Unlocked**: See [[floor-economy-agent]].
- The sword formation is now permanent — blades do not dissipate unless dismissed. The wielder can summon additional temporary blades by spending soul stones directly (burning wealth for combat advantage).

## Progression Logic
Stages advance through the wielder's deepening connection to Floor 1's mantle:
- **Stage 1**: Granted on attunement to the wand and initial mantle synchronization.
- **Stage 2**: Unlocks when at least one hand (Hour) is appointed and the wielder has completed registration Step 6.
- **Stage 3**: Unlocks when two hands are appointed and the wielder controls territory (at least one outpost or the school fully stabilized).
- **Stage 4**: Unlocks when all three hands are assigned and the floor economy reaches a threshold of accumulated soul stone wealth (see [[floor-economy-agent]]).
