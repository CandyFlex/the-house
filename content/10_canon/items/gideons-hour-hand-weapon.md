# Gideon's Hour Hand Weapon

## Summary
The manifested weapon of the Hour hand of Floor 1. Emerged during the appointment ritual when the Hour key was placed against the bearer's sternum — time stopped, whispers of the past spoke, and the key absorbed into the bearer's arm as a black line. From the palm emerged an 8-10 foot bronze whip-spear with a pulsing dualistic end. Described as a "high frequency connection" — one of the best possible resonances between bearer and floor mantle. The weapon grows with its bearer over time.

## Appearance & Theme
- A bronze whip-spear, 8-10 feet in its initial form. Bronze metallic coloration that ripples and shifts in light.
- The shaft transitions from rigid spear-form near the grip to flexible whip-form toward the tip.
- The dualistic end pulses between two states — one a cutting edge, the other a binding coil — representing opposing energies held in tension.
- A black line/tattoo on the bearer's arm traces where the key absorbed, connecting bearer to weapon at all times. The weapon can be dismissed and recalled through this connection.
- When active, faint gold-bronze temporal energy traces around the weapon — motes of frozen light, slowed particles, air distortion.

## The Hour's Authority
The weapon is intrinsically tied to the Hour hand's authority over Floor 1. The Hour carries approximately 50% of the floor mantle's consciousness load. The x10 speed zone (Hour Space) applies: the bearer acts at 10x temporal speed — 10 combat turns before others act, one minute real-time for 10 turns. See [[temporal-hierarchy]].

## Stage Progression

### Stage 1 — Unfurled Bronze (Base Form)
The weapon in its initial manifested state. A bronze whip-spear with both whip and spear properties. The dualistic end can alternate between cutting (slashing, piercing) and binding (entangling, tripping, lassoing). The Hour's x10 speed zone applies.

**Abilities:**
- Reach weapon (10 ft). Functions as both a whip (dexterity-based, slashing, flexible angles) and spear (strength-based, thrusting, piercing).
- The dualistic end: as a bonus action, switch between **cutting edge** (armor-piercing on the tip) or **binding coil** (can wrap around limbs, weapons, or objects — contested strength check to break free).
- Dismiss/recall as a free action through the arm-tattoo connection.
- Hour Space active: the bearer's initiative order is effectively 10 turns per standard round within the floor's domain.

### Stage 2 — The Hour's Chain (First Evolution)
The whip-spear extends its reach to 15-20 feet. The weapon begins to resonate with the floor mantle — the bearer can sense the flow of soul energy within a radius.

**Abilities (retains all Stage 1 abilities plus):**
- **Reach expansion**: 15-20 foot effective range.
- **Time-Snare**: On a hit, the wielder can choose to freeze the struck target in a brief temporal stasis. The target is restrained (or paralyzed on a failed save) for 1 round. One target at a time. Usable once per short rest per stage advancement.
- **Floor Resonance**: The bearer can sense concentrations of soul stone wealth within a limited radius (scales with floor control). The weapon hums more intensely near significant wealth.
- The dualistic end sharpens: the cutting edge ignores non-magical armor; the binding coil leaves temporal after-images that make the target easier to hit for one round.

### Stage 3 — Temporal Sweep (Area Control)
The weapon's command extends over an area. The bronze form begins to phase between solid and energy, trailing temporal distortion.

**Abilities (retains all prior plus):**
- **Sweep Attack**: As an action, attack all enemies in a 15-foot cone. Make a single attack roll against all targets in the area.
- **Time-Slow Zone**: Create a 10-foot radius zone where enemies move at half speed and act on a delayed initiative count. The zone persists for 1 minute. Usable once per short rest at this stage.
- **Temporal Tether**: The binding coil can now anchor a target to the current moment — preventing teleportation, phase-shifting, or dimensional movement for 1 minute. A restrained target cannot escape through non-physical means.
- The dualistic end splits more distinctly: the cutting end tears through temporal defenses; the binding end tethers the target to the present.

### Stage 4 — The Hand of the Hour (Final Form + Economy Agent Unlock)
The whip-spear transcends its physical form, becoming a bronze energy construct that can extend indefinitely within the bearer's domain. The weapon is now fully synchronized with Floor 1's mantle.

**Abilities (retains all prior plus):**
- **Energy Manifestation**: The weapon is now a construct of bronze temporal energy. Can extend or retract at will within the domain (up to the edge of controlled territory). Passing through the weapon deals temporal damage — ages or slows the affected area.
- **True Time-Stop (Limited)**: Once per long rest, the wielder can stop time for 1-2 rounds (enemies frozen, only the wielder acts). Within controlled territory (an allied outpost, the school, etc.), duration extends.
- **Economy Agent Unlocked**: See [[floor-economy-agent]].
- The weapon can now be split: the bearer can maintain the whip-spear in one hand while the binding coil operates independently as a secondary temporal lash (off-hand attack or independent grapple).

## Progression Logic
Stages advance through the bearer's deepening synchronization to Floor 1's mantle:
- **Stage 1**: Granted automatically on Hour appointment.
- **Stage 2**: Unlocks when the bearer has completed one additional mantle registration step (step 7 or 8 — either the Minute hand appointed or significant soul wealth contributed).
- **Stage 3**: Unlocks when the bearer controls at least one allied outpost or faction on Floor 1, and two hands are assigned.
- **Stage 4**: Unlocks when all three hands are assigned and the floor economy reaches a threshold of accumulated soul stone wealth (see [[floor-economy-agent]]).
