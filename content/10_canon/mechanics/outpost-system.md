# Outpost System

## Summary
A network of twelve outposts on [[floor-01-red]], each named for a calendar month. Built at the [[the-architect]]'s instruction as arrival/magistrate hubs. Most have been destroyed — Caesar eliminated some deliberately; attrition took others over the centuries. [[outpost-july]] is among the survivors.

## Established
- Twelve outposts total, one per calendar month.
- All named for months: January through December.
- Built as arrival and magistrate infrastructure for Floor 1.
- Destruction causes: Caesar's deliberate targeting; general attrition over vast time.
- Current survivor count: unknown. July confirmed surviving. Others unconfirmed.
- The twelve-outpost structure reflects [[house-mirroring]] — the calendar as a structural analogy embedded in Floor 1's infrastructure.

## Relationships
- [[floor-01-red]] — the twelve outposts are on this floor
- [[the-architect]] — commissioner of the system
- [[outpost-july]] — confirmed surviving outpost
- [[caesar]] — destroyed several of the outposts deliberately
- [[house-mirroring]] — calendar-mirror manifestation of the broader principle
