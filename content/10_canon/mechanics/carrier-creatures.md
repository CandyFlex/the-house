# Carrier Creatures

## Summary
A Floor 1 phenomenon: nocturnal creatures that carry structures larger on the inside than the outside (Tardis-topology). Most common are birds, owls, and bats. Spiders are the rarest — almost never seen. Valus's arachnid mount is spider-type, making it exceptional.

## Established
- Structures carried are larger inside than outside — non-Euclidean interiors on mobile hosts.
- Hosts are nocturnal in type: birds, owls, bats most common; spiders extremely rare.
- Spider-type carrier creatures are so rare that almost no one has encountered one.
- [[valus]]'s arachnid mount is a spider-type carrier creature — further imbued through his crafter diffusion and reformulation of a blood box.

## Relationships
- [[floor-01-red]] — Floor 1 phenomenon
- [[valus]] — built a spider-type carrier creature (the arachnid mount)
- [[blood-box]] — Valus repurposed one as the foundation for the arachnid

## Open Questions
- Are carrier creatures native to Floor 1, or do they appear on other floors?
- Do they choose their structures, or are structures imposed on them?
- Are the interiors stable or do they change?
- What lives inside them?

## Key Takeaways
- Spider-type = exceptionally rare. Valus having one is significant.
- The interior/exterior scale inversion is consistent with The House's non-Euclidean scale principles.
