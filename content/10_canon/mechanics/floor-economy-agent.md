# Floor Economy Agent

## Summary
A latent magical resonance between affinity weapons tied to Floor 1's mantle and the floor's accumulated soul stone wealth. When a weapon reaches its final stage of progression, it unlocks this scaling mechanic: the weapon draws power from the floor's economy. The more soul stones flowing through controlled territory, the stronger the weapon becomes. Creates a feedback loop: control territory → grow the economy → weapon scales → control more territory.

## How It Works

### Market Cap (Economic Base)
The total soul stone wealth within the party's controlled sphere on Floor 1 determines the scaling tier. This includes:
- Soul stones held by allied factions and party members.
- Soul stones stored at allied outposts (Outpost December vault, etc.).
- The output of Outpost December's reverse-osmosis refinement process (up to 15M/day, stored up to 500M).
- Wealth from conquered or subjugated hostile factions (Mr. Fine, Witches, Warlocks — their soul stones become part of the pool once subdued).
- Tributes from factions that recognize IO's mantle.

### Shared Value Multiplier
Not all wealth counts equally:
- **Allied wealth** (party members, allied factions, controlled outposts): 100% contribution.
- **Neutral wealth** (non-hostile factions, unaffiliated entities): 50% contribution.
- **Hostile wealth**: 0% contribution — must be conquered or allied to count.
- **Personal vs. collective**: The "shared value" among the party and connected entities creates the pool. Wealth held by isolated individuals (not networked into the floor's economy) does not contribute.

### Scaling Tiers

| Threshold (Cumulative Pool) | Bonus | Effect |
|---|---|
| 500M (Vault Capacity) | +2 | Weapon glows near significant wealth. Minor ability recharge on short rest. First major power spike. |
| 1B (Full Registration Cost) | +3 | Persistent aura or passive defense. Abilities improve. Second power spike. |
| 50B (Floor-Wide Economy) | +4 | Major ability milestones. Weapon's domain extends significantly. |
| 500B (Inter-Floor Influence) | +4 | Capstone offensive properties. Weapon transcends floor boundary — functions outside Floor 1 at full power. |
| 1T+ (Endgame Supremacy) | +4 | True capstone. Once-per-day ultimate ability. Weapon acts independently. Formation becomes permanent. |

Tiers are cumulative — reaching 1T grants all effects from all lower tiers. The +4 attack/damage bonus caps at 50B; beyond that, the growth is in qualitative abilities rather than raw numbers.

### Feedback Loop
1. Control territory (outposts, the school, other locations) → expands the economic base.
2. Ally with factions or subdue threats → adds their wealth to the pool.
3. Weapons grow stronger → easier to control more territory and subdue more threats.
4. If territory is lost or factions become hostile → the market cap shrinks, weapons weaken.
5. The floor does not need to reach 1B permanently — temporary wealth spikes (e.g., looting Mr. Fine's 500M) provide temporary bonuses; sustained wealth provides sustained scaling.

### Economy Agent Characteristics
- **Not sentient**. This is not an AI or conscious entity. It is a latent magical resonance — the affinity between Floor 1's claim (the mantle/registration system) and the natural soul-stone economy of the floor.
- The "agent" language is mechanical: it acts as an intermediary between the floor's wealth and the weapon's power.
- The weapon can be "fed" soul stones directly for temporary boosts (burning wealth for a single encounter bonus), or can scale passively based on controlled wealth.
- If the floor's economy collapses (vault emptied, outposts lost, factions turn hostile), the agent goes dormant until wealth re-accumulates.

## Weapon-Specific Manifestations

### Gideon's Hour Hand (The Hour)
The economy bonus manifests as **temporal amplification**:
- More effective time freezes.
- Longer reach per each scaling tier.
- The weapon's tie to the duration of Hour Space extends (one additional turn per tier in the speed zone).

### Caesar's First Mark (IO's Wand)
The economy bonus manifests as **magical amplification**:
- More spectral blades in the formation.
- Stronger spell amplification through the wand.
- Faster conjuration speed.
