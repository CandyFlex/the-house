# Originators

## Summary
Entities formed when a pure concept accumulates sufficient presence within the House to achieve its own sentience. Among the most powerful beings in the House. Caesar is the Originator of Cruelty. Nero is another (nature deferred).

## Established
- Form from ideas, feelings, witnessed realities — anything that accumulates enough weight in the House to develop sentience.
- Power scales with the concept's weight and prevalence within the House's reality.
- Multiple Originators exist; they conflict, conquer, and hold territory.
- When an Originator (or coalition) takes over a floor, they often reshape it as a pocket reality expressing their concept — the floor becomes an extension of what they are.
- Known Originators: [[caesar]] (Cruelty), [[nero]] (concept unknown, but confirmed as a betrayer).

### Typology
- **Intentional Originators**: Those that came into being from the Architect's intentional thought or action.
- **Unintentional Originators**: Those that formed as byproducts of the Architect's existence and actions — not consciously willed into being.
- The Will was created specifically to prevent further unintentional Originators from forming.
- Caesar himself was an unintentional Originator. An intentional Originator helped him become the last mantle-holder of Floor 1 — someone deliberately installed Caesar.

### Betrayers (Originator Subtype)
- A rare subtype of Originator that BETRAY the Will's protections that keep most Originators from open conflict.
- Distinct from most Originators, who are protected by the Will's laws from open escalation and conflict.
- Most Originators are NOT in constant strife with each other — the Will prevents this. Betrayers operate outside these protections.
- Betrayers can kill Originators, which is otherwise extremely difficult.
- Betrayers seek conflict to absorb, concentrate, or supersede other Originators.
- Known betrayer: [[nero]], the greatest — has killed hundreds of unintentional Originators (lost count) and exactly ONE intentional Originator.

## Relationships
- [[caesar]] — the Originator of Cruelty
- [[nero]] — an Originator; a betrayer; the most prolific Originator killer
- [[floor-extension]] — Originators commonly express their power by reshaping conquered floors
- [[the-will]] — created to prevent unintentional Originator spawning; protects Originators from open conflict. Betrayers operate outside these protections.
- [[the-architect]] — his existence caused both intentional and unintentional Originators to form
