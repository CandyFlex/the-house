# Temporal Hierarchy

## Summary
A ranked lieutenant structure present on every floor with a controlling entity. The controller designates chosen individuals as **Hours**, **Minutes**, and **Seconds** — each imbued with the power of the floor through that title. The structure mirrors the real-world clock hierarchy and reflects [[house-mirroring]]. Hours are the most powerful; Seconds the least of the three.

## Established
- Every controlled floor has this structure beneath its controlling entity.
- Three ranks: **Hours** (highest), **Minutes** (mid), **Seconds** (lowest of the three).
- Designation is by the floor controller — chosen individuals, imbued with a portion of the floor's power through the title.
- The imbuing is real and functional — the titles carry actual power, not just status.
- Under Caesar on Floor 1: his Hours, Minutes, and Seconds were the top agents of his cruelty-scape. Their identities are not yet established.
- The three-rank structure may extend further — other time units (days, months, years) may have corresponding roles at higher scales.
- Part of the [[house-mirroring]] system — the clock as organizational analogy made structural.
- **Hand rank weighting on Floor 1**: The Hour hand carries approximately 50% control of the floor mantle. The Minute hand carries approximately 30-40%. The Second hand carries approximately 10%.
- **Decision dynamic**: The Hour has superseding power over the other two unless the Minute and Second combine against it (50/40/10 ratio).
- **Origin of hands**: The hands derive from Caesar's suns (his 12 sons/heralds). Mundane clock hands were transformed into power-granting objects, creating a superficial but real magical tie to the House, the floor, and the mantle-holder.
- **Ten-step registration process**: Full registration of a floor mantle requires ten steps or stages.

### Steps 6-10 of Floor 1 Mantle Registration

- **Steps 1-5**: Administrative initialization — Tome/ledger setup, procedures for putting the right book in context to Floor 1's controller. Campaign-contextual specifics are not setting canon; the existence of administrative initialization steps IS canon.
- **Step 6 — Appointing the Hour hand**: The existence of the Hour hand AND the appointment of someone to it as a required step are canon. Who holds it is campaign-contextual.
- **Step 7**: Likely appointing the Minute hand.
- **Step 8**: Likely appointing the Second hand.
- **Step 9 — The Witness**: A qualified witness must observe the registration. Valid witnesses: Cardholders (Will fragments with emboldened bodies), former mantle-holders from other floors, and Intentional Originators. Unintentional Originators CANNOT witness.
- **Step 10 — The Oath + Payment**: The holder speaks a binding oath on a Will fragment while paying 1 billion soul gems. The oath binds to the House itself, not just the floor. The payment is the economic weight; the oath is the personal/cosmological contract.

## 1-Transcript Additions
- Hour Space mechanics: x10 speed zone — the Hour gets 10 combat turns before anyone else acts. One minute real-time = 10 turns for Hour bearer.
- Hand weightings on Floor 1: Hour = ~50% of floor mantle, Minute = ~25-40%, Second = ~10%.
- The appointment ritual: key placed on sternum, time stops, whispers of past, key absorbs into arm as black line/tattoo, weapon shape emerges from palm.
- The weapon that emerges develops and grows with the bearer over time.
- Appointment doubles the stabilization timer.
- Selection criteria: choose people of merit and power. Innate ability power > item-derived power. Relinquish souls = standardization cost.
- Originators are the ideal hand candidates (Caesar had multiple under him at once).
