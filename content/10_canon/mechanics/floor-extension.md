# Floor Extension

## Summary
When a sufficiently powerful entity — typically an Originator or coalition — conquers a floor, they can subvert its existing reality and reshape it as a pocket realm expressing their own nature. The floor becomes an extension of what the conqueror is.

## Established
- Conquest alone is not sufficient — the conqueror must be able to impose their reality on the floor's cosmetics and functional structure.
- The reshaped floor reflects the conqueror's concept, personality, or power — not a neutral territory but a living expression of them.
- Caesar did this to Floor 1: subverted the pre-existing Floor 1 reality, replaced it with his cruelty-scape. The soul storm, the desolation, the brutal arrival intake are all his imposition.
- Pre-Caesar Floor 1 had a different reality. That prior reality exists somewhere — possibly in the old pocket realm Caesar stored away.
- This reshaping can be partial or total. Caesar's version was near-total.

## Open Questions
- What was Floor 1 before Caesar? Who held it?
- Can a conquered floor be restored to a prior state if the conqueror dies?
- Do all floors have conquest histories, or are some original / unclaimed?
- Can multiple entities hold a floor in coalition and produce a blended reality?

## Key Takeaways
- Floors are not neutral settings. They are expressions of whoever holds them.
- Caesar's death leaves Floor 1's pocket realm status unresolved.
