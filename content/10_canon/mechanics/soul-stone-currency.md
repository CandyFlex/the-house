# Soul Stone Currency

## Summary
The currency hierarchy of Floor 1 has defined tiers: Generic Soul Gems (100-1,000), Stabilized Soul Gems (1,000-10,000), Exquisite Soul Gems (100k minimum), and L Dawn (white) / L Night (black) stones worth 1 million to 100 million units each. Higher tiers are produced at Outpost December via reverse-osmosis refinement of the soul storm.

## Established
- **Generic Soul Gems** — worth 100 to 1,000 units. Base tier.
- **Stabilized Soul Gems** — worth 1,000 to 10,000 units.
- **Exquisite Soul Gems** — worth 100,000 units minimum.
- **L Dawn (white) and L Night (black)** — the top confirmed tier. Worth 1 million to 100 million units depending on quality.
- These higher-tier stones are derived from specialized sources, including the reverse-osmosis refinement process at Outpost December.
- The red stone (raw soul stone) was previously thought to be the reverse-osmosis product, before Outpost December's actual function was understood.

## Details
- L Dawn and L Night may have value variance within their tier based on quality.
- The system may extend further beyond L Dawn/L Night to even higher tiers not yet revealed.
- Previously proposed terms "squizzes" and "scold gyms" were transcription artifacts and are not canonical.
