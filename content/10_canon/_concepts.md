# Load-Bearing Concepts

These concepts underpin [[the-house]]. Preserve terminology exactly.

## Mesophysical
Between metaphysical and physical. The operating layer of [[the-house]] — the layer where intention and matter are not yet fully separate. Preserve this term; do not substitute synonyms like 'metaphysical' or 'spiritual.'

## Scale is intentional
Floors and spaces within [[the-house]] are non-Euclidean. A location may be described as a continent, a planet, a galaxy, or a multiverse. These are not contradictions — they are features of the frame.

## No outside
No external space to [[the-house]] has ever been observed. Treat references to 'outside' as internal manifestations unless explicitly flagged as a cosmological question.

## Adjacency, not hierarchy
Floors connect sequentially [[floor-01-red]] ↔ [[floor-02-orange]] ↔ [[floor-03-yellow]] ↔ [[floor-04-green]] ↔ [[floor-05-blue]] ↔ [[floor-06-indigo]] ↔ [[floor-07-violet]]. No floor ranks above another. [[floor-01-red]] is the entrance only when entering from outside — internally, every floor is its own center.

## Originators
Entities formed when a pure concept achieves sentience within the House. Power scales with the concept's weight in the House's reality. Floor conquest and pocket realm reshaping are common expressions of Originator power. See [[originators]].

## The Will
[[the-architect]]'s intended living proxy — a unified conscious legal/cosmological document. Never fully enacted or completed; shattered into autonomous fragments with individual personalities and sentience. Scattered across the House. Central ongoing mechanic. See [[the-will]].

## Crafter Diffusion
Near-nothing beings involuntarily imbue their creations with pieces of themselves. The more eroded or shadow-adjacent a crafter, the more their works become them. [[valus]] is the exemplar; [[the-architect]] may be the intentional version. See [[crafter-diffusion]].

## Nothing / Nonite Distinction
`nothing` is the ambient, unformed substance associated with [[the-architect]]'s presence or byproduct. `nonite` is nothing in its worked or manifested form. Related but not identical. See [[nonite]].

## Floor Extension
When a sufficiently powerful entity (typically an [[originators|Originator]]) conquers a floor, they reshape it as a pocket reality expressing their nature. The floor becomes an extension of what the conqueror is. [[caesar]] did this to [[floor-01-red]]. See [[floor-extension]].

## House Mirroring
The House mirrors real-world measurement and organizational systems (clocks, calendars, card suits, solstice periods) as structural analogies with actual power attached. Not decorative — functional. Players from outside can use these mirrors as maps. See [[house-mirroring]].

## Temporal Hierarchy
The ranked lieutenant structure on every controlled floor — Hours (highest), Minutes, Seconds — each imbued with floor power by the controller. Part of the House's clock mirroring. See [[temporal-hierarchy]].
