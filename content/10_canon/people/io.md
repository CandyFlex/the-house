# IO

## Summary
The Originator of Freedom. Current mantle-holder of Floor 1. Took control of the floor upon Caesar's death. Where Caesar reshaped the floor into a cruelty-scape, IO reshapes it into a dungeon system — pocket realms that cycle, collapse, and spawn anew, driven by themed item pools and soul-stone economies. Freedom, not cruelty. The floor is becoming what IO is.

## Established
- **What IO is**: An [[originators|Originator]] — the concept of freedom itself achieving sentience within the House.
- **Mantle-holder**: Current holder of Floor 1's mantle. Caesar held it before. Caesar reshaped the floor into a cruelty-scape. IO is undoing that — the soul storm is breaking apart, the fabricated horizon is revealing itself, the cruelty is decomposing.
- **The dungeon system**: IO's expression of freedom imposed over Caesar's cruelty-scape. Each dungeon is a pocket realm that absorbs the floor's ambient history and transforms it. Completed dungeons collapse and merge, spawning something new. IO decides what direction the new spawn takes.
- **Caesar's spell focus**: Woe — Caesar's daughter — passed Caesar's original spell focus (a long silver metamorphic wand) to IO in her dying moments.
- **Castle metamorphosis**: Upon Caesar's death and IO taking the mantle, Caesar's tower and castle underwent a metamorphic transformation — the castle changed form in response to its new owner.
- **Relationship to Caesar**: Caesar was an unintentional Originator. An intentional Originator helped him become the last mantle-holder of Floor 1 — someone deliberately installed Caesar. IO's relationship to that prior installation is unresolved.

## Details
- Where Caesar's floor was freezing, brutal, and extractive, IO's floor is becoming warm, generative, and cyclical. The dungeon system is the mechanism.
- IO does not appear directly. IO's presence is felt through the dungeons — the pocket realms, the soul-stone economy, the admin architecture that lets backstage operators adjust difficulty, spawn rates, and reward pools.
- The mycelial network connecting dungeons (first observed between Crystal Trench and Fungal Depths) may be IO's doing, or may be an anomaly IO did not intend. Flagged for escalation.
