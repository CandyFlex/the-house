# Ball

## Summary
An outside entity stranded on Floor 1 as a child during the Architect's first recruitment era, now fully House-assimilated chipmunk. His appearance tracks his magical hunger: fully dressed carnival solar-punk steampunk = fed; elongated, malnourished, unclothed = starving. He is an analog for what the House does to outsiders.

## Established
- Form: chipmunk. Mischievous, chaotic.
- Originally an entity from **outside the House** — not native to it.
- Among the first children brought in during the Architect's early outside-recruitment phase.
- Stranded when something prevented the recruiters' return after his first run bringing people back to Outpost July. The cause is a sealed mystery.
- The stranding seeded the addiction; extended Floor 1 exposure transformed his form progressively into the chipmunk — the endpoint of full House assimilation of an outside entity.
- **Health spectrum (clothes as diagnostic)**:
  - Fed / high: Tailored carnival/solar-punk/steampunk ensemble, red frills, perky, vibrant.
  - Starving / depleted: Clothes fall away progressively; form elongates and thins; malnourished, creature-like.
  - The clothing is not fashion. It is a health indicator.
- **The hunger**: Consumes anything magical — soul stones, vials of suspended magic prevalent on Floor 1, soul rift energy, magical items. The drive is an unknown internal craving; Ball does not know its origin.
- Spent millennia wandering Floor 1 after the stranding. Periodically haunted Outpost July's exterior, approaching Valus aloofly, losing memories of what he was before.
- Valus knew him as a child, thought him dead, observed his returns — each time more eroded.
- **Mercenary era**: Brought arrivals in through various means for various reasons in great excess, as hunger worsened.
- **Introduction to players**: Hired by an unnamed blue-robed figure to bring the players in. When payment (addiction-substance related) fell through, Ball destroyed the man in front of the players, freed them, transported them to Floor 1.
- Liquefied on crossing Outpost July's threshold in front of the players. Cause unresolved: intentional, a quirk, or Valus's presence affecting him.
- Relationship with players: symbiotic-parasitic. Brought them in through nefarious means. Disclosed obliquely that he had done this many times before.
- **Wanted poster** at Outpost July's entrance arches: Caesar's bounty. Prior employment relationship with Caesar. Bring-in preferred; kill-order possible if debt or offense was severe enough.

## Details
- Ball is the clearest analog in canon for what the House does to outsiders: takes them in, transforms them completely, eventually makes them native.
- His two-axis state: hunger (immediate, visible in clothes) and assimilation depth (long-term, visible in form). The chipmunk is the endpoint of the assimilation axis.
- His oblique self-disclosure to the players suggests fragmented self-awareness rather than deliberate deception.
