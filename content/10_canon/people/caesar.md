# Caesar

## Summary
The Originator of Cruelty — not merely cruel, but the pure concept of cruelty achieving sentience and form. Conquered Floor 1 from its previous holder and reshaped it as a pocket reality expressing his nature. Android body with magical organs as power sources. Currently dead; his death is the present endpoint of the player storyline.

## Established
- **What he is**: An [[originators|Originator]] — the concept of cruelty itself achieving sentience within the House. Power scales with the concept's weight in the House's reality.
- **Form**: Android body with human-like features. May never have been human; deliberately obscures this ambiguity. Carries magical organs inside his chassis as power sources — infuses them, uses them, they degrade (half-life). May form new ones. Original organs may not be restorable.
- **Floor 1 conquest**: Fought other Originators for the floor; took it from its previous holder. Upon conquest, subverted the existing reality and reshaped Floor 1 as a pocket realm — an extension of his cruelty. The soul storm, the desolation, the brutal intake of arrivals: his design.
- **The mirror-kingdom**: His capital at the center of Floor 1. Mirrors channel the soul storm to power him directly. Twelve sons — manifestations of malignant power — serve as solar heralds.
- **Chimerakind**: A religion Caesar seeded unknowingly (to its adherents) across the old Floor 1 pocket realm. He scattered his degraded organs; civilizations formed religions around them believing them divine artifacts. Vampire-chimera-monster mythology aesthetic. Byproduct deities emerged from the organs — semi-sentient "children" who never knew their father.
- **Caesar's bounty on Ball**: Active wanted poster at Outpost July. Ball had a prior working relationship with Caesar; bounty covers unknown debt or offense. Bring-in preferred; kill-order possible.
- **Execution of Valus**: Staged or performed in front of Ball. Players witnessed it in a compromised reality. Whether it was real is unresolved.
- **The organ mission**: After the players returned from Floor 7 (being hunted by [[the-wight]]), Caesar leveraged [[nero]]'s situation and the WIGHT threat to force the players on a retrieval mission: recover his scattered organs from the old pocket realm. Players went reluctantly.
- **Death**: Caesar died after the players returned from the organ mission. Circumstances were partly the players' doing, partly circumstantial. Death described as lackluster — anticlimactic given his scale. Possibly weakened by needing the organs back more than he had accounted for. **This is the current endpoint of the player storyline.** Full details deferred to timeline dump.
- **Pre-cruelty nature**: Before taking the mantle of cruelty, Caesar was a very powerful spellcaster. He gave up his magic as his first act of cruelty — the first thing he sacrificed was the thing he loved most.
- **Spell focus**: Caesar's original spell focus was a long silver metamorphic wand, which carries the residue of his original magic. He gave the wand to his daughter Woe; she passed it to IO in her dying moments.
- **Daughter Woe**: Caesar had a daughter named Woe from the old displaced Floor 1 realm. She was a moon/moon-mana associated deity. Deceased — perished after bridging into current Floor 1. NOT a byproduct deity. See [[woe]].
- **Originator origin**: Caesar was an unintentional Originator. An intentional Originator helped him become the last mantle-holder of Floor 1 — someone deliberately installed Caesar on Floor 1.
- **Death aftermath — castle metamorphosis**: Upon Caesar's death and new ownership taking control, Caesar's tower and castle underwent a metamorphic transformation — the castle changed form in response to its new owner.
- **Death aftermath — pocket realm release**: Caesar's death released the displaced pocket realm's loose entities, including Woe and the false hydra known as Stable. A portion of the displaced original Floor 1 reality has retaken its place.

## Details
- Caesar is the floor-as-extension mechanic made explicit: he didn't just conquer Floor 1, he became it.
- The organ system makes him both powerful and vulnerable — the organs are sources of corruption and exertion, but their loss or degradation costs him.
- Chimerakind's unknowing worship is a recurring Caesar pattern: he benefits from devotion without proximity. The children-who-never-knew-the-father structure is intentional.
