# Slaughterkind

## Summary
An ethereal variant of Kind (the second false hydra). Kind publicly executed Slaughterkind to keep the peace, but Slaughterkind still haunts a cave beneath the Dark Manor as an ethereal entity.

## Established
- A variant of Kind — a descendant in the false hydra lineage.
- Ethereal type — potentially incorporeal or ghostly.
- Kind publicly executed Slaughterkind to maintain peace.
- Despite execution, Slaughterkind still haunts a cave beneath the Dark Manor.
- No equine relation — earlier transcription artifacts were garbled.

## Details
- Slaughterkind's continued haunting after execution suggests false hydras may persist in ethereal form after physical death.
- The cave beneath the Dark Manor is his current known location.
