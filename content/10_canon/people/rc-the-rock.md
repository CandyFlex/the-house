# RC the Rock

## Summary
A sentient, quantum-entangled stone — one of the few surviving stones of the Altar of Knowledge on Floor 1. The wisest of the wise stones of the altar. Knows the vast history of Floor 1 and an unabridged version of the House's creation story. Currently weakened or shackled due to his connection to the new mantle-holder.

## Established
- One of the first stones of the Altar of Knowledge on Floor 1.
- One of the few stones of the altar that remain.
- Quantum-entangled sentient stone — ever-visible and adaptable.
- Was formerly omnipresent on Floor 1: could appear anywhere at any time to perceive the floor's history and events in real time.
- Currently nerfed/shackled — his presence and awareness are reduced due to his connection to the new mantle-holder of Floor 1.
- Holds the most unabridged account of the House's creation mythos, including the true sequence of Originator emergence and the purpose of the Will.
- The Altar of Knowledge was the first place the Architect laid down the Will; RC and the other stones were infused by the Architect's presence and the Will itself.

## Details
- RC represents a mass repository of Floor 1's accumulated knowledge.
- His power level is tied to the will of the mantle-holder — the connection to IO limits his former omni-presence.
- Gave the party the "unembellished" creation story: Darkness as first Originator, intentional vs. unintentional Originators, the Will as codification to prevent unintentional spawning.
