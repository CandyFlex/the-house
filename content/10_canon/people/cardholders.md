# Cardholders

## Summary
Will fragments that have manifested strongly enough to form "emboldened bodies" — tangible, present forms. Emerged as an unintentional consequence of the Will's fragmentation. The Architect did not plan for this. Also referred to as "cards" or "cards of the Will." Can serve as qualified witnesses for Floor 1 mantle registration (Step 9).

## Established
- Will fragments that have manifested into tangible, present forms ("emboldened bodies").
- An unintentional consequence of the Will's fragmentation — the Architect did not design or anticipate cardholders.
- Can serve as qualified witnesses for the Step 9 (The Witness) of the Floor 1 mantle registration process.
- Also called "cards" or "cards of the Will" interchangeably.
- Their emboldened bodies are distinct from the typical fragment form — they are more present and embodied.

## Details
- The existence of cardholders suggests the Will's fragments have agency and evolutionary capacity — they can grow beyond fragment status into full manifestations.
- Their role as witnesses in the mantle registration process gives them a formal cosmological function despite their accidental origin.
- Nomenclature is fluid: cardholders / cards / cards of the Will all refer to the same phenomenon.
