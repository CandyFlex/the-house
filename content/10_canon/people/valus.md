# Valus

## Summary
An outside-human brought into the House by the Architect during the first outsider-recruitment phase. A retired casino/bar owner turned hospitality entrepreneur — selected for his welcoming nature. He ran Outpost April — the entry point of the House. Served "elder children" and "first children" of the House. Status: missing/dead — RC confirmed he is likely no longer around. Would have been the party's greatest potential ally.

## Established
- **Original form**: Human. Wealthy, go-getter, self-made entrepreneur. Affinity for hospitality and engineering. Retired casino/bar owner.
- **Recruitment**: Brought in by the Architect during the first outsider-recruitment phase — not at the House's founding, but when the Architect first decided to bring in people from outside.
- **Assignment**: The Architect assigned him Outpost April — the entry point of the House, not Outpost July. He served as hospitality for "elder children" and "first children" of the House.
- **Current form**: ~10-ft shadow humanoid, can scale 8-20 ft, 4-6 tentacles, wispy older-man's voice. Has been in this form for the vast majority of his existence. The human is effectively gone.
- **Skill evolution**: As Valus lost workers over time he developed the ability to create and modify magical instruments. Eventually added sentience to constructs — not original skillset, built out of necessity. Carries his hospitality instincts into engineering.
- **Ball**: Took Ball in as a child during the early years. Sent him out on the first recruitment run. Thought him dead after the stranding. Observed Ball's eroded returns over the millennia — aloof, haunting, never fully approaching.
- **Caesar association**: Caesar later worked with Valus or used his outpost as the entry point. Caesar's execution of Valus was witnessed (in compromised reality) by Ball.
- **Crafter diffusion**: Valus had become so close to nothing (shadow form, soul crystal episode) that his constructions carry fragments of him. The arachnid mount, the outpost systems, the haunted arachnid area — all hold pieces. Some of those creations know it. Some don't.
- **The blood-box arachnid**: Repurposed a blood-box to make the spider-type carrier creature (the arachnid mount).
- **RC's observation**: RC observed everything Valus did from outside but never personally interacted.
- **RC's assessment**: "One of the safest bets of people that would be great on your side" — but confirmed Valus is likely NOT around anymore. The party's potential greatest ally — now vanished.

## Details
- Valus is the Geppetto of the setting: a creator who poured himself into his work so completely that the work now holds him.
- The crafter diffusion mechanic is not unique to Valus — he is the players' first encounter with a world-level truth about near-nothing beings and their creations.
- His degradation arc mirrors Ball's but from a different cause: not consumption from outside but erosion from within — too much creation, too much loss, too much time.
- **Canon correction**: Earlier references to Valus running Outpost July were incorrect. Valus ran Outpost April. Outpost July content has been disassociated from Valus.
