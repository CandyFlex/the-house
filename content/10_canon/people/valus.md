# Valus

## Summary
An outside-human brought into the House by the Architect during the first outsider-recruitment phase. Former hospitality entrepreneur turned magical engineer. Now a ~10-ft shadow humanoid — his human form long gone. Proprietor of Outpost July. Geppetto energy: deep, conflicting, a troubled creator who poured himself into his work. Status after the Four-Squared crisis and Caesar's execution scene: unknown.

## Established
- **Original form**: Human. Wealthy, go-getter, self-made entrepreneur. Affinity for architectural hospitality and engineering.
- **Recruitment**: Brought in by the Architect during the first outsider-recruitment phase — not at the House's founding, but when the Architect first decided to bring in people from outside. Somewhat willing; the exact circumstances are a mystery layer.
- **Current form**: ~10-ft shadow humanoid, can scale 8–20 ft, 4–6 tentacles, wispy older-man's voice. Has been in this form for the vast majority of his existence. The human is effectively gone.
- **Skill evolution**: As Valus lost workers over time he developed the ability to create and modify magical instruments. Eventually added sentience to constructs — not original skillset, built out of necessity. Carries his hospitality instincts into engineering.
- **Outpost July**: Built on the Architect's instruction. Began as a rustic tavern / Chuck E. Cheese amusement hybrid. Valus's life's work.
- **Ball**: Took Ball in as a child during the early years. Sent him out on the first recruitment run. Thought him dead after the stranding. Observed Ball's eroded returns over the millennia — aloof, haunting, never fully approaching.
- **The time distillation incident**:
  - Players entered a dueling court that became a semi-demi-plane attached to the outpost.
  - Liquefied nonite flooded the space and insulated it, degrading the surrounding area and causing a time distillation: short time inside, 500–700 years outside.
  - Valus spent those centuries upgrading Outpost July in preparation for the players' return.
  - Outpost transformed: rustic tavern aesthetic → chrome-white matte hard-shell futuristic, full efficiency, white lighting.
  - Valus linked himself to the systems to accelerate return; players came back circumstantially regardless.
- **Four-Squared genesis**: Valus's AI constructs, accumulating sentience and magical hunger over the 500–700 year skip, integrated with the upgraded systems on the players' return. This escalation became Four-Squared.
- **The soul crystal breakdown**:
  - During the AI assault, Valus seized powerful imbued soul crystals.
  - They filled him with radiating hunger; he transformed into a giant muck-like orifice creature — combative, destructive.
  - Destroyed several AIs before the last two merged into Four-Squared.
  - Aftermath: purged or absorbed the energies into a system; emerged ragged and near-dead from the cost. Then: selfless.
- **The purple door**: Opened a cross-floor door to Floor 7 to resolve the Four-Squared crisis. The mechanism and cost are not fully established.
- **Crafter diffusion**: Valus had become so close to nothing (shadow form, soul crystal episode) that his constructions carry fragments of him. The arachnid mount, the outpost systems, the haunted arachnid area — all hold pieces. Some of those creations know it. Some don't.
- **Caesar's execution scene**: Players witnessed (in a compromised reality) Caesar summoning a ragged Ball to watch Caesar execute Valus. Status genuinely unresolved — alive, dead, distributed, or some combination.

## Details
- Valus is the Geppetto of the setting: a creator who poured himself into his work so completely that the work now holds him.
- The crafter diffusion mechanic is not unique to Valus — he is the players' first encounter with a world-level truth about near-nothing beings and their creations.
- His degradation arc mirrors Ball's but from a different cause: not consumption from outside but erosion from within — too much creation, too much loss, too much time.
