# The Architect

## Summary
The creator of the House. Known only through what he made. No confirmed description, form, or personal history. Missing for an immeasurably long time. Possibly distributed through his major constructions — not as erosion (as with Valus) but as an inherent aspect of what he built.

## Established
- Created the House.
- Created the Scaffolding.
- Created or is the origin of nonite/nothing — either wielded it or is the condition from which it is a byproduct.
- Created the Will: a living legal/cosmological document intended to govern the House in his absence.
- Brought Valus into the House; assigned him Outpost July.
- Recruited others alongside Valus for the Floor 1 project — some also from outside.
- Nobody has seen him in an immeasurably long time. Current status unknown — gone, absent, or present in some distributed form.
- **Possible distributed presence**: May exist across his major constructions intentionally — distinct from Valus's involuntary crafter diffusion. If so, it is design, not erosion.
- Nonite/nothing may be a byproduct of his existence or actions, not a substance he consciously deploys.
- **Creation narrative (via RC the Rock)**: The Architect came into being or was reality itself. Every action he took resulted in unintentional or intentional Originators — they were the pure embodiment of the intention or unintention of his existence.
- **Darkness** was the first Originator — it met the Architect when he came into being.
- The second Originator was most likely the intentional thought of the Architect greeting.
- The official propagandized version records the sequence as: Architect, then Darkness, then Light, then many more.
- The Will was written to codify reality and prevent further unintentional Originator spawning — it represents the true order and stabilized idea of what the House is.
- After the Will was completed, "encapsulation" occurred — the moment the House became what it is today.
- Everything outside the House is pure unintentional manifestation — the Architect's codification warded off the irradiation of reality outside his intentional design.

## Details
- The Architect is absence and inference. He is known by what he built, not by any direct account.
- The distinction between Valus's crafter diffusion (involuntary, from losing control) and the Architect's possible distributed presence (intentional, from total mastery) is unconfirmed but load-bearing if true.
