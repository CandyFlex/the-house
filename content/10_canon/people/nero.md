# Nero

## Summary
An Originator. The greatest betrayer — a rare Originator subtype that seeks conflict with other Originators to absorb, concentrate, or supersede them. Has killed many (likely hundreds) of unintentional Originators — lost count. His true feat: has killed exactly ONE intentional Originator, which is extraordinarily difficult. His nature as a betrayer makes him an anomaly in a system where most Originators are protected by the Will from open conflict.

## Established
- Is an Originator of "notable type" — a betrayer.
- Betrayers are Originators who seek conflict to absorb, concentrate, or supersede other Originators.
- This makes Nero distinct from most Originators, who are protected by the Will's laws from open conflict with each other.
- Nero is the worst (greatest) of all betrayers.
- Has killed many (likely hundreds) of unintentional Originators — he has lost count of how many.
- **Secret: has only killed ONE intentional Originator.** This is the real feat — intentional Originators are extraordinarily difficult to kill.
- Originators are very, very, very difficult to kill, making Nero's kill count remarkable.
- Most Originators are NOT in constant strife with each other — this is a misapprehension. The Will's laws and protections on multiple floors prevent open conflict.
- Betrayers operate outside these normal protections.
