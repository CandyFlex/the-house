# Woe

## Summary
Caesar's actual daughter from the old displaced Floor 1 realm. A moon/moon-mana associated deity. Deceased — perished after bridging into current Floor 1, one of the first pocket realm deities to die from the transition. Spoke to RC in the night and to IO in her final moments, giving IO Caesar's original spell focus wand.

## Established
- **NOT a byproduct deity.** Caesar's actual daughter from the old displaced Floor 1 realm.
- Caesar gave her his original spell focus wand before she perished.
- Deceased — she died after bridging into current Floor 1.
- Most pocket realm deities are dying after bridging into current Floor 1. Woe is one who has already perished.
- Spoke to RC (RC the Rock) in the night before her death.
- In her final moments, gave Caesar's spell focus wand to IO.
- A "magical god" — associated with moon and moon-mana.
- Praised by vampires for her magic; enabled vampires to walk during the day (at least for one festival night).
- **Chimerakind connection**: Chimerakind praised her and swore souls to her. On her deathbed, she regretted this connection — frustrated that it traced back to Caesar, even though Chimerakind never knew he was her father.
- Associated with the displaced pre-Caesar Floor 1 pocket realm area.

## Details
- Previously referred to as "Moon Goddess" before her name was established. That designation is deprecated.
- Her nature as Caesar's daughter rather than a byproduct alters understanding of pocket realm deities — they may be genuine offspring or creations rather than accidental byproducts.
- Her death is part of a broader phenomenon: pocket realm deities are dying as their displaced realms bridge into the current Floor 1 reality.
- The Chimerakind connection is religious/devotional in nature — they worshipped her unknowingly because of her father's nature, not because she was made from them.
