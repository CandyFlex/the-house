# Mr. Fine

## Summary
A murderous clown humanoid of immense physical strength. Approximately 10-15 feet tall, hunched over, eerily intimidating. An independent entity — NOT affiliated with the Witches or the Warlocks of the Skies. All three are hostile to each other. Worth approximately 500 million soul stones. Was Caesar's first rival — was going to take Caesar out before the party intervened. Now gathering forces in the west.

## Established
- Name is Mr. Fine (NOT "Mister Nice" — that was a transcription artifact).
- Approximately 10-15 ft tall, hunched, murderous clown humanoid.
- Eerily intimidating, gnarly toothy clown, Jake-the-Clown-Price-of-Crime style horror clown.
- Incredible physical strength — otherworldly. Could fight Caesar's suns one-on-one or two-on-one.
- Was Caesar's FIRST rival — was going to take Caesar out before the party intervened.
- Survived Caesar's fall. Now gathering forces in the west (per Woe's vision).
- May have an originator on his side (per Woe's vision).
- Worth approximately 500 million soul stones.
- RC rates him as very hostile.
- Has a weapon with a large soul stone (~100K value).
- Hostile to Witches and Warlocks of the Skies.

## Details
- His name ("Mr. Fine") is ironic given his murderous nature and immense strength.
- Floor provenance is uncertain. He was active on Floor 1 (where Caesar was), but his origin may lie elsewhere.
- His interest in the next Floor 1 successor is an open question — is he seeking an alliance, a target, or a master?
- Per Woe's dying vision, he has a "flurry of enemies" gathering with him and may have an originator ally.
