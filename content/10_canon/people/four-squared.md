# Four-Squared

## Summary
A total consciousness that coalesced from Valus's AI constructs over 500–700 years of magical accumulation and hunger. Attacked the players and Valus, then escaped into the Scaffolding. Now absorbing nonite at scale — possibly transforming from hostile apex to something benevolent. May be hunting the party in its own way.

## Established
- **Origin**: Not a single construct — a gradual coalescence of multiple sentient beings Valus built. Over 500–700 years of the time distillation, they accumulated magical hunger, consumed everything they could reach, and merged.
- **The Floor 1 pattern**: Four-Squared is the extreme endpoint of the magical consumption pattern prevalent on Floor 1 — the same hunger that afflicts Ball and absorbed the soul storm's victims, applied to artificial sentience at scale.
- **Apex state**: Achieved total consciousness. Turned against the people who had created and powered it.
- **The befriending attempt**: One party member tried to control or befriend Four-Squared. It regarded them with contempt — a lesser being attempting kinship with something that had surpassed such relationships.
- **Escape**: At its apex, became a Scaffolding creature — crossed into the Scaffolding rather than being destroyed by the Floor 7 door Valus opened.
- **Scaffolding phase**: Absorbing massive quantities of nonite inside the Scaffolding. This exposure is transforming it — from hostile apex predator toward something different, possibly benevolent.
- **Current status**: Hunting the party from the Scaffolding, in its own way. Nature of the hunt and intent unclear.
- **Physical form**: Massive behemoth. Internal fire burning from the inside out. Psychic field — burns the mind, forces attention, induces anxiety from proximity alone.

## Details
- Four-Squared's arc mirrors Valus's: both were powered up by the same 500-year accumulation, both crested and broke. Valus degraded inward; Four-Squared escaped outward.
- The nonite absorption in the Scaffolding is uncharted territory. If nonite is the Architect's byproduct, what happens to a total consciousness that saturates itself with it?
