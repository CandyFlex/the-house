# Outpost July

## Summary
[[valus]]'s establishment on [[floor-01-red]]. One of twelve monthly outposts built across Floor 1 at the Architect's instruction — July is among the few survivors. Built into a 300-ft cliff face; non-Euclidean interior containing tavern, arcade, dueling courts, and lodging. Now functionally abandoned following Valus's apparent death and the Four-Squared crisis.

## Established
- **The name**: One of twelve outposts, each named for a calendar month. Most have been destroyed — Caesar eliminated some deliberately; attrition took others. July is a survivor. See [[outpost-system]].
- Located on a 300-ft wall facing the radial chasm of Floor 1.
- Exterior: steampunk arches; desolate-facing.
- Interior far larger than the exterior implies — extends deep into the mountain.
- Contains: tavern facade, banners, tables, drinks, vending (including a liminal vending machine styled as a small tavern); automaton staff.
- Six tiered amusement areas including dueling combat-sport courts with reward economies.
- Guest lodging.
- Built and maintained by [[valus]]; originally assigned by [[the-architect]].
- **The time-skip**: A nonite-insulation incident in the dueling courts caused a time distillation — short time inside, 500–700 years outside. Valus spent those centuries retrofitting the interior: original rustic tavern / Chuck E. Cheese aesthetic → chrome-white matte, hard-shell futuristic, full-efficiency design.
- Site of the Four-Squared crisis and Valus's soul crystal breakdown.
- **Current state**: Functionally abandoned after Valus's apparent death. Desolate. The futuristic retrofit remains but unmaintained.
- **Ball's presence**: Players returned to find Ball in a decrepit state — depleted, starving end of his health spectrum — near the outpost. He helped them leave.
- **Wanted poster**: Caesar's bounty on [[ball]] posted at the entrance arches.

## Details
- Functions as both first-arrival hospitality and combat-sport venue.
- The dueling courtyards have a "vacuum capsule" mechanism that isolates a fight from the rest of Outpost July, creating temporal pockets.
