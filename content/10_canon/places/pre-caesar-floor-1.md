# Pre-Caesar Floor 1

## Summary
The original reality of [[floor-01-red]] before [[caesar]]'s conquest. A lush, infinite magical medieval forest with pockets of grim fairy-tale civilization. Displaced into Caesar's pocket realm during the Great Calamity. Still exists there, preserved. The party does not know this.

## Established
- **Landscape**: Lush, ever-going green meadows and magical medieval forest extending to infinite distance.
- **Civilization**: Pockets of settlement scattered through the forest — grim fairy-tale aesthetic in tone.
- **A prior controller**: Floor 1 had a controlling entity before Caesar who has not yet been identified.
- **Current location**: Displaced into the pocket realm Caesar took from this floor. Preserved there, inaccessible from the current Floor 1 under normal circumstances.
- **Party knowledge**: The players do not know the original Floor 1 exists or that Caesar displaced it.

## Relationships
- [[floor-01-red]] — this is what Floor 1 was before Caesar reshaped it
- [[caesar]] — conquered and displaced it
- [[the-great-calamity]] — the event that ended it
- [[chimerakind]] — religion that emerged within the displaced pocket realm here

## Open Questions
- Who was the prior Floor 1 controller?
- Is the displaced original floor still inhabited — did anyone survive the displacement inside it?
- Can it be accessed from within Caesar's pocket realm?
- Does Caesar's death change the pocket realm's accessibility?

## Key Takeaways
- Floor 1 was once green, infinite, and fairy-tale dark. Caesar erased it.
- It still exists — locked away. A world the players haven't found yet.
