# Outpost August

## Summary
One of the twelve monthly outposts on Floor 1. An observatory that can view the adjacent floor (Orange / Floor 2) and — uniquely — can look outside the House to the Unintentional Realms. No staff — functions independently as a mechanagical installation.

## Established
- One of the twelve monthly outposts on Floor 1.
- Functions as an observatory — can look outward into any adjacent space.
- Can view Floor 2 (Orange), the sequential neighbor of Floor 1.
- Is the only known way to look "outside" the House.
- Outside the House lies the Unintentional Realms — pure unintentional manifestations of reality.
- Part of the original outpost infrastructure commissioned by the Architect.
- **No staff.** Functions independently as a mechanagical installation.

## Details
- The observatory's ability to see outside the House refines the "no outside" principle.
- The Unintentional Realms visible through August are the actual outside of the House's ordered reality.
- The ability to see into adjacent floors may be a property shared by other outposts, or unique to August.
