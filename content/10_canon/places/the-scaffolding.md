# The Scaffolding

## Summary
The exterior construction-ground of the House. Not a project to be completed — a permanent condition. The zone where the House's architecture meets whatever lies outside it. Infinite and ongoing. May have been intended for a specific purpose that was never pursued. Currently hosts [[four-squared]], who is absorbing nonite there at scale.

## Established
- The Scaffolding exists at the exterior of the House's reality — inside the House, yet somehow external to it.
- Infinite and ongoing — not a work-in-progress but a permanent zone of active creation.
- Players understand it through the frame of a construction site; this is the nearest approximation, not an accurate description.
- May have been intended for a purpose the Architect never pursued. What that purpose was (if any) is unknown.
- Built throughout from [[nonite]] — the densest concentration of nonite yet observed.
- Atmosphere: overcast gray light, abysmal, glowy; surfaces obsidian black.
- Reachable from within The House; entered by the players from [[outpost-july]] during the Four-Squared crisis.
- **Four-Squared** escaped into the Scaffolding at its apex and is currently absorbing nonite there at scale.
- The Architect is the likely origin of the Scaffolding — possibly its first expression of nothing/nonite as structural substance.
- Categorized alongside [[the-spire]] as one of the House's "internal sub-realms" — neither a floor nor a pocket demiplane.
