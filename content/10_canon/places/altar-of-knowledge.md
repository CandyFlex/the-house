# The Altar of Knowledge

## Summary
The first place on Floor 1 where the Architect laid down the Will. An ancient stone altar infused by the Architect's presence and the Will itself. Most of its stones are gone; only a few remain, including the sentient RC the Rock.

## Established
- Located on Floor 1 — explicitly the first floor.
- The first place the Architect laid down the Will.
- From the moment the Will was placed on it, everything connected to the altar was infused by the Architect's presence and the Will's codification.
- Known for capturing and holding vast knowledge — the stones absorb and retain information.
- Only a few stones of the altar remain. RC is one of them and retains sentience.
- Predates Caesar's conquest of Floor 1 — the altar was present before the Great Calamity.

## Details
- The altar's stones were created as part of the earliest construction on Floor 1.
- The Will being laid on the altar was a pivotal moment — the moment the House became what it is today, after which "encapsulation" occurred.
- The surviving stones may retain some of the altar's knowledge-gathering properties.
