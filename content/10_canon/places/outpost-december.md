# Outpost December

## Summary
One of the twelve monthly outposts on Floor 1. A vault facility using a reverse-osmosis process that pulls soul-stone energy (red) from the soul storm and refines it into higher-quality currency increments. Can process up to 15 million units per day, with a vault capacity of up to half a billion. No staff — functions independently as a mechanagical installation.

## Established
- One of the twelve monthly outposts on Floor 1.
- Uses a reverse-osmosis process to extract and refine soul-stone energy from the red storm / soul storm.
- The red storm and soul storm are the same phenomenon — Floor 1's permanent soul-storm boundary.
- Refining capacity: up to 15 million units per day.
- Vault storage capacity: up to half a billion units.
- Produces higher-quality soul stone denominations, including L Dawn and L Night stones.
- Part of the original outpost infrastructure commissioned by the Architect.
- **No staff.** Functions independently as a mechanagical installation.

## Details
- The reverse-osmosis process takes raw soul-storm energy and remanifests it into refined, high-value stone increments.
- Soul stones refined here include the L Dawn (white) and L Night (black) denominations, worth 1 million to 100 million each.
- The vault's existence suggests the outpost system had economic as well as arrival/magistrate functions.
