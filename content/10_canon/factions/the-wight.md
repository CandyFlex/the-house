# The WIGHT

## Summary
The House's authority entity — a sentient force or organization that hunts those who violate the House's order. Actively hunted the players after they returned from Floor 7. Caesar leveraged the WIGHT's pursuit as coercive power over the party.

## Established
- Functions as the House's authority / enforcement.
- Sentient — not a mindless system.
- Was hunting the players when they returned from Floor 7.
- Caesar weaponized this pursuit: comply with his mission or be handed over to the WIGHT (or face Caesar directly).

## Relationships
- [[caesar]] — leveraged the WIGHT's pursuit as coercion against the players
- [[the-house]] — House-level authority entity

## Open Questions
- What is the WIGHT's form — a single entity, a faction, a distributed force?
- What law or authority does it enforce — the Will, Caesar's law, something older?
- Why was it hunting the players specifically?
- Does the WIGHT persist now that Caesar is dead?

## Key Takeaways
- House-level authority. Sentient. Dangerous enough that Caesar could use its threat as leverage.
