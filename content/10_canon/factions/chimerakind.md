# Chimerakind

## Summary
An ancient religion developed unknowingly around Caesar's scattered organs, inside the old Floor 1 pocket realm. Adherents believed they were worshipping foreign deities; they were worshipping Caesar under different names. Vampire-chimera-monster mythology aesthetic. Byproduct deities emerged from the organs themselves — semi-sentient children of Caesar who never knew their father.

## Established
- Caesar seeded his degraded organs throughout the old pre-Caesar pocket realm of Floor 1.
- Civilizations there developed religions around these organs — each believing the organs were divine artifacts of independent deities.
- The religions are unknowingly Caesar-worship under different names and mythological frames.
- Byproduct deities: the organs developed their own semi-sentience — children of Caesar who have no knowledge of their paternity.
- Aesthetic: vampire, chimera, monster mythology.
- The players were sent by Caesar to retrieve the organs from this realm.
- **Woe**: Chimerakind praised and swore souls to her. She regretted this connection on her deathbed — frustrated that it traced back to Caesar, even though Chimerakind never knew he was her father. See [[woe]].
