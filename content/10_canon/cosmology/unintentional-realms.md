# Unintentional Realms

## Summary
The meta-space outside the House's ordered reality. Includes: the "main universe" people come from before entering the House, any third-party settings and character backgrounds, any meta-narrative space, and any metaphysical/pocket dimensions created around the House without floor attachment. NOT part of the 7-floor Architect-codified reality. Visible only through the Outpost August observatory. Represents the actual outside — a refinement of the "no outside" principle.

## Established
- Exist outside the House's known 7-floor structure.
- Are pure unintentional manifestations of reality — what the Architect's codification and encapsulation did not shape.
- Include the "main universe" people originate from before entering the House.
- Include any third-party setting, character background, or meta-narrative space associated with the House.
- Include metaphysical/pocket dimensions created around the House that lack floor attachment.
- Are NOT part of the 7-floor Architect-codified reality.
- Visible through Outpost August's observatory — the only known means of observing them.
- The Architect's order, codification, and encapsulation warded off the "irradiation" or offspring of reality outside the House.
- The "no outside" principle requires refinement: no external space within the House's ordered reality has been observed — but the Unintentional Realms are the actual outside.

## Details
- The Unintentional Realms may be the source of the unintentional Originators that formed before the Will was written.
- They may or may not be accessible — only observation has been confirmed.
- Their existence refines (does not contradict) the "no outside" principle: within the House's ordered reality, no outside has been observed; the Unintentional Realms exist outside that ordered reality entirely.
