# The Will

## Summary
A living legal and cosmological document created by the Architect — intended to function as his proxy governing the House in his absence. Never fully enacted or possibly never completed. Shattered into autonomous fragments, each with its own sentience and personality. The fragments are scattered across the House. Central plot engine.

## Established
- **Intended form**: A single unified sentient entity — a living understanding of the Architect's complete legal and cosmological framework. Not just a document: a consciousness.
- **Purpose**: To maintain order across the House in the Architect's absence. The Architect's proxy — governing, adjudicating, manifesting his intent.
- **Unraveling**: Either the Will was never fully enacted before something went wrong, or it was never finished. Both are live possibilities. The cause is unknown. The shattering may have preceded the Architect's disappearance, caused it, or resulted from it.
- **The fragments**:
  - Each piece carries its own sentience and personality, shaped by what section of the Will it derives from.
  - Scale varies enormously — from large fragments holding substantial portions of the Will's intent, down to slivers of a page, each still magically alive.
  - Personality and behavior derive from content: a fragment concerning law behaves differently from one concerning hospitality, punishment, or creation.
  - Every fragment is a potential character, encounter, or plot engine.
- Valus alluded to the Will and its shattering as the most important thing he left the players with.

## Details
- The Will's shattering is a House-wide scatter of living legal/cosmological entities. Players will encounter fragments across all floors in all sizes.
- Reconstructing the Will — if possible — would be the equivalent of reassembling the Architect's proxy governance of the entire House.

## Key Takeaways

- Every fragment is alive, sentient, shaped by its content.
- The Will's scatter is the central ongoing mechanic of the setting.
