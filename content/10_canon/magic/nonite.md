# Nonite

## Summary
A House substance associated with the Architect — possibly his byproduct, possibly his medium. Exists as ambient `nothing` (raw, unformed) and as `nonite` (worked / manifested form). The Scaffolding is built from it. Liquefied nonite causes temporal distortion. Four-Squared is currently absorbing it at scale.

## Established
- **The pair**: `nothing` is the raw or underlying state of which `nonite` is the worked / manifested form. They are related but distinct: nothing exists ambient or unformed; nonite is nothing after being shaped or applied.
- **Architect association**: Nonite/nothing may be a byproduct of the Architect's existence or actions, rather than a substance he consciously deploys. Deeply present in the House's architecture — not incidental.
- **Building substance**: The Scaffolding is constructed from nonite throughout — the densest concentration yet observed.
- **Temporal distortion**: Liquefied nonite insulating an enclosed space causes time distillation — subjective time inside diverges from objective time outside. The time-distillation incident at Outpost July produced a 500–700 year external skip for a short internal experience.
- **Saturation effects**: Four-Squared is currently absorbing nonite at scale inside the Scaffolding; the effects on a total consciousness are uncharted.

## Details
- Works as both a passive material (Scaffolding construction) and an active reagent (temporal distortion when liquefied).
- Its association with the Architect ties it to the deepest cosmological layer of the House.
