# Floor 1 — Red

Part of [[the-house]]. See [[_concepts]] for load-bearing terminology.

## Summary
The first floor of [[the-house]]. Red in the ROYGBIV spectrum.

## Established
- Red is Floor 1.
- Floor 1 is the entrance when entering [[the-house]] from outside.
- **Caesar's pocket realm**: Floor 1 as it exists now is [[caesar]]'s reshaped pocket reality — a cruelty-scape imposed over the original Floor 1 reality after his conquest. The original [[pre-caesar-floor-1]] exists somewhere, displaced.
- **The soul storm**: A massive ongoing soul storm circling the floor's exterior — fed continuously by dissolving entities, combat casualties, and arrivals who don't survive transition. Permanent atmospheric condition, not an event. Created by Caesar as part of his conquest reshaping (see [[the-great-calamity]]).
- **Carrier creatures**: Nocturnal creatures on Floor 1 carrying Tardis-topology structures. Birds and owls are most common; spiders are exceptionally rare. See [[carrier-creatures]].
- **Magical hunger**: A prevalent pattern on Floor 1 — things that contact powerful magic tend to hunger for it and grow from continued consumption. [[ball]] and [[four-squared]] are extreme expressions of this pattern.
- **The Outpost System**: Twelve outposts, one per calendar month, were the original arrival infrastructure for this floor. Most are destroyed; [[outpost-july]] survives. See [[outpost-system]].
- **Temporal Hierarchy**: The floor's power structure under Caesar included Hours, Minutes, and Seconds as his top three lieutenants. Their identities are unestablished. See [[temporal-hierarchy]].
- Floor 1 is the arrival point from outside — Caesar designed its current state to expose arrivals to his cruelty immediately.
- **The Altar of Knowledge**: A surviving ancient altar on Floor 1 where the Architect first laid down the Will. Only a few stones remain, including the sentient RC the Rock. See [[altar-of-knowledge]].
- **Outpost December**: A surviving outpost functioning as a reverse-osmosis soul-stone refinery, pulling from the soul storm. 15M/day throughput, up to 500M vault. No staff — functions independently as a mechanagical installation. See [[outpost-december]].
- **Outpost August**: A surviving outpost functioning as an observatory. Can view adjacent Floor 2 (Orange) and the Unintentional Realms outside the House. No staff — functions independently as a mechanagical installation. See [[outpost-august]].
- **Outpost July**: Mostly ruined from Caesar's attack in the original campaign. See [[outpost-system]].
- **Soul storm confirmation**: The soul storm is also referred to as the "red storm" — confirming the red = Floor 1 color mapping for this phenomenon.
- **The Dark Manor**: Home of a council of vampires — some of the very first. Located in the displaced pocket realm area. NOT Woe's residence. Slaughterkind haunts a cave beneath it. See [[the-dark-manor]].
- **False hydra lineage**: The displaced pocket realm contains a false hydra lineage: Stable (original), Kind (unstable variant, holding Visha), and Slaughterkind (ethereal variant, executed by Kind, haunts beneath the Dark Manor). These are distinct entities. No equine relation — earlier transcription artifacts were garbled. See [[stable]], [[kind]], [[slaughterkind]].
- **Woe (corrected)**: Previously referred to as "Moon Goddess." Caesar's actual daughter, NOT a byproduct deity. Deceased. See [[woe]].

## Soul Storm
*(also referred to as the red storm)*

A permanent atmospheric phenomenon on Floor 1 — a massive storm of soul-stone energy circling the floor. Source material for Outpost December's reverse-osmosis refinement process. Already referenced in existing canon as Caesar's conquest boundary.

- Referred to interchangeably as the "red storm" and the "soul storm" in the transcript — these are the same phenomenon.
- Outpost December pulls raw soul-stone energy from the soul storm via reverse-osmosis refinement.
- The soul storm is the permanent boundary condition of Floor 1, part of Caesar's conquest reshaping.
- This is consistent with the existing canon description: "a massive ongoing soul storm circling the floor's exterior -- fed continuously by dissolving entities, combat casualties, and arrivals who don't survive transition."

- Does the refinement process affect the storm itself (shrink it, stabilize it)?
