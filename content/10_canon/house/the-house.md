# The House

## Summary
The world itself. Seven-floored, mesophysical, self-contained. All reality manifests from within The House; no external space has been observed.

## Established
- Seven floors, ROYGBIV (Red=1, Orange=2, Yellow=3, Green=4, Blue=5, Indigo=6, Violet=7).
- Floors connect sequentially but no floor is hierarchically above another.
- Floor 1 is the entrance when entering from outside.
- Scale within each floor is non-Euclidean.
- No outside has ever been observed.
- Colors are structural, not decorative. Each floor's color carries thematic weight, archetype, and cosmological signature.
- References to "outside" from within The House are treated as internal manifestations (psychological, spiritual, or metaphorical) unless explicitly flagged as a cosmological question.
