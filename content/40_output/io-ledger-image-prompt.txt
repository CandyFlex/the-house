=== IO'S SHIFTING LEDGER — IMAGE PROMPT (OIL PAINTING FORMAT) ===

1A fantasy oil painting with long, sweeping brush strokes that create a tactile,
painterly surface. Highlights are built up with thicker paint, while shadows are
smoothed with blended transitions and subtle glazing. Brush marks remain visible
and directional, guiding the eye and suggesting motion without relying on sharp
edges. Colors can softly bleed at boundaries for atmosphere, and lighting is
dramatic.

An open spellbook whose cover and spine refuse to commit to one material — in
places it reads as cracked black leather with tarnished silver corner caps, in
others as layered vellum stitched with thread that does not match the holes it
passes through. The cover shifts between these truths depending on the angle of
the eye. The spine reads "0211" in glyphs that subtly rearrange at the edges of
the number, as though the script is always mid-sentence. The book lies open near
its center. The left page — the anchor — is blank at first inspection, but silver
ink resolves into spell-sigils the longer the eye holds, only to fade when focus
wanders. The right side holds two pages that do not belong: one glowing low
orange and crinkled at its edges as if perpetually about to ignite, the other
pale blue-white and cold, the ink upon it heavy and still. Uneven thread holds
these pages together across the crease — waxed cord in some places, fine silver
wire in others, raw twine in a third — as though an unseen hand is perpetually
repairing the binding with whatever it can find. The book rests on a solid white
background, the faintest shimmer of heat rising from the orange page and a thin
film of frost dusting the edge of the blue-white one.

The lighting is split and disorienting. From the upper left, a warm amber glow
falls across the book's surface, catching the silver thread and the gilded edges
of the pages. But a colder light bleeds upward from the necromantic page itself,
casting pale blue shadows against the warm wash above. Between them, the anchor
page sits in contested light — neither warm nor cold, silver ink catching both
and reflecting neither. Small floating motes of silver pigment hang in the air
above the center crease, suspended as though the page is still writing itself.
