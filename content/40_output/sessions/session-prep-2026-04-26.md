# Session Prep — 2026-04-26
## "The Transfer"

---

## Where We Left Off

The party returned from the Floor 7 organ-retrieval mission. A **false hydra** allied with them used exposure to Caesar's organs to punch back through the portal into Caesar's lair. It accessed the establishment's control panels and began a fake ownership transfer — as if Caesar himself was reassigning the floor.

**IO** synced her **abjuration/illusion Book of Claims** with the system. Compatible alignment. The registration went through partially before the WIGHT's alert fired.

**Amarant** (Originator of Ambition, gorilla, golden dress suit) and **Vancent** (Originator of Misery, lankier ape, white suit, purple skin) arrived to intercept — WIGHT delegates. They'd dealt with the party before on Floor 7 over the Nero incident. They let it go then. They let it go again now, but with the tone of watching something walk toward a cliff.

Vancent **destroyed Caesar** — easy kill, he was already weakened and decrepit. Consumed his power in a red plume. Also destroyed the false hydra entirely.

They left the party with the floor and said almost nothing useful about what holding it actually means.

## The Stage — Active Elements This Session

### The Darkness
- All 12 Suns are down or falling. Floor 1 has no ambient light source.
- Carrier creatures are **nocturnal** — permanent darkness means they are active constantly now, not just at night.
- Soul energy that the Suns were consuming and redirecting has nowhere to go. It may pool, drift, or crystallize unpredictably across the floor.
- Chimerakind are witnessing their gods die in sequence. Expect significant cultural disruption across the floor.

### The Institution *(name pending — IO will name it)*
The central structure, transformed by IO's book alignment pulling from her deepest identity. See room breakdown below.

### R.C.
Amarant and Vancent said someone would come. He arrives shortly after the transformation — a small worn rock, smooth, faint carved traces on the surface. He moves with intention. He talks extensively about things nobody asked.

He believes he has no friends. Not as a complaint. As a fact.

He will find the Scriptorium of Marks and go quiet when he sees the far wall.

### IO's Registration
She holds the administrative layer (Layer 1) — the floor's systems recognize her. The floor itself is still transitioning from Caesar's imprint toward her nature. This will take time and commitment to complete.

## The Institution — Room Reference

### The Gatemark *(entrance)*
> *The arch is taller than it needs to be. There is no door — just a passage, and the passage notices you. Not intelligence. Attention.*

- Logs everything that enters to IO's book
- Those without a Book of Claims feel briefly tallied — not refused, recorded
- Currently just observes; IO can develop it over time

---

### The Hall of Meridian *(central atrium)*
> *Vaulted ceiling, dark stone ribbing. The light comes from soul stone veins running through the walls — cold blue-white, steady. The floor is a deep grey-black stone, unmarked. The proportions of somewhere built to make a point.*

- Where IO addresses the floor's inhabitants formally
- Where Hours/Minutes/Seconds stand when she speaks
- The ceiling holds markings of the floor's old sky — currently dark, accurate
- Will update to reflect whatever IO eventually puts in the sky above the floor

---

### The Scriptorium of Marks *(research archive)*
> *Long room, lower ceiling. Lined floor to ceiling with surfaces meant for records — most of Caesar's material is corrupted or gone. The walls themselves have carvings pressed into them: codes, old registration marks going back centuries. Someone recorded which Books aligned with Floor 1 in case the formal records were ever lost. This predates Caesar considerably.*

- R.C. comes here and does not leave voluntarily
- Research hub for the Books of Claims system — what alignments exist, what books have Floor 1 compatibility
- The old code-carvings predate Caesar; R.C. can read them
- Over time: IO can learn exactly how many Books could contest her claim

**What R.C. can tell them here:**
- "These carvings are older than Caesar's occupation. Someone maintained this before him."
- "The codes indicate alignment. There are seven recorded Floor 1-compatible marks. Yours is one of them."
- (Won't say more without trust being established first)

### The Watch *(observation spire — highest tower)*
> *The climb is significant. The top opens onto a platform with no rail. In the complete darkness below, you can see anything that generates light, heat, or soul-energy. The carrier creatures move in patterns. There are points of light where Chimerakind are gathering. There are dying embers where Caesar's organ-recipients burned out.*

- Strategic intelligence on Floor 1
- An old instrument here reads the floor's condition — population movement, soul energy density, anomalies
- Using it fluently takes practice; R.C. can explain what it does
- Range increases as IO's claim deepens

---

### The Chamber of Appointment *(title installation)*
> *Circular room. One marking on the floor — a register glyph, Floor 1's administrative signature, old enough that nobody knows who cut it. Bare stone walls. No seats. The room feels like it has memory.*

- Where IO installs Hours, Minutes, and Seconds
- The person stands in the glyph; IO uses her book; the floor recognizes the new title-holder
- Title-holders afterward feel the floor — actually feel it — and IO feels a new connection point
- Caesar's old bindings left residue in the glyph — coerced, wrong-aligned. IO can read that history.

---

### The Long Gallery *(Floor 1 history corridor)*
> *Longer than it should be. The walls hold Floor 1's history in carved relief and embedded material. Moving from the far end toward the entrance is moving forward in time.*
>
> *Toward the far end: older materials, different hands. The twilight forest. People who lived here before the Calamity. Things the party has no context for yet. These panels are intact. They are not wrong the way the middle sections are wrong.*

- Lore delivery — each pre-Calamity panel is a discrete piece of recoverable history
- The damaged panels are questions — something is missing from them
- New panels will form near the entrance as IO's era progresses
- R.C. will not explain the far end without being asked directly. When asked, he goes quiet longer than usual.

---

### The Practice Courts *(four training levels)*
> *Four tiered floors. The uppermost is marked with instructional sigils that predate Caesar — a graduated teaching methodology, recoverable if studied. The lowest is raw, impact-scored, scorched in patterns suggesting something was fought here that didn't want to be contained.*

- Training infrastructure for Hours/Minutes/Seconds and recruits
- The upper courts respond differently to registered title-holders vs unregistered individuals
- The sigil system is someone else's methodology — not Caesar's, not IO's. To be identified.

---

### The Undercroft *(control systems)*
> *Below the main floor. The panels here are what the false hydra accessed. This is where Floor 1's administrative infrastructure physically manifests. IO's registration overlays Caesar's existing architecture without having replaced it. Two systems in one space. It is not stable long-term.*

- Shows IO as current registered claimant
- Caesar's registration is a ghost in the system — not active, not gone
- Some things on Floor 1 still respond to Caesar's ghost-registration rather than IO's live one
- Clearing this is a long-term infrastructure project that progressively strengthens her claim
- R.C. will come here if asked. He will monitor the walls the entire time.

---

### The Descent *(pre-Calamity foundations)*
> *The stairs going down from the Undercroft change in material partway — the building's stone gives way to something older, cut by different hands or shaped by something other than tools. The air changes. This is what the building sits on top of.*

- Pre-Calamity Floor 1 saturated these foundations. That saturation never left.
- Carrier creatures nest here — some for generations, since before the Calamity.
- Soul storm origin residue is in the walls.
- Chambers that the transformation did not illuminate. Some have things in them.
- **R.C. on the Descent:** *"That's pre-Calamity. Most of what's down there has never been studied because nobody who wanted to study it survived long enough."*
- **Do not go down here without preparation.** This is not a tonight location unless the party pushes hard.

## NPCs On Deck

### R.C.
- **Voice:** Extensive. Volunteering information adjacent to what was asked. Not unfriendly — just operating as if friendship isn't on the table.
- **Motivation:** He's here because Amarant and Vancent sent him. He'll do the job. He doesn't expect anything more from it.
- **What he knows:** Pre-Calamity Floor 1 in full. Caesar's entire rise. The Spire. Fragments of the Will and the Architect. The Scriptorium's carvings. A lot.
- **What he won't say yet:** What R.C. stands for. What's in the Descent specifically. What he saw during the Calamity that he hasn't processed in 400+ years.
- **Arc trigger:** The first time a party member treats him like a person rather than a resource, something shifts.

### Amarant & Vancent *(not present — but relevant)*
- They're taking on the "gauntlet" of controlling the floor from somewhere above
- They gave the party essentially nothing about how floor control works
- This was a test and a mild death sentence and they know it
- They are NOT enemies. They are also not invested in the party surviving.

## Session Hooks — Moments to Deploy

1. **The Gatemark logs someone.** A name appears in IO's book that nobody recognizes. Someone has walked through.

2. **R.C. goes quiet in the Long Gallery.** He's looking at a specific panel in the pre-Calamity section. He doesn't explain. The panel shows a forest.

3. **The Watch reads an anomaly.** One bright point of soul-energy moving toward the Institution from the far edge of the floor. Too steady to be a carrier creature.

4. **A Chimerakind elder arrives.** Not hostile — shattered. Their gods are dead. They've come to the only lit structure on the floor because they have nowhere else to go.

5. **The Undercroft activates something.** Caesar's ghost-registration responds to a specific action — a door opens somewhere in the building that shouldn't be open. Something Caesar still "owns" is in the Institution.
