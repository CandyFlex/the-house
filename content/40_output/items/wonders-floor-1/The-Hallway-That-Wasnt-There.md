# The Hallway That Wasn't There
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A brass door handle, ornate but unattached — no door, no plate, no keyhole. Just a handle. It is cool to the touch, faintly tarnished, and carved with a spiral pattern that seems to move in your peripheral vision. When you hold it, you feel a gentle tug, like a dog on a leash that has caught an interesting scent three blocks away. The handle wants to be placed against a surface. It wants to open something.

Hold the handle against any solid surface — a wall, a floor, a cliff face, the side of a bookshelf — and turn. A door appears. It opens into a hallway that was not there before and will not be there after. The hallway leads somewhere. You do not know where. Neither does the handle. It finds places, not destinations. It is, in its own way, as surprised as you are.

## Properties

**Open the Wall (1/Long Rest).** As an action, press the handle against a solid surface and turn it. A door-sized opening appears, leading into a hallway that extends into the surface. The hallway is 5 feet wide, 10 feet tall, and extends a variable distance — from 30 feet to 300 feet (the DM rolls 3d100 in secret). At the end of the hallway is another door. Opening that door deposits you at a destination determined by the DM, which can be:

- Another room in the same building or structure
- A location within 1 mile that you have never visited
- A location up to 10 miles away that is conceptually "on the other side" of whatever surface you opened (through the mountain, under the river, inside the vault)
- A memory — a location you have been to before, but at a different time of day, season, or emotional register. The hallway leads to the *feeling* of a place, not the coordinates.

The hallway remains for 1 minute or until you and any companions exit through the far door. The hallway is lit by an indeterminate source (the walls themselves glow faintly). The hallway is absolutely safe; nothing can enter it except those who pass through the near door with you. Creatures inside when the hallway closes are ejected safely at the far end.

**The Handle Decides.** You do not choose the destination. The DM determines where the hallway leads, guided by the principles above. The handle favors interesting over convenient, poetic over practical. It has never stranded anyone lethally. It has stranded people inconveniently many times.

**Two Doors (1/Long Rest, after 7th successful use).** After the handle has been used successfully 7 times since you attuned to it, you may use it a second time per long rest. After 30 successful uses, you may use it three times per long rest. The handle warms up to you. It starts to like where you're going together.

## Balance Notes
A teleportation item with zero control and maximum narrative potential. Compare to *teleport* (7th level, controlled but risky) or *word of recall* (6th level, fixed destination) — the Hallway is lower level but completely unpredictable. The DM has full control over the destination, which makes this a storytelling tool rather than a fast-travel button. The scaling charges reward long-term use and build player attachment to the item. The absolute safety inside the hallway makes it a panic-room option (open a wall, step inside, close the door — 1 minute of guaranteed safety), which increases its power. Very rare feels right given the combination of teleportation, safety, and unlimited potential.

## Variants
- **Rare — The Familiar Hallway:** Always leads to the same place — a fixed location chosen at attunement. 1/long rest. Cannot be changed. Cost: ~180,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — The Handle of Anywhere:** 3/long rest immediately. After 50 uses, you may choose the destination type (memory, nearby, "through," etc.) though not the exact location. The hallway can also open into other planes of existence (DM's discretion on which planes are "on the other side" of your current plane). Cost: ~3,500,000 soul stones.
