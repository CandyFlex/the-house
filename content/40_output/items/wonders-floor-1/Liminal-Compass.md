# Liminal Compass
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A compass whose face is blank — no needle, no markings, just a disk of what appears to be fog trapped under glass. The fog swirls slowly when you hold the compass still and speeds up when you move, as if it's catching glimpses of something just out of frame. The compass does not point north. It points to the nearest **threshold** — any boundary, any transition, any place where one thing becomes another. A doorway. A window. The edge of a shadow. The moment between inhale and exhale. The gap between two cobblestones. The compass finds the cracks in reality.

When you arrive at a threshold, the compass can open it — not into the next room, but into the **Between**. The Between is the space that exists between all spaces: a gray, silent, empty version of the world where time moves differently. One minute in the Between is six seconds in the real world. You can walk through the Between and emerge from any other threshold within range. You can stop in the middle and just... breathe. The Between has no monsters, no weather, no demands. It is the universe's waiting room.

## Properties

**Find the Threshold (passive).** The compass always points to the nearest liminal space within 120 feet — a doorway, archway, window, gate, bridge, tunnel mouth, or any transition between defined areas. If there are multiple thresholds, the compass pulses more intensely toward the most "threshold-like" one (a freestanding arch is more liminal than an open doorway). You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice hidden doors, secret passages, and concealed entrances while holding the compass.

**Step Between (1/Short Rest).** As an action while standing within 5 feet of a threshold, you turn the compass bezel clockwise. You and up to three willing creatures within 5 feet of you step sideways into the Between. The Between is a gray, silent reflection of the world you left — geometry is the same, but color, sound, weather, and creatures are absent. Time passes at a 10:1 ratio: for every 1 minute you spend in the Between, only 6 seconds pass in the real world. The Between extends 500 feet in every direction from your entry point, bounded by an invisible wall you cannot pass.

While in the Between:
- You can see the real world faintly, as if through smoked glass.
- You cannot interact with or be perceived by creatures in the real world.
- You can move freely within the Between's boundaries.
- You can exit the Between as a bonus action through any threshold within the Between's 500-foot radius, emerging in the real world at that location.
- If you do not exit voluntarily, you are ejected at your entry point after 10 minutes of Between-time (1 minute of real time).

**Panic Room.** If you are being pursued, you can enter the Between as a reaction when a creature within 5 feet of you takes a hostile action against you. The reaction triggers before the action resolves. You enter the Between alone (no companions). This counts as your use of Step Between for the short rest.

## Balance Notes
A tactical repositioning and escape tool with a unique time-dilation mechanic. The 10:1 time ratio means 1 minute of real-world planning, healing, or stealth positioning costs you only 1 round of real time — powerful but not infinite. The 500-foot radius and threshold-entry requirement prevent it from being a free *dimension door*. The Panic Room reaction adds survivability without being a guaranteed escape (you can't bring allies, you need a threshold nearby). Compare to *etherealness* (7th level, full plane shift, much longer duration) — the compass is more limited but available multiple times per day (1/short rest). Very rare is appropriate.

## Variants
- **Rare — Threshold Key:** Step Between 1/long rest. No Panic Room. Between radius 100 feet. Time ratio 5:1. Can only bring yourself. Cost: ~180,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — The Infinite Threshold:** Step Between 3/short rest. Between radius 1 mile. Time ratio 60:1 (1 hour in the Between = 1 minute real time). You can open thresholds into the Between from anywhere (no existing threshold required). Cost: ~4,200,000 soul stones.
