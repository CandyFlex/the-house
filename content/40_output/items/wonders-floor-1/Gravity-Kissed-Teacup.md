# Gravity-Kissed Teacup
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

---

## Description
A delicate porcelain teacup painted with a pattern of falling leaves — except the leaves are falling upward, drifting toward the cup's rim from the saucer below. The glaze has a faint iridescence that shifts between pale blue and silver depending on how you hold it. The cup is always warm to the touch, even when empty, as if it just held hot tea moments ago.

The cup inverts gravity for its contents. Pour tea into it, and the liquid floats upward, forming a perfect, hovering sphere just above the rim. It does not spill. It does not drip. It hovers, gently rotating, waiting to be drunk. To drink, you tip the cup above your mouth — the tea falls "up" out of the cup and into your waiting lips. If you flip the cup upside down, the tea pools on what is now the top (the cup's bottom) and behaves normally. The cup does not care which way is up. It has its own opinions about gravity.

Potion effects are not altered by the cup — a *potion of healing* drunk from the Gravity-Kissed Teacup functions identically. The cup's only agenda is making tea more interesting.

## Properties

**Inverted Contents (passive).** Any liquid poured into this cup floats upward, forming a hovering sphere just above the rim. The liquid behaves as if gravity is reversed for it alone — it will not spill, drip, or fall unless the cup is turned upside down, at which point the liquid pools on the cup's "bottom" (now the top) and behaves normally. The cup can hold up to 8 ounces of liquid in this state.

**Teatime Invitation.** Once per long rest, you may fill the cup with any potable liquid, speak the words "join me," and offer it to a creature within 5 feet. That creature must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or feel compelled to accept and drink. If the liquid is tea (specifically tea, not just any hot beverage), the creature has disadvantage on the save. This is not a charm effect — it is the irresistible universal impulse to accept a cup of tea when offered. Creatures that do not consume liquid (constructs, some undead) are immune.

**Unspillable.** While liquid is held in the cup's inverted state, the cup cannot spill. You can walk, run, jump, or even fall prone — the liquid sphere remains perfectly suspended above the rim. If the cup is knocked from your hand, the sphere follows it. This property has no combat application. It is, however, excellent for reading while walking.

## Balance Notes
An almost entirely RP item with one minor social hook. The gravity inversion is pure flavor — it has no mechanical benefit but infinite charm. The Teatime Invitation is a weak social compulsion (DC 12, only to drink, no ongoing effect) that's mostly useful for getting a stubborn NPC to sit down and talk. The disadvantage-on-tea clause makes it genuinely useful against tea-drinking cultures, which in fantasy settings is approximately all of them. Uncommon is appropriate — compare to a *charm person* scroll (one-time, stronger effect), the teacup is reusable but much weaker.

## Variants
- **Common — Floating Saucer:** A saucer that holds liquid inverted but has no cup. The liquid sphere is visible to all. No Teatime Invitation. Cost: ~5,000 soul stones.
- **Rare — The Mad Hatter's Teacup:** Teatime Invitation DC 15, works on any liquid (not just tea), and the drinker makes all subsequent Charisma checks against you with disadvantage for 1 hour — you have shared a strange, intimate moment that they cannot forget. Cost: ~120,000 soul stones.
