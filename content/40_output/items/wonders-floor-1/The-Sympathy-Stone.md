# The Sympathy Stone
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A smooth river stone, roughly oval, small enough to close your fist around. It is gray-blue, like a storm cloud caught in mineral form, and polished to a soft luster by what must have been centuries of running water — except the stone has never been near a river. When you hold it, it is exactly body temperature. When someone else holds it, it is exactly *their* body temperature. The stone does not have a temperature of its own. It borrows yours.

But the stone borrows more than warmth. It absorbs the emotional and physical state of whoever holds it — and it never lets go. Hold the Sympathy Stone, and you feel, with perfect clarity, exactly what the last person who held it felt. Not as a memory, not as someone else's story — as your own emotion, in your own chest, right now. Joy, grief, fear, pain, love, hunger, exhaustion. The stone does not filter. It does not translate. It makes you them, for as long as you hold it.

## Properties

**Empathic Echo (passive).** While holding the stone, you feel the exact emotional and physical state of the last creature that held it — for at least 1 minute. You experience their strongest emotion as your own, and you are aware of any physical sensations they were experiencing at the time (pain, exhaustion, intoxication, warmth, cold). You do not gain their memories or thoughts, only their felt state. This effect lasts as long as you hold the stone.

**Emotional Insight.** While experiencing an empathic echo, you have advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks made against the creature whose emotions you are feeling. You know how they felt. You can guess what they might do next.

**Stone Memory.** The stone can hold one empathic imprint at a time. When a new creature holds the stone for at least 1 minute, the old imprint is replaced. You know when this happens — the stone grows slightly warmer or cooler as it adjusts to the new emotional signature. If the stone is destroyed, all stored imprints are lost.

**The Unfiltered Truth.** The stone does not distinguish between emotions the holder was aware of and emotions they were hiding. If the last holder was secretly furious while smiling, you feel the fury, not the smile. If they were in love and pretending not to be, you feel the love. The stone is an honest witness to dishonest hearts.

## Balance Notes
A social investigation item that trades direct mind-reading for something more intimate and less invasive. You can't read thoughts — you can only feel what someone felt. This makes it excellent for interrogations ("the suspect was terrified when we mentioned the murder weapon"), relationship drama ("she was in love with you when she held this stone yesterday"), and investigation ("the guard who went missing was calm — he wasn't attacked, he left voluntarily"). The limitation (only the LAST holder, must hold for 1 minute) prevents it from becoming a universal lie detector. Rare is appropriate — it's weaker than *detect thoughts* but more thematically rich and provides concrete mechanical advantage.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Sympathy Pebble:** Holds imprint for only 1 hour after the holder releases it. Advantage on Insight only for the first check. Cost: ~35,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — The Empath's Stone:** Stores up to 7 imprints simultaneously. You can choose which imprint to feel. While feeling an imprint, you can cast *detect thoughts* (DC 16) once per long rest targeting the creature who left that imprint, regardless of distance, as long as they are on the same plane. Cost: ~700,000 soul stones.
