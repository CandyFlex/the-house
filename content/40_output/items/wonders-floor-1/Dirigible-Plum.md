# Dirigible Plum
*Wondrous item, rare*

---

## Description
A fist-sized purple plum with a faint golden shimmer on its skin. It is perfectly ripe, perpetually warm, and smells faintly of summer vineyards despite having no connection to any vineyard that exists. When released from your hand, it does not fall — it hovers exactly where you let it go, bobbing gently like a balloon on an invisible string.

If you balance on top of it, the plum grows — swelling to the size of a small rowboat, its skin stretching taut, its flesh becoming buoyant and firm. It can carry up to 400 pounds. You steer it by leaning. It deflates on command (a firm pat) or when punctured, producing a sound that is unmistakably, mortifyingly flatulent — audible out to 300 feet. The deflation takes 6 seconds, during which you descend at 30 feet per round. If you reach the ground before it finishes, you land safely. If you are still aloft when it fully deflates, you fall.

The plum re-inflates to fist-size after 1 minute and can be used again. If destroyed — reduced to pulp — it regrows from the pit over 1d4 days.

## Properties

**Inflate (at will).** As a bonus action, you release the plum. It hovers in place. As an action while touching the plum, you command it to swell to its full dirigible size — a 5-foot-diameter floating platform. The inflated plum has AC 10, 20 HP, and hovers up to 5 feet off the ground. It moves with you if you ride it, using your movement speed. It cannot ascend higher than 5 feet unless you are riding it, in which case it can ascend to a maximum altitude of 60 feet at a rate of 15 feet per round.

**Deflate.** As a bonus action while touching the plum, or when the plum takes any piercing damage, the plum deflates with a thunderous flatulent noise audible to 300 feet. All creatures within 30 feet that can hear must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be affected as if by *hideous laughter* for 1 round. The plum descends at 30 feet per round during deflation. The rider lands safely if the ground is reached before deflation completes.

**Regrowth.** If the plum is destroyed, its pit remains. Planted in any soil (or left in a pocket), the pit regrows into a fully functional Dirigible Plum over 1d4 days.

## Balance Notes
A movement utility item with a comedic drawback. The flight is limited (60-ft ceiling, 15-ft/round ascent) compared to the *fly* spell (60-ft speed, unlimited altitude), but it requires no concentration, no attunement, and no spell slot. The 400-lb capacity means one medium creature with gear or two small creatures. The deflation laughter AoE turns an embarrassing moment into a tactical one — you can deliberately pop the plum to disrupt enemies. The regrowth mechanic ensures it's never permanently lost but prevents spamming. Compare to *broom of flying* (uncommon, faster, no drawback) — the Dirigible Plum trades speed and discretion for character and chaos. Comfortably rare.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Tether-Plum:** Cannot ascend. Hovers at a fixed 5-foot height regardless of rider. No laughter on deflation. Cost: ~40,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Sovereign Plum:** Flight ceiling 300 feet, can carry 1,000 lbs. Deflation produces *confusion* (DC 15) instead of laughter. Regrows in 1 hour. Cost: ~600,000 soul stones.
