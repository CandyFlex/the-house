# Thimble of the Borrowed Moment
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A silver thimble, seamless and perfectly smooth except for a single engraving: a tiny hourglass with the sand running upward. It fits any finger and cannot be removed except by the wearer's will — or when the debt comes due.

When you wear this thimble, you become acutely aware of time as a physical substance. Seconds feel like grains of sand you could reach out and grab. And you can. Once per long rest, you may borrow a moment — six seconds — from your future self. You act now. Future-you pays later.

The repayment is not negotiable. At some point within the next 24 hours — chosen by the thimble, not by you — you freeze mid-action for exactly one round. You are incapacitated during this time. You cannot take reactions. You are aware but paralyzed — caught in a debt-collection from the universe. The thimble glows faintly for the hour before repayment is due. You will know it's coming. You will not know exactly when.

## Properties

**Borrowed Moment (1/Long Rest).** As a bonus action, you activate the thimble. You immediately gain one additional action. This action can be used for anything an action can normally do — Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Use an Object, etc. It does not grant additional movement or bonus actions. You cannot use this additional action to activate another magic item that would grant additional actions.

**The Debt (automatic, within 24 hours).** At some point within the next 24 hours after using Borrowed Moment, the thimble reclaims the borrowed time. At a moment of the DM's choosing — ideally during combat, a critical negotiation, or a delicate physical maneuver — you become incapacitated for 1 round (until the end of your next turn). You cannot take reactions during this round. You are aware but frozen in place. The thimble glows for 1 hour before this occurs, giving you warning. The effect cannot be prevented by any means short of a *wish* spell.

**Debt Sense (passive).** While attuned to the thimble, you know approximately when the debt is coming due. In the hour before repayment, you feel a growing tension in the finger wearing the thimble — a sensation like a clock spring winding tighter. You cannot pinpoint the exact moment, but you know the window is closing.

## Balance Notes
An action-economy item with a built-in tax. The extra action is equivalent to a fighter's Action Surge — extremely powerful, especially for spellcasters who can drop two leveled spells in one turn. The repayment is harsh (incapacitated for a full round, no reactions) and arrives at a DM-chosen dramatic moment. The 1-hour warning glow lets the party plan around it (finish the fight before the debt hits, or position safely), but it also creates tension — you're racing a clock only you can feel. Very rare is appropriate: it's better than Action Surge (available to any class) but comes with a significant, unavoidable cost. Compare to *haste* — this gives more flexibility but a worse crash.

## Variants
- **Rare — Thimble of the Borrowed Half-Moment:** Grants 1 additional bonus action instead of a full action. Repayment is 1 round of being unable to take bonus actions (no incapacitation). Cost: ~250,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — Thimble of the Stolen Hour:** 3/long rest. Repayment is 1d4 rounds of incapacitation, deferred until your next long rest — all borrowed rounds are repaid consecutively during the rest. Cost: ~2,800,000 soul stones.
