# Mnemonic Puzzle-Box
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A palm-sized cube of interlocking brass and mother-of-pearl, its faces covered in sliding tiles engraved with symbols from no known language. When you hold it, you feel faint vibrations — the box is always solving itself, tile by tile, in an endless sequence no one has ever completed. The tiles are warm against your palm, as if the box has been working on its puzzle since before the floor existed.

Once per day, you may finish the puzzle. The final tile clicks into place, the box's seams split with a soft chime, and a door-sized rift peels open in the air before you — a rectangular hole edged in geometric light, leading to the **Puzzle Dimension**.

## Properties

**Solve the Puzzle (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you attune your mind to the box and complete its sliding puzzle. A 5-foot-wide, 8-foot-tall rectangular portal opens within 10 feet of you, leading to the Puzzle Dimension. The portal remains open for 10 minutes or until you close it as a bonus action. Creatures other than you must succeed on a DC 16 Intelligence saving throw to pass through — on a failure, they see only a flat rectangle of light and walk through it harmlessly.

**The Puzzle Dimension.** This demiplane is a recursive library of impossible architecture: staircases that fold into themselves, doorways that lead back to where you just were, halls where gravity points sideways. While inside, you are invisible to divination magic and cannot be targeted by spells originating from outside the dimension. The dimension is 200 feet across in every direction and contains:

- **The Memory Ledger.** A lectern holding a book that records the last thing you forgot. Opening it reveals a single handwritten sentence — always something you genuinely lost. If you read it aloud, you remember that thing permanently, and the book writes a new forgotten thing. You may read from the Ledger once per visit.
- **The Wrong Door.** A door that, when opened, deposits you in a random location within 1 mile of where you entered the dimension. It is always somewhere you have never been before. Roll a d8: on a 1, it's hostile territory; on an 8, it's somewhere safe.
- **The Echo Niche.** An alcove where any question spoken aloud is answered by your own voice from a timeline where you already know the answer. The reply is always cryptic — a fragment, a metaphor, a single word. It is never enough on its own, but it is always true.

When the portal closes, you and any creatures you brought with you are ejected to the nearest unoccupied space near the portal's entrance.

**Breaking the Box.** If the Puzzle-Box is destroyed, the Puzzle Dimension collapses, and everything inside it — including the Memory Ledger — is lost forever. The Ledger is not a magic item; it is a feature of the dimension.

## Balance Notes
A demiplane-access item gated at very rare. Compare to a *cubic gate* (legendary) — this is far more limited (10-minute duration, 1/day, no plane-hopping). The Puzzle Dimension's features are non-combat utility: the Memory Ledger is pure RP gold, the Wrong Door is chaotic exploration, and the Echo Niche is a hint engine for stuck players. The DC 16 Intelligence gate on the portal prevents enemies from following you in, giving the party a panic-room option. The dimension's anti-divination property makes it a secure meeting space. No damage output keeps it balanced at very rare — it changes how you explore, not how you fight.

---

## Variants
- **Rare — Lesser Puzzle-Box:** No Memory Ledger, no Echo Niche. Only the Wrong Door. Portal lasts 1 minute. Cost: ~120,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — The Solved Cube:** Portal lasts 1 hour. All three features. Additionally, 1/week you can step into the Puzzle Dimension and emerge from any other puzzle-box anywhere in the multiverse — if you know its location. Cost: ~3,200,000 soul stones.
