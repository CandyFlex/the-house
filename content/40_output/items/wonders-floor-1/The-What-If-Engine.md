# The What-If Engine
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A small brass orrery — a clockwork model of celestial spheres — that fits on a tabletop. Tiny planets orbit a central light source that is not fire, not magic, but something more fundamental: the light of possibility. When you wind the engine (a small key at its base), the planets accelerate, blur, and then stop — frozen in a configuration that represents not the present, but a present that almost was.

The What-If Engine shows you the immediate aftermath of a choice you didn't make. You must focus on a specific decision point from the last 24 hours — a fork in the road, a conversation where you held your tongue, a door you chose not to open — and the engine plays it back, not as a memory, but as a simulation. For 30 seconds, you experience the other path. You see where it would have led. You feel the emotional weight of what you avoided — or what you missed. The engine does not let you change the past. It only lets you know what you gave up. This is sometimes worse.

## Properties

**Simulate the Other Path (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you wind the engine and focus on a specific decision point from the last 24 hours — a moment where you made a clear choice between two or more options. The engine projects a 30-second vision directly into your mind, showing you the immediate aftermath of the choice you did NOT make. The vision is accurate — the engine simulates what would have happened based on all available information. It cannot see beyond the immediate aftermath (30 seconds), and it cannot simulate decisions involving creatures with legendary resistances, deities, or artifacts (their futures are too heavy for the tiny planets to model).

After the vision ends, you gain one of the following benefits (your choice):

- **Closure.** You are certain you made the right choice. You gain advantage on your next Wisdom or Charisma saving throw within the next hour.
- **Regret.** You realize you chose poorly. You gain advantage on your next ability check related to correcting or mitigating the consequences of your original choice.
- **Curiosity Satisfied.** You simply wanted to know. You gain inspiration (as the DMG rule: advantage on one roll of your choice within the next hour).

**The Weight of Paths Not Taken (passive).** The engine stores every simulation it has ever run. While attuned, you can touch the engine and close your eyes to briefly re-experience any previous vision — not the full 30 seconds, but the emotional echo. This has no mechanical benefit. It's just for when you need to remember why you made the choices you did.

**Parallel Insight.** If you have used the engine at least 3 times, you gain a permanent, intuitive understanding of branching possibilities. You have advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks made to determine what might happen next based on current information — reading a situation, predicting an opponent's move, or anticipating the consequences of an action.

## Balance Notes
A pure utility item built for roleplay and investigation. The 30-second simulation window is intentionally narrow — it shows you the first door, not the whole hallway. This prevents the engine from being a "solve the mystery" button while still providing genuine value. The three benefit options let the player interpret their vision however they want: closure, regret, or curiosity. The Parallel Insight perk at 3+ uses rewards long-term engagement with the item. Compare to *divination* (4th level, asks a question about the future) — the engine is backward-looking but more emotionally engaging, and the advantage is more flexible. Rare feels right.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Pocket What-If:** Shows 10 seconds. One benefit option (Closure only). No Parallel Insight. Cost: ~30,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — The Grand Orrery:** Shows 2 minutes. Can simulate decisions involving legendary creatures (not deities or artifacts). Three benefits: you may also gain proficiency in one skill relevant to the vision for 1 hour (the engine downloaded expertise from a version of you that specialized differently). Cost: ~400,000 soul stones.
