# Reciprocity Bracers
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A pair of iron bracers, unadorned except for a single inlaid line of copper that runs the length of each forearm and branches into smaller veins, like a circuit diagram drawn by someone who learned electricity from lightning strikes. The bracers are heavier than they look, and when you first put them on, they are cold — not unpleasantly, but attentively, like something is listening to your pulse.

The bracers believe in equivalent exchange. Every blow you take, they remember. Every drop of blood, every bruise, every moment of pain — they weigh it, measure it, and store the value. Then, when you strike back, they release what was stored. Not as revenge. As balance. The universe owes you exactly what was taken. The bracers collect.

You can also pay in advance.

## Properties

**Store the Blow (passive).** Whenever you take damage from a hostile source, the bracers store Potential equal to half the damage taken (rounded down). The maximum Potential the bracers can store is equal to 5 × your character level. Potential persists until used, until you finish a long rest, or until you take damage from a friendly source (the bracers consider friendly fire "off the books").

**Release the Balance (1/turn).** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack or an unarmed strike, you may release any amount of stored Potential. The attack deals additional damage equal to the Potential released, and the Potential is consumed. The additional damage is of the same type as the last damage you took that contributed to the stored Potential (if you've taken multiple types, choose one). If no Potential is stored, this property does nothing.

**Advance Payment.** As a bonus action, you may voluntarily take 1d4 slashing damage (cutting your palm on the bracers' edge). This damage is not reduced by resistance or immunity. The full amount is stored as Potential. You can do this at will, but the maximum Potential limit still applies.

**Balance Audit.** While wearing the bracers, you know exactly how much Potential is currently stored — you feel it as a warmth along the copper veins proportional to the stored value. A full charge (equal to your maximum) makes the bracers uncomfortably hot. You must release Potential before you can store more.

## Balance Notes
An equivalent-exchange combat item that rewards aggressive play. The half-damage storage means you need to take significant hits to build up meaningful Potential, which naturally balances the item — you can't turtle and charge it. The 5 × level cap prevents low-level characters from storing obscene amounts. The 1/turn release prevents nova turns. The Advance Payment feature lets you "pay in" before combat, but at only 1d4 per bonus action — efficient charging requires taking real hits. The same-damage-type feature is flavorful but occasionally a drawback (if you've only taken poison damage and you're fighting an undead, your release is less effective). Rare feels right — it's weaker than something like a *flame tongue* for consistent damage but offers a unique risk/reward loop.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Apprentice's Bracers:** Maximum Potential = 2 × level. Release 1/round (not 1/turn). No Advance Payment. Cost: ~40,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — The Equation Bracers:** Maximum Potential = 10 × level. Release 2/turn. Advance Payment deals 2d4. 1/long rest, you can release all stored Potential as a 15-foot cone of force energy (Dex save DC 15, half damage). Cost: ~550,000 soul stones.
