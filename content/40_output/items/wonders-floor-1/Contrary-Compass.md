# The Contrary Compass
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

---

## Description
A brass compass in a scratched rosewood case, its needle lazily drifting as if it has all the time in the multiverse. The face bears no cardinal directions — instead, it is inscribed with phrases like "Probably Not Here," "Definitely Not That Way," and "You're Getting Colder." The needle never points north. It points to the least important thing within 300 feet.

"Least important" is determined by cosmic indifference: the thing that, if you never found it, would change nothing about your life, your quest, or the fate of anyone you have ever met. The compass has a dry sense of what matters. It has never been wrong, though its definition of "importance" is famously inscrutable.

Sometimes — about one time in twenty — the needle swings around and points directly at the compass itself. When this happens, the compass is telling you that you are the least important thing in the vicinity. This is always a joke. Probably.

## Properties

**Contrary Direction (at will).** As an action, you hold the compass and ask it to point you toward something. The needle swings — not toward what you asked for, but toward the least important object, creature, or feature within 300 feet. The DM determines what this is. The compass does not reveal *what* it's pointing at, only the direction.

**Finding by Elimination.** The Contrary Compass is most useful in combination with logic. If you are searching for a hidden trapdoor, and the compass points to a loose floorboard, a dust mote, a spider, and a forgotten copper piece in sequence — the trapdoor must be the thing it *didn't* point at. Patient creatures gain advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks made to locate hidden objects or features in areas where the compass has been consulted at least 3 times.

**Self-Reference (5% chance).** When you consult the compass, roll a d20. On a 1, the needle points at the compass itself. The DM may use this to indicate that there is genuinely nothing of interest nearby, or that the compass has decided to mock you today.

## Balance Notes
A utility item that flips *locate object* on its head. Instead of finding the thing you want, it finds everything you don't want — and you deduce the target by exclusion. This makes it both more interesting and less powerful than a direct locator. The DM controls what counts as "least important," which makes the compass a roleplay device as much as a mechanical one. The advantage-granting mechanic rewards patience and cleverness. The 5% self-reference gag adds personality without mechanical cost. Uncommon is appropriate — it's a clever tool, not a powerful one.

## Variants
- **Common — Dithering Needle:** Works only once per day. No advantage on Investigation. Cost: ~8,000 soul stones.
- **Rare — The Contrarian's Compass:** The needle also points toward the SECOND-least-important thing (you choose which to follow). Advantage on Investigation after 1 consultation instead of 3. Cost: ~150,000 soul stones.
