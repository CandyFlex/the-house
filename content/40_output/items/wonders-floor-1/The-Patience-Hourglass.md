# The Patience Hourglass
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A heavy hourglass in a frame of petrified wood, the glass bulbs thick and slightly warped — not from poor craftsmanship, but from the weight of what's inside. The sand is not sand. It is crystallized time, each grain a second that someone, somewhere, chose to wait instead of act. The grains are faintly gold and fall with a sound like distant chimes, a fraction of a tone per grain.

When you turn the hourglass over, time inside its radius stretches. One round for you is one minute for everyone outside. Or, if you turn it the other way, one minute for you is one round outside. The hourglass does not speed time up or slow it down — it changes which side of the glass you're standing on. For the people inside, everything outside is a blur. For the people outside, everything inside is a frozen tableau.

## Properties

**Dilate Time (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you place the hourglass on a solid surface within your reach and turn it over, speaking the command word. A 15-foot-radius sphere of altered time extends from the hourglass for 1 minute (real time, external perspective). Creatures and objects inside the sphere experience time at a different rate from those outside. When you activate the hourglass, choose one mode:

**Slow-Sphere (You Inside, World Outside).** Time inside the sphere moves at a 1:10 ratio — every 1 minute that passes inside the sphere is only 1 round (6 seconds) outside. Creatures inside the sphere experience the full duration. The sphere's interior cannot be entered or exited by any means short of *wish* while active. Creatures inside can take actions, cast spells, heal, plan, and prepare as if they had a full minute to act — but only one round passes in the real world. When the sphere ends (after 1 minute inside / 1 round outside), creatures re-enter normal time at their current initiative count, with whatever resources they expended inside. This mode is for regrouping, healing, and mid-combat planning.

**Quick-Sphere (World Inside, You Outside).** Time inside the sphere moves at a 10:1 ratio — every 1 minute that passes outside is 10 minutes inside. Creatures inside the sphere are trapped in accelerated time. For them, 10 minutes pass while only 1 minute passes for you. At the start of each of their turns (inside time), they may repeat any saving throws against ongoing effects. Spell durations tick down 10 times faster. A *haste* spell cast before entering would expire almost instantly. A poisoned creature would burn through the poison. This mode is for speeding poisons, curses, or waiting out enemy buffs — or for interrogating someone who thinks they've been trapped for hours when it's only been minutes.

**Shared Rules for Both Modes:**
- The hourglass cannot be moved once activated.
- The sphere's boundary is visible as a faint golden shimmer.
- Creatures cannot pass through the boundary.
- If the hourglass is destroyed while active, the sphere collapses instantly with no ill effects other than the abrupt return to normal time.
- The sphere does not affect constructs, undead, or creatures that do not experience time linearly (such as sphinxes).

**Patience Rewarded.** If a creature spends a full Slow-Sphere duration (1 minute of inside-time) doing nothing but resting, meditating, or planning — no attacks, no spells cast, no damage dealt — they may spend one Hit Die at the end of the sphere as if they had taken a short rest. Only one Hit Die can be spent this way per use of the hourglass.

## Balance Notes
A time-manipulation item with two opposite modes, each powerful in different contexts. The Slow-Sphere is a mid-combat panic button — one round of real time for a full minute of preparation, healing, and buffing. Compare to *time stop* (9th level, 2-5 rounds for the caster only) — the hourglass affects the entire party but costs an action to activate and only lasts 1 round externally. The Quick-Sphere is a debuff accelerator — great for burning through enemy *haste*, *bless*, or poison, or for speeding up an interrogation. The 1/long rest gate and immobility during use keep it balanced. Very rare is appropriate — it's a tactical nuke, but you only get one shot per day.

## Variants
- **Rare — Pocket Hourglass:** 10-foot radius. Slow-Sphere mode only. 30 seconds inside / 1 round outside. No Patience Rewarded. Cost: ~200,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — The Aeon Hourglass:** 30-foot radius. Both modes, each 2/long rest. Creatures inside can choose to be affected or unaffected by the time dilation when the sphere activates. Patience Rewarded allows spending up to 3 Hit Dice. Cost: ~5,500,000 soul stones.
