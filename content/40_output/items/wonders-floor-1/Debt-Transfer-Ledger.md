# Debt-Transfer Ledger
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A heavy book bound in ledger-green leather, its pages crisp and lined but mostly blank. The first page bears a single entry in precise, looping handwriting:

> *All debts are transferable. All obligations may be reassigned. The universe keeps a balance sheet. This book is the white-out.*

The rest of the book is empty, waiting. When you write a debt into the ledger — any debt, any obligation, any binding promise made by anyone to anyone — it becomes transferable. The original debtor is released. The new debtor is bound. The universe accepts the substitution and adjusts its books accordingly.

In combat, the concept extends. A condition is a debt the universe owes you — you are charmed, so the universe owes you clarity. You are frightened, so the universe owes you courage. The ledger can transfer that debt to someone else. The universe collects.

## Properties

**Transfer Debt (1/Long Rest).** As an action, choose one willing creature and one obligation, debt, or binding agreement the willing creature currently owes — magical or mundane. Write the obligation into the ledger, followed by the name of a second willing creature who accepts the debt. The original debtor is released from the obligation. The new debtor is now bound by it with all the same terms, conditions, and magical enforcement. This transfer is permanent unless reversed by another use of the ledger. The ledger cannot transfer souls, life forces, or debts owed to gods — it works on agreements between mortals and on magical contracts of 6th level or lower.

**Transfer Condition (1/Short Rest).** As a bonus action, choose a condition currently affecting a willing creature within 30 feet — charmed, frightened, poisoned, paralyzed, stunned, blinded, deafened, or restrained. The condition transfers from the original bearer to a second creature within 30 feet. The second creature must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the condition is removed from the original bearer and applied to the second creature for the condition's remaining duration. The condition's save DC, if any, remains the same. On a success, the transfer fails, and the action is wasted.

Conditions caused by artifacts, deities, or curses of 7th level or higher cannot be transferred. Exhaustion levels cannot be transferred (the ledger considers exhaustion to be "earned," not "owed").

**The Balance Sheet (passive).** While attuned, you always know the rough nature of any magical contract, geas, or binding agreement affecting a creature you can see — not the terms, but the fact that they are bound and by what level of magic (cantrip to 3rd, 4th to 6th, 7th to 9th, or artifact-level). You see these as faint chains or threads of light connecting the creature to an invisible counterparty.

## Balance Notes
A hybrid legal/combat tool. Transfer Debt is pure narrative utility — reassign a warlock's pact, free someone from a devil's contract, let the rogue take the paladin's oath (temporarily? permanently? the DM decides what obligations are transferable). Transfer Condition is the combat payoff: move the fighter's paralysis onto the enemy caster, redirect the barbarian's fear onto their own minion, etc. The 1/short rest cooldown prevents spam, and the DC 15 Wisdom save keeps it from being automatic. The condition must be on a WILLING creature, which prevents the obvious cheese of transferring an enemy's buff condition onto your ally (conditions are debuffs; buffs like *bless* are not conditions). Rare is appropriate — weaker than *greater restoration* for removal but with the transfer twist.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Debtor's Notebook:** Transfer Debt only (1/long rest). No Transfer Condition. No Balance Sheet passive. Cost: ~40,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — The Absolute Ledger:** Transfer Debt 3/long rest. Transfer Condition 3/short rest. Can transfer conditions between unwilling creatures (both make saves; if both fail, the condition moves). Can transfer exhaustion. Can see the full terms of contracts, not just their existence. Cost: ~650,000 soul stones.
