# Skill-Stealer's Gloves
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A pair of fine black leather gloves, supple and thin enough to feel a pulse through the fingertips. The palms are embroidered with silver thread in a pattern that, if you stare at it long enough, resolves into the word "BORROWED" written in thirty-seven languages simultaneously. The gloves fit no one perfectly but everyone adequately. They are eager. They twitch toward interesting people.

When you touch someone while wearing these gloves, you feel — for just a moment — the shape of their competence. The way a blacksmith holds a hammer. The way a diplomat reads a room. The way a thief listens to locks. The gloves can borrow that shape. They can't keep it, but they can wear it for an hour — and while they do, the original owner finds the skill strangely slippery, just out of reach, like a word on the tip of their tongue.

## Properties

**Borrow Competence (1/Short Rest).** As an action, touch a creature within your reach. Choose one skill or tool proficiency that creature possesses. The creature must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, you gain that proficiency at the creature's bonus for 1 hour, and the creature loses it for the same duration. On a success, nothing happens and you cannot target that creature again with these gloves for 24 hours. A willing creature can choose to fail the save.

You can only borrow one proficiency at a time. Borrowing a second proficiency immediately returns the first one to its owner.

**Returned Competence.** When the effect ends — whether by duration, by borrowing a new skill, or by your death — the original owner regains their proficiency immediately. They are aware that it has returned. They may or may not be aware that it was ever gone. A creature who lost a proficiency makes ability checks using that skill with disadvantage until the end of their next turn after it returns, as they readjust to having the knowledge back in their head where it belongs.

**Lingering Echo.** After you return a borrowed proficiency, you retain a faint echo of it for 10 minutes. During this time, you have advantage on one check using that skill. Once you use this advantage, the echo fades. This represents the gloves' memory of the skill — a ghost of competence that fades quickly.

## Balance Notes
A utility item that is both powerful and socially dangerous. Borrowing the king's Insight (+9) before an audience is devastating — but he'll notice something is wrong. Borrowing the rogue's Thieves' Tools before a heist is practical — but you've crippled your own rogue for an hour. The short-rest cooldown prevents spamming, and the DC 15 Wisdom save keeps it from being automatic against high-value targets. The Lingering Echo provides a consolation prize even when you have to return the skill early. The disadvantage on the victim's return is a minor debuff that adds tactical weight. Compare to *borrowed knowledge* (2nd level, gives a skill, no stealing) — the gloves are a significant upgrade with a social cost, appropriately rare.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Apprentice's Gloves:** Borrow one tool proficiency only. No saving throw — the target must be willing. Duration 10 minutes. Cost: ~30,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Master Thief's Gloves:** 3/short rest. Can borrow saving throw proficiencies in addition to skills/tools. Lingering Echo grants advantage on two checks instead of one. Cost: ~500,000 soul stones.
