# The Antagonist's Mirror
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A standing oval mirror in a frame of black iron, tall enough to show you from crown to knee. The glass is not silvered — it is a sheet of obsidian so perfectly polished that your reflection appears in color, not monochrome. When you look into it, your reflection does not mimic you. It waits a beat. Then it acts — crossing its arms when you haven't, tilting its head, smiling when your own mouth is still. It is you, but it is the version of you that refused every decent impulse you ever had.

The mirror does not show you a monster. It shows you a person who made defensible, rational, incrementally selfish choices until they became the antagonist of someone else's story. It shows you what you would be if you stopped caring about being good.

Then it asks you to prove you're not that person. It gives you a quest.

## Properties

**Face Yourself (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you gaze into the mirror for 1 uninterrupted minute. The mirror analyzes your soul — your alignment, your recent actions, your deepest values — and generates a **Contrarian Quest**. This is a task that is the moral or ethical opposite of what you would normally do, calibrated to be achievable within 24 hours and not require you to violate your fundamental nature (a good character won't be asked to murder innocents, but might be asked to refuse to help someone in need).

The mirror speaks the quest aloud in a voice that is your own, slightly distorted. You have 24 hours to complete it.

**Contrarian Boon.** If you complete the quest within 24 hours, the mirror grants you a boon. Roll on or choose from the table below. The boon lasts until your next long rest.

| d6 | Boon |
|----|------|
| 1 | **Villain's Confidence.** You gain advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) and Charisma (Deception) checks. |
| 2 | **Antagonist's Foresight.** Once during the boon's duration, you can declare that an attack against you misses, no roll required — you saw it coming in the mirror. |
| 3 | **Narrative Weight.** You have advantage on death saving throws, and when you are reduced to 0 HP, you remain conscious for 1 additional round before falling. |
| 4 | **Dark Reflection.** As an action, summon a shadow duplicate of yourself (use *mirror image* statistics, but the duplicates have your proficiency bonus and can flank). Lasts 1 minute. |
| 5 | **The Reveal.** One creature of your choice that can see you must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or treat you as their most trusted ally for 1 minute — after all, the villain was the friend all along. |
| 6 | **Protagonist's Nemesis.** For 1 hour, you have resistance to the damage type of the last creature that reduced you to 0 HP. If no creature has done so, choose one damage type when you receive this boon. |

**Refusal.** If you refuse the quest, or if 24 hours pass without completion, the mirror marks you. For the next 7 days, creatures have advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks made against you specifically when you are being dishonest. The mirror wants you to know what it feels like to have your mask slip.

**Once Per Character.** The first time you complete a Contrarian Quest, you permanently gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Deception, Intimidation, or Insight. This represents the permanent perspective shift from having walked the other path. You cannot gain this benefit more than once, even if you unattune and reattune.

- **Rarity:** Very Rare
- **Attunement:** Yes
- **Charges:** 1/long rest (quest generation)
- **Save DC:** Wisdom 16 (for The Reveal boon)
- **Spell Equivalent:** — (narrative quest system; boons mimic various spells)
- **Damage/Effect:** Variable boons; permanent skill proficiency on first completion; social penalty on refusal

---

## Balance Notes
A daily quest-generator that rewards moral flexibility. The boons are strong but random and temporary (until next long rest). The permanent skill proficiency is a one-time reward — significant but not game-breaking. The refusal penalty (disadvantage on deception for 7 days) is meaningful but not crippling. The mirror scales well: low-level characters get modest boons they can use immediately, high-level characters get interesting tactical options (Antagonist's Foresight is essentially a once-per-day legendary resistance for attacks). The core balancing factor is the quest itself — the DM controls what constitutes a "moral opposite," which means the mirror can't be gamed for easy boons.

---

## Variants
- **Rare — The Rival's Hand Mirror:** Generates 1 quest per week. Boons last 1 hour. No permanent proficiency. Cost: ~200,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — The Tyrant's Mirror:** Generates 1 quest per short rest. Choose your boon instead of rolling. Mark of Refusal lasts 1 year. Permanent proficiency on first completion PLUS one feat of the DM's choice that reflects your character's darker potential. Cost: ~4,000,000 soul stones.
