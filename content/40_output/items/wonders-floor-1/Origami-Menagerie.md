# Origami Menagerie
*Wondrous item, rare*

---

## Description
A single sheet of paper, crisp and white, about the size of a letter page. It is blank on both sides. It never creases unless you fold it deliberately. It never gets wet (water beads and rolls off). It never tears unless you want it to. When held up to the light, faint lines are visible — fold-lines that haven't happened yet, a map of every creature the paper might become.

Here is how it works: you fold the paper into the shape of a creature. The folding takes 1 minute and requires a DC 15 Dexterity check — origami is an art, and the paper respects craftsmanship. On a success, the folded shape animates, unfolding into a living, three-dimensional creature, which obeys your verbal commands for 1 hour or until reduced to 0 hit points. On a failure, you produce an embarrassing paper lump. It does nothing. Try again.

The creature uses the statistics of the beast you folded, with the following changes: it is vulnerable to fire and water, it is immune to psychic damage, and when reduced to 0 HP it does not die — it simply unfolds back into the flat paper sheet, ready to be folded again.

The paper can be any CR 0–2 beast whose shape you can reasonably fold. The DM decides if your folding attempt is close enough.

## Properties

**Fold Creature (at will, 1-minute folding).** As part of a short rest or during downtime, you may spend 1 minute folding the paper into the shape of a beast of CR 0–2. Make a DC 15 Dexterity check. On a success, the paper animates into that creature for 1 hour. It has the beast's normal statistics with these modifications: vulnerability to fire, vulnerability to water (any water-based effect that would deal damage deals double), immunity to psychic damage. The creature understands your verbal commands and follows them to the best of its ability. It acts on its own initiative count. You can only have one origami creature active at a time. When the creature drops to 0 HP or the hour ends, it unfolds back into the paper, which can be reused.

**Failed Fold.** On a failed Dexterity check, the paper crumples into an unrecognizable lump. It does not animate. You may try again.

**Folding Lore.** If you have proficiency in a relevant tool (calligrapher's supplies, woodcarver's tools, or any artisan's tool that involves fine motor control), you have advantage on the Dexterity check to fold the paper.

**Creature Memory.** The paper remembers successful folds. If you fold the same creature shape twice, the DC drops to 12. If you have folded it three times, the DC drops to 10. The paper learns shapes it has held before.

## Balance Notes
A reusable summoning item gated by both time (1-minute fold) and skill (DC 15 check). The 1-hour duration and CR 0–2 limit keep it from being a combat-dominance tool — you're not summoning a pack of wolves mid-fight, you're preparing a scout bird or a guard dog in advance. Compare to *figurines of wondrous power* (rare, various): the Menagerie trades the figurine's guaranteed activation for unlimited reuse and creature variety. The vulnerability to fire and water is significant (two of the most common damage types) and keeps the origami creatures fragile. The Creature Memory mechanic rewards specialization — your third paper wolf is much easier to fold than your first paper badger. Rare feels right.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Origami Pet:** Maximum CR 0. No combat capability. DC 12 folding check. The creature lasts 8 hours but cannot attack. Cost: ~30,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Grand Menagerie:** Maximum CR 3. Can have up to 2 creatures active simultaneously. DC 15. Creatures gain resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing (paper is harder to cut than you'd think). Cost: ~500,000 soul stones.
