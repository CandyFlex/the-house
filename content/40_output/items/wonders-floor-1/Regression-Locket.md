# Regression Locket
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A tarnished silver locket on a fine chain, its surface etched with a spiral that tightens inward forever without reaching a center. It opens, but there is nothing inside — no portrait, no inscription, just a faint, persistent warmth, like the memory of a heartbeat. When you first attune to it, the locket takes a snapshot of you: this moment, this heartbeat, this exact arrangement of your soul in spacetime. It calls this snapshot **the Anchor**.

Thereafter, once per day, you may set a **Regression Point**. For the next minute, if you would die — truly die, not fall unconscious — the locket activates. Time reverses. Your body, mind, equipment, and position revert to the Regression Point. You remember everything that happened between. The death did not occur. The locket paid for it.

The cost is written on your body. Each time you regress, a small, permanent mark appears somewhere on your skin — a scar, a white streak of hair, a fingerprint that wasn't there before. These are **Death Scars**. They are visible, countable, and unsettling. A creature that sees your Death Scars intuitively understands that you have died before, even if they don't know how. You cannot hide them with illusions of 3rd level or lower.

## Properties

**Set Regression Point (1/Long Rest).** As a bonus action, press the locket to your chest and speak a single word. The locket records your current state: hit points, conditions, spell slots, equipment, position, and the exact moment. This Regression Point persists for 1 minute. While a Regression Point is active, the locket feels heavier against your skin.

**Regression.** If you would die while a Regression Point is active, the locket activates automatically (no action required). Time rewinds. You return to the Regression Point with full memory of everything that occurred between. Any resources you expended, damage you took, conditions you suffered, or death you experienced are undone. The world around you is exactly as it was at the Regression Point — other creatures do not remember the undone timeline. You gain one permanent Death Scar.

**Regression cannot save you from:**
- Death by old age
- Effects that destroy the soul rather than the body
- A *wish* spell specifically worded to prevent temporal reversion
- Death that occurs more than 1 minute after setting the Regression Point

**Death Scars (cumulative, permanent).** Each time you regress, you gain a visible mark. These cannot be removed by *lesser restoration*, *greater restoration*, or *remove curse*. Only a *wish* spell can erase Death Scars, and it erases all of them at once. Death Scars have no mechanical penalty, but intelligent creatures who see them understand their significance with a DC 10 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) check.

**Ten-Death Threshold.** If you ever accumulate 10 Death Scars, the locket's behavior changes. You no longer set Regression Points — instead, you regress automatically the first time you would die each day. You cannot prevent this. The locket has learned your death-pattern and considers you too valuable to lose. You gain one additional Death Scar each time this occurs. At 20 Death Scars, the locket shatters, and you permanently gain the effect of the *death ward* spell, which recharges at dawn — the locket has given you its last life.

## Balance Notes
A death-prevention item with permanent narrative cost. Compare to a *contingency* (6th level) with *death ward* (4th level) — the locket combines both but is self-only, requires attunement, and comes with escalating narrative consequences. The 1-minute window is a significant limiter — you can't set it at dawn and forget it. You must anticipate danger. The Death Scar system turns every regression into a story moment: other characters notice, enemies recognize a survivor, and at 10 deaths the locket develops its own protective personality. The capstone (20 deaths = permanent daily *death ward*) rewards long-term use with a fitting conclusion. Very rare is appropriate given the power ceiling and built-in narrative tax.

## Variants
- **Rare — Lesser Regression Locket:** Regression Point lasts 1 round (6 seconds). No Death Scar mechanic; instead, you gain 1 level of exhaustion after regressing. Cost: ~180,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — The Final Anchor:** Regression Point lasts 10 minutes. No Death Scars. You may choose to keep the original timeline instead of regressing (you experience both and remember both). Cost: ~4,500,000 soul stones.
