# Echo-Eater's Jar
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A thick glass jar, about the size of a small melon, sealed with a cork wrapped in what appears to be spun silence — a gray, wool-like fiber that absorbs sound on contact. The jar is deceptively heavy. When you hold it to your ear, you hear nothing at all, which is wrong, because a sealed glass jar should at least give you the ocean-in-a-shell hum of your own blood. This jar gives you the sound of a room where no one has ever spoken.

The jar eats echoes. Any sound that reaches its open mouth is captured — not recorded, but stolen. The original sound still occurs (you hear the bell ring), but the echo that follows — the reverberation, the decay, the trailing ring — is gone. The jar swallows it. You can store up to 7 echoes at a time. Uncork the jar, and you release the stored echo — the exact sound, in the exact acoustic, at the exact volume it was captured. If you release all 7 at once, they play simultaneously in a glorious, deafening cacophony.

## Properties

**Echo Capture (at will).** As a reaction when a sound occurs within 60 feet of you, you may open the jar. The jar captures the echo — the reverberation and decay — of that sound. The original sound is unaffected. The jar can hold up to 7 echoes. If you attempt to capture an 8th, the oldest stored echo is released involuntarily (no action required). You choose which echo to overwrite if the jar is full; otherwise, the oldest one escapes.

**Echo Release (at will).** As an action, you uncork the jar. Choose one stored echo, or all stored echoes. They play at their original volume as if the source were in your space. The sound is perfectly reproduced — if you captured a dragon's roar, a dragon's roar comes out of a jar in your hand. Creatures familiar with the original sound recognize it immediately. The jar's echo is acoustically indistinguishable from the real thing.

**Cacophony (once per jar-full).** If you release all 7 stored echoes at once, each creature of your choice within 30 feet that can hear must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 4d6 thunder damage and be deafened for 1 minute. On a success, half damage and no deafness. Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until the jar has been filled with at least 7 new echoes.

**Clearing the Jar.** As an action, you can empty the jar of all stored echoes. They are lost. No sound is produced.

## Balance Notes
A hybrid utility/combat item. The Echo Capture is pure creativity fuel — steal a guard's whistle, a noble's incriminating conversation, a monster's roar for intimidation, a musical performance for later. The Echo Release lets you frame someone, create diversions, or prove what you heard. The Cacophony is a once-per-fill AoE that deals damage comparable to *shatter* (3d8 at 2nd level) but with a better control rider (deafen). The 7-echo cooldown means you can't spam it — you need to curate your jar's contents carefully. Rare is appropriate given the tactical versatility and creative ceiling.

---

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Echo Phial:** Holds 1 echo. No Cacophony. Cost: ~35,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Symphony Jar:** Holds 13 echoes. Cacophony deals 6d6 thunder, DC 17. Once per long rest, you can release a single echo so perfectly that it acts as *suggestion* (DC 17) on one creature that hears it, as if the original speaker were commanding them. Cost: ~750,000 soul stones.
