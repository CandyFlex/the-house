# The First Mark of Cruelty (Caesar's Spell Focus)

*Weapon (wand-longsword), legendary (requires attunement)*

**Rarity**: Legendary (Scales with Attunement)
**Weight**: 2 lb (wand) / 3 lb (longsword)
**Damage**: 1d8 slashing (longsword, versatile 1d10)
**Properties**: Finesse, Light, Special, Spell Focus

---

A long silver wand of metamorphic metal — appearing liquid while holding its shape. It ripples like mercury, reflecting light in shifting patterns that mirror the pulsing red veins of Floor 1. In wand form, it is an elegant spell focus. In blade form, it solidifies into a longsword of liquid silver.

The wand carries the residue of Caesar's original magic — the magic he gave up when he took the Mantle of Cruelty. It is pure magic, not cursed, but those who wield it should beware: power corrupts.

---

## Base Properties

- **Spell Focus**: This item can be used as a spellcasting focus. Spells cast through it have advantage on Constitution saving throws made to maintain concentration.
- **Metamorphic**: As a bonus action, you can transform the wand into a longsword or back into wand form. In longsword form, it retains its finesse and light properties.
- **Mantle Sense**: While attuned, you can sense concentrations of soul stone wealth, the locations of outposts, and the general health of Floor 1's soul economy within 1 mile of your position.
- **Spellblade Channel**: While in longsword form, you can deliver touch-range spells through the blade. When you hit a creature with a melee attack using the longsword, you can cast a spell with a range of touch as part of the same attack, targeting the creature hit.

---

## Scaling

This weapon grows in power as its wielder deepens their connection to Floor 1's mantle. Additional properties unlock at attunement thresholds.

### Dormant

The weapon in its base state with the properties above.

---

### Awakened

The wand learns to take combat form more aggressively. Gains the following additional properties:

- **Spectral Blades**: As a bonus action, you manifest 1-2 spectral silver swords that orbit your position. Each lasts for 1 minute (or until dismissed). As a bonus action on subsequent turns, you can direct one spectral blade to strike a creature within 30 feet. The blade deals 1d8 force damage on a hit and vanishes. You can summon new blades on subsequent turns, but you cannot have more than 2 orbiting at once.
- **Mantle Synchronization**: When cast through the wand, spells that deal damage deal additional damage equal to your spellcasting ability modifier. Spells that restore hit points restore additional hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.
- The wand counts as a +1 spell focus and the longsword as a +1 weapon.

---

### Exalted

The spectral blade formation expands. Gains the following additional properties:

- **Blade Formation**: As a bonus action, you manifest 4-6 spectral swords that orbit your position. They last for 1 minute. You can direct them as follows:
  - **Volley**: As an action, launch all orbiting blades at once at one or more creatures within 30 feet. Each blade makes a separate attack roll dealing 1d8 force damage on a hit.
  - **Turret**: As a bonus action, station up to 3 orbiting blades at points within 30 feet. A stationed blade attacks the nearest hostile creature that passes within 10 feet of it, making one attack per round for 1d6 force damage. Stationed blades last until destroyed (AC 18, 1 HP) or until you recall them.
  - **Barrier**: As a reaction when you are hit by an attack, you can collapse your orbiting blades into a defensive barrier, gaining a +5 bonus to AC against that attack. All orbiting blades are consumed.
- The wand counts as a +2 spell focus and the longsword as a +2 weapon.
- The longsword gains the **versatile (1d10)** property in addition to its existing properties.

---

### Champion

The wand fully awakens. Gains the following additional properties:

- **Grand Formation**: You can now manifest up to 12 spectral swords. The formation is permanent until dismissed — swords orbit indefinitely without action cost.
- **Autonomous Manifestation**: As a bonus action, you can release the wand from your hand. It transforms into a floating longsword that acts on its own initiative, making one attack per round against a creature of your choice within 30 feet. While the wand fights independently, you can still cast spells through the spectral blade formation. You can recall the wand to your hand as a free action.
- **Economy Agent**: The weapon draws power from Floor 1's accumulated soul stone wealth (see *Floor Economy Agent* scaling table).
- The wand counts as a +3 spell focus and the longsword as a +3 weapon.
