# Ash-Nest
*Weapon (flail), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Ash-Nest was forged from the ashes of the first phoenix to ever die — not the first to burn and be reborn, but the first to truly, permanently end. When that phoenix knew its final death was coming — not the cyclical death of flame, but the true death that comes when a creature of immortal fire finally exhausts its last ember — it flew into a dying star and burned there, refusing rebirth, pouring every remaining spark of its being into a single egg that would never hatch. That egg was found by a smith who walked between planes, and from its shell — fused with the star's own collapsing matter — the flail's head was shaped. Inside the cage of iron bands, a single ember glows: the last living piece of the first phoenix, burning eternally without fuel, waiting for a reason to ignite.

The flail's head is a hollow iron cage, its bars twisted into the suggestion of a bird in flight — wings swept back, beak upturned, tail-feathers trailing into the chain. Inside the cage, the ember pulses slowly, like a heartbeat. When the flail is swung, the ember flares brighter, casting dancing shadows. The chain is black iron that leaves trails of faint orange afterglow in the air. The handle is wrapped in something that looks like singed feathers — soft, warm, smelling faintly of woodsmoke.

The consciousness within Ash-Nest is the phoenix's memory — not its soul (that moved on), but its *habit*, the pattern of burning and rising that was etched so deeply into the creature's being that it imprinted on the ember. It communicates through warmth: a sudden flush of heat in the wielder's palm means danger, a gentle pulsing warmth means approval, a sharp searing heat means the blade is angry. It is protective, almost maternal — it remembers what it was to nurture a fire that would never go out. It favors wielders who protect others, who put themselves between danger and the defenseless, who would burn for someone else. It dislikes wanton arson, destruction without purpose, and people who put out campfires by kicking dirt on them rather than waiting for the embers to die naturally.

## Sealed State

Ash-Nest is a **+2 weapon**, granting a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls. When you hit a creature with it, the cage opens a crack on impact and the ember lashes out — the attack deals an additional **2d4 fire damage**.

When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points with Ash-Nest, the ember pulses with warmth and you gain **temporary hit points** equal to your Constitution modifier plus your proficiency bonus. The ember feeds on endings — each death is a tiny rebirth, and you are the beneficiary.

While holding Ash-Nest, you have **resistance to fire damage**. The ember is older than fire itself, and no mortal flame can harm you while it shelters you. The flail sheds **dim light in a 15-foot radius**, which you can suppress or rekindle as a bonus action. The light is warm and orange, the color of a dying campfire, comforting and melancholy at the same time.

Additionally, you can cast the ***produce flame*** cantrip at will while holding Ash-Nest, using the flail's cage as the origin point. The flame emerges directly from the ember — a flickering sphere of orange-gold fire that hovers above the cage, waiting for your command.

## Awakened State

Feed the ember with ash from a creature that died by fire within the past 24 hours. Hold the flail over the ash as an action, and the ember will drink — the ash draws upward into the cage, spiraling into the ember like a reverse pyre. When the last speck of ash is consumed, the ember speaks the true name: a single syllable in the primordial language of elemental fire, a word that means *again*.

Speak the name as a bonus action, and the cage opens. The bars uncurl like a flower blooming — or like a bird spreading its wings after a long sleep. The ember bursts into full flame, shaping itself into the form of a phoenix the size of a hawk, its body the flail's head, its wings stretching upward along the chain, its tail-feathers streaming behind in ribbons of orange and gold. This state lasts for **1 minute**. Once invoked, the blade cannot be awakened again until you finish a long rest.

While awakened, the fire bonus damage increases to **3d6**. The flail's reach extends to **15 feet** as the phoenix-form uncoils — the chain lengthens with wings of flame, and the head lunges forward like a striking raptor.

At the start of each of your turns while awakened, burning **Cinders** fall in three 5-foot squares of your choice within 15 feet of you — embers shed from the phoenix's wings. A creature that enters a cinder square or starts its turn in one takes **1d4 fire damage**. The cinders persist until the awakening ends. You can reposition no more than two cinder squares to new locations within range as a bonus action.

The ember's nurturing instinct awakens fully. At the start of each of your turns, if a friendly creature within 30 feet of you has 0 hit points and is making death saving throws, it gains **5 temporary hit points** as the phoenix's warmth reaches out to shelter the dying. These temporary hit points do not stabilize the creature, but they may buy it another hit before the darkness takes it.

Once per awakening, when you would be reduced to 0 hit points while the phoenix-form is active, you can invoke **Ember Rebirth** as a reaction. Instead of dropping to 0, you drop to **1 hit point**. The phoenix-form detonates in a **20-foot-radius burst** of concussive flame — each creature of your choice in the area must make a **DC 17 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **6d6 fire damage** on a failure and half as much on a success. The awakening immediately ends, and the blade cannot be awakened for **1d4 days** as the ember recovers inside its cage. The ember is not extinguished — it has simply burned through everything it had, and it needs time to remember how to be a fire.

## The True Name

The true name of Ash-Nest is a word in the primordial language of elemental fire — a single syllable that means *again*, or perhaps *once more*, or perhaps *not yet done*. To learn it, the wielder must gather ash from a creature that died by fire within the past 24 hours — natural fire, not magical cold-fire or necrotic burn. The ember will consume the ash, and as the last particle is drawn into the cage, the word will form in the wielder's mind like a blister popping: sudden, sharp, and impossible to ignore. Different creatures' ashes teach the same word. The ember does not care whose ash it drinks — only that it drinks, and remembers, and decides that one more time might be worth it.
