# Singularity Edge
*Weapon (greatsword), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Forged in the collapse chamber of a dying star — not by a smith, but by the star itself, in the instant before it became a singularity. The process compressed celestial matter beyond any physical limit, then froze it in the moment before the mass became infinite. The result is a greatsword whose blade does not gleam. It *absorbs* light near its edge, bending it around the apex like a lens of pure gravity. Looking directly at the blade's terminus for too long causes a pressure headache and a creeping sense of falling sideways.

The weapon weighs five hundred pounds. To anyone not attuned, it may as well be welded to the planet. To its wielder, it floats — heavy and dense and impossibly present, but light as a held breath.

The true name of Singularity Edge is not a word spoken aloud. It is a subsonic frequency — a tone so low it borders on silence, felt in the chest and the base of the skull. To learn it, the wielder must stand in absolute silence until they can hear the note beneath the silence — the hum of compressed space, the bass string of a black hole. When the name is finally spoken, it is not heard so much as *endured*: the air cracks, a shockwave radiates outward, and the blade vanishes into invisibility, visible only as a gravitational distortion — heat shimmer, but black.

## Properties

### Sealed State
*The blade rests. The weight is present but patient.*

**+2 Weapon.** You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with Singularity Edge.

**Spatial Shear.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, the attack deals an additional 2d6 force damage.

**Gravitic Anchor.** You cannot be moved against your will while holding this weapon. You are immune to being pushed, pulled, shoved, or otherwise forcibly relocated by any effect. Spells that would teleport you against your will (such as *plane shift* or a hostile *teleport*) automatically fail — the blade's mass anchors you to your current location in spacetime.

**Event Horizon Sight.** The blade hums at a subsonic frequency within 10 feet of invisible creatures, hidden doors, or extradimensional spaces (such as a *bag of holding*, *portable hole*, or the mouth of a *rope trick*). The hum is felt, not heard — a pressure change in your inner ear. The blade feels the fabric of space and tells you when something is wrong with it.

**Critical Mass.** When you score a critical hit with this weapon, the target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 15 feet directly toward you and knocked prone. The gravity of the strike lingers.

---

### Awakened State — *"Singularity Edge"*
*Speak the true name. The blade vanishes into pure gravitational distortion. Gravity becomes a weapon that grows hungrier by the second.*

**Invocation.** As an action, you speak the blade's true name — a subsonic frequency that requires a full breath to produce correctly. When the name is spoken, a shockwave radiates from you: each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone. The blade's physical form vanishes, visible only as a gravitational lensing distortion in the air — like heat shimmer, but black, an outline of bent light. This state lasts for 1 minute. Once you invoke the blade, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

**Compressed Edge.** While awakened, the blade must be wielded two-handed, and your reach with it increases to 10 feet. The distortion extends the effective cutting edge. Attacks with the awakened blade deal the weapon's normal damage plus 4d6 force damage (replacing the 2d6 force from the sealed state). The damage type remains slashing for the base weapon dice and force for the bonus dice.

**Event Horizon Critical.** While awakened, your attack rolls with this weapon score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

**Gravity Well.** At the start of each of your turns while awakened, the blade's gravitational influence expands outward. The Gravity Well is a sphere centered on you with the following cumulative effects based on the current round of the awakening:

| Round | Radius | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 ft | Creatures of your choice within the Well have their speed halved. |
| 2 | 15 ft | Speed halved, and the area is difficult terrain for creatures of your choice. |
| 3 | 20 ft | Above, plus creatures of your choice within the Well have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws. |
| 4 | 30 ft | Above, plus any creature of your choice that ends its turn within the Well must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 15 feet toward you. |
| 5+ | 30 ft | Same as round 4. |

The effects persist at the round-5+ level for the remainder of the awakening. The Gravity Well moves with you. The radius and effects are cumulative — each round adds to or expands the previous round's effects.

**Singularity (1/Awakening).** As an action, you can collapse the Gravity Well into a single point of crushing gravitational force. Each creature of your choice currently within the Gravity Well must make a DC 17 Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature is pulled to the nearest unoccupied space within 5 feet of you, takes 10d10 force damage, and is restrained until the end of your next turn as residual gravity holds it in place. On a success, a creature takes half as much damage and is not pulled or restrained. Huge and larger creatures have advantage on this saving throw.

## Balance Notes
Singularity Edge is a battlefield control weapon disguised as a damage dealer. At Very Rare, its sealed state provides strong, reliable damage (2d6 + 2d6 + 2 force) comparable to a *flame tongue* (2d6 fire, rare) with an additional +2 enhancement and a powerful forced-movement immunity — a rare defensive property that hard-counters a common control tactic. The spatial anomaly detection is a ribbon with significant exploration utility.

The awakened state transforms the wielder into a gravity nexus. The scaling Gravity Well creates a tactical clock: enemies must decide whether to flee early (when the effects are mild) or stand and fight (and risk being pulled into melee against a greatsword wielder). The design rewards patience — the first two rounds are setup, rounds 3-4 create kill conditions, and round 5+ is the payoff. Single-target boss monsters with legendary resistances will shrug off the pull, but minions and lieutenants will be dragged into the blender.

The 10d10 Singularity (avg 55) is on par with a 5th-level *cone of cold* (8d8, 36 avg) but with a smaller AoE (Gravity Well radius) and the additional restraint rider. Since it consumes the only awakening use, it's a finisher, not a spammable nuke.

The 500-lb weight is a double-edged sword: the wielder can never be disarmed, but if the blade is ever unattuned, retrieving it becomes a logistical challenge requiring specialized equipment or magic. This is intentional — the blade chooses its wielder, not the other way around.
