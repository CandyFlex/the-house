# Corona
*Weapon (greatsword), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A greatsword whose blade contains the captured heart of a dying star — not a metaphor, but literal stellar matter suspended in a magnetic bottle of white-gold metal. The blade is warm to the touch at all times, like a stone that has sat in sunlight for hours. Veins of amber-orange light pulse slowly along the fuller in a steady rhythm: a heartbeat in light, the star's core still burning, still alive, still dying.

The true name of Corona is a harmonic frequency — a specific note that the blade emits as a faint electromagnetic song, audible only to its wielder as a ringing in the teeth. To learn the name, the wielder must learn to sing the matching note back — not with their voice, but with their *presence*, a resonance achieved through proximity to great heat and light. Solar forges, volcanic calderas, the heart of a forest fire. The blade listens for the echo.

When the name is sung correctly, the blade erupts into stellar plasma — pure white at the core, amber at the edges, shedding light that is not merely bright but *hungry*. Matter that touches the edge is not burned. It is annihilated. Converted to light and heat on contact. The blade cycles through the phases of a star's life in miniature: ignition, main sequence, red giant, and finally — if you choose — nova.

The spirit within Corona does not speak in words. It sings in electromagnetic frequencies — the song of hydrogen fusing into helium, of stellar wind across magnetic field lines, of a billion years compressed into a single held note. It is proud. It was a star. It has seen the birth of elements. It does not serve easily, but when it sings with its wielder rather than at them, the harmony is blinding.

## Properties

### Sealed State
*The star sleeps. Its heartbeat is felt but not seen.*

**+2 Weapon.** You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with Corona.

**Stellar Edge.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, the attack deals an additional 1d6 radiant damage.

**Coronal Flare (1/Short Rest).** As a bonus action, you can cause the blade to flare with stellar intensity. Until the start of your next turn, the blade sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. This light is true sunlight. Any creature of your choice that starts its turn within the bright light must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be unable to take reactions until the start of your next turn. The flare does not deal damage — it is a warning, not a weapon. Yet.

**Electromagnetic Song.** While holding Corona, you have blindsight out to 10 feet. The blade emits a continuous electromagnetic frequency that maps the shape of nearby objects and creatures and sings the results into your nervous system. This blindsight is not blocked by the blinded condition — it is not sight, but song.

**Child of the Hearth.** You have resistance to fire damage while attuned to Corona. Additionally, you suffer no ill effects from extreme heat (as if under the effects of a *ring of warmth*, but only for heat).

---

### Awakened State — *"Pillar of Dawn"*
*Sing the harmonic. The star wakes. Its lifecycle plays out in minutes instead of eons — and you are the conductor.*

**Invocation.** As a bonus action, you sing the blade's true name — the harmonic frequency that matches its electromagnetic song. The blade must be in at least dim light to ignite. The blade erupts into white-gold stellar plasma and remains awakened for 1 minute. Once you invoke the blade, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

**Plasma Form.** While awakened, Corona can be wielded one-handed (gaining the versatile property) or two-handed. The blade's base damage remains 2d6 slashing (or 1d10 one-handed), but its radiant bonus damage changes based on the current phase (see below).

**Stellar Phase Cycle.** The plasma that forms the awakened blade evolves through the lifecycle of a star, compressed into a single minute. At the start of your first turn after awakening, the blade enters **Ignition**. The phase advances automatically every 2 rounds. The current phase ends at the completion of your second turn in that phase, and the next phase begins at the start of your following turn.

| Rounds | Phase | Radiant Bonus | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Ignition** | +5d6 | **Unstable Surge.** The first time you hit with Corona on each of your turns, the attack deals the bonus radiant damage. On a hit: the target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or ignite (2d6 fire damage at the start of each of its turns, save ends at end of turn). **Unstable.** The plasma is raw and unrefined — attack rolls against you have advantage while in this phase. |
| 3–4 | **Main Sequence** | +4d6 | **Sustained Burn.** Every hit deals the bonus radiant damage. Your attack rolls with Corona score a critical hit on a roll of 18–20. **Ambient Radiation.** Any creature of your choice that ends its turn within 10 feet of you takes radiant damage equal to your proficiency bonus. |
| 5–6 | **Red Giant** | +3d6 | **Eruption.** Every hit deals the bonus radiant damage. Your reach with Corona increases by 5 feet. On a hit, you can push the target up to 10 feet away from you. The blade swells, trembles, and leaks ribbons of amber light like solar prominences. |
| 7+ | **Collapse** | — | The awakening ends. You may release a **Nova Pulse** (see below) as a parting action before the blade returns to its sealed state. |

**Coronal Ejection (Recharge 6).** As an action during any phase, you can sweep Corona in a wide arc, ejecting a wave of stellar plasma in a 40-foot cone. Each creature in the cone must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d8 radiant damage on a failure and half as much on a success. A creature that fails the save is also blinded for 1 minute. A blinded creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. Using Coronal Ejection instantly advances the current phase — skip to the next phase immediately. You cannot use this during the Collapse phase.

**Nova Pulse (1/Awakening).** During the Collapse phase of the awakening (round 7, before the blade returns to its sealed state), you may choose to release the star's remaining energy in a catastrophic pulse rather than letting it fade quietly. Each creature of your choice within 60 feet of you must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 12d10 radiant damage on a failure and half as much on a success. A creature that fails the save is also blinded and takes 2d10 radiant damage at the start of each of its turns for 1 minute. A blinded creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending both effects on a success. After releasing the Nova Pulse, the blade returns to its sealed state and cannot be awakened again for 1d4 days as the star's heart cools.

## Mechanics Block (DM Quick Reference)

- **Rarity:** Very Rare
- **Attunement:** Yes
- **Save DC:** 17
- **Activation:** Bonus action (must be in dim light or brighter), 1/long rest, 1 minute
- **Sealed DPR (avg):** 2d6 + 1d6 + 2 + STR = 13 + STR, plus 1/short rest flare (reaction denial)
- **Phase DPR at STR +5:**
  - Ignition: 2d6 + 5d6 + 2 + 5 = 32 avg (first hit only), attacks vs you at advantage
  - Main Sequence: 2d6 + 4d6 + 2 + 5 = 28 avg per hit, crit 18-20, 10ft passive PB damage
  - Red Giant: 2d6 + 3d6 + 2 + 5 = 24 avg per hit, reach +5ft, push 10ft on hit
- **Nova Pulse:** 12d10 (66 avg) 60ft AoE + blind + 2d10/round ongoing — costs 1d4 days of downtime
- **Coronal Ejection:** 8d8 (36 avg) 40ft cone + blind, skips current phase — tactical acceleration

## Balance Notes
Corona is a weapon that plays a game of risk timing rather than resource management. The sealed state is intentionally restrained — you're a fire-resistant fighter with a glowing sword and a reaction-denial tool, comparable to a *frost brand* with a utility rider. The power is in the awakening, and the awakening is a tactical puzzle.

**Ignition (rounds 1–2):** Maximum damage output (32 DPR on first hit each turn), but attacks against you have advantage. This is the "vulnerable berserker" phase — devastating against a disabled or isolated target, suicidal in a crowd. Smart players will use this against a priority target that's already controlled, or pair it with defensive buffs from allies.

**Main Sequence (rounds 3–4):** The "golden window." Crit on 18-20 with 28 damage per hit is elite-tier sustained DPR, and the 10ft ambient radiation punishes creatures that linger. This is where you want to spend your Action Surge, smites, or other nova resources.

**Red Giant (rounds 5–6):** Lower damage but reach and push create control options. Push enemies off ledges, break grapples, reposition the battlefield.

**Coronal Ejection** adds a strategic layer: do you skip a favorable phase to get an AoE blind off? Do you rush to Red Giant for the push or linger in Main Sequence for the crit-fishing?

**Nova Pulse** is a double-edged sword. 12d10 in a 60ft radius is encounter-ending at most tables. But silencing your Very Rare weapon for 1d4 days is a genuine cost — this is a dungeon boss's last resort, not a daily driver.

The true cost of Corona is decision-making. Every round, the phase forces you to reassess your tactics. The weapon doesn't just scale — it *changes*, and the wielder must change with it.

## Variants
**Corona, Eversun (Legendary).** +3 greatsword. The phase cycle now loops: after Red Giant (rounds 5–6), the blade returns to Ignition (rounds 7–8) and repeats — it does not collapse. Coronal Ejection recharges on a 5–6. Nova Pulse can be activated as an action at any time (still 1/awakening, still silences the blade for 1d4 days). Radiant damage from Corona ignores resistance. Cost: 15,000,000 soul stones.
