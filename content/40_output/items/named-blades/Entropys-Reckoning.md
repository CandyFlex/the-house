# Entropy's Reckoning
*Weapon (greatsword), legendary (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
If Corona is a star's birth captured in a blade, Entropy's Reckoning is the universe's final heat-death — the last moment of the last photon, frozen in metal. The blade is the color of absolute zero: a blue so deep it reads as black, veined with white lines that spread across the surface in fractal patterns like frost on a windowpane. The frost is not frozen water. It is the *absence of molecular motion* — atoms that have forgotten how to vibrate, spreading outward in geometric stillness.

The blade is cold in a way that has nothing to do with temperature. Holding it, the wielder feels their heartbeat slow. Their breath does not fog. Sound near the blade seems to die before it reaches the ear — not silenced, but *exhausted*, the vibrations losing energy before they can travel. Flowers wilt when the blade passes. Candles gutter and fail to relight.

The true name of Entropy's Reckoning is not a sound and not a word. It is a *shape of silence* — a specific void in the air that must be held perfectly still, a negative space that exists only when all other sound has ceased. To learn the name, the wielder must stand in absolute silence and absolute stillness — no heartbeat, no breath, no thought — until the void acquires a form. And then they must hold that form in their mind while the blade judges whether the emptiness is genuine or merely performed. The blade has waited for the heat-death of the universe. It has patience beyond patience. It will know if you are pretending.

When the name is invoked — the shape of silence held and released — the blade does not ignite or transform. It simply *stops pretending to be a sword*. The frost veins spread from the blade into the air itself, a growing field of absolute entropy where healing magic curdles, sound fails, and living things begin to forget how to be alive. The field expands outward each round, hungrier and wider, until the battlefield is a frozen tableau and only the wielder remains in motion.

The spirit within Entropy's Reckoning does not speak, does not sing, does not pulse or tighten or hunger. It waits. It is the last thing that will exist when everything else has stopped. It is not cruel. Cruelty requires caring. The blade simply knows how everything ends, and it sees no reason to rush.

## Properties

### Sealed State
*The blade waits. The cold is patient.*

**+3 Weapon.** You have a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with Entropy's Reckoning.

**Heat-Death Edge.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, the attack deals an additional 2d6 cold damage and 2d6 necrotic damage. The cold is absolute — it reduces the target's speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**Terminal Critical.** Your attack rolls with Entropy's Reckoning score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20. When you score a critical hit, the target is paralyzed until the end of your next turn. This paralysis is not a magical effect — it is the molecules in the target's nervous system being briefly convinced that motion is no longer possible. Creatures immune to cold damage are immune to this paralysis.

**Heat-Death Claim.** When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with Entropy's Reckoning, that creature cannot regain hit points or be the target of healing or resurrection magic until the start of your next turn. The moment of death is frozen in time — suspended, final, irreversible. If the creature dies from this (fails death saves while under this effect), its body remains at exactly the moment of death — no decay, no cooling, no biological process of any kind.

**Absolute Zero.** You are immune to cold damage while attuned to Entropy's Reckoning. You have resistance to necrotic damage. You suffer no ill effects from extreme cold. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against effects that would reduce your speed, paralyze you, or petrify you — your body has learned, through proximity to the blade, how to continue moving when physics demands stillness.

---

### Awakened State — *"Entropy's Reckoning"*
*Release the shape of silence. The blade stops pretending. The field grows. Nothing is permitted to continue.*

**Invocation.** As an action, you release the true name — the shape of silence held perfectly still. A pulse of absolute quiet radiates 30 feet from you: all sound ceases in this area. Spells with verbal components cannot be cast within it. Creatures within the area are deafened while they remain inside. The blade weeps wisps of frozen void — visible as trails of absolute black that fall from the edge like cold smoke. This state lasts for 1 minute. Once you invoke the blade, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

**Frozen Reach.** While awakened, the blade must be wielded two-handed, and your reach with it increases to 10 feet. The entropy trailing from the edge extends the blade's effective cutting surface.

**Absolute Edge.** While awakened, damage dealt by Entropy's Reckoning becomes 2d6 slashing + 3d6 cold + 3d6 necrotic. Your attack rolls with the awakened blade score a critical hit on a roll of 18, 19, or 20. On a critical hit, the target is paralyzed until the end of your next turn (as per Terminal Critical, above).

**Entropy Field.** At the start of each of your turns while awakened, the blade's entropic influence expands outward. The Entropy Field is a sphere centered on you with the following cumulative effects. The field moves with you. Creatures of your choice are affected.

| Round | Radius | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 ft | Affected creatures cannot regain hit points while inside the field. Healing spells cast on them fail. Regeneration abilities cease. |
| 2 | 20 ft | Above, plus affected creatures have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws. |
| 3 | 25 ft | Above, plus affected creatures' speeds are halved. |
| 4 | 35 ft | Above, plus any affected creature that ends its turn within the field must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 level of exhaustion. A creature can gain no more than 3 levels of exhaustion from this effect. Exhaustion gained from the Entropy Field fades after the creature finishes a long rest (instead of the normal recovery rate). |
| 5+ | 35 ft | Same as round 4. |

The effects persist at the round-5+ level for the remainder of the awakening.

**Zero State (1/Awakening).** As an action, you can compress the Entropy Field into absolute stillness centered on up to 3 creatures of your choice within the field. Each target must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature is paralyzed for 1 minute (it can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success). If a creature fails the initial save by 5 or more, it is instead petrified — encased in ice that cannot be melted by nonmagical means. The ice is not cold; it is the absence of thermal energy. A *greater restoration* or similar magic ends the petrification. Additionally, all spell effects of 3rd level or lower currently active within the Entropy Field are immediately dispelled.

**Stasis Claim (Passive in Awakened State).** Any creature that starts its turn within the Entropy Field with 0 hit points cannot make death saving throws while it remains in the field. It does not stabilize, it does not bleed out, it does not die — it is held at exactly 0 hit points, frozen in the instant between life and death. The moment it leaves the Entropy Field (or the field ends), its death saves resume from wherever they were, or it remains at 0 HP and stable if its death saves had not yet been failed. This effect cannot be used to indefinitely forestall death — it pauses the clock, it does not reset it.

## Balance Notes
Entropy's Reckoning is the dark mirror of Singularity Edge — where the gravity blade pulls everything toward you, the entropy blade makes everything *stop*. At Legendary tier, the sealed state alone is formidable: 30 average DPR on a +3 weapon with 19-20 crit range and on-crit paralysis that bypasses legendary resistance (it's not a spell, it's physics). The Heat-Death Claim passive is a boss-execution tool that prevents last-second healing or *death ward* triggers — niche but devastating when it matters.

The awakened Entropy Field is a siege weapon. It doesn't pull enemies toward you — it traps them in a growing zone of negation. The exhaustion mechanic (up to 3 levels, fading on long rest) is the most dangerous effect in the arsenal: 3 levels of exhaustion means disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws, which is effectively a death sentence against a legendary-tier wielder. The Con save DC 19 gates this behind a save that high-CR creatures are good at, but the cumulative pressure of "make this save every round or get worse" will eventually crack even ancient dragons.

Zero State is a triple-target "save or suck" button: paralyze for 1 minute with recurring saves, or petrify outright on a -5 margin. Combined with the field's disadvantage on Dexterity saves (and therefore the inability to effectively dodge follow-up attacks), a paralyzed target within the field is nearly guaranteed to eat multiple critical hits from Entropy's Reckoning — and each crit re-paralyzes them. This is the blade's kill pattern: field → exhaust → paralyze → crit-chain → dead.

Stasis Claim (pausing death saves) is a utility ribbon with disturbing narrative potential. A dying ally can be preserved indefinitely within the field while the wielder finishes the fight. A dying enemy can be held at 0 HP while the party decides what to do with them. It changes the tempo of death itself.

The counterplay is range. The Entropy Field is centered on the wielder and grows slowly. Enemies who recognize the pattern can flee early, forcing the wielder to chase. Mobile enemies, teleporters, and flyers can kite the field. The blade is a zone-control tool — devastating inside its radius, irrelevant outside it.

## Variants
**Entropy's Reckoning, Final Silence (Artifact).** +4 greatsword. Damage becomes 4d6 cold + 4d6 necrotic (awakened). Entropy Field begins at round 1 with a 20-foot radius and affects all creatures (not chosen). Zero State affects all creatures in the field and paralyzes on any failed save, petrifying on a -3 margin. Stasis Claim becomes permanent — creatures at 0 HP in the field can be sustained indefinitely at the wielder's will. Cost: unpriced — cannot be bought, only found or forged through the death of a god.
