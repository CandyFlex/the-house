# Chronos Severance
*Weapon (longsword), legendary (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Chronos Severance was never forged in the conventional sense. It was *severed* — cut away from the timestream at a point where two incompatible futures intersected and one had to be sacrificed. The paradox of that moment — a timeline that both happened and didn't — was extracted by entities unknown, compressed, and shaped into a blade that exists slightly ahead of its own present.

The blade is silver-white with a permanent blur at its edge — not motion blur, but *temporal* blur, the blade existing across multiple adjacent moments simultaneously, each one fractionally offset from the last. Looking at it directly causes a faint pressure behind the eyes and a sense of déjà vu that deepens the longer you stare. Sometimes, in peripheral vision, you catch the blade in a position it wasn't in a moment ago. Sometimes you catch it *before* it moves. These are not illusions. The blade exists in a superposition of moments, and your eyes are simply not built to resolve them all at once.

The true name of Chronos Severance is not a sound, not a word, not a frequency, not a shape of silence — it is a *moment*. A single instant of absolute consequence that the wielder must witness while holding the blade: a death, a birth, a betrayal, a forgiveness, the exact second a decision branches reality. The blade will show you the moment when it comes. Your task is to recognize it. Many wielders have held Chronos Severance for years and never learned its name, because they were looking for a word when they should have been paying attention to their own lives. The blade does not reward cleverness. It rewards presence.

When the name is invoked — the moment recognized and accepted — the blade does not erupt or vanish or unfold. It *splits*. Three translucent temporal echoes fan out from the hilt in a ghostly arc: the blade as it was one second ago, the blade as it is now, the blade as it will be one second from now. All three are real. All three can cut. And the wielder's personal timestream accelerates — moving faster than causality, acting when others are still reacting, striking from moments that haven't happened yet.

The spirit within Chronos Severance is neither proud nor hungry nor patient. It is *urgent*. It communicates through the blade's blur — a flicker that means "move," a shimmer that means "wait," a sudden clarity that means "now." It has seen every possible version of the next five seconds and it is trying, desperately, to tell you which one you should choose. It does not speak because speech takes time, and time is the one thing it cannot spare.

## Properties

### Sealed State
*The blade flickers. It is already slightly ahead of you — waiting for you to catch up.*

**+3 Weapon.** You have a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with Chronos Severance.

**Out-of-Phase Edge.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, the attack deals an additional 2d6 force damage. The blade's edge exists slightly out of phase with normal matter — it does not cut flesh so much as it severs the target's connection to the present moment.

**Temporal Lag.** When you hit a creature with Chronos Severance, the target's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of your next turn, as its personal timestream decouples slightly from objective time. This effect can stack. If a creature's speed is reduced to 0 by this effect, it must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw at the end of its turn or be paralyzed until the end of your next turn — it is trapped in the wrong second, unable to act in the one where everything else is happening. Creatures immune to the paralyzed condition are not immune to this effect; they are not paralyzed, they are *absent*.

**Foresight Reflex.** Initiative rolls made while you are holding Chronos Severance have advantage. Additionally, once per round when a creature you can see misses you with an attack roll, you can use your reaction to make one melee weapon attack against that creature — you saw the miss coming half a second before the attacker decided to swing.

**Temporal Anchor.** You are immune to the slowed condition. Your speed cannot be reduced by magical effects (though difficult terrain and other physical impediments still apply). Effects that would banish you to another plane, shunt you forward in time, or otherwise remove you from the present moment (such as *banishment*, *maze*, or a sphinx's lair action) are made at disadvantage against you, and you have advantage on saving throws against them. You are too firmly rooted in your own timestream to be easily displaced.

---

### Awakened State — *"Chronos Severance"*
*Recognize the moment. The blade splits. You exist in multiple seconds at once.*

**Invocation.** As a bonus action, you recognize the name — the moment the blade showed you, finally understood. The blade splits into three translucent temporal echoes that fan out from the hilt in a ghostly arc: the blade-as-it-was, the blade-as-it-is, the blade-as-it-will-be. All three are real. Your personal timestream accelerates. This state lasts for 1 minute. Once you invoke the blade, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

**Fan of Moments.** While awakened, Chronos Severance can be wielded one-handed (gaining the versatile property) or two-handed. Damage becomes 1d8 slashing + 3d6 force (one-handed) or 2d8 slashing + 3d6 force (two-handed). The Temporal Lag speed reduction and paralysis effects from the sealed state continue to apply when you hit.

**Accelerated Action.** On each of your turns while the blade is awakened, you can take one additional action. This action can be used to take the Attack action (one weapon attack only, not the full Attack action), Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, or Use an Object. You cannot use this additional action to cast a spell or activate a magic item. You are not faster — you are *earlier*, acting in the gaps between moments where no one else is moving yet.

**Temporal Echo.** At the start of each of your turns while awakened, a translucent echo of yourself — a version of you from approximately 0.5 seconds in the future — flickers into existence in an unoccupied space of your choice within 5 feet of you. The echo can make one melee weapon attack against a creature within its reach using your attack bonus and dealing force damage instead of the weapon's normal damage type (the echo's blade has no physical edge, only temporal). The echo scores a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20. After making its attack, the echo vanishes until the start of your next turn. The echo is not a creature; it cannot be targeted, damaged, or affected by any effect.

**Sever the Timeline (1/Awakening).** As an action, you can sever a creature's connection to the present moment. Choose a creature you can see within 60 feet of you. That creature must make a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature vanishes — it is shunted exactly 1 round into the future. At the start of your next turn, the creature reappears in the space it vanished from (or the nearest unoccupied space if that space is now occupied) with the same hit points, conditions, and ongoing effects it had when it vanished. The creature has no memory of the intervening time — to it, the world simply skipped. Creatures that cannot be affected by time-altering magic (at the DM's discretion, such as creatures specifically noted as existing outside of time) are immune. On a successful save, the creature takes 8d10 psychic damage and has its speed halved until the end of your next turn as its mind recoils from glimpsing its own severed timeline branching into paths that should not exist.

## Balance Notes
Chronos Severance is the speed-blade — the opposite of the pull-and-stop patterns of Singularity Edge and Entropy's Reckoning. Where those blades control space, Chronos Severance controls *time*. The sealed state is deceptively strong: +3 weapon with 2d6 force, stacking speed reduction, and a once-per-round free reaction attack on any miss against you. The speed-reduction stacking is the bread-and-butter — hit a creature twice and it's at -20 speed, three times and it's paralyzed unless it makes a DC 19 Wisdom save. This punishes melee enemies who engage you and gives you a powerful kiting tool against anything that needs to close distance.

The Foresight Reflex reaction attack is free damage that costs a reaction — a fair trade, but it also means the wielder has to choose between this and opportunity attacks, *shield*, etc. The Temporal Anchor passive is a hard counter to *slow*, *banishment*, and labyrinth effects that many legendary monsters use — it's specifically designed to let the wielder stay in the fight when other martials would be sidelined.

The awakened state is where Chronos Severance becomes arguably the highest DPR weapon in the Named Blades collection. With Extra Attack (2 attacks), the Temporal Echo (1 attack), and the Accelerated Action (1 attack), a level 11+ fighter can make 4 attacks per round — each dealing ~28 damage two-handed — for ~112 average DPR before accounting for hit chance. Add Action Surge for two full Attack actions plus the echo attack plus the accelerated action and you're looking at 7 attacks in a single round (~196 avg). This is legendary-tier burst.

Sever the Timeline is the signature. Removing a creature from combat for 1 full round — no save to resist the removal — is encounter-warping. The creature reappears with the same HP and conditions, so it's not a kill button, but it's a guaranteed momentum swing. Use it on the enemy caster concentrating on a devastating spell — the spell drops because the caster isn't present to concentrate. Use it on the enemy tank guarding the boss — the boss is now exposed for a full round. Use it on the boss itself while the party wipes its minions. The 8d10 (44 avg) on a successful save means it's never a wasted action.

The counterplay is focus fire. The wielder of Chronos Severance has high DPR but no defensive steroids — no healing, no Temp HP, no AC boost beyond the +3 weapon's accuracy enabling defensive builds. A creature that can survive the initial barrage and hit back hard will pressure the wielder. The blade rewards aggression and punishes hesitation — fitting for a weapon whose spirit is urgency incarnate.

## Variants
**Chronos Severance, First Moment (Artifact).** +4 longsword. Temporal Echo makes two attacks instead of one. Accelerated Action can be used to take the full Attack action (not limited to one attack). Sever the Timeline can target up to 3 creatures and on a failed save they are shunted 2 rounds into the future instead of 1. Cost: unpriced — must be forged at the exact moment of a universe's birth, which is logistically challenging.
