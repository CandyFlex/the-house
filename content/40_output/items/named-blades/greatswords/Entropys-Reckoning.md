# Entropy's Reckoning
*Weapon (greatsword), legendary (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
If Corona is a star's birth captured in a blade, Entropy's Reckoning is the universe's final heat-death — the last moment of the last photon, frozen in metal. The blade is the color of absolute zero: a blue so deep it reads as black, veined with white lines that spread across the surface in fractal patterns like frost on a windowpane. The frost is not frozen water. It is the *absence of molecular motion* — atoms that have forgotten how to vibrate, spreading outward in geometric stillness.

The blade is cold in a way that has nothing to do with temperature. Holding it, the wielder feels their heartbeat slow. Their breath does not fog. Sound near the blade seems to die before it reaches the ear — not silenced, but *exhausted*, the vibrations losing energy before they can travel. Flowers wilt when the blade passes. Candles gutter and fail to relight.

The true name of Entropy's Reckoning is not a sound and not a word. It is a *shape of silence* — a specific void in the air that must be held perfectly still, a negative space that exists only when all other sound has ceased. To learn the name, the wielder must stand in absolute silence and absolute stillness — no heartbeat, no breath, no thought — until the void acquires a form. And then they must hold that form in their mind while the blade judges whether the emptiness is genuine or merely performed. The blade has waited for the heat-death of the universe. It has patience beyond patience. It will know if you are pretending.

When the name is invoked — the shape of silence held and released — the blade does not ignite or transform. It simply *stops pretending to be a sword*. The frost veins spread from the blade into the air itself, a growing field of absolute entropy where healing magic curdles, sound fails, and living things begin to forget how to be alive. The field expands outward each round, hungrier and wider, until the battlefield is a frozen tableau and only the wielder remains in motion.

The spirit within Entropy's Reckoning does not speak, does not sing, does not pulse or tighten or hunger. It waits. It is the last thing that will exist when everything else has stopped. It is not cruel. Cruelty requires caring. The blade simply knows how everything ends, and it sees no reason to rush.

## Properties

Entropy's Reckoning is a **+3 weapon**, granting a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls. When you hit a creature with it, the attack deals an additional **2d6 cold damage** and **2d6 necrotic damage** — the cold is absolute, and it reduces the target's speed by **10 feet** until the start of your next turn.

Your attack rolls with Entropy's Reckoning score a critical hit on a roll of **19 or 20**. When you score a critical hit, the target is paralyzed until the end of your next turn. This paralysis is not a magical effect — it is the molecules in the target's nervous system being briefly convinced that motion is no longer possible. Creatures immune to cold damage are immune to this paralysis.

When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with this weapon, that creature cannot regain hit points or be the target of healing or resurrection magic until the start of your next turn. The moment of death is frozen in time — suspended, final, irreversible. If the creature dies under this effect, its body remains at exactly the moment of death: no decay, no cooling, no biological process of any kind.

You are **immune to cold damage** while attuned to Entropy's Reckoning. You have **resistance to necrotic damage**, and you suffer no ill effects from extreme cold. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against effects that would reduce your speed, paralyze you, or petrify you — your body has learned, through proximity to the blade, how to continue moving when physics demands stillness.

---

Release the shape of silence. The blade stops pretending.

As an action, you release the true name — the shape of silence held perfectly still. A pulse of absolute quiet radiates **30 feet** from you: all sound ceases in this area. Spells with verbal components cannot be cast within it. Creatures within the area are deafened while they remain inside. The blade weeps wisps of frozen void — visible as trails of absolute black that fall from the edge like cold smoke. This state lasts for **1 minute**. Once invoked, the blade cannot be awakened again until you finish a long rest.

While awakened, the blade must be wielded two-handed, and your reach extends to **10 feet** as the entropy trailing from the edge extends the blade's effective cutting surface. Damage dealt by the awakened blade becomes **2d6 slashing plus 3d6 cold plus 3d6 necrotic**. Your attack rolls with the awakened blade score a critical hit on a roll of **18, 19, or 20**, and the on-crit paralysis from the sealed state continues to apply.

At the start of each of your turns, the blade's entropic influence expands outward in an **Entropy Field** centered on you that moves with you. Creatures of your choice are affected, and the effects are cumulative. On the **first round**, the field reaches **15 feet** — affected creatures cannot regain hit points while inside; healing spells cast on them fail, and regeneration abilities cease. On the **second round**, the field expands to **20 feet** and affected creatures also have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws. On the **third round**, the field reaches **25 feet** and affected creatures' speeds are halved. On the **fourth round** and beyond, the field expands to **35 feet**, and any affected creature that ends its turn within the field must make a **DC 19 Constitution saving throw** or gain 1 level of exhaustion. A creature can gain no more than 3 levels of exhaustion from this effect, and exhaustion gained from the Entropy Field fades after the creature finishes a long rest rather than at the normal recovery rate. The field persists at this level for the remainder of the awakening.

Once per awakening, as an action, you can compress the Entropy Field into absolute stillness targeting up to **3 creatures** of your choice within the field — a **Zero State**. Each target must make a **DC 19 Constitution saving throw**. On a failure, a creature is paralyzed for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns to end the effect. If a creature fails the initial save by 5 or more, it is instead petrified — encased in ice that cannot be melted by nonmagical means. The ice is not cold; it is the absence of thermal energy. A *greater restoration* or similar magic ends the petrification. Additionally, all spell effects of 3rd level or lower currently active within the Entropy Field are immediately dispelled.

While the blade is awakened, any creature that starts its turn within the Entropy Field with 0 hit points cannot make death saving throws while it remains in the field. It does not stabilize, it does not bleed out, it does not die — it is held at exactly 0 hit points, frozen in the instant between life and death. The moment it leaves the field or the field ends, its death saves resume from wherever they were. This **Stasis Claim** pauses the clock. It does not reset it.
