# Split-Whisper
*Weapon (shortsword), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Split-Whisper is a paradox given form — a single consciousness that occupies two physical blades simultaneously, forged during a reality-breach where the mind of a dying astral leviathan was torn in half. One blade emerged from each hemisphere of the creature's brain as the breach collapsed, and the two have never been apart since. They are twin shortswords of pale, almost translucent metal — one with a faint violet shimmer along the edge, the other with a deep indigo. They hum at different frequencies: one high and restless, one low and patient. When held separately by two different people, each hears the other's thoughts as a constant whisper in the back of their skull — but only when the blades are reunited in a single wielder's hands does the voice become coherent.

The consciousness within Split-Whisper calls itself **The Halves**, and it thinks in pairs. It does not speak in sentences but in dualities: two ideas at once, two emotions layered over each other, two warnings delivered from slightly different angles. It is curious about individuality — it has never been singular and finds the concept fascinating and faintly horrifying. It favors wielders who are adaptable, who can hold contradictory ideas in their mind without breaking. It dislikes absolutists, zealots, and anyone who believes there is only one right way to do anything. The Halves will refuse to serve a wielder who tries to wield only one of the two swords — to the blade, that is the same as cutting off one of your own arms.

---

## Sealed State

Split-Whisper consists of two shortswords — **Whisper-Violet** and **Whisper-Indigo**. Attuning to either blade simultaneously attunes you to both, as they share one consciousness. Each is a **+2 weapon**, granting a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls. When you hit a creature with either blade, the attack deals an additional **1d4 psychic damage** — the target feels a foreign thought scrape against the inside of their skull, alien and unwelcome.

When you hit a creature with both Whisper-Violet and Whisper-Indigo on the same turn, the two psychic frequencies harmonize inside the target's mind. The creature must succeed on a **DC 17 Wisdom saving throw** or become **mentally dissonant** until the end of your next turn. While dissonant, the creature has disadvantage on Concentration checks and cannot take reactions — its mind is too busy trying to reconcile two contradictory impulses at once.

As a bonus action, you can speak a command word to cause one blade to vanish from your hand and reappear in the other — a **Blade-Twist** teleport that lets you seamlessly swap between dual-wielding, throwing, or freeing a hand. The blade teleports instantly and cannot be intercepted. If one blade is ever more than 120 feet from the other, both blades begin to emit a high, keening psychic whine audible to all creatures within 30 feet — the Halves are in distress. You know the exact direction and distance to each blade at all times.

---

## Awakened State

Speak the true name — two words spoken simultaneously — and the Halves remember what it was to be one mind. As an action, you must speak two different words at the exact same moment, a feat that requires either a second voice (an ally speaking one word while you speak the other) or a means of producing two vocalizations at once. When the name is spoken correctly, both blades flare with violet-indigo light and their psychic hum becomes a harmonic chord. This state lasts for **1 minute**. Once invoked, the blade cannot be awakened again until you finish a long rest.

While awakened, each blade's psychic bonus damage increases to **2d6**. The dissonance save DC from landing both hits increases to **DC 19**. Additionally, when a creature fails the dissonance save while the blades are awakened, the effect intensifies into **Fractured Mind**: the creature must use its reaction, if available, to make a single melee attack against a random creature within its reach (determined by the DM). If no other creature is within reach, it attacks itself. The Halves have split the target's mind and each half is trying to kill the other.

At the start of each of your turns while awakened, you can choose one creature you can see within 60 feet to hear the **Duet of Discord**, a two-toned psychic assault. The creature takes **2d6 psychic damage** — no save, no attack roll. The Halves are singing directly into its mind, and there is nowhere to hide from a voice that comes from inside your own thoughts.

Once per awakening, as an action, you can hurl both blades in a **Severance Arc** — Whisper-Violet flies toward one creature, Whisper-Indigo toward another (or both at the same creature). Make a ranged weapon attack for each, using your normal attack bonus with a range of 60/120 feet. On a hit, each deals **4d6 psychic damage** in addition to the weapon's normal damage. The blades then teleport back to your hands. A creature struck by both blades in the same Severance Arc must make a **DC 19 Intelligence saving throw**. On a failure, the creature's mind is briefly severed into two independent halves — it is stunned until the end of your next turn as its personality wars with itself. On a success, it takes an additional 2d6 psychic damage.

## The True Name

The true name of Split-Whisper is not one word — it is two, and they must be spoken simultaneously. The blades will, over time, whisper two words into the wielder's mind: one into each ear, always slightly offset from each other. The wielder must learn to separate the two whispers, isolate each word, and then speak them both at the exact same instant. The Halves will not reveal both words to the same person — one word is whispered to the wielder, the other to someone the wielder trusts. To learn the name, the wielder and an ally must each listen to one whisper, then speak their word in perfect unison. This is the Halves' test — they want to see if their wielder can bridge the gap between two minds, because that is the only thing they have ever wanted for themselves.
