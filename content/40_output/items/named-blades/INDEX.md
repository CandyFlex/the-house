## Design Philosophy

### The Cosmic Force Blades
- **Singularity Edge (Very Rare):** Gravity — pulls enemies toward you. Growing gravity well. Controller.
- **Entropy's Reckoning (Legendary):** Entropy — pulls enemies into stillness. Growing entropy field. Disabler.
- **Star-Fall (Legendary):** Kinetic — a billion years of falling compressed into impact. Embed-and-detonate artillery.

### The Stellar Fire Blades
- **Corona (Very Rare):** Stellar lifecycle — phases of a star in miniature. Scaling risk/reward cycle.
- **Dawn-Sever (Legendary):** Pure dawn — captured first light. Anti-undead, cover-ignoring beam.
- **Ash-Nest (Very Rare):** Phoenix ember — eternal fire, death-triggers, rebirth cheat-death.

### The Mind & Memory Blades
- **Split-Whisper (Very Rare):** Split consciousness — two blades sharing one mind. Dual-wielding psychic dissonance.
- **Mind-Shard (Legendary):** Memory incision — invisible dagger that cuts what you know rather than what you are. Steals memories permanently.

### The Hunger & Consumption Blades
- **Grafted Fang (Very Rare):** Living symbiote — feeds through combat, starves without it. Satiety point economy for burst damage, reach, healing.
- **Hunger's Edge (Very Rare):** Blood-drinker — lifesteals on hit, applies stacking bleed, punishes starvation.
- **Spine-Eater (Legendary):** Spine-collector — reduces AC, suppresses reactions, severs vertebrae. Anatomical precision.

### The Time & Space Blades
- **Chronos Culling (Legendary):** Temporal acceleration — extra actions, echo attacks, initiative manipulation. Moves you faster than causality.
- **Sundial (Very Rare):** Temporal positioning — steals movement on hit, rewinds position, swaps initiative. The blade that tells you *when*.

### The Death & Grief Blades
- **Lament (Very Rare):** Weaponized sorrow — overwhelms with grief, scales with wielder's personal loss. Incapacitating keening wail.
- **Grave-Call (Legendary):** Death knell — frightens on hit, claims souls against resurrection, mass-death Funeral Toll.

### The Nature & Elemental Blades
- **Rattle-Root (Very Rare):** Living wood — seeds burrow into wounds, sprout minions from corpses. Regeneration while grounded.
- **Brine-Singer (Very Rare):** Abyssal sea — creates puddles and geysers, siren pull, whale-song thunder.
- **Spire-Kin (Very Rare):** Sky and storm — vertical teleport, force platforms, lightning strikes from above.
- **Salt-Heart (Legendary):** Desiccation — exhaustion aura, salt-storm, mirage of terminal thirst.

### The Oath & Bond Blade
- **Vow-Keeper (Legendary):** Promises made physical — devastating opportunity attacks with teleport, detects all lies, binds creatures to single-sentence oaths enforced by radiant damage.
