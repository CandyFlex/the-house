## Design Philosophy

### The Three Pull Blades
- **Singularity Edge (Very Rare):** Pulls enemies *toward* you. Growing gravity well. Controller.
- **Entropy's Reckoning (Legendary):** Pulls enemies *into stillness*. Growing entropy field. Disabler.
- **Corona (Very Rare):** Pulls *itself* through time — phases of stellar lifecycle. Scaling damage.

### The Two Speed Blades
- **Chronos Culling (Legendary):** You accelerate. Extra actions, echo attacks. The blade moves you faster than causality.
- **Grafted Fang (Very Rare):** You sustain. HP drain, THP generation, healing transfer. The blade keeps you in the fight.

### The True Name as Discovery
Each blade's name is protected by a unique discovery mechanism — none can be learned through *identify* or similar magic:
- **Singularity Edge:** Subsonic frequency — learned through listening to silence.
- **Corona:** Harmonic note — learned through resonance with great heat/light.
- **Grafted Fang:** Muscle-contraction sequence — learned through starvation and withdrawal.
- **Entropy's Reckoning:** Shape of silence — learned through absolute stillness.
- **Chronos Culling:** A moment of consequence — recognized through paying attention to your own life.
