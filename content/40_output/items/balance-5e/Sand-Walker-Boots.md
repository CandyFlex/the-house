# Sand-Walker Boots
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
These calf-height leather boots are caked with arena sand so deeply embedded in the leather that no amount of brushing will remove it. They were worn by the sand-rakers — the unseen caste of workers who dragged corpses off the arena floor between bouts, raked the blood into the sand, and were forbidden from making eye contact with the crowd. One raker, an old woman named Mira, wore these boots for forty years. She knew every body that hit the sand, and on the night of the revolt, she unlocked every gate she had spent a lifetime closing. The boots are perpetually silent — as though the sand within them absorbs sound itself.

## Properties

**Sand-Stalker.** While wearing these boots, your footsteps leave no tracks in sand, dirt, or gravel. You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to move silently across these surfaces.

**Corpse-Walk.** You can move through spaces occupied by corpses as if they were unoccupied. Corpses do not count as difficult terrain for you.

**Arena Dust (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can kick up a handful of the arena sand embedded in the boots, which expands into a 20-foot-radius sphere of swirling red dust centered on a point you can see within 10 feet of you. The area is heavily obscured for 1 round, then lightly obscured for 1 minute thereafter. The dust smells of iron and sweat.

## Balance Notes
An exploration-and-control hybrid. Sand-Stalker is a specialized *boots of elvenkind* (uncommon) — narrower terrain scope but adding trackless, which is useful against trackers. Corpse-Walk is pure flavor that rewards creative players in battlefields and crypts. Arena Dust is the combat centerpiece: heavy obscurement for 1 round is a full party disengage or ambush opener, and the lingering light obscurement creates a soft control zone for a full minute. Compare to an *eversmoking bottle* (rare) — the boots offer more controlled, shorter-duration obscurement on a short-rest timer, with movement utility layered on top.

---

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Raker's Sandals:** Trackless on sand/dirt/gravel; advantage on Stealth checks to move silently across sand. No Corpse-Walk, no Arena Dust. Requires attunement. Cost: ~50,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Blood-Sand Striders:** Sand-Stalker applies to all natural terrain. Corpse-Walk extended to all corpses and dead vegetation. Arena Dust 2/short rest (30-ft radius). Additionally, 1/long rest you can cast *meld into stone* (sand only) — you sink into any sand surface and emerge elsewhere within 60 feet. Requires attunement. Cost: ~1,800,000 soul stones.
