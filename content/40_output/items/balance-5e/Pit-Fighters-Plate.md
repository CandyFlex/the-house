# Pit-Fighter's Plate
*Armor (breastplate), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This breastplate is scarred and dented from thousands of arena bouts — slash-marks across the ribs, a puncture wound over the heart, hammer-blow concavities on the pauldron. It was worn by seventeen different gladiators, and fourteen of them died in it. The armor absorbed their muscle memory: the way Marcus dropped his shoulder before a thrust, how Severa twisted her hips to deflect a grapple, the exact angle Helena held her blade on the morning she finally won her freedom. When you put it on, it fits too well — as though it has been waiting for your body for a very long time.

## Properties

**You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor.**

**Muscle Memory.** When you finish a long rest while wearing this armor, you can choose one melee weapon with which you are not proficient. You gain proficiency with that weapon until you finish your next long rest. The armor's past wearers guide your hands — your movements feel borrowed, but correct.

**Last Guard (1/Long Rest).** When damage would reduce you to 0 hit points and not kill you outright, you can instead drop to 1 hit point. When you do, the armor tenses around your body — a phantom grip adjusting your stance — as a dead gladiator's final defensive reflex fires through the steel.

---

## Balance Notes
A versatile martial armor that rewards creative weapon selection. +1 breastplate sets a solid AC floor (15 + Dex). Muscle Memory is the unique mechanic — floating weapon proficiency lets martials adapt to found magic weapons without feat investment, which is powerful in the right campaign. The 1/long rest Last Guard is a half-*death ward* (no automatic stabilization after, no protection from instant-death effects beyond the immediate trigger). Together these create an item that feels like it's actively training and protecting its wearer. Compare to *+1 breastplate* (rare) — this adds two significant non-AC properties, putting it at the strong end of rare.

---

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Trainee's Cuirass:** +1 breastplate only. No Muscle Memory, no Last Guard. Requires attunement. Cost: ~100,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Champion's Plate:** +2 breastplate. Muscle Memory grants proficiency in two weapons. Last Guard 2/long rest. Additionally, after you use Last Guard, your first attack on your next turn is made with advantage. Requires attunement. Cost: ~2,800,000 soul stones.
