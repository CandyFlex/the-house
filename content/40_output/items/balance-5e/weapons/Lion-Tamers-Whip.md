# Lion-Tamer's Whip
*Weapon (whip), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This braided leather whip is wrapped at the grip with the tawny fur of a Nemean lion that a tyrant imported for the arena's centennial games. The whip belonged to Kaelus — the only beastmaster in the history of the pits to survive one hundred bouts. He didn't survive through cruelty; he survived because the beasts respected him more than they feared the tyrant. On his hundredth bout, he opened every cage and let the lions choose their own targets. The whip still carries the faint musk of lion and the sharper scent of an old man's defiance.

## Properties

**You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.**

**Lion's Reach.** This whip has a reach of 15 feet instead of 10 feet.

**Drag to the Sand.** When you hit a Large or smaller creature with this whip, you can force the target to make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failure, the creature is pulled up to 10 feet toward you and knocked prone.

**Tamer's Crack (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can crack the whip in the air. Each beast of your choice within 30 feet of you that can hear the crack must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the beast is charmed by you for 1 minute. While charmed in this way, the beast will not attack you unless you attack it first, and it regards you as a creature it has no reason to fear or fight. This effect ends early if you or your allies deal damage to the beast.

## Mechanics Block (DM Quick Reference)

- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** Yes
- **Charges:** 1/short rest (Tamer's Crack)
- **Save DC:** 15 (Strength vs pull/prone; Wisdom vs charm)
- **Spell Equivalent:** Tamer's Crack ≈ *animal friendship* (mass, short rest recharge); Drag to the Sand ≈ *thorn whip* (enhanced)
- **Damage/Effect:** +1 whip (1d4+1+mod), reach 15 ft, on-hit pull 10 ft + prone (STR 15), 1/short rest mass beast charm (WIS 15)

---

## Balance Notes
A battlefield control weapon that rewards tactical positioning. +1 puts it at the rare floor, but the extended reach, on-hit displacement, and mass beast pacification push it well into rare territory. The pull-and-prone combo is devastating when allies are positioned to capitalize — drag enemies into melee range of your fighter, or yank archers off ledges. Tamer's Crack is situational (beasts only) but encounter-changing when relevant. Compare to a *+1 weapon of certain death* — this offers less raw damage but dramatically more control. The combination of reach extension, forced movement, prone, and a niche AoE charm makes this a standout rare.

---

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Handler's Lash:** +1 whip, reach 15 ft. No Drag to the Sand, no Tamer's Crack. Requires attunement. Cost: ~60,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Pride-Master's Whip:** +2 whip, reach 20 ft. Drag to the Sand (DC 17, pulls up to 15 ft, Huge or smaller). Tamer's Crack 2/short rest, affects beasts and monstrosities. Additionally, 1/long rest you can cast *conjure animals* (lions only) — they arrive already charmed. Requires attunement. Cost: ~2,400,000 soul stones.
