# Orator's Torc
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A heavy golden neck-ring, open at the throat and tipped with two eagle heads that face one another — the sigil of Caesar's propaganda corps. These torcs were worn by the speech-writers and criers who translated Caesar's atrocities into the language of glory. The last to wear this one was a man named Silvius, who spent a decade convincing the populace that the arena was justice. On the night of the revolt, he used the torc's power to convince the gate guards to abandon their posts — then walked into the mob and was never seen again. When you wear it, your words feel heavier, as though each syllable has been dipped in gold leaf.

## Properties

**Voice of the Crowd.** While wearing this torc, you have advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) and Charisma (Deception) checks made when speaking to a group of ten or more creatures that can hear and understand you.

**Iron Word (1/Long Rest).** You can cast the *command* spell at 3rd level (affecting up to three creatures within 30 feet of you). The saving throw DC is 15. When you do, your voice takes on a metallic resonance — as though iron itself is speaking.

**Rhetoric's Veil.** Magical means cannot determine whether you are lying while you wear this torc. Spells such as *zone of truth* reveal no inconsistency in your words, and features that would detect falsehood — such as a *ring of truth telling* or a paladin's Divine Sense — register your statements as neither truth nor lie. The torc doesn't make you honest; it makes your dishonesty indistinguishable from conviction.

---

## Balance Notes
A social powerhouse with one solid combat option. The crowd-only restriction on advantage prevents it from overshadowing dedicated face characters in one-on-one negotiations — it's for speeches, rallying, and deception at scale. Iron Word gives the wearer a once-per-day mass *command* that can end encounters (flee, grovel, drop) against up to three targets. Rhetoric's Veil is the signature: permanent immunity to magical truth-detection is rare-tier appropriate but narrowly scoped (social only, no combat protection). Compare to a *rod of rulership* (rare) — the torc is less combat-dominant but more versatile socially.

---

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Crier's Collar:** Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks when speaking to a crowd of 10+. Iron Word 1/long rest (1st level, single target). No Rhetoric's Veil. Requires attunement. Cost: ~45,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Senate Speaker's Torc:** Advantage on all Charisma checks when speaking to any number of creatures. Iron Word 2/long rest (5th level, up to 5 targets, DC 17). Rhetoric's Veil extends to protecting all allies within 30 ft who can hear you. Additionally, 1/long rest you can cast *mass suggestion*. Requires attunement. Cost: ~3,000,000 soul stones.
