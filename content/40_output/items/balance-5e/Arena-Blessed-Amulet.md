# Arena-Blessed Amulet
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This amulet is a small bloodstone sphere suspended in a cage of twisted iron — inside, a pinch of sand from the blood-sands swirls in a single drop of blood contributed by every gladiator who ever won their freedom. There were only seven in the history of the pits. When the last of them, a woman named Antonia, walked out of the arena a free woman, she cut her palm and let the blood fall onto the sand of the floor, mixing with the ghosts of everyone who had bled there. An artificer-sympathizer captured that moment in this amulet. It is warm against the chest, and when held to the ear, you can hear a distant roar — not of pain, but of a crowd cheering for the underdog.

## Properties

**Opening Roar.** When you roll initiative while wearing this amulet, you gain temporary hit points equal to 2d10 + your level. The roar of an unseen crowd surges in your ears.

**The Crowd Goes Wild.** When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points, each allied creature of your choice within 30 feet of you that can see you gains a +1d4 bonus to its next attack roll made before the end of your next turn. The air fills with the phantom roar of the arena crowd, urging your allies to greater heights.

**History's Weight (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can rally your allies with the weight of every rebellion that came before. You cast *crusader's mantle* without requiring concentration. The spell's bonus damage is psychic damage instead of radiant — the crushing realization that every tyrant falls, eventually.

## Balance Notes
A momentum-swinging item for aggressive party leaders. Opening Roar gives the wearer a significant durability boost at the start of every fight — 2d10 + level is roughly 16-31 temp HP, enough to absorb one solid hit without consuming action economy. The Crowd Goes Wild transforms every killing blow into an offensive buff for allies, encouraging the wearer to finish off weakened enemies. History's Weight is the capstone: concentration-free *crusader's mantle* means the party deals an extra 1d4 psychic on every weapon attack for a full minute, which adds up to enormous damage across a fight. Making it psychic damage is a sidegrade — fewer creatures resist radiant, but the flavor is strong. Compare to a *belt of fire giant strength* (very rare) — this offers less personal damage but dramatically more party support.

## Variants
- **Rare — Gladiator's Pendant:** Opening Roar gives 1d10 + half your level (rounded down) temp HP. The Crowd Goes Wild affects one ally of your choice within 30 ft. History's Weight 1/long rest: cast *bless* without concentration on up to three allies. Requires attunement. Cost: ~350,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — Heart of the Arena:** Opening Roar gives 4d10 + level temp HP. The Crowd Goes Wild affects all allies within 60 ft and the +1d4 lasts for all attacks on their next turn (not just one). History's Weight 2/long rest, and you can choose radiant or psychic damage. Additionally, while the amulet's magic is active, allies within 30 ft have advantage on saving throws against being frightened. 1/long rest you can cast *heroes' feast* — the food is simple arena rations, but it feeds up to 12 creatures. Requires attunement. Cost: ~5,000,000 soul stones.
