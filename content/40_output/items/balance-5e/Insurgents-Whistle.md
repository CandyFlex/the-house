# Insurgent's Whistle
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

---

## Description
A crude whistle carved from the femur of Duro — the first gladiator to refuse to kill. When the arena-master ordered him to deliver the death-blow to a defeated opponent, Duro threw down his blade and was executed on the arena floor. That night, his cell-mate took his bone and carved this whistle, which became the signal for the first escape attempt. The whistle is pale yellow, worn smooth by countless lips, and carved with a single word in a dead language: *enough*.

## Properties

**Silent Signal (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can blow this whistle. It produces no audible sound, but each allied creature of your choice within 60 feet of you hears it clearly — a sharp, two-tone note that cuts through any noise or distraction. Each ally that hears the signal gains a +5-foot bonus to their walking speed for 1 minute.

**Freedom's Frequency.** Any creature that has heard this whistle before can hear it from up to 1 mile away, recognizing it as the same signal. The whistle's silent sound travels through barriers but does not pass through solid stone more than 5 feet thick or any thickness of lead.

## Balance Notes
A utility item with no combat damage but strong tactical and narrative applications. The +5-ft speed boost to all allies is a mini *longstrider* that affects the whole party — strong for positioning, chases, and escapes. Gated at 1/short rest so it's available for most encounters without being spammable. The 1-mile signal range makes it a superb coordination tool for split-party scenarios, heists, and ambushes. No attunement keeps it accessible. Compare to a *horn of silent alarm* (common) — this is a strict upgrade with combat utility, comfortably uncommon.

## Variants
- **Common — Runner's Whistle:** Silent Signal 1/long rest, +5 ft speed for 1 round (not 1 minute). No mile-range recognition. Cost: ~5,000 soul stones.
- **Rare — Revolt Commander's Whistle:** Silent Signal 2/short rest, +10 ft speed for 1 minute. 1/long rest you can blow a three-tone signal that lets all allies within 1 mile hear it and know your exact location for 1 minute. Requires attunement. Cost: ~200,000 soul stones.
