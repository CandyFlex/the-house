# Martyr's Vambraces
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
These leather forearm guards are tooled with an unbroken spiral — the rebel symbol for "truth spoken once echoes forever." They were strapped onto the arms of Elara, a former scribe who walked into Caesar's throne room unarmed, stood before his court, and listed every crime he had committed, by name, by date, by victim. She spoke for twelve minutes before the guards cut her down. Her last words — *"You cannot kill an idea"* — are stitched in silver thread along the inside of each vambrace, invisible when worn. The leather is warm, even in winter, as though her conviction still burns.

## Properties

**Last Truth (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can speak the words stitched inside the vambraces and declare your "Last Truth." For 1 minute, you cannot be silenced, gagged, or prevented from speaking by any magical or nonmagical means — spells such as *silence* fail to suppress your voice, and physical restraints cannot prevent your mouth from forming words. Additionally, all creatures of your choice within 60 feet of you understand the meaning of your words, regardless of what language you speak.

**Defiant Final Breath.** If you are reduced to 0 hit points while your Last Truth is active, you do not fall unconscious immediately. Instead, you remain conscious for 1 additional round. During this round, your voice is amplified — all creatures of your choice within 300 feet can hear your words clearly regardless of intervening noise, magical silence, or language barriers. At the end of this round, you fall unconscious as normal.

## Balance Notes
A narrative-shattering item for social encounters and climactic confrontations. Last Truth is uniquely defensive — it prevents the classic "silence the whistleblower" move from tyrannical villains, and the universal comprehension turns every confrontation into a potential public revelation. Defiant Final Breath is the emotional spike: going down while speaking truth gives a dying broadcast that can change the course of a rebellion or expose a traitor. Compare to a *helm of telepathy* (uncommon) — this is more powerful (anti-silence, death trigger, universal comprehension) but 1/long rest and built for specific story moments rather than constant utility. Rare is appropriate for a power that can reshape narrative but offers zero combat damage.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Truth-Speaker's Wraps:** Last Truth 1/long rest (anti-silence + universal comprehension for 1 minute). No Defiant Final Breath. Requires attunement. Cost: ~60,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Saint's Vambraces:** Last Truth 2/long rest, duration 10 minutes, affects creatures out to 120 ft. Defiant Final Breath: you remain conscious until the end of your next turn, amplified to 1,000 ft, and your words deal 2d6 psychic damage to one creature of your choice that can hear you. Additionally, 1/long rest you can cast *heroes' feast* (the feast is a simple meal of bread and water, but the words spoken over it fortify the spirit). Requires attunement. Cost: ~2,400,000 soul stones.
