# Stormcaller Hammer
*Weapon (light hammer), Uncommon (requires attunement)*

> [!tip]+ Quick Stats
> - **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Attunement:** Yes
> - **Type:** Weapon (light hammer)
> - **Save DC:** 13 | **Value:** 40,000 soul stones
> - **Charges:** 4 (1d4 at dawn)

---

## Description
The head of this hammer is forged from a dark, pitted metal shot through with veins of silver that pulse faintly like distant lightning. When gripped, a low hum reverberates through the wielder's arm — the echo of a thunderhead waiting to break. Tempest clerics and storm sorcerers favor these weapons, and many are recovered from shipwrecks claimed by the gods of the deep sky.

## Properties
**Thunderous Return.** This hammer has the thrown property with a normal range of 30 feet and a long range of 90 feet. Immediately after the attack hits or misses, the hammer crackles with electricity and flies back to your hand.

**Storm Strike.** When you hit a creature with this hammer, you can expend 1 charge to deal an additional 1d6 lightning damage and force the target to succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be deafened until the end of your next turn.

**Herald of the Squall.** While you are outdoors during a storm (heavy rain, high wind, or lightning present), the hammer regains all expended charges at the start of each hour instead of at dawn, and the lightning damage from Storm Strike increases to 1d8.

> [!abstract]- Mechanics (DM Reference)
> - **Rarity:** Uncommon
> - **Attunement:** Yes
> - **Charges:** 4, regain 1d4 at dawn (accelerated during storms)
> - **Save DC:** 13 (Constitution vs deafened)
> - **Spell Equivalent:** — (1d6 bonus damage is cantrip-tier, but the return property and conditional power bump justify uncommon)
> - **Damage/Effect:** +1d6 lightning per charge spent; deafened on failed save; returning property

> [!example]- Balance Notes
> The +1d6 damage is gated by charges (4/day, recharge 1d4) which caps the per-day bonus damage at 4d6 (avg 14). A standard *+1 weapon* at uncommon gives +1 to hit *and* +1 damage on every attack — the Stormcaller Hammer offers higher spike damage but less consistency, which is a fair trade. The deafen rider is a minor control effect (uncommon-appropriate). The storm-conditional power bump is flavorful and DM-controlled, so it rarely unbalances encounters. Compare to *javelin of lightning* (uncommon, 1/day 4d6 line) — this is less bursty but more flexible.

> [!note]- Variants
> - **Rare — Tempest's Hammer:** Increase charges to 6 (regain 1d6), Storm Strike deals 1d8 lightning, and on a critical hit the target is also pushed 10 feet and knocked prone (no save). DC increases to 15. Cost: ~250,000 soul stones.
> - **Very Rare — Skybreaker:** +1 weapon, charges 10 (regain 2d4+2), Storm Strike deals 2d8 lightning + 1d8 thunder. When you reduce a creature to 0 hp with this hammer, you regain 1 charge. While outdoors, you can cast *call lightning* (save DC 15) once per day. Cost: ~1,500,000 soul stones.
