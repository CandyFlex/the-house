# Red Sand Cloak
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This hooded cloak is dyed a permanent rust-red — not with pigment, but with the blood-soaked sand of the blood-sands, gathered in secret by a sand-raker named Theron who carried fistfuls of it out each night in the lining of his tunic. After a year, he had enough to dye a single cloak and gift it to the resistance. The fabric is perpetually gritty to the touch and carries the faint iron smell of dried blood. When the hood is raised, the sand within the weave shifts almost imperceptibly, as though the arena floor still churns beneath.

## Properties

**Blood-Sand Camouflage.** While wearing this cloak in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

**Arena Blind (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can sweep the cloak forward, scattering a cloud of red sand in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the end of your next turn. The sand smells faintly of iron.

- **Spell Equivalent:** Arena Blind ≈ *color spray* (single-target blind, shorter range, but CON save instead of HP-based)
- **Damage/Effect:** Advantage on Stealth in dim light/darkness; 1/long rest 15-ft cone blind (1 round)

---

## Balance Notes
The stealth advantage is a conditional *cloak of elvenkind* (uncommon) — narrower (no disadvantage-on-Perception rider) but same tier. Arena Blind is a short-range, short-duration debuff that can swing an ambush or escape. The CON save is appropriate for a physical irritant (sand in eyes), and the one-round duration keeps it from dominating fights. Compare to a *wand of color spray* — the cloak's blind is more reliable (save-based instead of HP-based) but much smaller area and only 1/day. Strong uncommon, well within ceiling.

---

## Variants
- **Common — Sand-Duster's Wrap:** Stealth advantage in dim light/darkness only. No Arena Blind. No attunement. Cost: ~8,000 soul stones.
- **Rare — Arena-Master's Mantle:** Stealth advantage in dim light/darkness; enemies have disadvantage on Perception checks to see you. Arena Blind 3/long rest (DC 15, 30-ft cone). Additionally, 1/long rest you can cast *pass without trace* (the sand muffles your passage). Requires attunement. Cost: ~350,000 soul stones.
