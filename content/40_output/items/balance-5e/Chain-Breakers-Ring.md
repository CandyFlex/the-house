# Chain-Breaker's Ring
*Ring, uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This ring was forged from the broken manacle of Lyra, the slave who led the first mass escape from Caesar's pits through the sewer channels beneath the arena. She filed through her chains with a stolen nail over six months, then freed forty others on the night of the blood-moon. Lyra didn't survive the escape — she stayed behind to hold a gate — but her manacle was recovered by those who made it out. The ring is crude iron, shaped like a serpent biting its own tail, and it is always cold against the skin.

## Properties

**Unbound.** While wearing this ring, you have advantage on ability checks made to escape grapples, restraints, manacles, bindings, or any nonmagical effect that physically restrains or confines you.

**Chain-Snap (1/Long Rest).** As a bonus action, you can twist the ring and speak the word Lyra whispered as she broke her chains. For 1 round, you and up to three allied creatures of your choice within 30 feet of you gain the benefits of the *freedom of movement* spell. Additionally, any nonmagical manacles, chains, shackles, or similar restraints worn by creatures in the area snap open and fall away.

---

## Balance Notes
The passive escape advantage is narrow but flavorful — useful against grapple-heavy monsters, traps, and imprisonment scenarios. Chain-Snap is the star: *freedom of movement* on up to 4 targets for 1 round is a strong panic button for when the party gets webbed, restrained, or paralyzed. One round is enough to reposition but not enough to trivialize an entire encounter. The nonmagical restraint-breaking is pure flavor that will delight players in the right scenario. Compare to a *ring of free action* (rare) — this is weaker (1/long rest, team-burst instead of personal-permanent) but affects allies, landing it solidly in upper uncommon.

---

## Variants
- **Common — Shackle-Twist Band:** Advantage on escape checks only. No Chain-Snap. No attunement. Cost: ~10,000 soul stones.
- **Rare — Liberator's Loop:** Advantage on escape checks; Chain-Snap 2/long rest (5 targets, 2 rounds). Additionally, while wearing the ring, your walking speed cannot be reduced by nonmagical means. Requires attunement. Cost: ~280,000 soul stones.
