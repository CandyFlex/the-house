# Glacier's Heart
*Wondrous item (amulet), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A fist-sized geode crystal worn as an amulet on a chain of frosted silver links, pulsing with a slow, rhythmic light — a heartbeat frozen in crystal. The Trench's deepest chamber holds the geode from which this was carved, and the miners who extracted it reported that the amulet was already beating when they found it, as though it had been waiting. To wear it is to feel the cold not as an enemy but as an old, patient ally — one that answers when called.

## Properties

**Frozen Heart.** You have immunity to cold damage. Additionally, when you deal cold damage to a creature, you ignore resistance to cold damage.

**Glacier's Pulse (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can hold the amulet aloft and speak its command word to cast the *investiture of ice* spell (spell save DC 17) without expending a spell slot or requiring material components. For the duration, the amulet glows with intense blue-white light, shedding bright light in a 20-foot radius.

**Summon Frost Guardian (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can crush a handful of snow or ice against the amulet (consuming material worth at least 1 gp) to summon a **frost guardian**. The guardian uses the statistics of a **water elemental** with the following changes: it is immune to cold damage instead of acid, its Slam attack deals cold damage instead of bludgeoning, and it has vulnerability to fire damage. The guardian appears in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you, acts on its own initiative count, and obeys your verbal commands (no action required). It remains for 1 hour, until it drops to 0 hit points, or until you dismiss it as a bonus action. The amulet cannot summon another guardian until you finish a long rest.

## Balance Notes
Five properties at Very Rare — the upper ceiling of the tier. Cold immunity (Very Rare for a single type — compare to *ring of fire elemental command*, legendary for immunity+multiple spells). The ignore-cold-resistance ensures the wielder's cold spells remain viable against cold-resistant foes. *Investiture of ice* 1/long rest is a 6th-level spell, appropriate for Very Rare. Summoning a modified water elemental (frost guardian) 1/long rest creates an action-economy advantage with a CR 5 ally for 1 hour. This is a cold-focused character's dream item — strong but not game-breaking when cold damage is one of the most commonly resisted types.
