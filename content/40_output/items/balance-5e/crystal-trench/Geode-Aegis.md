# Geode Aegis
*Armor (shield), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A broad shield carved from a single cross-section of the Crystal Trench wall — concentric rings of blue-white crystal expanding outward from a dark geode center, like the rings of a frozen tree. It is heavier than steel and colder than stone, yet it floats into position when raised. When struck, the shield does not ring — it answers in silence, the facets along its rim flaring with pale light as though the blow were an insult the crystal intends to return.

## Properties

**+1 Shield.** While holding this shield, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. This bonus is in addition to the shield's normal bonus to AC.

**Shard Retort.** When a creature within 5 feet of you misses you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to cause crystalline shards to erupt from the shield's face. The attacker takes 1d8 cold damage and must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be pushed 5 feet away from you.

**Crystal Bastion (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you plant the shield in the ground, causing a wall of jagged crystal to erupt from it. The wall is 15 feet wide, 10 feet high, and 1 foot thick, extending from the shield in a direction you choose. The wall provides half cover to creatures behind it. Each 5-foot section of the wall has AC 15 and 30 hit points. A creature that enters or starts its turn in a space adjacent to the wall on the side facing away from you takes 1d4 cold damage from the sharp crystalline edges. The wall lasts for 1 minute, until you use a bonus action to dismiss it, or until you move more than 30 feet away from it.

## Balance Notes
A +1 shield (rare-tier chassis) with a reactive melee punish and a once-per-day deployable wall. Three well-defined properties:

1. **+1 shield** — +3 total AC while held. Excellent on its own; the attunement requirement is the tradeoff vs a standard *+1 shield*.
2. **Shard Retort** — a reaction that requires being missed in melee, which makes it most effective on frontline characters who already have high AC. The 1d8 cold + push provides a satisfying "don't touch me" deterrent without being spammable (one reaction per round).
3. **Crystal Bastion** — the setpiece ability. A deployable wall that provides cover, blocks movement, and punishes adjacency. At 15×10 ft with 30 HP per section, it can absorb 1–2 rounds of focused fire before collapsing — enough to reshape the battlefield without trivializing encounters.

The cold damage on the wall's far side creates a tactical choice: plant it between you and enemies (punishing their advance) or trap enemies against it. The 30-ft tether prevents kiting abuse.
