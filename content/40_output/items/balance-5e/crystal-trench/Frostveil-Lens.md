# Frostveil Lens
*Wondrous item, Rare (requires attunement)*

> [!tip]+ Quick Stats
> - **Rarity:** Rare | **Attunement:** Yes
> - **Type:** Wondrous item
> - **Value:** 250,000 soul stones

---

## Description
A polished disc of geode crystal no wider than a coin, ground so thin that light passes through it in tight, disciplined rays. When held to the eye, the world sharpens into cold geometry — ice becomes glass, shadow becomes shape, and falsehoods show their seams. The lens is set in a simple wire frame that hooks over the ear, and it is always cool against the skin, as though it cannot forget the depth from which it was cut. Prospectors in the Trench say the lens shows you what the crystals have already seen.

## Properties
**Crystal Sight.** While wearing this lens, you can see through ice, crystal, and naturally frozen surfaces as though they were clear glass, to a thickness of up to 1 foot. The lens does not penetrate magical barriers such as a *wall of ice* spell.

**Refracted Truth (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can peer through the lens to see the world as the crystals do. For 1 minute, you gain truesight out to a range of 30 feet. During this time, your pupil visibly refracts pale blue-white light.

**Frostveil Discernment.** You have advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks made to detect illusions, and on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to see through heavily obscured areas caused by fog, mist, snow, or similar cold-weather phenomena.

> - **Save DC:** —
> - **Spell Equivalent:** Refracted Truth ≈ *true seeing* (6th level) scaled to 30ft and 1 minute
> - **Damage/Effect:** See through ice/crystal (1 ft); 1/long rest truesight 30ft for 1 min; advantage on Investigation vs illusions + Perception through cold weather obscurement

> [!example]- Balance Notes
> A utility-focused Rare item with no combat damage — pure information gathering. Three properties:
> 
> 1. **Crystal Sight** — narrow but flavorful. In a frozen dungeon (like the Crystal Trench itself), the ability to see through ice walls means the wearer can spot ambushes, hidden chambers, and treasure caches others would miss. The 1-foot thickness cap prevents it from being a universal X-ray; it rewards clever positioning.
> 2. **Refracted Truth** — truesight 30 ft for 1 minute, once per day. This is the item's spike ability. A *gem of seeing* (rare) gives truesight 120 ft for 10 minutes, but is single-use (3 charges, no recharge). The Frostveil Lens gives a much smaller radius and shorter duration, but it recharges daily — more of a "solve this one room" tool than a "solve the dungeon" tool.
> 3. **Frostveil Discernment** — a passive that layers on the illusion-piercing theme and adds cold-weather utility. Fog and snow are common DM tools for limiting visibility; this lens lets the wearer cut through that without burning the truesight charge.
> 
> The item rewards preparation (don't waste the truesight) and environmental awareness (look through that ice wall before charging in).

> [!note]- Variants
> - **Uncommon — Glint Lens:** Crystal Sight only. No Refracted Truth, no Frostveil Discernment. Does not require attunement. Cost: ~35,000 soul stones.
> - **Very Rare — Veilpiercer Lens:** All Rare properties. Crystal Sight penetrates up to 5 feet of ice/crystal and can see through *wall of ice* and similar magical ice barriers. Refracted Truth 2/long rest, range 60 ft. Additionally, while Refracted Truth is active, you can see the true form of shapechangers. Cost: ~1,600,000 soul stones.
