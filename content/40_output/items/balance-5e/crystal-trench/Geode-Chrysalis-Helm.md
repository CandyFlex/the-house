# Geode Chrysalis Helm
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A helm formed not by forge or hand but by accretion — layers of blue-white crystal that grew outward from a single geode seed into the shape of a closed crown. It fits only the one who first placed it, the inner facets having shaped themselves to that specific skull. When danger crests, the helm answers the wearer's pulse and blossoms: crystal plates slide outward from the crown until the wearer stands inside a faceted cocoon, a silent geometry of frozen light. Emerging from it feels like being born into winter — cold, clean, and entirely remade for the violence to come.

## Properties

**Crystalline Chrysalis (1/Short Rest).** As a reaction when you take damage from a creature you can see, or as a bonus action on your turn, you encase yourself in a cocoon of living crystal. The chrysalis forms around you instantly, and you gain the following benefits until the start of your next turn:

- You gain a +5 bonus to AC.
- Your speed becomes 0, and you cannot take reactions.
- You are immune to cold and poison damage.
- You have resistance to all other damage types.
- You automatically succeed on Strength and Dexterity saving throws against effects that would move you against your will.

The chrysalis lasts until the start of your next turn. You can end it early as a bonus action on your turn.

**Emergence Shatter.** When the chrysalis ends — either by duration or by early dismissal — the crystal shell detonates outward in a spray of razored shards. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d8 cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one, and being pushed 10 feet away from you. A creature that succeeds on the save is not pushed.

**Geode Senses.** While wearing this helm, crystalline filaments extend from the inner surface to rest against your temples and jaw. You gain blindsight out to a range of 10 feet. This blindsight functions even while you are inside the chrysalis.

## Balance Notes
A Very Rare defensive helm with distinct phases — the cocoon, and the emergence. Five property-like elements:

1. **Crystalline Chrysalis** — the core ability, now a reaction or bonus action. As a **reaction**, it triggers off taking damage from a visible creature — catch a crit, a dragon's breath, or a rogue's sneak attack and negate most of it. As a **bonus action**, you can preemptively cocoon when you know a big round is coming (surrounded, about to eat legendary actions, holding a choke point). The reaction mode is the stronger panic button; the bonus action mode is the tactical setup. The tradeoffs (no movement, no reactions while inside, immobile) remain the same regardless of trigger. Once per short rest means you must choose carefully between preemptive and reactive use.
2. **Emergence Shatter** — converts the defensive cocoon into an offensive exit. The push breaks grapples and creates distance. The 4d8 cold (avg 18) gives the wearer something to do on the round they emerge rather than just standing up. When emerging early via bonus action dismissal, you can immediately use your action to attack — cocoon → emerge (damage + push) → full attack sequence in a single turn.
3. **Geode Senses** — 10-ft blindsight is modest but always-on and cannot be suppressed by blindness, darkness, or fog. A quality-of-life passive that reinforces the crystal theme.

**Bonus Action → Full Turn Combo.** Using the chrysalis as a bonus action opens a powerful sequence: move into position → bonus action cocoon → enemies waste attacks on the shell → start of your next turn, emerge early as a bonus action (Emergence Shatter triggers) → use your action to attack, cast a spell, or activate Geode Cataclysm. The cocoon absorbs a full round of enemy focus, then you emerge dealing damage and pushing enemies away before unloading your own offense.

**Synergy with Geode Heart:** If a character wields both the Geode Heart greatsword and this helm, the combo is: bonus action cocoon → survive the enemy round → bonus action emerge (4d8 cold + push) → action Geode Cataclysm on the survivors. Or as a reaction: take a big hit → cocoon → emerge next turn → Geode Cataclysm.

**Anti-abuse:** The cocoon prevents reactions while inside, so the wearer cannot *shield* or *counterspell* during the protective shell (this is the opportunity cost). If triggered as a reaction, you've already spent your reaction for the round. The immobility prevents the "cocoon then teleport" escape. The once-per-short-rest limit prevents chaining.

## Variants
- **Rare — Geode Half-Helm:** +2 AC inside chrysalis (not +5), no cold/poison immunity inside (resistance only), Emergence Shatter 3d8 cold, DC 14. Blindsight 5 ft. Cost: ~600,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — Geode Cathedral Helm:** Crystalline Chrysalis 2/short rest. +7 AC inside, immune to cold/poison/necrotic, resistance to all others. Emergence Shatter 6d8 cold, DC 18, radius 20 ft. While in the chrysalis, you can use your bonus action to extend its duration for one additional round (once per chrysalis use). Additionally, while wearing this helm, you can cast *investiture of ice* once per long rest without expending a spell slot. Cost: ~100,000,000 soul stones.
