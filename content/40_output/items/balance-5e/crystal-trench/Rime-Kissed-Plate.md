# Rime-Kissed Plate
*Armor (half plate), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A suit of half plate whose steel plates are veined through with seams of blue-white crystal — as though the armor was forged, then dipped into the frozen heart of the Trench and pulled out before the crystal could consume it entirely. Frost traces the edges of every plate, never melting. The cold does not harm the wearer; instead, it seems to coil inward when danger draws near, waiting to repay any blow with a flash of winter's teeth. To don this armor is to wear the Trench's silence — the plates make no sound when they shift.

## Properties

**+1 Armor.** You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor.

**Crystalline Veins.** While wearing this armor, you have resistance to cold damage.

**Frostbite Reprisal (1/Short Rest).** When you take cold damage while wearing this armor, you can use your reaction to absorb a portion of the frost. The cold damage you take is halved, and the armor's crystal veins flare with stolen power. Until the end of your next turn, when a creature hits you with a melee attack, it takes 1d8 cold damage. This retaliation damage ignores resistance to cold damage.

## Balance Notes
Rare armor with three properties — +1 AC (the base rare chassis), cold resistance (a tier-appropriate passive), and a reactive absorption/retaliation ability. Compared to *armor of resistance* (rare, +0 AC, one resistance), this trades the pure defensive focus for an active retaliation loop. The synergy is intentional: take cold damage → halve it with Frostbite Reprisal → cold resistance halves the remainder again (quarter damage total from one cold hit per short rest). The melee retaliation punishes enemies who follow up, creating a mini *armor of Agathys* effect. The once-per-short-rest limit and the requirement that you actually take cold damage to trigger it keep this from being a universal defensive tool — against a red dragon it's just +1 armor with cold resistance, which is appropriate for Rare.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Frost-Touched Mail:** +1 armor (no cold resistance, no Frostbite Reprisal). Simple +1 half plate. No attunement. Cost: ~50,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Rimeheart Plate:** +2 armor. Cold immunity instead of resistance. Frostbite Reprisal 2/short rest, retaliation damage increases to 2d8 cold, and it triggers regardless of whether you actually take damage (you can use the reaction whenever you are targeted by cold damage, even if immune). Additionally, while wearing this armor you can walk across the surface of water by freezing it beneath your feet. Cost: ~2,500,000 soul stones.
