# Geode Heart
*Weapon (greatsword), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Carved from the central geode at the heart of the Crystal Trench — a chamber silent as a held breath, where light itself refracts into geometry. The blade is not steel but a single enormous crystal shard, blue-white at its core fading to translucent at its impossibly sharp edges. It pulses with a slow, rhythmic light, as though the geode's heart still beats within. To hold it is to feel the weight of a frozen cathedral pressing down, and to know that the cathedral answers to you now.

## Properties

**+2 Weapon.** You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Geode Heart Strike.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, it takes an additional 1d8 cold damage.

**Crystalline Prison.** When you score a critical hit with this weapon, shimmering crystal erupts across the target's body. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be restrained as ice encases it. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. Constructs and creatures immune to cold damage automatically succeed on this save.

**Geode Cataclysm (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you slam the blade into the ground, causing jagged crystalline growths to erupt in a 30-foot radius centered on you. Each creature of your choice in that area must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d6 cold damage and 6d6 radiant damage on a failed save and becoming restrained by crystal formations, or half as much damage on a successful one and not restrained. A creature restrained by this effect can use its action to make a DC 16 Strength check, freeing itself on a success. The crystalline growths remain for 1 minute, crumbling to dust thereafter.

While the crystalline growths remain, the blade drinks deeply. When a creature of CR 1 or higher dies within the cataclysm area, its life force is drawn into the sword through the formations. The sword gains a cumulative +1 bonus to damage rolls for each creature slain this way (maximum +3), which lasts until you finish a long rest. Additionally, for each such creature, roll a d20. On a roll of 15 or higher, the creature's body crystallizes entirely, leaving behind 5d10 × 10,000 soul stones among the shards. The sword's pulse brightens visibly with each soul consumed.

**Geode Pulse.** While holding this weapon, you have resistance to cold damage. The sword sheds bright blue-white light in a 15-foot radius and dim light for an additional 15 feet. The light pulses gently, perceptible even through a closed scabbard.

## Mechanics Block (DM Quick Reference)

- **Rarity:** Very Rare
- **Attunement:** Yes
- **Charges:** —
- **Save DC:** 16 (Constitution vs restrained on crit; Dexterity for half damage on Cataclysm; Strength to break free)
- **Spell Equivalent:** Geode Cataclysm ≈ 7th–8th level area spell (12d6 split-type, 30-ft radius, restrained rider)
- **Damage/Effect:** +2 greatsword + 1d8 cold per hit; crit → restrained (save ends); 1/long rest 30-ft 6d6 cold + 6d6 radiant + restrained; cold resistance; light
- **Soul Harvest:** Kills within Cataclysm area stack +1 damage (max +3) until long rest. On kill, 30% chance to crystallize target into 5d10 × 10,000 soul stones (avg ~275,000)

## Balance Notes
Five distinct properties (upper end of Very Rare):
1. **+2 weapon** — standard Very Rare chassis.
2. **1d8 cold rider** — brings average damage per hit to 2d6 + 1d8 + 2 + STR (avg 16.5 + STR). Comparable to a *flame tongue greatsword* (rare, +2d6 fire, no +X) — Geode Heart trades some raw damage for the +2 accuracy and the cold/resistance package.
3. **Crystalline Prison** — crit-only save-or-restrain is a satisfying spike that rewards the 5% excitement without dominating every round. The cold/construct immunity clause prevents boss-nullification.
4. **Geode Cataclysm** — the setpiece ability. 12d6 split damage (avg 42) in 30-ft radius with a restrained rider. Comparable to *sunburst* (8th level, 12d6 radiant in 60-ft + blind) — Cataclysm has a smaller radius and split damage type but adds restrained. Now carries the **Soul Harvest** rider: kills within the cataclysm zone feed the blade (stacking +1 damage, max +3, until long rest) and have a 30% chance to crystallize the dead into 5d10 × 10,000 soul stones each. This converts the cataclysm from a pure combat nova into an economic engine — a single use against a group of 4–6 creatures of CR 1+ expects ~1–2 soul stone procs, producing anywhere from 50,000 to 500,000 soul stones per proc. The +3 damage cap prevents the stacking from snowballing indefinitely, and the once-per-long-rest restriction ensures the soul stone income doesn't trivialize the floor's economy.
5. **Geode Pulse** — cold resistance + light. Quality-of-life passive that reinforces the crystal theme and provides defensive value without consuming the power budget.

Watch for: Great Weapon Master synergy — the +2 accuracy partially offsets the -5 penalty, making GWM + Geode Heart a very strong combination. This is intentional; a Very Rare weapon should enable powerful build choices.

## Variants
- **Rare — Geode Shard:** +1 weapon, Geode Heart Strike (1d6 cold), Geode Pulse (cold resistance + light). No Crystalline Prison, no Geode Cataclysm. Cost: ~500,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — Geode Cathedral:** +3 weapon. Geode Heart Strike 2d8 cold. Crystalline Prison triggers on a hit (not just crit) when you expend a use — 3/long rest. Geode Cataclysm 2/long rest, damage increases to 8d6 cold + 8d6 radiant. Additionally, you can cast *investiture of ice* once per long rest without expending a spell slot. The Geode Pulse sheds light in a 30-ft radius. Cost: ~75,000,000 soul stones.
