# Rimespire
*Weapon (spear), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A spear whose shaft is a column of interlocked crystal facets, frozen together into a seamless whole that hums with trapped cold. The head is a single broad geode shard, translucent at its thinnest edges and opaque blue-white at its core. When the spear strikes, frost blooms from the wound in a web of pale crystalline growth — the Trench's chill reaching out to claim what was struck.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Creeping Hoarfrost.** When you hit a creature with this spear, frost spreads from the wound. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of your next turn. This effect does not stack with itself.

**Glacial Field (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can thrust the spear into the ground, causing crystalline ice to erupt outward. The ground in a 20-foot radius centered on you becomes covered in slick, faceted ice for 1 minute. This area is difficult terrain. When a creature enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. The ice persists even if you leave the area.

**Frost Claim.** When you reduce a creature of CR 1 or higher to 0 hit points with this spear, its body freezes solid and shatters. Roll a d20. On a roll of 18 or higher, the creature crystallizes into 3d10 × 10,000 soul stones, which clatter to the ground where it fell. The spear's shaft glows faintly brighter for a moment as the soul is drawn through the crystal.

- **Damage/Effect:** +1 spear; speed reduction on hit (no action cost); 1/long rest 20-ft ice zone

---

## Balance Notes
Three properties — a +1 weapon, an on-hit control rider, and a once-per-day area denial effect — plus a soul harvest mechanic that ties the spear into Floor 1's economy. The speed reduction requires no action economy beyond the attack itself, which is strong but constrained by the save and the no-stacking clause. Glacial Field is non-damaging zone control (comparable to a *spike growth* that trades damage for the prone hazard).

**Frost Claim** gives the spear an economic dimension without consuming its combat power budget — it triggers on kills you were already getting, with a conservative 15% proc rate (18+ on d20). The CR 1+ gate prevents farming vermin. When it does trigger, 3d10 × 10,000 soul stones (avg ~165,000, range 30,000–300,000) is a meaningful windfall at Rare-tier play. Compare to *frost brand* (very rare) which offers +1d6 cold, fire resistance, and light — Rimespire is control-and-economy oriented instead of damage-oriented, earning its Rare slot.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Rimeshard:** +1 weapon. Creeping Hoarfrost only (DC 12). No Glacial Field. Cost: ~35,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Rimetower:** +2 weapon. Creeping Hoarfrost DC 16, stacks up to two times (speed reduced by 20 ft). Glacial Field 2/long rest, radius 30 ft. Additionally, while holding this spear you have resistance to cold damage. Cost: ~1,800,000 soul stones.
