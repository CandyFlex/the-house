# Geode Fang
*Weapon (dagger), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Chipped from a single blue-white geode shard pulled from the frozen walls of the Crystal Trench, this dagger is cold to the touch even in warm hands. Its edge is not forged but naturally faceted — a blade of raw crystal with no two angles the same. A faint mist curls from the surface when the dagger is drawn, and the wielder can feel a brittle tension within the blade, as though the crystal wants to break.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Frostbite Shatter.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can choose to shatter the blade's outer layer against the impact, dealing an additional 2d4 cold damage. The crystal regrows to its full shape after you finish a short rest.

## Balance Notes
The +1 bonus plus a 1/short rest 2d4 cold spike (avg 5 extra damage) is firmly in uncommon territory. Compare to a *+1 weapon* (uncommon, no attunement) — this requires attunement in exchange for the burst damage rider. The 2d4 is intentionally d4s rather than d6s to keep the spike moderate: a crit with Frostbite Shatter deals 4d4 bonus cold (avg 10), which is exciting but not encounter-warping. The "shatter and regrow" flavor gives the DM a visual tell when the ability is available.

---

## Variants
- **Rare — Geode Stiletto:** +2 weapon. Frostbite Shatter recharges on a short rest *or* when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with this dagger (whichever comes first), and the damage increases to 3d4 cold. Cost: ~120,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Geode Heart Spike:** +2 weapon. Frostbite Shatter deals 3d6 cold and is available once per turn (no rest restriction). Additionally, when you use Frostbite Shatter, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have its speed reduced by 10 feet until the end of your next turn as frost creeps up its body. Cost: ~1,200,000 soul stones.
