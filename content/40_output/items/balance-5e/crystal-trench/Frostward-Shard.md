# Frostward Shard
*Wondrous item, Uncommon (requires attunement)*

> [!tip]+ Quick Stats
> - **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Attunement:** Yes
> - **Type:** Wondrous item
> - **Value:** 18,000 soul stones

---

## Description
A palm-sized fragment of blue-white geode crystal, rough on one face where it was chipped from the Trench wall and smooth on the other where centuries of frozen mist polished it to a glassy finish. It is perpetually cold — not painfully so, but enough to remind the holder that the Crystal Trench does not forget its own. When danger threatens, the shard draws the cold inward, leaving the wielder untouched by frost that would fell others.

## Properties
**Frost Ward.** While holding or carrying this shard, you have resistance to cold damage.

**Surefooted on Ice.** You can move across icy surfaces without needing to make Dexterity saving throws to avoid falling prone, and icy terrain is not difficult terrain for you.

> [!example]- Balance Notes
> Two modest passive properties: cold resistance and a narrow terrain immunity. Cold resistance on its own is an uncommon-tier benefit (compare to *ring of warmth*, uncommon, which gives cold resistance + immunity to extreme cold weather). The ice-walk rider replaces the weather immunity with a more adventure-specific benefit — it will matter in frozen dungeons and against *sleet storm* but is useless in a desert, making it a sidegrade. The small physical size and low weight make it easy to share among a party, but attunement prevents abuse. No charges, no activated abilities — this is a set-it-and-forget-it defensive trinket suitable for early-tier play in cold environments.

> [!note]- Variants
> - **Rare — Frostward Heart:** Cold immunity instead of resistance. The shard now glows faintly blue when within 60 feet of a source of extreme cold (white dragon's lair, *cone of cold* trap, etc.). Requires attunement. Cost: ~150,000 soul stones.
