# Cryo-Lattice Staff
*Staff (quarterstaff), rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

---

## Description
A quarterstaff wrapped in a helical lattice of living geode crystal that pulses with a slow arctic rhythm. The wood beneath is fossilized — a tree that grew in the Trench before the Trench knew what it was becoming. When spells pass through the staff, they emerge colder, sharper, hungrier. The lattice tightens around the wood in response to magic, as though feeding on the energy that passes through it.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff.

**Cryo-Focus.** While holding this staff, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls for spells that deal cold damage. Additionally, the staff can be used as a spellcasting focus.

**Spells.** While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend charges to cast one of the following spells (spell save DC 15): *ice knife* (1 charge, cast as 2nd level), *sleet storm* (2 charges), or *ice storm* (3 charges). The spells manifest as eruptions of crystalline shards. If you expend the staff's last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the crystal lattice shatters, destroying the staff in a harmless puff of frost.

This staff has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

## Balance Notes
A hybrid melee/spellcasting staff at Rare. +1 weapon (uncommon baseline), +1 to cold spell attacks (half a *wand of the war mage +1*, uncommon), and a charge system that covers 2nd (*ice knife* upcast), 3rd (*sleet storm*), and 4th (*ice storm*) level spells. At 5 charges with 1d4+1 recharge, the wielder gets roughly 3–4 spell casts per day, comparable to a *staff of frost* (very rare)'s lesser sibling. The attunement-by-spellcaster restriction adds power budget. The self-destruct on last-charge natural 1 is standard staff design and encourages not fully draining the staff unless desperate.

## Variants
**Uncommon Variant (Geode-Laced Rod).** +1 weapon only (no Cryo-Focus, no spells). Can be used as a spellcasting focus. Cost: 100,000 soul stones.

**Very Rare Variant (Heartstaff of the Trench).** +2 weapon. Cryo-Focus becomes +2. Charge pool increases to 7, regain 1d6+1 at dawn. Add *cone of cold* (4 charges, DC 17). Save DC increases to 17. Cost: 1,200,000 soul stones.
