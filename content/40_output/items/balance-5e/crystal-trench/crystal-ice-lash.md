# Crystal Ice Lash
*Weapon (whip), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A whip woven from the crystalline sinew of a frost salamander, each segment a faceted geode link that clinks like chimes in a cold wind. The handle is a solid geode core wrapped in pale leather, and the lash leaves a trail of frost in the air — a pale scar that fades a heartbeat after the strike lands. It is cold enough to numb where it touches, and wicked enough to pull a foe off their feet.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Crystal Lash.** When you hit a creature with this whip, it takes an additional 1d4 cold damage.

**Frozen Snare.** Once per turn when you hit a Large or smaller creature with this whip, you can pull the target up to 10 feet toward you. If the target ends this movement within your reach, frost seizes its limbs. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn.

**Rime Coil.** When you score a critical hit with this weapon, crystalline ice explodes across the target. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be restrained by ice until the end of your next turn. Constructs and creatures immune to cold damage automatically succeed on this save.

## Balance Notes
A reach weapon with control properties. +1 whip with +1d4 cold (avg 6.5+mod per hit) and a pull+snare effect on hit (once per turn). The crit-restrain is the spike. Four properties at Rare is aggressive but each is individually modest: the base damage is low (whip is 1d4), the +1d4 cold rider is half what a *flame tongue* offers, the pull is limited to 10ft, and the snare strips speed to 0 for one round (not restrained, just immobilized). The crit-restrain is a satisfying 5% excitement spike. Compare to *flame tongue whip* would be rare (+2d6 fire, no +X, no control). Crystal Ice Lash trades raw damage for battlefield manipulation.

---

## Variants
**Very Rare: Blizzard Lash.** Increase the enhancement bonus to +2, the cold rider to 2d4, and the Frozen Snare pull distance to 15 feet. The Rime Coil save DC increases to 17.
