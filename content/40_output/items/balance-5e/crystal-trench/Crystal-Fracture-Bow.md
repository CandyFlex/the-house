# Crystal Fracture Bow
*Weapon (longbow), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
The limbs of this bow are carved from two matched geode shards, their inner faces lined with facets that catch and split every scrap of ambient light. The grip is wrapped in pale leather cured in the Trench's frozen mist — cold to the cheek when drawn, but never numbing. There is no string. When the wielder draws back, a thread of refracted light snaps into existence between the crystal tips, and an arrow loosed from this bow does not fly so much as it *splinters* through the air, shedding slivers of blue-white brilliance behind it.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Refracting Volley.** When you hit a creature with an arrow from this bow, you can cause a shard of refracted light to split from the arrow mid-flight. One additional creature of your choice within 10 feet of the target takes 1d6 radiant damage as the shard pierces it. You can use this property a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

**Prism Burst (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you fire an arrow that detonates into a sphere of refracted crystal light at a point within the weapon's normal range. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 radiant damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. The area is filled with bright light until the end of your next turn, and any invisible creature in the area when the burst detonates becomes visible until the end of your next turn.

## Mechanics Block (DM Quick Reference)

- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** Yes
- **Charges:** Refracting Volley uses = PB/long rest; Prism Burst 1/short rest
- **Save DC:** 14 (Dexterity vs Prism Burst)
- **Spell Equivalent:** Prism Burst ≈ 2nd-level area spell (3d6 radiant, 10-ft radius)
- **Damage/Effect:** +1 longbow; PB/long rest splash 1d6 radiant to a secondary target; 1/short rest 10-ft radius 3d6 radiant + light + anti-invisibility

---

## Balance Notes
Three properties — a +1 weapon, a PB-scaling splash damage rider, and a once-per-short-rest AoE that doubles as an anti-invisibility tool. The Refracting Volley is essentially a weaker *horde breaker* that costs a limited resource — PB uses per day keeps it relevant as you level but never overwhelming (2d6 bonus radiant per day at level 5, up to 6d6 at level 17). Prism Burst's 3d6 in a 10-ft radius is modest damage (avg 10.5) but the anti-invisibility rider gives it utility against foes that rely on *greater invisibility* or similar effects. Compare to *oathbow* (very rare, single-target focus) — the Fracture Bow is less boss-killer and more crowd-scatterer, fitting Rare.

---

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Glintbow:** +1 weapon. Refracting Volley only (PB uses). No Prism Burst. Cost: ~40,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Cataclysm Bow:** +2 weapon. Refracting Volley deals 1d8 radiant. Prism Burst 2/short rest, damage increases to 5d6 radiant, radius 15 ft. Additionally, you can cast *daylight* once per long rest without expending a spell slot, the light appearing as harsh blue-white crystal refractions. Cost: ~2,200,000 soul stones.
