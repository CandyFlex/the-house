# Ember-Eye Lens
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A single lens of smoked glass, thick as a coin and set in a tarnished brass frame, designed to be worn over one eye. These lenses were issued to the old empire's secret police — the Ember-Eyes — who hunted escaped slaves by tracking the invisible heat-sigils branded into their skin during processing. The lens reveals what the naked eye cannot: the heat of a body hiding behind a wall, the fading warmth of a recently occupied bed, the signature of a spell hiding a creature from sight. The Ember-Eyes were feared more than the arena lions, and when the revolt came, they were the first to be dragged into the sand.

## Properties

**Smoke-Sight.** While wearing this lens over one eye, you have darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. If you already have darkvision, its range increases by 30 feet while wearing the lens.

**Heat-Haze Revelation (1/Long Rest).** You can cast *see invisibility* without expending a spell slot. When you do, invisible creatures and objects appear to you as translucent forms wreathed in faint heat-haze, like air above sun-baked stone.

**Blood-Heat Sight.** While wearing this lens, you can see the heat signatures of warm-blooded creatures through barriers up to 6 inches of wood, 1 inch of common stone, or 1 foot of earth. Such creatures appear as glowing orange silhouettes. You cannot discern specific features — only size, approximate shape, and whether the creature is moving. This sight does not penetrate metal of any thickness, lead, or barriers warded against divination magic.

## Balance Notes
A reconnaissance item built for infiltration and ambush-heavy parties. Darkvision is standard uncommon fare. *See invisibility* 1/long rest is uncommon-tier. Blood-Heat Sight is the rare-tier mechanic: wall-penetrating vision with strict limitations (thin barriers, no metal, silhouettes only). It rewards careful scouting without trivializing dungeons — you can see that three guards are in the next room, but not their equipment, species, or readiness. Compare to a *robe of eyes* (rare) — the lens trades 360-degree vision and passive Perception advantage for wall-penetrating heat vision, a fair sideways trade.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Smoked Spyglass Lens:** Darkvision 60 ft (or +30 ft). Heat-Haze Revelation 1/long rest. No Blood-Heat Sight. Requires attunement. Cost: ~80,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Ember-Eye's Truth:** Darkvision 120 ft (or +60 ft). Heat-Haze Revelation 3/long rest, and you also gain the benefits of *truesight* out to 30 ft for 1 minute when you activate it. Blood-Heat Sight penetrates up to 1 ft of wood, 3 inches of stone. Additionally, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to spot hidden creatures. Requires attunement. Cost: ~2,600,000 soul stones.
