# Hundred-Death Shield
*Armor (shield), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This tower shield is covered in tally marks — exactly one hundred, each carved by a different hand. It was passed from gladiator to gladiator in Caesar's pits, each survivor adding their mark after watching a friend fall. The gouges are uneven: some are deep and angry, others shallow and trembling. The hundredth mark was carved by a child named Livia, who survived Caesar's purge of the slave quarters by hiding beneath a pile of bodies — she scratched her line with a broken spoon while the soldiers were still in the next room. The shield remembers every death it has witnessed, and it will not let the next one pass unmarked.

## Properties

**You have a +1 bonus to AC while holding this shield.**

**Death-Tally.** When a creature you can see within 5 feet of you drops to 0 hit points, one of the tally marks on the shield glows with a faint silver light and then fades away, and you gain a +1 bonus to your AC until the end of your next turn. This bonus can stack up to a maximum of +3 AC (in addition to the shield's normal bonus). Each tally mark can only glow once — after one hundred uses, the shield becomes a nonmagical shield.

## Balance Notes
A high-risk, high-reward defensive item. The +1 shield base puts it at uncommon-equivalent; the Death-Tally mechanic pushes it to rare. The stacking AC bonus rewards aggressive play near dying enemies — perfect for frontliners holding a chokepoint or standing in a melee scrum. The 100-use hard cap creates dramatic tension: players will count uses and the shield's eventual "death" becomes a story moment. AC stacking is powerful (potentially +3 AC on top of a +1 shield is +5 total shield AC), but it's conditional on proximity to death and only lasts one round, so it rewards tactical positioning. Compare to a *+2 shield* (rare) — this is +1 base but can spike to +4 total for a round, trading consistency for drama.
