# Liberator's Edge
*Weapon (longsword), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This longsword was forged from the broken blades of twelve rebels executed by Caesar for the crime of hope. After each execution, a sympathizer in the palace smithy collected the shattered steel, hiding the fragments beneath the forge. The thirteenth rebel — a woman named Severa — survived her sentence by killing her executioner with his own axe. She recovered the fragments, melted them down, and forged this blade herself in a cave outside the city while Caesar's hunters searched the hills. The sword's steel is streaked with twelve different shades of grey where the original blades fused imperfectly. When a tyrant is near, the fuller bleeds a thin line of blood-red light — the memory of its makers, still watching.

## Properties

**You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.**

**Cut the Bonds.** When you hit a creature with this sword, you can choose to deal no damage and instead attempt to sever one magical effect controlling or incapacitating the target. Choose one of the following conditions currently affecting the target: charmed, frightened, paralyzed, restrained, or stunned. The target can immediately repeat its saving throw against the effect that caused the condition, with advantage, ending the effect on a success.

**Tyrant's Bane.** This sword weeps a thin line of blood-red light along the fuller whenever a creature of lawful evil alignment with a challenge rating of 10 or higher is within 120 feet of you. The light provides dim light in a 5-foot radius.

**Rebel's Dismissal (1/Long Rest).** You can cast *dispel evil and good* from the sword, using only the Dismissal option. The saving throw DC is 17.

- **Damage/Effect:** +2 longsword (1d8/1d10+2+mod); on-hit condition removal (no cost, at-will); tyrant detection (120 ft); 1/long rest dismissal (DC 17)

## Balance Notes
An anti-oppression weapon built for campaigns with mind-controlling villains. Cut the Bonds is the unique mechanic: sacrificing damage to give an ally a re-save with advantage against five debilitating conditions is extraordinarily powerful in the right encounter — but it costs your attack and deals zero damage, creating genuine tactical tension. Tyrant's Bane is a flavorful detection tool that helps the party identify the Big Bad. Rebel's Dismissal rounds out the kit with a once-per-day "banish the fiend/elemental/fey/undead" button. Compare to a *+2 weapon of certain death* — this is weaker on damage but dramatically stronger on ally support. The ability to clear paralysis, stun, or domination at-will puts it firmly in very rare territory.

## Variants
- **Rare — Freedom's Fang:** +1 longsword. Cut the Bonds (charmed and frightened only). Tyrant's Bane (CR 13+). No Rebel's Dismissal. Requires attunement. Cost: ~350,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — Edge of the Unchained:** +3 longsword. Cut the Bonds (all conditions except petrified and dead; auto-success — no saving throw needed). Tyrant's Bane detects any creature that has ever committed an act of tyranny (DM's discretion), no range limit on the same plane. Rebel's Dismissal 3/long rest (DC 19). Additionally, 1/long rest you can cast *freedom of movement* on up to six creatures within 60 feet. The sword cannot be wielded by a creature of evil alignment — it burns them for 4d6 radiant damage per round. Requires attunement by a creature that has personally overthrown a tyrant. Cost: priceless.
