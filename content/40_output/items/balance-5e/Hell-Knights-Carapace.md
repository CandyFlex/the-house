# Hell Knight's Carapace
*Armor (plate), Very Rare (requires attunement)*

> [!tip]+ Quick Stats
> - **Rarity:** Very Rare | **Attunement:** Yes
> - **Type:** Armor (plate)
> - **Save DC:** 17 | **Value:** 3,200,000 soul stones

---

## Description
Forged from infernal iron mined beneath the plains of Avernus and quenched in the blood of a pit fiend general, this suit of blackened plate devours light like a dying star. The pauldrons are sculpted into snarling devil faces, and between each plate runs a seam of sullen crimson — not fire, but the color of a heartbeat beneath obsidian glass. When the wearer stands still, the only sound is a low, rhythmic thrum, as if something within the metal is waiting. Hell Knights of the Nine Hells do not wear this armor as protection. They wear it as a promise: *you will be found, and you will burn*.

## Properties
**Infernal Bastion.** While wearing this armor, you gain a +2 bonus to AC and you have resistance to fire damage. In addition, you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened, and you can speak, read, and write Infernal.

**Hellfire Retribution (3/Day).** When a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to loose the hellfire bound within the armor. The attacker must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, it takes 6d10 fire damage and is pushed 10 feet directly away from you. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is not pushed. This fire damage ignores resistance to fire damage.

**Warden's Decree (1/Day).** As an action, you slam your fist against the breastplate and issue a command in Infernal that seizes the bones of all who hear it. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you that can hear you must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you for 1 minute. While frightened in this way, a creature's speed is 0 and it cannot take reactions. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Fiends have disadvantage on this saving throw.

**Scorn the Weak (1/Short Rest).** When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you is reduced to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to streak toward the next target. You fly up to your walking speed without provoking opportunity attacks. The first attack you make before the end of your next turn deals an additional 2d6 fire damage.

> [!abstract]- Mechanics (DM Reference)
> - **Rarity:** Very Rare
> - **Attunement:** Yes
> - **Save DC:** 17
> - **Charges:** None (daily and short-rest uses)
> - **Spell Equivalent:** Hellfire Retribution ≈ upcast *hellish rebuke* (6th level, 3/day); Warden's Decree ≈ *fear* + paralysis-lite (5th-level equivalent, 1/day)
> - **Damage/Effect:** +2 AC; fire resistance; adv vs frightened; 3/day reaction 6d10 fire (ignores resistance) + 10ft push; 1/day AoE fear + root (speed 0, no reactions); 1/short-rest kill → fly + damage rider

> [!example]- Balance Notes
> Built to the new Very Rare ceiling: +2 AC with 4 distinct properties, each layering passive/active/spike functions. Hellfire Retribution at 6d10 (avg 33) three times a day is a meaningful reaction resource — the 10-ft push adds control, letting the Hell Knight peel melee attackers. Warden's Decree is a custom effect: the fear + root + reaction denial is stronger than the *fear* spell but narrower (30 ft, creatures that can hear). Scorn the Weak borrows Severance's "kill → move" energy at a smaller scale (1/short rest, no advantage rider). Synergies: Retribution pushes enemies into Warden's Decree range; Scorn the Weak gives mobility to reapply Warden's Decree pressure. Compare to *efreeti chain* (Legendary) — this is deliberately one tier down but punches close.

> [!note]- Variants
> - **Rare — Squire's Cuirass:** +1 AC, fire resistance, advantage on saves vs frightened, Infernal Tongue. Hellfire Retribution 2/day (4d10, DC 15, no push). No Warden's Decree, no Scorn the Weak. Requires attunement. Cost: ~600,000 soul stones.
> 
> - **Legendary — Archfiend's Panoply:** +3 AC. Fire immunity (not resistance). Advantage on all Wisdom saving throws. Infernal Tongue. Hellfire Retribution 5/day (8d10, DC 19, push 15 ft). Warden's Decree 2/day, affects all creatures regardless of hearing. Scorn the Weak 2/short rest, fly speed equal to double walking speed. Additionally, as an action 1/day you can cast *plane shift* (self only, targeting the Nine Hells). While wearing this armor, devils of CR 10 or lower are indifferent to you unless you attack them. Attunement by a creature of lawful alignment only. Cost: ~550,000,000 soul stones.
