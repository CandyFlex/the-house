# Siege-Breaker
*Weapon (maul), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This maul's head is a single block of black granite veined with red — granite taken from the threshold of the imperial palace, shattered on the night of the final revolt against the old empire. A slave-turned-sapper named Goran swung this maul for six hours against the palace gate, trading off with two other rebels when his arms gave out. When the gate finally cracked, the maul's head fractured too — a spiderweb of fissures that still hold, impossibly, as though the weapon refuses to break before the next wall falls. Goran died three days later of his wounds, but he died outside the palace, a free man, with the maul still in his hands.

## Properties

**You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.**

**Gate-Breaker.** This weapon deals double damage to objects and structures.

**No Cover, No Quarter.** When you hit a creature that has half cover, three-quarters cover, or total cover with this weapon, the cover is destroyed (if nonmagical) or suppressed until the end of your next turn (if magical). The creature takes the attack's damage as normal, ignoring any AC bonus from the destroyed or suppressed cover.

**Ruinous Quake (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can slam the maul into the ground to create an *earthquake* effect in a 60-foot-radius circle centered on you. This effect functions as the *earthquake* spell (save DC 17), except that creatures within the area are not directly damaged or knocked prone by the shaking — only structures and terrain are affected. Creatures can still be harmed by collapsing structures, falling into fissures, or being struck by falling debris as normal for the spell.

- **Spell Equivalent:** Ruinous Quake = *earthquake* (8th level, structure-only damage, 1/long rest); Gate-Breaker ≈ *shatter* (continuous, objects only)
- **Damage/Effect:** +2 maul (2d6+2+STR); double damage to objects/structures; cover destruction/suppression on hit; 1/long rest structure-only *earthquake* (DC 17)

---

## Balance Notes
A siege weapon that transforms encounters with fortifications, barriers, and entrenched enemies. +2 maul is the very rare floor. Gate-Breaker's double damage to objects makes the wielder the party's breaching specialist — castle gates, portcullises, walls, and constructs all fall faster. No Cover, No Quarter is the combat centerpiece: it punishes enemies using cover without requiring a separate action to destroy it. Ruinous Quake is an 8th-level spell on a 1/long rest timer — but the creature-damage exclusion keeps it from being a combat nuke and focuses it on terrain transformation. Compare to a *maul of the titans* (very rare, +3, double damage to objects) — this trades +1 attack/damage for cover destruction and an *earthquake*, making it more versatile.

## Variants
- **Rare — Gate-Crasher:** +1 maul. Gate-Breaker (double damage to objects/structures). No Cover, No Quarter (cover destruction only, no suppression). No Ruinous Quake. Requires attunement. Cost: ~280,000 soul stones.
- **Legendary — World-Breaker:** +3 maul. Gate-Breaker (triple damage to objects/structures). No Cover, No Quarter (magical cover is also destroyed, not suppressed). Ruinous Quake 2/long rest, full *earthquake* effect including creature damage (DC 19). Additionally, the maul can cast *disintegrate* (1/long rest) — but only targeting structures and objects, not creatures. The maul's head is replaced with a piece of the palace's foundation stone. Requires attunement. Cost: priceless.
