# Slave-Brand Bracers
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
These heavy iron bracers were heated until they glowed and pressed onto the forearms of Cassia, a slave who organized a work-stoppage in Caesar's quarries. The branding left her flesh permanently scarred with Caesar's seal — a screaming eagle wreathed in chains — but it also left her unbreakable. After a tyrant has burned his mark into your skin, what threat can a lesser master possibly make? The bracers still radiate faint warmth, as though the forge-fire of that day has never fully died.

## Properties

**Already Broken.** While wearing these bracers, you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened by a creature whose challenge rating is higher than your level. You have already endured worse than anything they can do to your mind.

**Branded Fists.** Your unarmed strikes deal bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 + your Strength modifier. When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike while wearing these bracers, you can choose to deal an additional 1 fire damage as the brands flare with phantom heat.

## Balance Notes
The mental defense is tightly scoped: it only works against creatures of higher CR than your level, so it's most useful against boss-type enemies — exactly when the party needs protection most. It does nothing against equal or lower-CR threats or minions. The unarmed strike boost makes this attractive to monks and unarmed builds without overshadowing dedicated weapons. At uncommon, this is a strong defensive item with a flavorful offensive rider. Compare to a *ring of mind shielding* (uncommon) — this is narrower (frightened/charmed only) but has the unarmed bonus, so it's a fair trade.

## Variants
- **Common — Scarred Shackles:** Advantage on saves vs frightened only (higher-CR creatures). No unarmed bonus. No attunement. Cost: ~5,000 soul stones.
- **Rare — Tyrant's Bane Bracers:** Advantage on saves vs charmed and frightened from any creature; unarmed strikes deal 1d6 + 1d4 fire; 1/long rest you can cast *heroism* on yourself without concentration (1 minute). Requires attunement. Cost: ~300,000 soul stones.
