# Exercise — Three Conflicting Rumors

Use this exercise to develop texture around any entity — a person, 
place, faction, event, or item. The rumors don't have to be true. 
The conflict between them is the point.

---

## The Exercise

Pick any entity in your world. Write three rumors about it. Each 
rumor should:
- Come from a different source (different floor, faction, or perspective)
- Contradict at least one of the other two in some way
- Be the kind of thing someone would genuinely believe

Then answer: **What is actually true? And why are all three rumors 
partly wrong?**

---

## Example (for demonstration only — not canon)

Entity: A faction called "the Green Hand"

**Rumor 1** (from a Floor 3 merchant):
"The Green Hand built the road between Floors 3 and 4 two hundred 
years ago. They keep it safe for a toll. Reasonable people. Hard 
bargainers."

**Rumor 2** (from a Floor 5 scholar):
"The Green Hand didn't build that road — they seized it from the 
engineers who built it. The original builders were erased. The 
faction's entire legitimacy is a theft they're still covering up."

**Rumor 3** (from a Floor 4 guard):
"There is no Green Hand anymore. What you're seeing is the shell of 
the organization. The real power left two generations ago. Whoever's 
running it now is just collecting the toll and calling themselves 
by the old name."

**What's actually true:**
The road was built by a coalition that included early Green Hand members, 
but the founding of the faction post-dates the road. Rumor 1 conflates 
the two. Rumor 2 is a myth propagated by a rival. Rumor 3 is half-right — 
the current leadership is weak — but the real power didn't leave, it went 
underground.

---

## Variations

- **Three rumors about a place:** What happened there. Who controls it. 
  Whether it's safe.
- **Three rumors about a person:** What they did. Who they serve. What 
  they want.
- **Three rumors about an event:** What caused it. Who benefited. 
  Whether it's really over.
- **Three rumors from three floors:** What Floor 2 says about Floor 6's 
  religion. What Floor 6 says. What someone who has been to both says.
