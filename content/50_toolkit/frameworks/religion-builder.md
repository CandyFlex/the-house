# Framework — Religion and Belief System Builder

Religion in The House is different from standard D&D cosmology. There 
are no confirmed outer planes. Gods may or may not exist. The 
mesophysical layer means that belief and intention can have real 
effects — which makes religion dangerous in a specific way.

---

## 1. The Core Claim

Every religion makes a cosmological claim. What is this one's?

- What does this belief system say about The House? (Origin? Purpose? Fate?)
- Does it acknowledge all seven floors, or privilege some over others?
- What does it say about the "no outside" fact? 
  (Denial? Acceptance? Reframing?)
- What does it say about the mesophysical layer specifically?

---

## 2. The Object of Devotion

- Is there a deity, pantheon, force, concept, or ancestor being worshipped?
- If a deity — is it confirmed to exist? Has it ever been encountered 
  directly? By whom?
- What does devotion *do*? Does it produce results? If so, what kind?
- What happens to doubters?

---

## 3. Practice

- What do adherents do daily/weekly/seasonally?
- What is the religion's relationship with the floor it lives on?
  (Does it reflect the floor's thematic color? Oppose it? Transcend it?)
- What are the taboos? What is the worst sin?
- What are the rites of passage (birth, adulthood, death, conflict)?

---

## 4. Structure

- Is this religion organized (hierarchy, clergy, institutions) or 
  decentralized (personal practice, local variation)?
- Who has authority to interpret doctrine?
- What is the relationship between the religious authorities and the 
  political/factional authorities on this floor?

---

## 5. History and Schism

- How old is this religion?
- Has it ever split? What caused the split and what happened?
- What did it look like at its founding vs. now?
- What heresy is currently the most dangerous to the mainstream?

---

## 6. Relationship to Other Floors

- Does this religion claim authority across floors?
- Does it have missionaries, or is it floor-specific?
- What does it teach about the other floors?
- Is there a version of this religion on another floor that is in 
  conflict with this one?

---

## 7. What It Gets Wrong

Every religion gets something wrong about the world it lives in. What 
does this one misunderstand, suppress, or actively invert about The House?
