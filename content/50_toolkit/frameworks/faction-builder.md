# Framework — Faction Builder

A faction that makes sense from the inside out. Start with founding 
purpose, follow the drift, find the rot.

---

## 1. Origin

- What problem did this faction form to solve?
- Who founded it, and what did they believe?
- What was the founding moment — the event that crystallized the group?
- What floor did it originate on? When did it (if ever) spread to others?

---

## 2. Current State

- What does the faction *say* it stands for?
- What does it *actually* do when it has power?
- Are those the same? If not, when did they diverge?
- What is its current size (tiny cult / regional force / floor-spanning power)?

---

## 3. Resources and Leverage

- What does this faction control that others want or fear?
- What would it lose if it lost power? (Land? Knowledge? Access? Belief?)
- What does it owe, and to whom?
- Who does it depend on that it can't admit it depends on?

---

## 4. Internal Structure

- How are decisions made? (Council? Hierarchy? Charismatic leader? Ritual?)
- Where are the internal fault lines? Who disagrees, and about what?
- What is the faction's relationship with its own history? 
  (Mythologized? Suppressed? Disputed?)
- Has it ever split? If so, what happened to the splinter?

---

## 5. External Relationships

For each significant other faction or entity:
- Ally / Rival / Enemy / Uses / Is Used By / Complicated
- What's the public relationship vs. the real one?

---

## 6. How It Has Betrayed Itself

Every faction eventually betrays its founding purpose. Name five ways 
this faction has done so — or will. (One for each of: belief, 
action, membership, allies, and cost paid.)

1. **Belief betrayal** — They used to believe X. Now they believe Y but 
   still call it X.
2. **Action betrayal** — They said they'd never do Z. They do Z now.
3. **Membership betrayal** — The founding membership would not recognize 
   who's in charge now.
4. **Ally betrayal** — They're in bed with exactly the kind of entity 
   they were formed to oppose.
5. **Cost betrayal** — The price of maintaining the faction is now 
   greater than the problem it was solving.

---

## 7. What It Wants From You (The PCs / The Story)

- What would this faction ask of the players, if they had access?
- What would it never ask, because doing so would expose the contradiction 
  at its core?
- What happens to people who join and later try to leave?
