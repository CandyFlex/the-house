# Prompt — Floor Development Questionnaire

Use these questions when you want to develop a floor's identity. 
Don't answer all at once — pick the ones that spark something. 
The ones you can't answer are as useful as the ones you can.

---

## The Color as Emotion

- If [color] were a feeling, what would it be?
- What does it feel like to arrive on this floor for the first time?
- What does it feel like to have lived here your whole life?
- What emotion does this floor suppress in the people who stay too long?

---

## The People

- Who is born here? What are they like by default?
- Who comes here from other floors, and why?
- Who leaves, and what does leaving cost them?
- What kind of person does this floor make you become if you stay?
- What does a "good person" look like on this floor? A "bad person"?
- What does this floor think of Floor 1? Of Floor 7?

---

## The Power

- Who has power here, and how did they get it?
- What is the currency of power? (Violence? Knowledge? Lineage? 
  Access to something scarce?)
- What does this floor's version of "justice" look like?
- What does this floor's version of "mercy" look like?
- What is the thing no one with power here will say out loud?

---

## The Landscape

- What does the physical (or mesophysical) landscape look like?
- What is the most iconic location on this floor — the place that 
  defines it in the imagination?
- What is the strangest thing about the geography here?
- Where on this floor would you go if you wanted to disappear?
- Where would you go if you wanted to be found?

---

## The History

- What is the founding myth of this floor?
- What is the event that people here still haven't recovered from?
- What was this floor like before that event?
- What do the dominant powers here want everyone to forget?

---

## The Edges

- Where does this floor bleed into Floor [N-1]?
- Where does this floor bleed into Floor [N+1]?
- Is there anywhere on this floor where the border is unstable?
- What crosses that border most often — people, goods, ideas, monsters?

---

## The Lie

Every floor has a lie at its center — something it tells itself 
to keep functioning.

- What is the lie?
- Who benefits from the lie?
- What would happen if the lie were exposed?
