# Floor Redemption Questionnaire — IO's Floor 1 Transformation
## ROGUELIKE EDITION

**Concept:** Caesar's Floor 1 was a cruelty-scape — desolation, ice, soul storms, brutal intake. You hold the mantle now. You're replacing the **Originator of Cruelty** with the **Originator of Freedom**. The floor is becoming what *you* are. The core game loop: **run dungeons → earn resources → invest in the floor → unlock harder dungeons → earn more**. The floor *is* a roguelike dungeon hub, and the economy is the loop.

This questionnaire has 4 phases. Do them in order.

---

# PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION
*Set the frame. Short answers, 1-3 sentences each.*

### 1.1 The Concept
What does **Freedom** mean as a floor-defining concept? One sentence.

> Example: *"Freedom means the House is no longer a prison. Everyone who wants to leave gets a chance to earn that right."*

### 1.2 The Originator Form
Do you (IO) **become** the Originator of Freedom, or **install** it as a separate entity?
What physical form does it take when interacting with the world?

### 1.3 The Transfer
Caesar's residue — The Descent, the soul storm, the castle, the 12 sons/heralds, the Temporal Hierarchy (Hours/Minutes/Seconds) — do they:
- **Dissolve** — fade as Freedom takes hold
- **Transform** — become something that serves Freedom
- **Tank** — contained as preserved remnants, accessible but sealed

### 1.4 Willing Allies
Who among the old floor's entities *willingly* sides with the new order?
- Chimerakind (they worshipped Caesar unknowingly — do they accept Freedom?)
- The vampires of the Dark Manor
- The Warlocks of the Skies (Woe is dead; their moon-shadow is empty)
- Any others

# PHASE 2 — AESTHETIC LAYER (The Look & Feel)
*These are visual filters. They stack. You can pick 2-4. They blend together to create the floor's atmosphere. Not a point cost — just taste.*

Below are **10 visual components** with blurbs. Pick the ones that resonate. The final floor look is a composite of your picks.

01 — **Enchanted Evening**
Permanent twilight. Silver and violet gradients. Stars always visible. The world exists in the hour between day and night. Soft. Attentive. Colors are muted but luminous. Magic feels more present in this light.

02 — **Isekai Fantasy**
Anime-filter reality. Exaggerated colors — grass is too green, water is too blue, sunsets are too vibrant. Character-defining aura effects. Dramatic lighting that follows emotional beats. The world looks like it *wants* to be a story.

03 — **.hack//slash / MMO Interface**
Floating UI elements visible in the air. Glowing teleport points with nameplates. Monster camps with visible respawn timers. Quest markers shimmer at chest height. Damage numbers float off enemies. The world renders like a game you can't minimize.

04 — **Moon-Pulled / Woe's Legacy**
Everything connected to Woe's memory. Silver mana pools. Tidal magic. The moon (or moons) dominate the sky. Shadows have weight. The landscape has a melancholy beauty — like it's remembering someone.

05 — **High Fantasy / Golden Hour**
A classic fantasy adventure palette. Gold and amber light. Verdant landscapes. Castles with pennants. Rivers that glitter. The world of epic quests and ancient prophecies. Familiar but beautiful.

06 — **Dreamscape**
Soft edges. Impossible geometry that feels natural. You know where things are without understanding the map. Time passes oddly here — you can spend hours in a dungeon and emerge to find only minutes passed. Memory-logic applies.

07 — **Sword & Sorcery / Romantic Ruin**
Lean into the ruins. Caesar's old structures, overgrown with new flora. Moss on stone. Torches that never go out. Ancient magic left by a prior age. The beauty of something that was broken and is now healing.

08 — **Starlit Void**
The sky is absolute black with too many stars. Constellations that don't exist in any real sky. The ground is dark earth that absorbs light. Creatures here look like they're made of constellations. Lonely. Infinite.

09 — **Crystal Garden**
Everything grows crystals. Trees are faceted. Rivers run over crystal beds. Light refracts in colored patterns everywhere. Sound echoes oddly — crystals resonate with footsteps. The entire floor is a geodesic wonderland.

10 — **Verdant Wilds**
Overgrowth. The floor is aggressively reclaiming itself. Vines cover old structures. Trees grow through ruined walls. Flowers bloom in impossible colors. Wildlife is returning — some of it new, some of it transformed from old Floor 1 creatures.

> **Pick 2-4 that feel right.** They blend. For example: Enchanted Evening + Isekai Fantasy + Dreamscape gives you a soft, dramatic, slightly-unreal world. Crystal Garden + Starlit Void + Moon-Pulled gives you a cold, beautiful, melancholy one.

> **After picking, give the floor a new NAME** (not "Floor 1 — Red" anymore). Something that captures the composite. Example: *The Twilight Verge, The Gilded Gate, The Dreaming Fields.*

# PHASE 3 — THE ROGUELIKE ENGINE (Core Game Loop)

*This is the heart of the transformation. Floor 1 becomes a dungeon destination — a hub where visitors from across the House come to run dungeons, earn resources, and spend them on the floor's economy. The dungeons **are** the economy.*

## 3.1 The Budget

You have **6 Dungeon Points (DP)** to start. Each point unlocks one dungeon at its **Entry Tier**.

As the floor economy grows and dungeons are run repeatedly, they evolve through **3 Tiers**:
- **Entry (1 DP)** — The dungeon in its first form. Appropriate for levels 3-5. Drops basic resources.
- **Deep (2 DP)** — Evolved version. Levels 6-8. Drops refined resources + unique materials.
- **Core (3 DP)** — Peak form. Levels 9+. Drops legendary resources + access to rare vendors or outside connections.

**You don't have to spend all 6 DP now.** Unspent points represent future potential — dungeons the floor is *ready* to generate but hasn't yet.

**Bonus DP:**
- +1 if you keep one of Caesar's POIs "tanked" (contained) rather than transforming or dissolving it
- +1 if you explain how this dungeon connects to IO's personal story
- +1 for accepting a shadow cost (see Phase 4)

## 3.2 Dungeon Archetypes (Pick Up To 6)

Each archetype has a **resource** it generates (the thing that feeds into the floor economy) and a **metamorphic path** (how it changes as you invest more DP in it).

---

### 01 — SPIRE DUNGEON
*A tower that rises from mist when approached. Floor-by-floor ascent. Boss at the top.*

**Resources:** Clarity Shards — used to attune equipment to the floor's magic. Basic crafting component. High demand.

| Tier | What It Looks Like | What Drops |
|------|--------------------|------------|
| Entry | 5 floors. Physical challenges + minor puzzles. Boss: a guardian of old Floor 1. | Clarity Shards (common), entry gear |
| Deep | 8 floors. The tower has grown. Environmental hazards. Mid-bosses on floors 3, 5, 7. | Clarity Shards (refined), +1 attuned gear, floor lore fragments |
| Core | 12 floors. The tower is self-aware. Floors rearrange. Boss: an echo of Caesar's 12 sons — one who chose to remain. | Clarity Shards (pure), legendary attuned gear, the son's herald-mark (key item) |

---

### 02 — MAZE DUNGEON
*A labyrinth of shifting walls. Layout changes every run. Time pressure from closing passages.*

**Resources:** Echo Dust — memory residue of past runners. Used for scrying, mapping, and navigating other dungeons. Information is the resource.

| Tier | What It Looks Like | What Drops |
|------|--------------------|------------|
| Entry | Single floor, moderate size. 3-5 dead ends with treasure. Exit appears after X rooms explored. | Echo Dust (raw), maps of other Entry dungeons |
| Deep | Two floors connected by hidden stairs. Walls shift while you're in them. Dead ends may contain ambushes or treasures. | Echo Dust (refined), partial map of one Deep dungeon |
| Core | The maze becomes a hypercube. 4+ dimensions of movement. True completion requires mapping across multiple runs. | Echo Dust (crystallized), the Maze Key — grants access to a locked section of any other dungeon |

---

### 03 — RUINS DUNGEON
*Remains of Caesar's outposts and pre-Caesar civilization. Lore-heavy. Traps. Puzzles from a dead culture.*

**Resources:** Archive Pages — lore fragments from before the cruelty-scape. Collect sets to unlock faction bonuses and discover secrets.

| Tier | What It Looks Like | What Drops |
|------|--------------------|------------|
| Entry | One outpost ruin. 3 puzzle rooms, 2 trap corridors, 1 boss chamber. | Archive Pages (common), minor lore completions |
| Deep | Connected ruin network — the outpost wasn't alone. 7 rooms. Ancient defenses still active. | Archive Pages (rare), faction reputation with discoverers |
| Core | The pre-Caesar archive — a buried library that predates the cruelty era entirely. Contains secrets about the House's design. | Archive Tomes (capstone), knowledge of one House mechanic previously unknown to the party |

---

### 04 — FIELD DUNGEON
*An open-air zone that transforms when entered. Mobs spawn. Environment shifts. A world-boss arena on a timer.*

**Resources:** Core Samples — raw mesophysical essence. Used to stabilize the floor's new reality. Foundations of the economy.

| Tier | What It Looks Like | What Drops |
|------|--------------------|------------|
| Entry | A large clearing ringed by standing stones. 3 waves of enemies. 1 field boss. Resets daily. | Core Samples (raw), stable terrain tags |
| Deep | The field has expanded. Multiple zones. Weather effects. Boss spawns on a rotating cycle. | Core Samples (stabilized), terrain-attuned items |
| Core | The field is now a biome unto itself. Environmental events (storms, eclipses) trigger super-boss spawns. | Core Samples (resonant), the ability to temporarily reshape one hex per week |

---

### 05 — POCKET DUNGEON
*A self-contained reality. Different physics. A door that opens into an entire small world. Exit returns you exactly where you entered.*

**Resources:** Reality Threads — the fabric of a pocket world. Used to create safe zones, expand the floor's boundaries, or repair damage.

| Tier | What It Looks Like | What Drops |
|------|--------------------|------------|
| Entry | A small room that contains a field, a village, or a dungeon — one location only. | Reality Threads (frayed), one-use pocket retreats |
| Deep | The pocket has expanded. Multiple connected locations. Its own day/night cycle. Inhabitants who don't know they're inside a dungeon. | Reality Threads (woven), ability to create a small personal safe zone on Floor 1 |
| Core | The pocket is now a full sub-floor — a dimension layered on top of Floor 1. Permanent residents. Trade between pocket and main floor. | Reality Loom (capstone), ability to anchor one pocket permanently as a new territory |

---

### 06 — LIVING DUNGEON
*The dungeon is a creature. You explore inside a colossal beast that sleeps on the plains.*

**Resources:** Vital Essence — life energy. Used for healing, resurrection, and empowering the floor's native creatures. Ties into the mesophysical layer.

| Tier | What It Looks Like | What Drops |
|------|--------------------|------------|
| Entry | A small sleeping beast. 3 interior "organs" as rooms. The creature stirs when you reach its core. | Vital Essence (raw), minor healing items |
| Deep | A larger beast, ancient. Multiple entry points. The creature's immune system fights you. Navigate veins, lungs, heart. | Vital Essence (concentrated), regenerative items, a creature-tame token |
| Core | The Titan — one of the first creatures of Floor 1, sleeping since before Caesar. Its interior contains an entire ecosystem. Full biomes inside. | Vital Essence (primordial), titan-bond — the creature becomes a mobile terrain feature you can direct |

### 07 — PHANTOM DUNGEON
*Memory-based. The dungeon replays events from Floor 1's history. Fight echoes of the past.*

| Tier | What It Looks Like | What Drops |

| Core | The full timeline of Floor 1, from its creation to Caesar's fall, rendered as a dungeon you walk through. Key moments are bosses. | Echo Matrix (capstone), the ability to call a past figure's echo for one consultation per session |

## 3.3 — Choosing & Upgrading

**Phase A** — Pick 2-4 dungeons at Entry tier (2-4 DP spent). These are your starting roster. The floor manifests them immediately.

**Phase B** — As the floor economy grows (players run dungeons, resources accumulate, visitors arrive), you can spend more DP to:
- Upgrade an existing dungeon to Deep (costs 1 additional DP)
- Upgrade to Core (costs 1 additional DP after Deep)
- Add a new dungeon at Entry (costs 1 DP)
- Add an Empty Throne upgrade (costs DP per upgrade)

**The economy loop:** Resources from dungeons → invest in floor infrastructure → attracts more visitors → more dungeon runs → more resources → unlock harder tiers.

# PHASE 4 — THE ECONOMY & THE OUTSIDE

*The dungeon resources feed the floor's economy. But what does that economy BUY?*

## 4.1 Resource Exchange

| Resource | What It's Used For | Who Wants It |
|----------|--------------------|--------------|
| Clarity Shards | Attuning equipment, crafting, trade | Adventurers, crafters |
| Echo Dust | Scrying, mapping, information trade | Scholars, spies |
| Archive Pages | Lore completion, faction rep | Factions, historians |
| Core Samples | Stabilizing new terrain, building | Builders, the floor itself |
| Reality Threads | Creating safe zones, repairs | Residents, factions |
| Vital Essence | Healing, creature bonding | Healers, beast-handlers |
| Echo Shards | Memory items, bargaining | Collectors, old entities |
| Catalyst Particles | Accelerating growth, temporary boosts | Everyone — volatile |
| Faction Favor | Political capital | Power brokers |

## 4.2 What Visitors Can Buy

Visitors come to Floor 1 to run dungeons. They spend dungeon resources at:

**The Market Hubs** (one per village hex):
- Gear upgrades attuned to Floor 1 (requires Clarity Shards)
- Maps and intel on dungeon layouts (requires Echo Dust)
- Safe passage zones (requires Reality Threads)
- Temporary buffs for next run (requires Catalyst Particles)

**The Exchange** (central):
- Convert dungeon resources to soul stones (standard House currency)
- Convert dungeon resources between types (arbitrage)
- Buy *access to harder dungeons* — the real draw

**The Archive** (at the Transformation School):
- Spend Archive Pages to unlock lore about the House
- Lore completions give mechanical bonuses
- Complete sets reveal secrets about other floors

## 4.3 The Outside Connection

*The player wants to give people access to "outside." The question is: what does "outside" mean, and how does the dungeon economy make it possible?*

**Option A — The Unintentional Realms**
A dungeon, when run at Core tier, punches a temporary hole to the Unintentional Realms. Visitors can step through for a limited time. High risk. High discovery potential. Cannot be sustained — each trip is a pilgrimage.

**Option B — The Memory of Outside**
Not real outside, but *tasting* it. Phantom Dungeons of pre-House memories. Run enough, and you can synthesize a stable vision of what "outside" was. People who have never been outside can experience it. A dream of the real world.

**Option C — The Scaffolding Gate**
Four-Squared, from the Scaffolding, can open a route. The Scaffolding isn't "outside" — it's the exterior construction ground. But from the Scaffolding, you can *see* outside. Maybe reach it. This requires Four-Squared's cooperation.

**Option D — Freedom's Price**
The Originator of Freedom can, once per month, open a true gate to outside. Someone must step through. They don't come back. IO decides who. This is the hardest version — Freedom's greatest gift costs the giver something each time.

**Option E — The Cumulative Gift**
When the floor's economy reaches a certain threshold (total resources generated across all dungeons), a permanent gate manifests. Repeatable. Earnable. The whole floor works toward it together.

## 4.4 The Shadow — What Does Freedom Cost?

Every Originator pays for their concept. Caesar paid in loss of self, loss of magic, isolation. What does Freedom pay?

Pick one:

- **The Lost** — Some visitors can't handle the freedom Floor 1 offers. They wander the floor aimlessly, unable to commit to any path. Not dead, not alive. Adrift. IO sees them sometimes, in the peripheral.
- **The Coming Storm** — Every dungeon opened, every gate connected, every resource generated — it creates a *signal*. Something in the deeper floors is noticing. The more successful the economy, the more attention is drawn.
- **The Fracture** — Freedom means people leave. Not just adventurers — residents, allies, friends. The floor is transient by design. It can never hold onto anything, because the whole point is letting go.
- **IO's Toll** — IO must personally maintain the boundaries between dungeons and the real floor. Every dungeon, every tier upgrade, every new connection — it adds to the weight IO carries. Freedom is light for everyone else. It's heavy for the Originator.

# QUICK-START — 3 Complete Variants

## Variant A: "The Spire Market"
**Light aesthetic:** Enchanted Evening + Sword & Sorcery
**Dungeons:** Spire (Entry, upgrading to Deep), Maze (Entry), The Empty Throne
**Economy focus:** Clarity Shards → gear upgrade market. Visitors come for attuned equipment.
**Outside:** The Cumulative Gift — when the market processes 1M clarity shards, a gate forms.
**Shadow:** The Fracture — the market town is always changing. Nothing lasts. IO visits and finds old friends have moved on.

## Variant B: "The Memory Fields"  
**Light aesthetic:** Moon-Pulled + Dreamscape + Starlit Void
**Dungeons:** Ruins (Entry→Deep), Phantom (Entry→Core), The Empty Throne + Hall of Echoes
**Economy focus:** Archive Pages + Echo Shards → lore trading. Knowledge is currency.
**Outside:** The Memory of Outside. Phantom runs produce stable visions. A generation born in the House will see the sky for the first time in memory-form.
**Shadow:** IO's Toll — every Phantom dungeon and Archive completion adds to IO's burden. The weight of knowing.

## Variant C: "The Catalyst Bazaar"
**Light aesthetic:** Isekai Fantasy + .hack//slash + Crystal Garden
**Dungeons:** Field (Entry→Deep), Ephemeral (Entry), Living (Entry), The Empty Throne
**Economy focus:** Catalyst Particles + Vital Essence → temporary buffs and creature bonds. The "consumables floor."
**Outside:** The Scaffolding Gate — Four-Squared returns. IO brokers a deal: Four-Squared holds the gate, IO holds the floor.
**Shadow:** The Coming Storm — the constant ephemeral resets and field spawns create a rhythm that something in the deep floors has started to follow.
