## PART III — DUNGEON INSTALLATION

### The Economy

- **Starting budget:** 50 points
- **Max slots:** 5 dungeons installed at once
- **Clearing a dungeon:** gives loot + refunds a % of its cost
- **Refunded points** go back into your budget to buy better dungeons
- **Loop:** cheap dungeons refund fast → build economy → swap in premium dungeons
