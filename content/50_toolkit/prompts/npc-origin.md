# Prompt — NPC Origin Questionnaire

Use these questions to develop a person — not a stat block, but a 
person. Someone who came from somewhere, wants something, and is 
running from something else.

---

## Origin

- What floor are they from? If they've moved, what floor are they on now?
- What was their childhood like in one sentence?
- What is the one thing about their origin they've never told anyone?
- What color do they associate with home, and is it the same color as 
  the floor they're from?

---

## The Want

- What do they want right now, in the immediate term?
- What do they want in the long term — the thing that drives everything?
- What do they think they want that is actually a substitute for 
  something they can't have?
- What would they trade everything for?

---

## The Wound

- What is the thing that happened to them that they haven't recovered from?
- How do they cope with it? (Suppress it? Channel it into work? Project it?)
- What triggers it?
- Who knows about it?

---

## The Mask

- What do most people think of this person on first impression?
- What do people who know them well think?
- What is true about them that almost no one sees?
- What lie do they tell themselves daily?

---

## The Relationships

- Who do they love? (And would they admit it?)
- Who do they fear? (And would they admit it?)
- Who do they owe?
- Who owes them?
- Who did they leave behind?

---

## The Floor Question

- How does their floor of origin show up in how they move through the 
  world? (Red: urgency, aggression, heat? Indigo: distance, 
  scholarship, coldness?)
- If they've been on their current floor long enough, has it changed 
  them? How?
- Is there a floor they refuse to go to? Why?
- Is there a floor they'd go to if they could? What are they looking for there?
