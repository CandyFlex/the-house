# Prompt — Culture Development Questionnaire

Use these questions to develop a culture — a people, a society, a 
way of life tied to a floor or spanning several.

---

## Identity

- What do members of this culture call themselves?
- What do outsiders call them? Is that name neutral, respectful, or a slur?
- What are they most proud of?
- What are they most ashamed of, and how do they hide it?
- What belief or practice makes them feel most distinct from everyone else?

---

## Everyday Life

- How do people spend a typical day?
- What do they eat, and what is the social significance of food?
- What are the markers of status? (Clothing, speech, possessions, behavior?)
- What is considered rude? What is considered generous?
- What do children learn first?

---

## The Cosmological View

- What does this culture believe about The House?
- Do they know they're inside a seven-floored structure?
- If yes — how do they relate to the other floors?
- If no — what do they think the world is?
- What do they believe about the mesophysical layer? Do they have a word for it?

---

## Death and Time

- What happens when someone dies, according to this culture?
- How is death grieved, marked, and remembered?
- How does this culture measure time? What is a "year" here?
- What is the oldest thing this culture venerates?
- What is the newest thing this culture has had to absorb, and how is 
  it being digested?

---

## Conflict and Resolution

- How does this culture handle internal conflict?
- How does it handle conflict with outsiders?
- What is the worst thing you can do to a member of this culture?
- What is the proper response to betrayal?
- What does this culture forgive that other cultures don't?

---

## Change

- What force is currently pulling this culture apart?
- What force is currently holding it together?
- What is the generation gap — what do young people believe that their 
  elders don't?
- What would have to happen for this culture to fundamentally change?
