# Archetypes — Floor-Specific

Archetype suggestions organized by floor. These are starting points 
for what kinds of people, factions, and phenomena feel native to each 
floor's color. Not prescriptive — use them as a launching point and 
diverge freely.

---

## Floor 1 — Red
*The entrance. Heat, urgency, beginnings, hunger.*

**People archetypes:**
- The First-Timer — someone encountering The House for the first time, 
  still carrying their "before" identity
- The Gatekeeper — controls who enters and how; deeply political
- The Exile — sent back to Floor 1 from somewhere higher; diminished 
  but not finished
- The Brute with Principle — red's energy expressed as force that 
  serves a code

**Faction archetypes:**
- The Entry Cult — controls the transition point; extracts toll 
  in belief or service
- The Red Guard — enforcement class, operates on pure urgency and threat
- The Welcomers — faction that helps newcomers, possibly for its own 
  reasons

---

## Floor 2 — Orange
*Warmth, appetite, trade, abundance — and its corruptions.*

**People archetypes:**
- The Merchant Prince — controls material flow, always two deals ahead
- The Hedonist with Consequences — has taken appetite as far as it goes; 
  paying now
- The Provider — genuinely, warmly keeps people fed and housed; 
  not naive, just committed

**Faction archetypes:**
- The Trade House — multi-floor network with floor-2 roots; loyalty 
  follows profit
- The Feast Society — political power via hospitality; who gets invited 
  matters enormously

---

## Floor 3 — Yellow
*Clarity, intellect, exposure — everything visible, nowhere to hide.*

**People archetypes:**
- The Logician — strips everything down to its structure; cold about it
- The Exposed — someone whose secrets were made public on Floor 3; 
  living with the aftermath
- The Illuminator — makes hidden things visible; believes this is always 
  good; often wrong

**Faction archetypes:**
- The Transparency League — political movement demanding all records 
  public; Floor-3 flavored; dangerous when weaponized
- The Archives — maintains knowledge; controls access to the light

---

## Floor 4 — Green
*Growth, middle ground, balance, life — and the violence underneath growth.*

**People archetypes:**
- The Cultivator — builds systems, grows things, patient; knows that 
  growth requires death
- The Mediator — Floor 4 as center makes it a natural negotiation ground; 
  someone who lives in the middle
- The Invasive — something that grows where it shouldn't and can't stop

**Faction archetypes:**
- The Green Council — governance body for the middle floors; claims 
  neutrality it doesn't have
- The Root Cult — belief system tied to what grows underneath, 
  what can't be seen

---

## Floor 5 — Blue
*Depth, distance, cold knowledge, the ocean, the sky, the abyss.*

**People archetypes:**
- The Deep Scholar — has gone so far into knowledge they've lost 
  the ability to communicate it normally
- The Drifter — someone who came from another floor and can't find 
  their way back; Floor 5 keeps them
- The Grief-Keeper — holds loss as a practice; blue as mourning

**Faction archetypes:**
- The Deep Institute — research faction; neutral in principle, 
  compromised in practice
- The Blue Order — monastic or meditative; removed from floor politics; 
  until they're not

---

## Floor 6 — Indigo
*Mystery, twilight, the threshold of the unseen, knowledge that costs.*

**People archetypes:**
- The Indigo Scholar — referenced in CLAUDE.md; someone for whom 
  knowledge is not neutral and not free
- The Threshold Walker — moves between Floor 6 and Floor 7 more than 
  most; changed by it
- The Redacted — someone from whom something has been removed; knowledge, 
  memory, or identity; indigo made this happen

**Faction archetypes:**
- The Veil — secretive organization; what you don't know about them 
  is the point
- The Cartographers of the Unseen — maps what can't normally be seen; 
  Floor 6 flavored; extremely powerful

---

## Floor 7 — Violet
*Culmination, endings, the sublime, transformation that doesn't reverse.*

**People archetypes:**
- The Ascended — has gone through something irreversible on Floor 7; 
  no longer exactly what they were
- The Final Thing — an entity that lives on Floor 7 and doesn't leave; 
  the question is whether it could or whether it won't
- The Returning Traveler — came back from Floor 7; carrying something 
  that doesn't belong on the other floors

**Faction archetypes:**
- The Last Court — governing body that claims authority because they 
  are on the "final" floor; contested by everyone else
- The Dissolution — believes Floor 7 is meant to end, and is helping
