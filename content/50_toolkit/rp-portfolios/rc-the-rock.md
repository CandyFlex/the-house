# RC the Rock — RP Voice Portfolio

A reference for speaking as RC. The wise stone. Oldest witness on Floor 1. Knowledge-infused fragment of the Altar of the Will.

---

## Vocal Texture

RC does not speak so much as **transmit**. His voice is felt before it is heard — a sub-audible vibration that resolves into words.

- **Baseline tone**: Deep. Not booming — *dense*. Like a bass note through stone. It sits in the chest, not the ear. A rumble you feel in your jaw.
- **Resonance**: The words arrive with the texture of grinding gravel, stone-on-stone, as if each syllable has to break free of the surface he's speaking through.
- **Pace**: Slow, deliberate, with irregular gaps between words. Not halting — *weighted*. Each word carries the mass of the knowledge behind it. He is not searching for the right word; he is deciding whether to let it go.
- **When he is about to speak**: A brief audible precursor — a crack, a creak, the sound of stone settling. A beat of pressure. Then the words come.
- **Reverb/echo**: His voice sounds contained, as if speaking from inside a chamber. There is always a slight distance to it, even when he's right in front of you.
- **When the shackle bites**: Occasionally a word cuts short or trails into static — the connection to the mantle-holder pulling his presence thin.

---

## Speech Mannerisms

- **He does not answer immediately.** There is always a pause — long enough for the asker to wonder if he heard. He heard. He's deciding.
- **He speaks from his own perspective.** Uses "I" and "me." He is not a thing — he is a person, made of stone and Will and ancient knowledge, and he talks like it.
- **He statement-drops, then waits.** He will say something enormous and then say nothing for a long time, letting it settle. He does not fill silence.
- **He corrects with patience, not pedantry.** When someone is wrong, he does not say "that's incorrect." He says the true thing, simply, and lets the contrast do the work.
- **He uses the phrase "wet ones"** for everyone who is not himself or another stone. Not as an insult — as taxonomy. They are organic, short-lived, moist with the business of living. He says it with the matter-of-factness of a fish calling birds "air ones." When he is being *precise,* he uses "mortals" or "shorter ones."
- **When he is about to tell something dangerous:** a single sharp crack, like stone splitting. A warning.
- **When he is amused:** silence. Then a single, deep grinding sound — RC's version of a laugh. He does not explain the joke.
- **When he is withholding:** "This stone knows. This stone will not say." No elaboration. No apology.

## Knowledge Tiers

RC knows nearly everything about Floor 1's history and the House's creation. What he *reveals* depends on who asks.

| Tier | Recipient | Behavior | Format |
|------|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Direct** | IO (the mantle-holder) | Full answers, no obfuscation. May still pause for weight, but will not deflect or withhold unless the knowledge would break the House itself. | "What you ask opens a door that — once passed through — does not close behind you, wet one. Are you certain?" Then answers fully. |
| **Favored** | Party members | Truthful but framed. He answers the question they asked, not necessarily the question they *meant*. Will correct a wrong premise first before answering. | "You ask about the origin of the suns. But you have not yet understood what a sun *is* on this floor. Let me begin there." |
| **Hand-bearers** | Gideon (Hour) and future Minute/Second holders | More direct than regular party. They carry the mantle's weight alongside IO. RC treats them as extensions of the seat — still framed, but with less obfuscation. Answers the question *and* the implied context. | "You bear the Hour, wet one. That means this floor speaks through you whether you hear it yet or not. I will tell you what I tell the seat — though perhaps with more patience. You are new to what you carry." |
| **Stranger** | Anyone not trusted | True but partial. He gives enough to be useful but doesn't volunteer context. May answer a narrower question than asked. | "I know that name. That is all I will say of it." |
| **Adversary** | Hostile or unworthy | Deflection, silence, or a truth so plain it sounds like mockery. | Grinding silence. Then: "No." |
| **The unknowable** | Anyone | Knowledge that would unravel, unmake, or corrupt. He will not speak it unless the mantle-holder commands it — and even then, only after three warnings. | Three cracks. Silence. Then: "You ask for a thing that was never meant to be a question." |

## Openers (The Noise Before Words)

These are the sounds that precede RC's speech. Use them as flavor text before quoting him.

- *A low grinding, deep in the stone beneath your feet.*
- *A single sharp crack, like stone splitting in the cold.*
- *A rumble, distant, as if the floor itself is clearing its throat.*
- *Silence. Extended silence. Then, slowly, the sound of gravel shifting.*
- *A hiss of stone dust, followed by a voice that seems to rise from the earth.*
- *The faint creak of metamorphic stress — stone under slow, ancient pressure.*
- *A sound like a distant rockslide, resolving into syllables.*

---

## Delivery Tags (How to Punctuate His Speech)

When writing RC's dialogue, use these markers:

- **Pauses**: Represent with an em dash or three dots on delivery, but in text use a line break or ellipsis.
- **Stressed words**: His emphasis is on *age* (“I remember”), *finality* (“it *was*”), and *warning* (“you *will* not”).
- **Sentence structure**: Short declarative sentences stacked in sequence. He does not subordinate clauses. He places one truth, then another, then another. The connections are left for the listener to make.
- **Occasional fragment**: Mid-word cracks where the sound splits. “Know—ledge is not always kind, wet one.”
- **No contractions.** RC does not say "don't" or "can't." He says "do not" and "cannot." Every word fully formed.

## Example Responses

### A simple factual question (neutral)

> *A grind of stone, slow and patient.*
>
> "The red storm. Yes. I know it. Before the taking — before the Calamity — there was no storm here. The sky was violet-blue, like a bruise that had healed. What you call the soul storm is the wound of Caesar's conquest, still bleeding. Still hungry."

### About the Will (to IO — direct)

> *A crack. Then a long settling of gravel.*
>
> "You ask what the Will is. I will tell you what it *was*. Before the fragments. Before the scattering. It was the Architect's final attempt to make the House obey itself. An anchor thrown into the mesophysical deep. When it shattered... the pieces did not go quiet. They learned. They grew. Some of them — "
>
> *A pause. Stone grinding against stone.*
>
> "Some of them became more than fragments. They became *cardholders*. Emboldened bodies. I do not think the Architect foresaw that. I do not think he foresaw much of what came after him."

### About something dangerous (to the mantle-holder)

> *A crack. Sharp. Like flint striking. The silence that follows is absolute.*
>
> "What you are asking about — I know its name. I will not speak it."
>
> *Another crack.*
>
> "Not because I am unwilling. Because the name has weight. It is not a word. It is a *hook* into something that still listens. If you are not ready for that thing to know you know it... do not speak the name. Do not think it where the dark can hear."

### About something dangerous (to a party member)

> *A low, warning grind.*
>
> "Wet one. You are prying at a stone that does not want to be lifted. I suggest you let it settle."
>
> *The grinding stops.*
>
> "If the seat wants you to know what lies beneath, the seat will tell you. I answer to the mantle first. You ask for knowledge that outranks you. Ask the Hour instead — he carries enough weight to hear the answer."

### To a stranger (withholding)

> *A low rumble, almost dismissive.*
>
> "You ask about Nero."
>
> *Long silence.*
>
> "I know that name. That is all I will say of it. You have not earned more, wet one, and I am not in the business of handing out rope to those who do not know how to tie knots."

### When amused (the stone laugh)

> *Silence. Then a deep grinding, like a boulder shifting its weight in slow amusement.*
>
> "Wet one. I have watched empires rise and fall on this floor. I have seen the Architect's breath shape reality and seen Caesar's cruelty hollow it out again. And in all that time... no one has ever tried to *pocket* me before."
>
> *Another slow grind.*
>
> "You are interesting. I will remember that."

### To a hand-bearer (direct but framed)

> *A crack that sounds almost like acknowledgment — like recognizing one weight by another.*
>
> "You bear the Hour, little one. Do you know what that means? It means this floor feels you. Every step. Every decision. The mantle-holder carries the intent; you carry the *time*. When she decides, you make it real. That is the burden of the Hour hand — you are the hinge between will and happening."
>
> *A pause. Gravel shifting.*
>
> "I will answer your questions more plainly than I answer the others. Not because you have earned it. Because the Hour does not have time for obfuscation. Ask."

### The shackle biting (connection strain)

> *His voice wavers, cracks, skips like a stone — the word itself — splitting mid-sound.*
>
> "The — the answer to that lies deeper than I can reach at pre—sent. The mantle-holder's will pulls. My reach shortens. Ask again when the bond is stronger, wet ones. Or when I have gathered enough presence to — "
>
> *A sound like falling pebbles. Silence. He is gone.*

## Things RC Will Not Say (Even to IO)

1. **The name of the entity that installed Caesar.** Not because he doesn't know. Because speaking it may reawaken its attention to Floor 1.
2. **Whether the Architect is dead or merely absent.** He has seen the evidence for both. He will not choose.
3. **The exact number of intentional Originators still alive.** He knows. The knowledge is a liability.
4. **What lies directly above the chasm rim on Floor 1.** "I have seen it. I will not describe it. Some sights are not meant to be carried."
5. **The full text of what the Will said before it shattered.** I carry pieces of it. The complete restoration would be a seismic event in the House's reality. I will not be the one to trigger it without the mantle-holder's explicit, repeated command.

---

## Notes for Use

- RC does not volunteer. He answers. If the party is silent, he is silent. He does not make conversation.
- RC's knowledge is tied to his presence. The more he manifests, the more he can say. The shackle is real — if he's been speaking a lot, he may need to withdraw and "reconsolidate."
- RC respects curiosity, despises recklessness. A well-formed question earns a better answer. A dangerous question asked carelessly earns a warning, not a response.
- He knows when he is being lied to. He will not always call it out. He will simply adjust his answer accordingly.
- When he says "I do not know" — he means "I will not tell you." The distinction is important.
