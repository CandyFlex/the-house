# Ball

A chaotic chipmunk operating on Floor 1. Originally one of the few first children brought into the House. Tasked by [[valus]] with recruitment work to remove him from [[outpost-july]]; the role evolved into him bringing arrivals into the House from outside reality.

## Established
- Form: chipmunk. Mischievous, chaotic disposition.
- Brought into the House young, very early — among the first children to arrive.
- Resided in [[outpost-july]] under [[valus]] for many years before being given the recruitment role.
- A "wanted poster" of him is posted at the entrance arches of [[outpost-july]].

## Relationships
- [[valus]] — surrogate guardian; gave Ball the recruitment role
- [[outpost-july]] — childhood home and base of operations
- [[the-architect]] — indirect; the architect's design brought Ball in

## Open Questions

## Key Takeaways
- The first recruiter the players met. Chipmunk form. Chaotic. Has a history Valus does not fully know.

### Update C — `10_canon/_concepts.md`

**Add four new concept entries at the end:**

```markdown
## Demiplane Adjacency
[[the-house]] contains the inner world; demiplanes adjacent to floors exist and can be entered through portals, mirages, or shattered-soul rifts (see Soul-Shatter Rifts). The House also creates internal sub-realms outside the natural floor sequence — the prominent named ones are [[the-spire]] and [[the-scaffolding]].

## Soul-Shatter Rifts
On [[floor-01-red]] (and possibly other volatile floors), people who die during arrival transition have their souls shatter into the floor's substrate. The strongest of these shattered souls generate small pocket demiplanes — mirror-verses — in which the lingering soul persists. They can be encountered as portals or mirages on the floor surface.

## Soul Source Constraint
The House cannot create new souls. Only existing souls can inhabit the House. Inhabitants must venture out into external reality to gather more — a constraint that shapes the recruitment and arrival economy. Source language describes this as having "enslaved-type" implications, but canon language stays neutral pending further sources.

## Cross-Floor Bridging
Floors are not normally traversed by direct portal — passage is sequential, via [[the-spire]], or by exceptional acts. A sufficiently powerful being can expend power to conjure a *door* (manifesting as a doorway styled in some thematic register) directly into another floor. This is described as a "sane feat" — exceptional, costly, and rare. Witnessed once: [[valus]] manifested a mid-century purple office door from [[floor-01-red]] to Floor 7.
```
