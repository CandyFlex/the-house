## Contradictions Flagged

### 1. "Unintentional Realms" vs. "No Outside" — REFINED, NOT CONTRADICTED

**Canon (current):** `_concepts.md` states: "No external space to the House has ever been observed. Treat references to 'outside' as internal manifestations unless explicitly flagged as a cosmological question."

**Transcript + Verbal Correction:** Outpost August is "the only way to look outside of the House to what are considered the Unintentional Realms." The verbal session confirmed: "no external space within the House's ordered reality has been observed — but the Unintentional Realms are the actual outside."

**Assessment:** This is not a contradiction — it is a refinement. The "no outside" principle holds for everything within the House's 7-floor Architect-codified reality. The Unintentional Realms exist outside that ordered reality entirely. The principle needs to be updated to include this nuance:

> *"No external space within the House's ordered reality has been observed. The Unintentional Realms exist outside this ordered reality entirely — they are the actual outside. References to 'outside' within the House's context should be treated as internal manifestations unless flagged as cosmological."*

### 2. Caesar's spellcaster nature vs. Originator nature

**Canon (current):** Caesar is the Originator of Cruelty — "not merely cruel, but the pure concept of cruelty achieving sentience and form." Android body, never confirmed as human.

**Transcript:** Caesar "used to be actually a very powerful spellcaster who then took the mantle of cruelty." Verbal correction confirms: Caesar was an unintentional Originator who was installed as mantle-holder by an intentional Originator.

**Assessment:** The transcript implies a before-and-after transition — Caesar existed as a spellcaster first, then took on the mantle of cruelty. The verbal session confirms this model. This means:
- Originators can be "created" from existing beings who take on a mantle (rather than only forming spontaneously from concepts).
- Caesar's Originator nature is an acquired mantle, not an innate identity.
- This conflicts with the current understanding that Originators are concepts made sentient from the start.

**Recommendation:** Flag for Jarred. The transition model (spellcaster -> took mantle of cruelty -> became Originator) should be reconciled with the concept-made-sentient model.

### 3. Caesar's 12 sons — suns vs. distinct entities

**Canon (current):** "Twelve sons — manifestations of malignant power — serve as solar heralds. The hands come from the bodies of the dead suns (Caesar's 12 sons)."

**Transcript introduces ambiguity:** The suns are described both as Caesar's sons/heralds AND as carrying the hands (hour/minute/second). It is unclear if all 12 sons carried hand pieces, or if only 3 held the hour/minute/second hands while the other 9 were something else.

**Assessment:** Clarify with Jarred. Not a direct contradiction but a missing detail.
