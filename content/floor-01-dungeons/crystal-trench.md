# Hex 1 — Crystal Trench

*Easy (6 pts). A frozen chasm of blue-white geode crystal — entrance, depths, and heart chamber.*

## Dungeon

A yawning chasm in a frozen forest floor, jagged ice formations climbing the rim. The depths are a vast underground ravine walled in raw crystal, blue-white shards jutting from every surface like teeth, frozen mist hugging the ground. At the heart: a cathedral-like chamber of solid crystal with a massive central geode cracked open, blue light spilling in hard geometric beams.
