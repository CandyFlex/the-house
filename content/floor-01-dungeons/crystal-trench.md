# Crystal Trench

*Hex 1. Easy (6 pts). Three room nodes → Boss. Active daily. Clean status.*

---

## Brief

A frozen chasm carved through a forgotten forest floor on Floor 1. The public sees a yawning sinkhole ringed in hoarfrost, blue-white light pulsing from deep within. Inside, a pocket realm of raw geode crystal: jagged shards jutting from every surface, fractured light bending through a thousand angled faces, a cathedral-like heart chamber where a massive cracked geode spills cold light in hard geometric beams. The crystals are alive — not sentient, but resonant. They remember every delver who has entered. They sing a frequency just below hearing.

**Style ideology:** Cold geometry. The Trench is order imposed on wilderness — the Architect's instinct to codify, frozen solid. Every surface is faceted. Every light source is refracted. The beauty is mathematical. The danger is that you will stand still too long and become part of the pattern.

---

## Public Façade

A yawning chasm in a frozen forest clearing. The ground around it is bare — no plants grow within thirty feet of the rim. Jagged ice formations climb the edges like reaching fingers. A tunnel mouth at the bottom of the ravine is crusted in hoarfrost, and a faint blue-white glow pulses from deep within, visible even at noon. The wind through the gap produces a low, sustained note — almost a chord. Locals call it the Trench's welcome. Delvers call it the first warning.

The entrance is always open. Daily cycle. Resets at midnight.

---

## Admin Sub-Entrance

Two hundred yards east of the public chasm, behind a collapsed watchtower from the old imperial occupation, a maintenance shaft descends straight into the earth. It is a spiral staircase carved from the same blue-white geode that lines the dungeon — but here the crystal is dark, dormant, waiting. A narrow slot at the top accepts an admin token (any attuned item from the Trench's pool). The staircase illuminates step by step as you descend, shedding cold light only where you are about to place your foot.

The shaft deposits you into the **Interstitial Layer** — the liminal space between the dungeon's pocket realm and Floor 1's reality. Here, the dungeon is transparent. You can see the rooms below you through walls of what appears to be thick ice: the entrance crevasse, the refracting gallery, the frozen vault, the heart chamber. Each room has a maintenance hatch in its ceiling — a hexagonal plate of darker crystal that opens only from this side. You can drop into any room from above, or traverse the interstitial walkways that connect them.

**Admin challenges:**
- The interstitial layer is painfully cold. Every 10 minutes spent here without cold resistance forces a DC 13 Constitution save or gain one level of exhaustion.
- The crystals resonate with your presence. The longer you stay, the more the dungeon notices you. After 30 cumulative minutes, the Geode Heart Sentinel in the boss chamber becomes aware of your admin presence and enters a heightened state (adds +2 to all rolls, gains truesight 60 ft).
- Congealing frost on the walkways makes them slick. Movement requires DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or fall through to the room below — which means you enter the room the public way, without admin advantage.

## Room Nodes

### Node 1 — The Entrance Crevasse

**Public experience:** Delvers descend a frozen tunnel, the walls narrowing as they go. The floor is shattered crystal — loose shards that crunch underfoot and echo for hundreds of feet. Ahead, three paths diverge, each marked by a different color of refracted light: blue (left), white (center), violet (right). The blue path is the shortest but the narrowest. The white path is straightforward but patrolled by two **Crystalline Constructs** (use animated armor stats, add cold immunity and vulnerability to thunder). The violet path is the longest but quietest.

**Room challenge:** Navigate without drawing attention. Stealth DC 13 to cross without waking the Constructs. Combat if failed.

**Admin options:**
- *From the interstitial walkway above:* You can see all three paths simultaneously. The constructs' patrol routes are visible as pulsing nodes on the dungeon's "skeleton" — a map of crystal veins in the walls.
- *Maintenance hatch:* Drop into the blue path's midpoint to bypass the Constructs entirely.
- *Control node (blue path, halfway):* A small crystal panel set in the wall. Interfacing with it lets you adjust the Constructs' patrol speed (slower = easier public runs, faster = harder), disable them entirely for 1 hour, or increase the stealth DC to 17 (training mode for advanced delvers).
- *Corruption check:* Clean. No anomalies detected.

**Possible items:** Frostward Shard (hidden behind an ice wall on the violet path, visible from the interstitial layer).

---

### Node 2 — The Refracting Gallery

**Public experience:** A long corridor whose walls are angled crystal mirrors, floor to ceiling, a hundred facets each. The light from the entrance refracts through them in tight, disciplined beams. The delver's own reflection does not match their movements — it lags by half a second, then a full second, then stops mirroring entirely and begins moving on its own. Three **Mirror Shards** (use specter stats, add vulnerability to thunder and immunity to cold) attack from within the reflections, stepping out of the crystal surfaces to strike and retreating back inside before retaliation.

**Room challenge:** Combat with creatures that attack from cover. Each Mirror Shard has 3/4 cover (+5 AC) while inside the crystal. To expose them, a delver must shatter a mirror panel (AC 13, 10 HP, thunder damage destroys it instantly). Shattering a panel releases the Shard but also destroys whatever item might have been hidden behind the mirror.

**Choice:** Shatter the mirrors (guaranteed combat win, lose bonus items) or solve the reflection puzzle (DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) to identify which reflections are Shards and which are harmless, then attack the real ones). Solving the puzzle without breaking any mirrors reveals a hidden alcove containing the Frostveil Lens.

**Admin options:**
- *From the interstitial walkway above:* You can see which reflections are Shards (they don't have reflections in the interstitial layer — they appear as dark humanoid voids in the crystal).
- *Maintenance hatch:* Drops you behind the mirror panels, in the service gap between the mirror backs and the dungeon wall. The Shards cannot reach you here.
- *Control node (behind panel 7):* Adjust the Shard count (1–5), toggle the reflection lag (adds disadvantage on attacks for public delvers), or activate "training mirrors" (Shards deal psychic instead of necrotic, non-lethal).
- *Corruption check:* Minor resonance echo. The Shards sometimes whisper fragments of previous delvers' conversations. Harmless but unsettling. Note for later investigation.

**Possible items:** Frostveil Lens (hidden alcove, puzzle reward), Prism Weave Cloak (behind panel 4, accessible from admin service gap).

---

### Node 3 — The Frozen Vault

**Public experience:** A circular chamber, its walls lined with alcoves. In each alcove, a frozen adventurer from a previous dungeon cycle — bodies preserved in crystal, faces frozen mid-expression, their equipment still visible through the ice. A cold-wight (use wight stats, add cold immunity, replace life drain with *freezing touch*: DC 13 CON or paralyzed 1 minute) patrols the chamber, shattering and re-freezing the bodies in an endless loop. It was once a delver who stayed too long in the heart chamber. Now it tends the vault.

**Room challenge:** The cold-wight attacks any creature that enters. Looting a frozen body (bonus action, DC 12 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) to extract an item without waking the wight if it hasn't been aggroed) can yield gold, minor magic items, or Soul Stone fragments. But every body looted increases the wight's power — it gains +1 AC and +5 HP for each disturbed corpse.

**Choice:** Loot for resources (risk empowering the boss) or fight clean. The wight drops Rimespire on death regardless.

**Admin options:**
- *From the interstitial walkway:* You can see the frozen bodies' contents rendered as faintly glowing item icons. Prioritize which corpses to recommend looting.
- *Maintenance hatch:* Drops into the chamber's center, from the ceiling. If you drop silently (DC 12 Stealth from the interstitial layer), you land behind the wight's patrol route.
- *Control node (north wall, behind the largest frozen body):* Purge the frozen bodies (resets the vault to empty for the next cycle), freeze the wight in place for 10 minutes (admin override), or adjust the loot quality in the corpses (common/uncommon/rare — affects what public delvers find).
- *Corruption check:* One frozen body in the southwest alcove does not match any delver record. It predates the dungeon's creation. Its face is indistinct, but its hand is raised, pointing toward the heart chamber. Do not thaw. Flag for IO review.

**Possible items:** Rimespire (wight drop), Geode Fang (frozen body in northeast alcove), Geode Band (frozen body in southeast alcove, requires DC 15 Sleight of Hand), Crystal Fracture Bow (encased in the ceiling ice, visible from the interstitial layer — requires admin intervention to release).

---

### Boss Chamber — The Heart Chamber

**Public experience:** A cathedral-like space of solid crystal. At its center, a massive geode — twelve feet tall, cracked open, spilling blue-white light in hard geometric beams that paint the walls in infinite recursive patterns. The **Geode Heart Sentinel** (use stone golem stats, add cold immunity, replace Slow with *Crystal Prison*: DC 15 CON save or restrained by crystal growths, repeat save at end of turn) stands before the geode, its body a shifting lattice of crystal shards that reconfigure with every blow it takes. The Sentinel does not speak. It hums — the same chord the wind makes at the entrance. When it is defeated, the geode cracks fully open, revealing the treasure room within.

**Boss mechanics:**
- **Phase 1 (above 50% HP):** The Sentinel attacks with crystal slam (3d8+5 bludgeoning + 2d8 cold). It can use *Shard Volley* (recharge 5-6): launches crystal shards at up to 3 targets within 60 ft (4d6 piercing, DC 15 DEX half).
- **Phase 2 (below 50% HP):** The Sentinel's lattice destabilizes. It gains *Crystal Prison* as a legendary action (1/round) and its attacks create 5-ft patches of crystalline difficult terrain.
- **Treasure room:** The geode interior is a small hexagonal chamber, walls lined with crystal growths that pulse gently. Items float in the center, suspended in a column of cold light.

**Admin options:**
- *Interstitial layer:* The boss chamber's ceiling has a large maintenance hatch directly above the geode. From here, you can observe the fight and intervene.
- *Admin override (crystal panel on the geode's back face, accessible only from the interstitial service ring):*
  - **Surge the Sentinel:** Adds +3 to all its rolls for one fight (for testing high-tier delvers).
  - **Pacify:** Freezes the Sentinel for 30 seconds — enough time to extract an admin trapped in the chamber or adjust the treasure pool mid-fight.
  - **Phase Skip:** Forces the Sentinel directly to Phase 2. Useful when delvers are struggling and you want to check if they can handle the pressure.
  - **Emergency Shutdown:** Collapses the pocket realm immediately, ejecting all delvers to the entrance. Use only if the dungeon is corrupted or a delver is about to die permanently.
- *Corruption check:* The geode's light shows faint interference patterns — ripples in the blue-white that shouldn't be there. Ancient residue, possibly. The frozen body in the vault was pointing here. Monitor.

**Boss drop items (random 1–2 from pool):** Geode Heart, Geode Aegis, Geode Chrysalis Helm, Rime-Kissed Plate.

- **Boss Chamber — Light Interference:** Geode light shows faint ripple patterns inconsistent with normal crystal resonance. May relate to the anomalous body. **Do not thaw the body. Flagged for IO.**
