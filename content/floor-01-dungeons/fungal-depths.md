# Fungal Depths

*Hex 2. Easy (6 pts). Three room nodes → Boss. Active daily. Light corruption detected.*

---

## Brief

A sinkhole in the forest floor, tangled with ancient roots, exhaling warm damp air that smells of sweet rot and wet stone. The depths below are a sprawling cavern ruled by fungus — shelf mushrooms the size of buildings, veins of bioluminescent purple and toxic green pulsing through every surface, spores drifting in lazy currents. At the heart, a colossal mushroom with a cracked cap rains golden spores into a garden of impossible density. The mycelium network beneath your feet is alive and aware. It does not think — but it remembers.

**Style ideology:** Decay as renewal. The Depths are the counterpoint to the Crystal Trench — where the Trench is frozen order, the Depths are warm entropy. Everything here is in a state of becoming something else. The mushrooms consume the dead and bloom from their bones. The spores carry memory. The air itself is thick with transformation. This is the old world's cruelty decomposing — the rot the old order left behind, now turning into soil.

---

## Public Façade

A gnarled root-tangled sinkhole in the forest floor, its edges softened by moss and shelf fungi. Bioluminescent spores drift upward like embers from a dying fire, visible even in daylight. The shaft descends into warm, damp darkness. Mushrooms glowing sickly purple and teal cluster around the rim, and the smell — sweet rot, wet earth, something almost floral underneath — rises in gentle waves. Locals leave offerings at the rim: bread, flowers, small carvings. The fungus accepts everything. Nothing is ever found where it was left.

Daily cycle. Resets at midnight. Weekly phase-lock: closed on Mournsday (the fungus rests).

---

## Admin Sub-Entrance

One hundred fifty yards north of the public sinkhole, a collapsed vent hidden beneath a fallen oak. The vent was part of an old imperial geothermal survey — abandoned when the survey team never returned. A root-ladder descends through the vent shaft: thick, woody fungal growths that form handholds and footholds, pulsing faintly violet. The roots respond to touch, tightening their grip when you step, loosening when you reach for the next rung.

The ladder deposits you into the **Interstitial Layer** — a narrow, humid crawlspace between the dungeon's pocket realm and the floor's reality. Here, the walls are not stone but compressed mycelium, white and fibrous, shot through with veins of bioluminescent fluid. You can see the rooms below through translucent fungal membranes: the spore-choked entrance, the shelf-fungus terraces, the rot garden, the colossal mushroom. Maintenance hatches — circular openings ringed in a sphincter of pale fungus — are positioned in the ceiling of each room. They open when touched with an attuned item from the Depths' pool.

**Admin challenges:**
- The air in the interstitial layer is thick with spores. Every 15 minutes spent here without poison resistance forces a DC 13 Constitution save or gain the poisoned condition for 1 hour.
- The mycelium network is mildly aware. It does not attack, but it shifts. Passageways you used on the way in may not be in the same configuration on the way out (DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) to navigate back).
- **Corruption note:** The mycelium has begun growing into the interstitial layer in places — pale tendrils that brush against your skin as you pass. They are not hostile, but they are curious. The dungeon is watching you more actively than it should.

## Room Nodes

### Node 1 — The Spore-Choked Descent

**Public experience:** The sinkhole opens into a vertical shaft, its walls lined with glowing fungi. The descent is a controlled slide down a slope of packed mycelium — soft enough to land on, steep enough that you cannot climb back. At the bottom, a wide chamber: the air is a haze of drifting spores, visibility reduced to 20 feet. Three **Spore Husks** (use zombie stats, add: explode on death in a 10-ft spore cloud dealing 2d6 poison, DC 12 CON half) shamble through the haze. They are former delvers, their bodies colonized by fungus, their eyes replaced with glowing purple caps.

**Room challenge:** Combat in low visibility. The Husks are slow but explode on death, creating overlapping hazard zones. Delvers must manage positioning or take stacking poison damage.

**Admin options:**
- *From the interstitial crawlspace:* The Husks appear as bright violet nodes on the mycelial map — their fungal colonies broadcast their position clearly.
- *Maintenance hatch:* Drops you behind a large shelf mushroom at the chamber's rear, out of the Husks' patrol zone.
- *Control node (east wall, a raised fungal node that pulses when touched):*
  - Adjust spore density (visibility 10 ft / 20 ft / 40 ft).
  - Toggle Husk explosion (enable/disable the death burst for training).
  - Purge Husks (releases a fungicide mist that dissolves all Husks — resets the room for the next cycle).
- *Corruption check:* One Husk does not match the patrol pattern. It stands still in the chamber's northwest corner, facing the wall. It has been there for three cycles. It does not respond to delvers. The mycelium will not reclaim it. Flag for extraction.

**Possible items:** Sporewalker's Cloak (tucked behind a fungal growth on the west wall), Glowcap Lantern (held by the anomalous Husk — retrieval requires admin intervention or defeating it in combat), Sporepuff Slippers (buried in a soft patch of puffball mycelium beside the west wall), Mycelial Messenger (clutched in the grip of a dead delver half-absorbed into the fungal floor — its last message still inside).

### Node 2 — The Shelf-Fungus Terraces

**Public experience:** A vast cavern whose ceiling is lost in darkness. Massive shelf mushrooms — each the size of a building — grow in stepped tiers, creating a natural staircase that zigzags upward. The terraces are patrolled by **Mycelial Drones** (use giant spider stats, replace web with *spore web*: ranged attack, DC 13 DEX or restrained, 2d4 poison/round). The Drones cultivate the shelves, tending fungal gardens that produce the dungeon's spore supply.

**Room challenge:** The terraces are unstable. Each shelf has HP (20) and collapses if it takes more than 10 thunder damage or if a creature of Large size or larger lands on it. The fall is 40 feet to the cavern floor — into a pool of glowing green slime mold that deals 2d6 acid damage per round. Crossing requires careful movement across 4 terraces.

**Choice:** Fight the Drones (risk collapsing shelves, losing access to higher terraces and their items) or sneak past (DC 14 Stealth — the Drones sense vibration through the mycelium, not sight).

**Admin options:**
- *From the interstitial crawlspace:* The terraces are visible from above as translucent platforms. You can see which shelves are weakened (shown as dim orange on the mycelial map) before delvers step on them.
- *Maintenance hatch:* Drops onto the highest terrace, above the Drones' patrol routes.
- *Control node (top terrace, inside a hollow mushroom stalk):*
  - Reinforce shelves (doubles their HP for the cycle).
  - Weaken shelves (halves HP — for hard-mode runs).
  - Adjust Drone count (1–4).
  - Activate emergency webbing (covers the slime pool below, removing fall damage — safety override).
- *Corruption check:* The third terrace shows abnormal mycelial growth — the fungus is forming patterns. Circles within circles. A spiral. The mycelium is writing something. The text is not in any known language but the shape is recognizable: a map. Of what? Unknown. Do not disturb the growth. Document and flag.

**Possible items:** Mycelial Net (woven into a Drone nest on terrace 2), Sporewood Longbow (embedded in the hollow mushroom stalk at the top terrace), Chitin-Bark Carapace (worn by a dead delver collapsed on terrace 3, partially absorbed by the spiral-pattern mycelium), Fungal Spore-Launcher (grown into a giant shelf mushroom on terrace 2 — the stalk has formed around the stock, must be cut free), Root-Grip Greaves (worn by the corpse of a Sovereign's guard collapsed against the back wall of terrace 4 — centuries old, still anchored), Seedling Satchel (tucked inside a hollow mushroom stalk on the lowest terrace, still producing seedlings).

### Node 3 — The Rot Garden

**Public experience:** A chamber of impossible density. Fungal stalks thicker than trees rise from a floor that squelches with every step — living mycelium, soft and warm, pulsing faintly. Caps drip phosphorescent fluid in slow, hypnotic rhythms. At the chamber's center, a clearing. In the clearing, a single figure: the **Rotwarden** (use druid stats, CR 4, add: can cast *entangle* at will as fungal tendrils, *stinking cloud* 1/day, *blight* 1/day). It was once a delver who chose to stay. The fungus accepted the offer. Now it tends the garden, speaking to the mushrooms in a language of wet clicks and exhales, answering questions no one asked.

**Room challenge:** The Rotwarden is not immediately hostile. It speaks — in Common, haltingly, as though remembering how words work. It asks each delver a single question: *"What did you bury that you should have burned?"* The question is different for each person. The Rotwarden knows things it should not know. If the delver answers truthfully, the Rotwarden steps aside and allows passage to the boss chamber. If they lie, or refuse to answer, the Rotwarden attacks — and the garden attacks with it (3 **Fungal Stalkers** — use vine blight stats, add poison immunity — animate from the walls).

**Choice:** This is not a combat encounter unless you make it one. The Rotwarden is a test of honesty, not strength. The items in this room are in the garden around the clearing — looting them while the Rotwarden watches requires DC 16 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) or negotiating permission.

**Admin options:**
- *From the interstitial crawlspace:* The Rotwarden is visible as a pulsing golden node — distinct from all other fungal signatures. It is the only entity in the Depths that registers as *friendly* on the admin interface.
- *Maintenance hatch:* Drops you directly into the clearing, beside the Rotwarden. It acknowledges you with a slow nod. It knows you are admin. It has been waiting to speak with you.
- *Admin dialogue:* The Rotwarden will tell you what it has observed — which delvers lied, which items the garden has absorbed, whether the mycelium is growing in a direction it shouldn't. It speaks in fragments. It does not always make sense. But it is never wrong.
- *Control node (the Rotwarden itself — touch the fungal growth on its chest):*
  - Adjust the question (cycle through three variants: *"What did you bury?"* / *"What did you leave behind?"* / *"What are you still carrying?"*).
  - Reseed the garden (replenishes loot items absorbed by the mycelium).
  - Dismiss the Rotwarden (sends it into the mycelial network for 24 hours — the room becomes a standard combat encounter against the Stalkers).
- *Corruption check:* The mycelium here is deeply integrated with the Rotwarden's consciousness. The Rotwarden reports dreams — visions of a room that does not exist in this dungeon, a room made of glass. It draws the same spiral pattern seen on terrace 3. The mycelium is trying to communicate something across dungeons. This should not be possible.

**Possible items:** Decayer's Gauntlets (grown into a fungal stalk — requires the Rotwarden's permission to harvest), Fungi-Touched Staff (embedded in the mycelial floor at the clearing's edge), Ring of Rot and Renewal (worn by the Rotwarden — it will offer the ring as a gift if the admin treats it with respect), Rot-Tender's Sickle (held by the Rotwarden — offered to admins who demonstrate a commitment to the cycle of death and renewal), Marrow-Mesh Gambeson (worn by the Rotwarden beneath its fungal robe — gifted if the admin answers its question truthfully and treats the garden with care).

---

### Boss Chamber — The Spore-Sovereign's Throne

**Public experience:** The garden opens into a cathedral of fungus. A single colossal mushroom — its stalk thirty feet wide, its cap lost in the darkness above — dominates the space. The cap is cracked, and from the crack rains a continuous fall of golden spores, pooling on the floor in drifts that shift like sand in wind. Before the stalk, seated on a throne of interlocking shelf fungi, waits the **Spore-Sovereign** (use **treant** stats, add: immunity to poison, vulnerability to fire, can cast *cloudkill* once per fight as a lair action on initiative 20, its slam attacks deal an additional 2d6 poison). It is ancient, patient, and entirely aware that you are not a delver.

**Boss mechanics:**
- **Phase 1 (above 50% HP):** The Sovereign attacks with fungal slam (3d6+6 bludgeoning + 2d6 poison) and *Spore Wave* (recharge 5-6): a 30-ft cone of golden spores dealing 4d8 poison, DC 15 CON half.
- **Phase 2 (below 50% HP):** The Sovereign roots itself into the mycelium. Its speed becomes 0 but it regenerates 10 HP per round and summons 1d4 **Spore Husks** (from Node 1) at the start of each turn. The golden spore rain intensifies — all non-fungal creatures in the chamber take 1d4 poison damage at the start of their turns.
- **Lair action (always active, initiative 20):** The Sovereign can: (a) cast *entangle* (fungal tendrils) on one target, (b) move the golden spore drift 15 feet in any direction (creatures starting their turn in the drift take 2d6 poison), or (c) cause a shelf fungus to collapse from the ceiling (DC 14 DEX or 3d6 bludgeoning).
- **Treasure room:** When the Sovereign is defeated, the colossal mushroom's cap fully opens, revealing a hollow chamber within the stalk. Inside, items are suspended in golden amber, slowly rotating in the spore-light.

**Admin options:**
- *Interstitial layer:* The boss chamber's ceiling has no maintenance hatch — instead, a spiral of mycelial growth descends from the crawlspace into the stalk itself. You can enter the boss chamber from inside the colossal mushroom, emerging from a split in the stalk behind the throne.
- *Admin override (the throne itself is the control node — touch the left armrest):*
  - **Soothe the Sovereign:** Calms it for 1 minute. It will speak with you rather than fight. It knows things about the mycelial network that extend beyond this dungeon. Ask it about the spiral pattern.
  - **Spore Surge:** Intensifies all poison effects in the chamber (adds +1d6 to all poison damage, increases save DCs by 2). Hard mode.
  - **Hasten Collapse:** The Sovereign enters Phase 2 immediately at 25% HP rather than 50%. For speed-running parties.
  - **Emergency Harvest:** Forces the treasure room to open without defeating the Sovereign. The Sovereign remains alive and hostile. The items inside are random (no admin selection). This is an extraction, not a victory.
- *Admin dialogue:* The Sovereign speaks in complete sentences, unlike the Rotwarden. It is old — perhaps older than the dungeon. It refers to IO by name and speaks of the old empire with what might be contempt. It knows the spiral pattern appears in both this dungeon and the Crystal Trench. It will not explain what it means. *"Some things grow toward each other. That is enough."*

**Boss drop items (random 2–3 from pool):** Heart of the Mycelium, Crown of the Spore Sovereign, Scepter of Spore Sovereignty.

**Non-drop boss room items:** Pallid Spore Bulwark (growing from the side of the throne like a shelf fungus — can be carved free), Bloom of the Wilt-King (resting in a hollow in the colossal mushroom's stalk base, visible as a faint bruise-purple glow — only blooms if a creature has died in the chamber within the last hour).

## Corruption Log

- **Node 1 — Anomalous Husk:** Single Spore Husk in northwest corner, non-responsive for 3 cycles. Mycelium will not reclaim it. **Flagged for extraction.**
- **Node 2 — Spiral Growth:** Terrace 3 mycelium forming patterned spirals. Appears to be writing or mapping. Shape matches a pattern reported in Crystal Trench Node 2. **Cross-dungeon anomaly. Do not disturb. Document.**
- **Node 3 — Glass Dreams:** Rotwarden reports visions of a room made of glass. Draws the same spiral. Mycelial network appears to be forming connections to other dungeons. This should be mechanically impossible — pocket realms are isolated demiplanes. **Escalate to IO.**
- **Boss Chamber — Sovereign's Knowledge:** The Spore-Sovereign is aware of other dungeons. It references "the frozen one" (Crystal Trench) and "the one of glass that is coming" (Glass Cathedral). It knows things it should not. The mycelial network may be acting as a bridge between pocket realms.
