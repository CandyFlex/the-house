# Hex 2 — Fungal Depths

*Easy (6 pts). A root-tangled sinkhole descending into a bioluminescent fungal cavern — entrance, depths, and heart chamber.*

## Dungeon

A gnarled root-tangled sinkhole in the forest floor, bioluminescent spores drifting upward like embers. The depths are a sprawling subterranean cavern with ceiling lost in darkness, massive shelf mushrooms the size of buildings growing in stepped tiers, glowing fungal veins pulsing slow purple and toxic green through every surface. At the heart: a fungal garden of impossible density, a single colossal mushroom towering with a cracked cap raining golden spores, the air thick and hazy with floating light.
