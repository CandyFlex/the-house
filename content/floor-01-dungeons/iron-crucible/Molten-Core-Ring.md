# Molten Core Ring
*Ring, uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A band of dark, pitted iron that looks as though it was pulled still-glowing from the slag pits and quenched directly onto a finger. At the ring's center sits a tiny bead of captive magma — a sphere no larger than a lentil that churns perpetually with orange-gold light, shedding a gentle warmth into the wearer's hand. Iron Crucible smiths crafted these rings as protective charms for apprentices working dangerously close to the pour-spouts, and this one was recovered from a soot-caked tool rack in the casting chamber.

## Properties

**Ember Ward.** While wearing this ring, whenever you take fire damage you can use your reaction to reduce that damage by 2d6. Once you use this property, it cannot be used again until the next dawn.

**Forgeblood Warmth.** You have a minor resistance to fire. Whenever you take fire damage, reduce that damage by an amount equal to your proficiency bonus (after all other reductions).

---

## Balance Notes
Priced at 35,000 soul stones — the upper end of Uncommon — this ring essentially bundles a fire-only *absorb elements* (1st-level spell, 1/day) with a permanent damage-reduction rider that scales with level. The constant PB reduction is deliberately modest (2–6 points) so the ring remains Uncommon rather than Rare; a full Ring of Fire Resistance would cost significantly more. The one-two punch of constant mitigation plus a stronger reaction-based dodge makes this feel active and tactical without dominating encounters.

---

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Ember Band:** Common variant. No attunement. Only the Forgeblood Warmth property (fire damage reduced by PB). No reaction ability. Cost: ~12,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Volcanic Heart Ring:** Rare variant. Grants full fire resistance. The reaction reduces fire damage by 4d6 and also deals 2d6 fire to the damage's source. Usable twice per long rest. Cost: ~320,000 soul stones.
