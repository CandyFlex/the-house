# Slag-Walker Breastplate
*Armor (breastplate), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This breastplate is forged from slag-crust — the dense, glassy skin that forms on the surface of a cooling slag pan, harvested in thin sheets and then layered, heated, and hammered until it fuses into a single piece harder than wrought iron. The surface is an obsidian swirl of dark green, black, and rust-orange, feather-light for its strength and perpetually warm to the touch. The lower edges of the breastplate are scorched and dripping, as though the metal is always on the verge of melting but never quite does. Found embedded in the wall of a slag tunnel beneath the Crucible, where a failed escape attempt ended with the previous wearer fused into the cooling waste — only the breastplate survived intact.

## Properties

**Slag-Forged.** You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while wearing this armor.

**Slag-Walker's Stride.** You ignore difficult terrain created by heat, fire, magma, or molten metal. You can walk on the surface of molten metal — including lava, molten iron, and similar substances — as though it were solid ground, and you take no damage from contact with such surfaces.

**Crucible Stomp.** As an action, you can stomp the ground to create a molten fissure that erupts in a 15-foot line originating from you. Each creature in the line must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a success. The fissure remains as difficult terrain until cleared. Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

---

## Balance Notes
At 400,000 soul stones — the top end of Rare — this breastplate earns its price through unique environmental utility with a respectable once-per-day damage option. The lava-walking ability is situationally game-changing (the Crucible itself is full of molten metal hazards) but offers zero combat benefit against most enemies. Crucible Stomp's 3d6 in a 15-foot line is comparable to a 3rd-level *lightning bolt* (8d6) with a much smaller area, keeping it from outshining pure-damage spells. The combination of +1 AC, terrain immunity, and a modest AoE makes this ideal for the party's forward scout or tank who draws enemies across hazardous ground.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Slag-Tread Boots:** Uncommon variant. No attunement. Only Slag-Walker's Stride — no +1 AC, no Crucible Stomp. Cost: ~25,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Magma-Walker Plate:** Very Rare variant. +2 armor. Ignore difficult terrain from any source (not just fire/heat). Lava-walking extends to the wearer and up to 5 allies within 10 feet. Crucible Stomp creates a 30-foot line and deals 6d6 fire. Cost: ~3,000,000 soul stones.
