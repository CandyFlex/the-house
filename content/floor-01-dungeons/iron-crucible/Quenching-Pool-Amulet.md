# Quenching Pool Amulet
*Wondrous item (amulet), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A teardrop-shaped phial of unbreakable glass hangs from a simple iron chain — inside the phial, a small volume of liquid swirls in perpetual, slow motion, the color shifting from midnight-blue to silver-white and back again. This is a preserved sample from the Crucible's deepest quenching pool, the very trough where the greatest blades of the forge-city were plunged to temper their edges. The glass is cold to the touch, perpetually cooler than the surrounding air, and when a flame erupts nearby the liquid inside stirs faster — as though yearning to quench it. The amulet was retrieved from a drowned body at the bottom of the pool itself, the chain tangled around the skeleton's neck.

## Properties

**Quenching Ward.** This amulet holds 3 charges. When you or an allied creature within 30 feet of you fails a saving throw against an effect that deals fire damage, you can use your reaction to expend 1 charge and grant that creature resistance to fire damage for the triggering effect only — the damage is halved as though the creature had succeeded on the save.

**Reclaimed Heat.** When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points, the amulet draws a wisp of dying heat from the fallen foe and regains 1 expended charge. The amulet cannot exceed its maximum of 3 charges.

**Tempered Reservoir.** The amulet regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

## Balance Notes
At 225,000 soul stones, the Quenching Pool Amulet is a defensive-support rare that occupies a unique design space: reactive fire resistance for the whole party, gated by charges. With 3 charges replenished at 1d3 per dawn plus kills, aggressive parties can sustain the amulet through combat, while cautious parties will hoard charges for emergency use. The 30-foot range and reaction cost make it a tactical decision rather than a passive buff. Compared to a *Ring of Fire Resistance* (~200K, selfish only), the amulet trades personal protection for team utility. The kill-recharge mechanic adds a satisfying gameplay loop — the healer or support character who wears this gets rewarded for finishing off weakened enemies.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Splash-Phial:** Uncommon variant. No attunement. 1 charge, no kill-recharge, no daily recharge — the phial is single-use and shatters after use. Cost: ~15,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Quenching Basin:** Very Rare variant. 5 charges. The ward can be applied to any number of creatures within 30 feet for a single charge (all failing allies in range). Kills restore 2 charges. Regains 1d4+1 daily. Cost: ~2,000,000 soul stones.
