# Master-Smith's Mantle
*Wondrous item (cloak), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
At first glance this appears to be a heavy cloak of interwoven chain links and leather, the kind a master smith might wear to protect against flying sparks and spattered slag. But on closer inspection the chain is impossibly fine — thousands of micro-links no larger than pinheads, forming a flexible mesh that drapes like silk but clinks like armor plate when caught by wind. The collar is lined with a strip of salamander hide, warm and pulse-soft, and the inner lining bears the faded makers' marks of seventeen smiths who each contributed a single link to the mantle over a century of collaborative forging. Found draped across the master's seat in the anvil-gallery, still holding the faint shape of shoulders, as though the previous wearer had dissolved into heat and left only their protection behind.

## Properties

**Linked Protection.** While wearing this mantle, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

**Forgeborn Immunity.** You are immune to fire damage from nonmagical sources. This includes heat from forge-fires, molten metal splashes, lava proximity, ordinary flame, alchemist's fire, and similar mundane fire hazards. Magical fire damage — from spells, magical weapons, or supernatural creatures — is unaffected.

**Tempering Blessing.** As an action, you can invoke the mantle's deepest forging magic to "temper" yourself and your allies. Choose up to three creatures you can see within 30 feet of you; those creatures and yourself gain resistance to all damage except psychic damage for 1 minute. Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until the next dawn.

## Balance Notes
At 2,600,000 soul stones, the Master-Smith's Mantle is a Very Rare defensive powerhouse that competes directly with a *cloak of displacement* and *mantle of spell resistance*. The +1 AC is the baseline Very Rare cloak benefit. The nonmagical fire immunity is spectacularly flavorful — in the Crucible's lava channels and forge-fires, the wearer is effectively invulnerable — but has limited combat application against most spellcasters and supernatural foes. The Tempering Blessing is the crown jewel: near-universal damage resistance for four creatures for a full minute is comparable to multiple simultaneous *protection from energy* spells (3rd level) without concentration. The "except psychic" carve-out is standard for such broad resistance effects (*bear totem barbarian* uses the same exclusion). The once-per-long-rest gating prevents this from trivializing every major encounter.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Apprentice's Mantle:** Rare variant. No attunement. +1 AC only. No fire immunity. Tempering Blessing affects only the wearer and grants resistance to fire and cold for 1 minute, once per long rest. Cost: ~280,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Grandmaster's Mantle:** Legendary variant. +2 AC. Immune to both nonmagical and magical fire damage. Tempering Blessing affects up to 6 creatures within 60 feet and grants resistance to all damage including psychic. Once per long rest, the wearer can use a reaction to apply Tempering Blessing to a single ally who just took damage (lasting 1 round only, no action cost). Cost: ~7,500,000 soul stones.
