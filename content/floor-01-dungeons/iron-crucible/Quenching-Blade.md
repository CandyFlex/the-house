# Quenching Blade
*Weapon (shortsword), uncommon*

---

## Description
This shortsword is never fully dry — a perpetual sheen of condensation beads along the flat of the blade, and a faint hiss of steam curls from the edge whenever it is drawn from its scabbard. The steel itself is mottled: half midnight-blue from the oil quench, half silver-bright where the grindstone bit deepest, the transition between the two marked by a wavy temper-line like a frozen ripple on dark water. It was fished from one of the Crucible's quenching pools — a narrow trough of viscous, mineral-thick water where newly forged blades are plunged screaming — its last owner's hand still gripping the hilt, bleached bone-white by the alchemical brine.

## Properties

**Quenching Strike.** Once per short rest, when you hit a creature with this weapon you can force the target to make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature is wracked by flash-freezing cold — it takes an additional 1d8 cold damage, and its speed is reduced by 15 feet until the end of your next turn. On a success, it takes half the cold damage and suffers no speed reduction.

**Hiss of Steam.** When you draw this weapon or score a critical hit with it, a burst of steam billows out in a 5-foot radius around you. The steam provides half cover against ranged attacks until the start of your next turn or until a wind of moderate or greater speed disperses it.

## Balance Notes
The Quenching Blade is tuned as a strong Uncommon weapon at 32,000 soul stones. The Quenching Strike delivers a tooled-down *branding smite* equivalent (1d8 instead of 2d6, 1/short rest instead of per-spell-slot) with a speed-reduction rider that gives it a clearer identity than a simple damage proc. The steam burst on draw and crit is flavorful utility — half cover is meaningful but situational, and the trigger conditions (drawing or critting) mean it cannot be spammed. The lack of a +1 bonus keeps this firmly Uncommon while still feeling impactful on a hit.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Dampened Dagger:** Common variant. Dagger. Quenching Strike deals 1d4 cold and reduces speed by 5 feet. No steam burst. Cost: ~10,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Frost-Quench Rapier:** Rare variant. Requires attunement. +1 weapon. Quenching Strike recharges on a short or long rest, deals 2d8 cold, and restrains the target on a failed save. Steam burst provides half cover and lightly obscures a 10-foot radius. Cost: ~240,000 soul stones.
