# Anvil-Echo Earring
*Wondrous item (earring), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A single earring — just one; its twin was never found — formed from a shaving of the central Crucible anvil, hammered into a thin crescent that curls around the earlobe like a sliver of dying moon. The metal is impossibly resonant: tap it with a fingernail and the note hangs in the air for a full ten seconds before fading. When a melee blow lands nearby, the earring catches the impact's echo and multiplies it, sending a sympathetic shockwave back toward the attacker that feels — according to one surviving wielder — "like being smacked in the sternum with a tuning fork the size of a man." Recovered from the anvil-chamber's echo gallery, a vaulted hall where smith-priests studied the acoustics of perfect hammer-falls.

## Properties

**Echo of the Anvil.** When a creature within 30 feet of you that you can see hits with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to resonate the impact's echo. The attacking creature takes 1d4 thunder damage, and the target of the triggering attack gains a +2 bonus to Armor Class until the start of your next turn as the lingering vibration wards off follow-up strikes. You can use this property a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

**Hammer-Sense.** While wearing this earring, you are always aware of the exact direction and approximate distance (within 5 feet) of any impact noise within 60 feet — hammer-falls, sword clashes, slammed doors, a creature hitting the ground after a fall. This grants you advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to locate the source of bludgeoning, thunder, or impact-based sounds.

## Balance Notes
Priced at 180,000 soul stones — the lower end of Rare — the Anvil-Echo Earring is a reaction-tax item for support-minded players. The auto-damage on a reaction (1d4 thunder, no save) is small but guaranteed, while the +2 AC buff to an ally is the real value — equivalent to a mini *shield of faith* that costs no concentration and can be used only when needed. The PB scaling ensures the earring grows with the character. The 30-foot range means the wearer must position near the front line, adding risk-reward tension. At 180K, it is priced below comparable rare defensive items because the damage is trivial and the AC bonus lasts only until the wearer's next turn — a single round of protection, not a sustained buff.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Echo-Shard:** Uncommon variant. No attunement. A small crystal shard hung from the ear. Echo of the Anvil usable once per short rest, 1d4 thunder only — no AC bonus. No Hammer-Sense. Cost: ~12,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Grand Anvil Echo:** Very Rare variant. Echo of the Anvil deals 2d4 thunder and the AC bonus is +3. Unlimited uses (no PB limit). Hammer-Sense extends to 120 feet and now includes any sound of metal striking metal (drawing a blade, loading a crossbow, donning armor). Cost: ~1,700,000 soul stones.
