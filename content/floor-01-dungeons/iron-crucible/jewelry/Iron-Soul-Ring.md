# Iron Soul Ring
*Ring, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A simple, almost crude iron band — unpolished, unadorned, with no gem, no engraving, no flourish of any kind. It is warm, as all Crucible-forged things are, but the warmth is deeper than mere temperature: it feels like the heat of conviction, the smolder of a decision that will not be unmade. The ring was forged from ore mined at the exact basalt seam where the first shovel struck earth to dig the Crucible's foundation — the primordial stone that had never known fire before the forge-city was built. Wearing it feels like having an anvil where your heart should be: immovable, unshakeable, and utterly certain. Recovered from a small iron coffer buried beneath the central hearth-stone of the smelting temple.

## Properties

**Forge-Certainty.** While wearing this ring, you cannot be charmed or frightened. Any attempt to impose either condition on you automatically fails. If you are already charmed or frightened when you put on the ring, those conditions are suppressed for as long as you wear it.

**Iron Soul.** As a bonus action, you can steel your spirit with the inexorable certainty of forged iron. For 1 minute, you gain the following benefits:
- You gain a +2 bonus to all saving throws.
- The first time each turn that you take damage, you reduce that damage by 1d10.

Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until the next dawn.

## Balance Notes
At 2,400,000 soul stones, the Iron Soul Ring is a Very Rare defensive ring that prioritizes mental resilience over physical protection. The permanent immunity to charmed and frightened is a powerful passive — comparable to a *ring of mind shielding* (uncommon, blocks thought-reading and alignment detection) but targeting the game's two most debilitating mental conditions outright. The Iron Soul state's +2 to all saving throws is equivalent to a no-concentration *bless* affecting only the wearer, and the 1d10 damage reduction (once per turn) functions like a partial *stoneskin* without the nonmagical-weakness limitation. The bonus-action activation means the wearer can pivot to "tank mode" without losing their turn's main action, a significant quality-of-life feature. At 2.4M, it's priced between defensive Very Rare rings like the *ring of evasion* (~2M) and offensive ones like the *ring of telekinesis* (~3M).

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Iron Resolve Ring:** Rare variant. Requires attunement. Advantage (not immunity) on saves against charmed and frightened. Iron Soul state grants +1 to saves and reduces damage by 1d6 (1/long rest). Cost: ~350,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Absolute Iron Soul:** Legendary variant. Immunity to charmed, frightened, possessed, and stunned. Iron Soul state grants +3 to saves, reduces first damage each turn by 2d10, and can be used twice per long rest. Cost: ~6,500,000 soul stones.
