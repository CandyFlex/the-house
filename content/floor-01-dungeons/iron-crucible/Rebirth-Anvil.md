# Rebirth Anvil
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement by a creature proficient with smith's tools)*

---

## Description
A portable anvil no larger than a halfling's travel chest, cast from iron that has been folded one thousand times — a ritual technique the Crucible smiths called "soul-layering," believed to trap a fragment of the smith's own spirit within the metal with every fold. The anvil's face is mirror-smooth despite its great age, and when struck it produces not a clang but a deep, resonant hum that seems to vibrate on the same frequency as memory itself. The sides are etched with the formulae for seventeen different alloys, and a single permanent groove runs down the center — the ghost of the very first blade ever forged upon it, whose shape the anvil has never forgotten. Found at the base of the central Crucible dais, directly beneath the pour-spout, as though it had been waiting for someone to retrieve it.

## Properties

**Reforge the Broken.** Over the course of 1 minute, you can use the Rebirth Anvil to repair a destroyed nonmagical weapon, shield, or suit of armor. Place the broken pieces upon the anvil and strike them with any hammer — the anvil hums, the metal grows warm, and the fragments flow together like quicksilver, restoring the item to flawless condition. Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until the next dawn.

**Soul-Remembering.** If you place a magic item on the Rebirth Anvil and spend at least 1 hour of a long rest in contact with it, you learn its properties as though you had cast the *identify* spell on it. This does not remove curses, though it does reveal them.

**Awaken the Dormant Steel.** Once per week, you can attempt to "awaken" a nonmagical melee weapon by forging it anew on the Rebirth Anvil. Make a DC 15 Wisdom (Smith's Tools) check. On a success, the weapon becomes a +1 weapon permanently, and a faint impression of the Rebirth Anvil's central groove appears along its blade or haft. On a failure, the anvil's magic is disrupted — it cannot be used for any of its properties until the next dawn, and the weapon is destroyed in the attempt.

## Balance Notes
At 1,800,000 soul stones — the lowest-priced Very Rare item in the Crucible collection — the Rebirth Anvil is a pure utility item with no combat application whatsoever. This is deliberate: the 1/week +1 weapon creation is powerful over a long campaign but effectively "spends" the anvil's power budget on downtime rather than combat turns. A +1 weapon is Uncommon (~15K-40K), and creating one per week could theoretically print money — but the DC 15 check with weapon-destruction-on-failure means the expected value requires a skilled smith (proficiency +3, likely INT +2, for a +5 bonus = 55% success rate). The Reforge ability is a superior *mending* (which can't restore destroyed objects), situationally invaluable when a fighter's greatsword gets sundered by a rust monster. Soul-Remembering is a quality-of-life upgrade that saves the party a 1st-level ritual slot. At 1.8M, the Anvil is fairly priced for patient, crafting-oriented parties.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Apprentice's Anvil:** Rare variant. Requires attunement. Reforge takes 1 hour and can only be used once per week. Soul-Remembering works but reveals only basic properties (no curses). No Awaken property. Cost: ~200,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Crucible-Found Anvil:** Legendary variant. Requires attunement. Reforge works on magical items as well (restoring destroyed magical weapons/armor). Soul-Remembering also removes curses. Awaken can create +2 weapons (DC 18), usable once per long rest with no destruction on failure. Cost: ~5,500,000 soul stones.
