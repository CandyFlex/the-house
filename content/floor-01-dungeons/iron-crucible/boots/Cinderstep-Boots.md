# Cinderstep Boots
*Wondrous item (boots), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
These knee-high boots are stitched from the hide of a salamander that laired in the Crucible's smelting galleries, their leather the color of cooling charcoal shot through with faint orange vein-lines that pulse like embers when the wearer moves. The soles are studded with chips of slag glass — sharp, iridescent fragments that strike sparks against stone — and the whole construction radiates a dry, pleasant warmth even on the coldest dungeon floor. Found half-buried in a drift of cinder near the secondary ventilation shafts, the boots still carried a ghost of their former owner's last desperate sprint.

## Properties

**Cinder Trail.** When you take the Dash action or move at least 20 feet on your turn, you leave a trail of burning cinders in the spaces you vacated. The trail occupies your path and lasts until the start of your next turn. A creature that enters the trail or starts its turn there takes 1d4 fire damage. A creature can take this damage only once per turn.

**Forge-Stride.** You have advantage on saving throws against effects that would reduce your speed or knock you prone while standing on stone, metal, or worked surfaces such as masonry or tile.

**Ember Glow.** While in darkness, the cinder-trail sheds dim light in a 5-foot radius for the duration of the trail.

## Balance Notes
The Cinderstep Boots add a modest zone-control layer to mobile characters — particularly rogues and monks who routinely Dash or cover distance. The 1d4 fire is deliberately small (comparable to the *create bonfire* cantrip's 1d8 but spread across multiple spaces and easier to avoid) because the effect is passive and costs no action economy. At 28,000 soul stones, this sits in the upper third of Uncommon pricing, justified by the combat utility of free zone denial. The Forge-Stride property is niche but flavorful, adding incidental value without shifting power budget.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Sparkstep Slippers:** Common variant. Trail deals 1 fire damage (no die roll) and fades at the end of your turn. No Forge-Stride property. Cost: ~5,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Blazetrail Boots:** Rare variant. Trail deals 1d8 fire damage and lasts until the end of your next turn. Once per short rest, you can ignite the entire trail as a bonus action, dealing 2d8 fire to all creatures standing in it (DEX save DC 15 for half). Cost: ~180,000 soul stones.
