# Crucible Plate
*Armor (half plate), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A suit of half plate forged from iron that has been smelted, poured, and re-smelted seventeen times — the ritual number of the Crucible's smith-priests — each cycle burning away more impurity until the metal is a deep, lustrous blue-grey the color of a quenching tank's surface just before the blade enters. The pauldrons are shaped like miniature pouring ladles, and the breastplate bears a raised relief of the central Crucible itself, with thin channels in the metal that glow orange when the wearer absorbs flame. It was found standing upright on an armor rack in the Crucible Warden's quarters, as though its owner had simply stepped out for a moment and never returned.

## Properties

**Seventeen-Times Tempered.** You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while wearing this armor.

**Crucible-Forged.** You have resistance to fire damage while wearing this armor.

**Anvil-Light.** When you take fire damage while wearing this armor, the thin channels carved across the breastplate flare with brilliant orange light. For 1 minute, the armor sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. Allied creatures within the bright light gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class. This effect ends early if you doff the armor or fall unconscious.

## Balance Notes
At 380,000 soul stones, Crucible Plate is priced aggressively within the Rare band — it bundles +1 armor (uncommon, ~15K), fire resistance (ring of fire resistance, rare, ~200K), and a conditional +1 AC aura for allies. The aura's trigger condition (the wearer must take fire damage) creates an interesting incentive structure: the wearer wants to "activate" the plate by wading into fire, turning a liability into a team asset. This self-sacrificial flavor matches the Crucible's ethos. The aura's 10-foot radius forces close-quarters positioning, preventing it from becoming a passive group-buff for spread-out parties. At 40 pounds, the weight is notable — strength-based characters only.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Apprentice's Breastplate:** Uncommon variant. No attunement. +1 AC only, no fire resistance, no aura. Cost: ~22,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Crucible-Warden's Plate:** Very Rare variant. +2 armor. The Anvil-Light aura extends to 20 feet bright and grants allies +2 AC. While the aura is active, the wearer also heals 1d4 hit points at the start of each of their turns. Cost: ~2,800,000 soul stones.
