# Bellows-Fury Bow
*Weapon (longbow), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
The bowstave is a single piece of petrified ironwood — a tree that grew too close to the Crucible's exhaust vents and slowly turned to living metal over centuries of drinking iron-rich fumes. When the string is drawn, the bow exhales: twin bellows-nozzles set into the riser open and trail a ribbon of superheated air along the arrow's flight path, a shimmering scar across the battlefield that lingers like a heat-mirage. The bow was found mounted above the great bellows-engine in the southern turbine hall, its string still vibrating with a note that hadn't faded since the Crucible was last in full operation.

## Properties

**Ironwood Tempered.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Heated Wake.** When you fire an arrow from this bow, the projectile trails a line of superheated air along its flight path. This line occupies a 5-foot-wide corridor from you to the target and lasts until the start of your next turn. A creature that moves through the line or starts its turn there takes 1d4 fire damage. A creature can take this damage only once per turn.

**Bellows-Fury Shot.** Once per short rest, when you hit a creature with an arrow fired from this bow you can release a concussive blast of hot wind at the point of impact. Each creature within 10 feet of the target must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. A creature takes 3d6 thunder damage on a failed save, is pushed 10 feet away from the impact point, and is deafened until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and suffers no push or deafness.

## Balance Notes
The Bellows-Fury Bow at 340,000 soul stones rewards battlefield geometry — the Heated Wake creates persistent lane-denial that punishes enemy movement patterns, especially in tight dungeon corridors. The 1d4 fire per creature moving through the line is modest (comparable to a cantrip) but adds up when the archer positions themselves to force enemies through the wake to reach them. The Bellows-Fury Shot offers a powerful short-rest nova with a high damage ceiling (3d6 thunder is roughly a 2nd-level *shatter* that also pushes and deafens). At 340K, this is priced between the Char-Brand (350K) and Forge-Warden Hammer (275K), reflecting its hybrid zone-control-and-burst identity. The Heated Wake line is deliberately narrow (5 feet) to avoid dominating large open-field battles.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Heated-Wake Shortbow:** Uncommon variant. Shortbow, no +1 bonus. Heated Wake deals 1 fire damage (no roll). No Bellows-Fury Shot. Cost: ~28,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Great Bellows-Fury Bow:** Very Rare variant. +2 longbow. Heated Wake deals 1d6 fire. Bellows-Fury Shot deals 5d6 thunder, and the push distance is 20 feet. Bellows-Fury Shot can be used twice per short rest. Cost: ~2,600,000 soul stones.
