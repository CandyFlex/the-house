# Heart of the Crucible
*Weapon (greatsword), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This is not a sword so much as a captured cataclysm. The blade is forged around a fist-sized shard of the Crucible's molten core, extracted during the one and only time the great furnace was allowed to cool — a ritual known as the Quenching of the First Flame, which nearly destroyed the forge-city. The core-shard pulses visibly beneath a translucent layer of diamond-hard slag-glass that encases the blade's fuller, radiating orange-gold light in time with the wielder's heartbeat. When swung, the sword leaves afterimages of molten metal hanging in the air like a paused waterfall, and the ground beneath the wielder's feet cracks and weeps tiny threads of liquid iron. Recovered from the sealed core-chamber beneath the central Crucible dais, in a room that had not been opened since the forge-city's founding.

## Properties

**Core-Forged.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The sword deals an additional 2d6 fire damage and 1d6 force damage on a hit — the fire is the heat of the primordial forge, and the force is the concussive blast of the core's heartbeat.

**Crucible's Warmth.** While you are holding this sword, you have resistance to fire damage.

**Heartbeat Eruption.** As an action, you can plunge the blade into the ground and trigger a cataclysmic surge of the core's power. The ground erupts in a 20-foot radius centered on you: each creature of your choice in the area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d6 fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a success. The eruption leaves the area coated in cooling molten metal — the affected ground becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute. You are immune to this eruption's damage. Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until the next dawn.

## Balance Notes
At 3,200,000 soul stones, Heart of the Crucible is the most expensive weapon in the Iron Crucible collection and one of the heaviest Very Rare items overall. The damage output is exceptional: a +2 greatsword (2d6+2 base) with an additional 3d6 in rider damage (2d6 fire + 1d6 force) averages 23.5 damage per hit before ability modifiers — roughly on par with a *Frost Brand* (2d6+1d6 cold) plus the +2 bonus. The 6d6 self-centered AoE is comparable to a *fireball* (8d6) but in a smaller radius (20ft vs 20ft sphere at range) and cannot hit the wielder — a deliberate tradeoff. Fire resistance while held is standard Very Rare weapon behavior (*Frost Brand* gives fire resistance, making this somewhat redundant for the wielder but thematically essential). The weight (12 lbs) is punishing for low-Strength characters.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Crucible Shard:** Rare variant. +1 greatsword. Deals +1d6 fire on hit (no force). No fire resistance. Heartbeat Eruption deals 4d6 in a 15-foot radius and recharges on a long rest. Cost: ~400,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Primordial Crucible Core:** Legendary variant. +3 greatsword. Deals +3d6 fire and +2d6 force on hit. Heartbeat Eruption deals 10d6 fire in a 30-foot radius, and the molten terrain deals 2d6 fire to any creature ending its turn there for 1 minute. Recharges on a short rest. Cost: ~8,000,000 soul stones.
