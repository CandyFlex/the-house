# Char-Brand
*Weapon (greatsword), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
The blade of this greatsword is not forged in the traditional sense — it is cast from a single pour of molten iron into an open-faced mold, then left to cool just long enough to hold its shape before being quenched in oil. The result is a sword whose surface is a chaos of cooling cracks, slag-pocks, and carbon-scorched whorls, the metal dark as black bacon except along the edge, which glows a sullen cherry-red. A smith's touch-brand — the personal mark of the Crucible's master iron-pourer — is stamped deep into the ricasso: a ring of seven stars surrounding a single flame. The sword was found impaled in the chest of a slag-golem in the smelting chamber, still radiating heat after what must have been centuries.

## Properties

**Pour-Forged.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Searing Brand.** Once per turn when you hit a creature with this greatsword, you can brand the target with a searing mark. For 1 minute, the branded creature takes 1d6 fire damage at the start of each of its turns. Additionally, the brand glows visibly through the target's flesh or hide — the branded creature cannot benefit from the invisible condition for the duration. A creature can have only one brand active at a time; branding a new target ends the previous brand.

**Kindling Strike.** If you hit a branded creature with this greatsword, the brand flares and the 1d6 fire damage triggers immediately (in addition to triggering at the start of the target's turn).

## Balance Notes
At 350,000 soul stones, the Char-Brand is one of the stronger Rare weapons in the Crucible lineup. The Searing Brand applies a save-free 1d6 DoT that can last up to 10 rounds — a theoretical maximum of 10d6 fire damage per brand, though in practice most targets fall in 3–4 rounds. The per-turn restriction prevents a nova-brand scenario where a fighter brands three targets in one Action Surge. The invisibility counter is a powerful tactical rider that justifies the higher price point. Compared to a *Flame Tongue* greatsword (2d6 fire on every hit, ~350K), the Char-Brand rewards sustained combat over burst, making it ideal for boss fights and durable foes rather than trash-clearing.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Ember-Brand:** Uncommon variant. Greatsword, no +1 bonus. Brand deals 1d4 fire and only lasts until the end of your next turn. No Kindling Strike. No invisibility counter. Cost: ~40,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Infernal Char-Brand:** Very Rare variant. +2 greatsword. Brand deals 2d6 fire per round. The invisibility counter now extends to any illusion that hides the target (including *blur*, *mirror image*, and *displacement*). Once per long rest, you can detonate all active brands as an action — each branded creature takes 4d6 fire (CON save DC 17 for half). Cost: ~3,200,000 soul stones.
