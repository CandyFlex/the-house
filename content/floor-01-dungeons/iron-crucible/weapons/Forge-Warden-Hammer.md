# Forge-Warden Hammer
*Weapon (warhammer), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
The head of this warhammer is a single block of forge-blackened steel, rectangular and brutal, with a striking face on one side and a tapered back-spike on the other. Heat-ripples shimmer perpetually above the metal as though it had only just been lifted from the coals, and the leather-wrapped haft is warm to the touch — never burning, but insistent, like a handshake that lingers too long. It was recovered from the Forge-Warden's pulpit, a raised stone platform overlooking the central Crucible where a single smith-priest would direct the twenty-four-hour rhythm of hammer and bellows, striking the anvil to signal each shift-change.

## Properties

**Forge-Tempered.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Spark of the Anvil.** When you score a critical hit with this hammer, the target becomes Heated. While Heated, the creature has disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn. The Heated condition then ends.

**Forge-Warden's Command.** While holding this hammer, you can cast the *heat metal* spell (save DC 15). Once you cast the spell in this way, you cannot do so again until the next dawn.

**Smith-Sense.** You know the location of any metal object weighing 1 pound or more within 30 feet of you, though you cannot discern its shape or composition beyond the metal itself.

## Balance Notes
Priced at 275,000 soul stones, this hammer sits comfortably alongside other +1 rare weapons with spell riders — a *Flame Tongue* deals 2d6 fire every hit, while the Forge-Warden Hammer trades sustained damage for a once-daily *heat metal* (a 2nd-level spell) and a critical-hit debuff. The Heated condition on crit is an elegant anti-snowball mechanic: it gives the target a brief window of weakness without piling on damage, rewarding the wielder's allies rather than the wielder alone. Smith-Sense is pure ribbon, adding flavor without combat power. The save DC of 15 matches standard rare-item benchmarks.

---

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Apprentice's Hammer:** Uncommon variant. No attunement. No +1 bonus. *Heat metal* 1/day (DC 13), crit only inflicts Heated — no Smith-Sense. Cost: ~35,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Grand Forge-Warden Hammer:** Very Rare variant. +2 weapon. *Heat metal* 3/day (DC 17). On a crit, the target is Heated and also takes an extra 2d8 fire damage. Cost: ~2,100,000 soul stones.
