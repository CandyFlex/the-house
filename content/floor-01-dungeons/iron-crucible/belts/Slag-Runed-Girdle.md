# Slag-Runed Girdle
*Wondrous item (belt), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A broad belt of interlocking iron scales, each one etched with a single caustic rune that seeps a faint green-yellow glow — the color of the sulphurous slag-fumes that curl off the Crucible's waste pits. The scales are riveted to a backing of salamander leather, and the buckle is a fist-sized chunk of raw slag-stone, glassy and sharp-edged. When the wearer strikes with their bare hands, the runes flare and the blow leaves behind a sizzling residue that eats into armor and flesh alike. The belt was recovered from the slag-dumping galleries at the Crucible's lowest level, cinched around the waist of a half-dissolved skeleton whose bones were pitted as if by acid — though the belt itself was untouched.

## Properties

**Slag-Touch.** Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 acid damage instead of their normal damage, and you can use your choice of Strength or Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls. The acid seeps into wounds with a sizzling hiss, leaving smoking pits in whatever it touches.

**Caustic Bite.** When you attack a creature wearing metal armor with an unarmed strike, you ignore the Armor Class bonus granted by that armor. The runes flare with green light as the acid eats through plate and mail like salt through ice.

**Slag-Born.** You have resistance to acid damage, and you are immune to the harmful effects of nonmagical acid — including green slime, ochre jelly secretions, and the acidic fumes of the Crucible's slag pits.

## Balance Notes
At 200,000 soul stones, the Slag-Runed Girdle is a transformation item for unarmed builds — monks and barbarians who eschew weapons. The 1d6 acid damage replaces base unarmed damage entirely (not adding to it), which means it scales suboptimally for high-level monks (whose Martial Arts die eventually reaches 1d10) but remains relevant through the mid-levels. The metal-armor-bypass ability is devastating against humanoid foes (knights, guards, fellow adventurers) but useless against most monsters — a deliberate asymmetry that rewards tactical target selection. Acid resistance and environmental immunity round out the package without pushing it into Very Rare territory.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Slag-Touched Wraps:** Uncommon variant. No attunement. Wraps for the hands — unarmed strikes deal an additional 1d4 acid damage (not instead of base damage). No armor-bypass, no acid resistance. Cost: ~18,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Crucible-Core Girdle:** Very Rare variant. Unarmed strikes deal 2d6 acid damage. Armor-bypass works against any manufactured armor (wood, bone, chitin, not just metal). Once per long rest, as an action you can exhale a 30-foot cone of acidic slag-fumes (6d6 acid, DEX save DC 17 for half). Cost: ~1,900,000 soul stones.
