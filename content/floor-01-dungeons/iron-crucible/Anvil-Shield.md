# Anvil Shield
*Armor (shield), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
This shield was never meant to be a shield at all — it is the broken face of a master smith's anvil, sheared clean in half by some cataclysmic hammer-blow during the Crucible's forging of a legendary blade. The surviving half was fitted with iron straps and carried into battle by a forge-priest who believed the anvil's spirit still resonated within the metal. The face still bears the ghost-imprints of ten thousand hammer-strikes, and when struck with sufficient force it rings with a clear, bell-like tone that seems to absorb the blow's fury and cast it aside. Found propped against the central Crucible dais, arranged as though waiting for a wielder.

## Properties

**Anvil's Answer.** When a creature hits you with a bludgeoning attack, you can use your reaction to gain a +3 bonus to your AC against the triggering attack, potentially causing it to miss. The shield rings out with a hammer-on-anvil peal audible out to 60 feet when you use this property.

**Set Against the Charge.** While you are holding this shield, you have advantage on saving throws against being moved involuntarily — including being pushed, pulled, shoved, or knocked prone — provided you are standing on solid ground.

**Iron Memory.** The shield counts as both a shield and an improvised melee weapon. When you use it to make an attack, you are considered proficient and it deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage on a hit.

## Balance Notes
At 25,000 soul stones, the Anvil Shield occupies a niche between a standard shield (+2 AC passive) and the *shield* spell (+5 AC for a round). The reaction bonus is +3 and only against bludgeoning — roughly one-third of monster attacks — which prevents it from overshadowing the *shield* spell while remaining tactically meaningful. The forced-movement advantage and improvised-weapon proficiency are ribbon abilities that reinforce the shield's identity as an immovable object. The reaction cost is the key balancing lever: it competes with opportunity attacks, *absorb elements*, and other valuable reactions.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Forged Buckler:** Common variant. No attunement. Only the Anvil's Answer property (no improvised weapon, no forced-move advantage). AC bonus is +2. Cost: ~9,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Grand Anvil Shield:** Rare variant. AC bonus becomes +5 and works against all physical damage types (bludgeoning, piercing, slashing). Once per long rest, when you use the reaction, you can reflect half the triggering damage back to the attacker as thunder damage. Cost: ~260,000 soul stones.
