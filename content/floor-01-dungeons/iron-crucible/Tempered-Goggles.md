# Tempered Goggles
*Wondrous item (goggles), uncommon*

---

## Description
Two thick lenses of smoke-darkened glass are set into a frame of hammered brass, the metal still bearing the faint peen-marks of the crucible-smith who shaped them. A leather strap — scorched at the edges from proximity to the forge — secures them snugly against the wearer's face, and the lenses carry an almost imperceptible shimmer, as though they were dipped in the same quenching oil the smiths use to harden fresh-forged steel. They were discovered on a workbench in the anvil-gallery overlook, abandoned mid-task beside a half-finished blade.

## Properties

**Heat-Shimmer Sight.** You can see normally through nonmagical smoke, steam, and heat-haze, and you treat areas of such obscurement as unoccluded for the purpose of Perception checks relying on sight. Magical fog, smoke, or steam is unaffected.

**Tempered Lens.** You have advantage on saving throws against being blinded by effects that involve bright light, heat, or fire — including spells such as *color spray*, *sunbeam*, or the flash of a forged metal explosion.

**Forge-Hardened.** The goggles never fog, crack from temperature extremes, or accumulate soot or ash on their lenses.

## Balance Notes
This is a pure utility item with no combat damage ceiling, priced at the lower end of Uncommon (22,000 soul stones) — comparable to *Goggles of Night* (also uncommon, no attunement). The advantage against blindness from light/fire effects is situationally potent but narrow enough that it doesn't push this into Rare territory. The smoke-piercing vision synergizes beautifully with other Crucible items that create smoke or steam effects, rewarding thematic party composition without breaking baseline balance.

---

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Smoked Lens:** Common variant. Only the smoke/steam vision — no blindness advantage, no forge-hardening. Cost: ~8,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Master-Smith's Spectacles:** Rare variant. Requires attunement. Grants all base effects plus the ability to cast *see invisibility* once per short rest, and you can determine the AC of any metal armor or shield you can see. Cost: ~160,000 soul stones.
