# Ash-King's Crown
*Wondrous item (helm), very rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

---

## Description
A jagged crown of cooled obsidian and iron — three asymmetrical spires rise from a circlet that looks as though it were poured molten and allowed to set mid-drip, frozen rivulets of black glass curling down toward the wearer's temples. The obsidian is shot through with veins of captured cinder that glow a sullen, smoky orange, and a fine layer of ash is perpetually sifting from the crown's surface, never depleting, never accumulating — just a constant dusting of grey that trails behind the wearer like a ghost's train. This was the crown of the last Ash-King, a sorcerer-lord who ruled the Crucible in its decadent final century and who, according to murals in the anvil-gallery, commanded cinder-elementals and spoke in a voice that turned iron to rust. The crown was found resting on a black iron throne at the apex of the Crucible's highest observation balcony, beneath a skylight choked with centuries of soot.

## Properties

**Crown of the Ash-King.** While wearing this crown, you gain a +1 bonus to your spell save DC and spell attack rolls.

**Cinder Bolts.** While wearing the crown, you can cast the *scorching ray* spell at 3rd level (creating four rays) at will, without expending a spell slot. The rays manifest as bolts of black cinder and ash — they deal fire damage as normal but appear as streams of dark, particulate ember rather than traditional flame. The spells you cast in this way ignore the effect of spells such as *investiture of flame* or *protection from energy* that grant resistance to fire damage against spells of 3rd level or lower (but not higher-level protections or innate fire resistance).

**Ash-Wall.** While wearing the crown, you can cast *wall of fire* once per long rest without expending a spell slot. The wall appears as a curtain of swirling ash and glowing ember rather than pure flame, and the damage it deals is necrotic instead of fire. The spell save DC for this casting is 17. When you cast the spell in this way, you may choose to shape it as a circle with a 20-foot diameter rather than a line.

## Balance Notes
Priced at 3,500,000 soul stones — the absolute top of Very Rare — the Ash-King's Crown is designed as the ultimate caster reward from the Iron Crucible. The +1 to spell DC and attack rolls is the baseline Very Rare helm benefit (comparable to an *arcane grimoire +1* stacked into the head slot). At-will *scorching ray* at 3rd level delivers 8d6 fire damage as an action — roughly equivalent to a 3rd-level *fireball* (8d6) but split across attack rolls, which means it can crit but can also miss. The resistance-bypass against low-level fire protections is a subtle but powerful feature: it punishes enemies who think they've prepared for fire by wearing a *ring of fire resistance* or casting *protection from energy*, though innate fire resistance (red dragon, fire elemental) and higher-level protections still work. Ash-Wall dealing necrotic damage is a brilliant anti-synergy-breaker — enemies immune to fire aren't also immune to necrotic, giving the crown a fallback against fire-immune foes. At 3.5M, this is deliberately expensive; it's a build-defining item for any blaster caster.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Cinder Circlet:** Rare variant. Requires attunement by a spellcaster. +1 to spell attack rolls only (no DC boost). Cinder Bolts at-will but only at 2nd level (3 rays). No Ash-Wall. Cost: ~420,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Ash-Emperor's Crown:** Legendary variant. +2 spell DC and attack. Cinder Bolts can be cast at 5th level (6 rays) at-will and bypasses all fire resistance (though not immunity). Ash-Wall can be cast once per short rest. Additionally, while wearing the crown, any creature that dies within 30 feet of you rises as a cinder-ash zombie under your control for 1 minute. Cost: ~9,000,000 soul stones.
