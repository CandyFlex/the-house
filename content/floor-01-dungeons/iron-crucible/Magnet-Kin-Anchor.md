# Magnet-Kin Anchor
*Weapon (whip), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
At first glance this looks less like a weapon and more like a length of industrial chain salvaged from the Crucible's overhead crane-works — and in truth, that is exactly what it is. Each link is forged from lodestone-laced iron that thrums with a low, barely-audible hum, and the last link swells into a barbed anchor-hook the size of a fist. When it strikes a target, the links lock rigid for a heartbeat, and the wielder can feel the magnetic pull — a bond between the anchor and whatever metal it touched that begs to be yanked tight. Found coiled beside a collapsed gantry in the Crucible's northern loading bay, still attached to a winch-drum the size of a wagon wheel.

## Properties

**Lodestone-Tempered.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The whip has the reach property (10 feet) and deals slashing damage.

**Magnetic Draw.** When you hit a creature with this whip, you can use a bonus action to pull the target up to 10 feet directly toward you. If the target is wearing or carrying metal objects weighing 10 pounds or more, the pull distance is 15 feet. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

**Iron-Glow.** When you hit a creature wearing or carrying metal with this whip, the metal objects on the target's person begin to glow with a dull red magnetic aura, as though affected by the *faerie fire* spell (no saving throw). The glow lasts until the end of your next turn, and attack rolls against the target have advantage while the glow persists.

## Balance Notes
The Magnet-Kin Anchor at 250,000 soul stones is a battlefield-manipulation weapon rather than a raw-damage dealer — appropriate for a rare whip, which deals only 1d4 base damage. The bonus-action pull is unconditional on a hit (no save) but costs action economy, and the Iron-Glow's auto-advantage makes this an outstanding support weapon for parties with multiple martial characters. The pull distance scaling based on metal content rewards clever targeting (pull the knight, not the owlbear). Compared to a *Flame Tongue whip* (2d6 fire every hit, ~300K), the Anchor trades damage for control and team utility, which justifies slightly lower pricing.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Loadstone Chain:** Uncommon variant. No attunement. No +1 bonus. Pull is 5 feet (10 with metal). Iron-Glow only lasts until the end of the current turn. Cost: ~30,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Magnet-Kin Sovereign:** Very Rare variant. +2 weapon. Pull target up to 25 feet. Iron-Glow lasts for 1 minute (no concentration required). Once per long rest, you can activate a magnetic pulse as an action — all creatures wearing metal within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 17 Strength save or be pulled 20 feet toward you and knocked prone. Cost: ~2,400,000 soul stones.
