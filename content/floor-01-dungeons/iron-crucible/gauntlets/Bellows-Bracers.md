# Bellows Bracers
*Wondrous item (bracers), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
These heavy leather bracers are reinforced with articulated iron plates that expand and contract with the wearer's breathing — a miniature bellows mechanism stitched directly into the vambrace. When activated, the plates snap open along accordion-fold seams and blast forth a concentrated gale that smells of hot iron and charcoal dust. They were recovered from the bellows-station beneath Crucible Row, where teams of laborers worked great turbine-fans to feed air to the smelting pits — the bracers still coated in a fine layer of powdered coke.

## Properties

**Bellows-Gust.** As an action, you can thrust your arms forward and release a concentrated blast of superheated air in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet directly away from you and knocked prone. Creatures of Large size or larger have advantage on this saving throw. You can use this property a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

**Stoke the Flame.** As a bonus action, you can feed a gale into a nonmagical fire within 10 feet of you — such as a torch, lantern, campfire, or alchemist's fire — instantly intensifying it. For 1 minute, the fire sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius (up from its normal range) and deals 1 additional fire damage to any creature that takes damage from it. Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

- **Save DC:** 13 (STR)

---

## Balance Notes
Priced at 20,000 soul stones — the middle of the Uncommon band — the Bellows Bracers fill a battlefield-control niche without adding direct damage at their primary tier. The scaling PB-uses-per-day means the item grows with the character, a design pattern that keeps uncommon items relevant. The save DC of 13 is intentionally modest; the push-and-prone combo on multiple targets is comparable to a weak *thunderwave* without damage, and the bracers' lack of sustained wind (unlike *gust of wind*) prevents them from trivializing environmental hazards. Stoke the Flame is mostly flavor with rare tactical applications.

---

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Apprentice's Bellows:** Common variant. No attunement. Bellows-Gust only (same DC, PB uses), no Stoke the Flame. Cost: ~7,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Greatforge Bracers:** Rare variant. Save DC becomes 15. Bellows-Gust now deals 2d8 fire damage to creatures that fail the save, and Stoke the Flame can intensify magical fire as well. Cost: ~210,000 soul stones.
