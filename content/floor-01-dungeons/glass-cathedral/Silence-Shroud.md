# Silence-Shroud
*Wondrous item (cloak), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A hooded cloak made of perfectly clear cathedral glass that has somehow been rendered as flexible as silk — a sheet of frozen silence, impossibly thin, impossibly quiet. Light passes through it unaltered, but sound does not; where the cloak covers, sound simply stops, bent around the wearer like water flowing around a stone. Found draped over the rafters of the Confessional of Echoes, where the person who wore it had hidden for so long that even the Cathedral forgot they were there. When fully wrapped around yourself, you cease to exist in any sense that depends on vibration — you are a void in the world's acoustic map, a hole where a person should be, and even creatures that navigate by tremor or echo find nothing but empty air.

## Properties

**Acoustic Void.** While wearing this cloak, you cannot be detected by blindsight, tremorsense, or any sense that relies on sound or vibration to perceive creatures. You make no sound involuntarily — your footsteps, breathing, heartbeat, and the rustle of your clothing are swallowed entirely by the cloak. You can still choose to speak or intentionally create noise, though the cloak cannot amplify those sounds or carry them beyond their natural range.

Additionally, you can move through areas of *silence* or similar sound-negating effects without being affected by them — you can speak, cast spells with verbal components, and hear normally within a *silence* field, as the cloak normalizes the acoustic environment immediately around your body.

**Absolute Quiet.** Once per long rest, as an action, you can pull the cloak's hood up and wrap the shroud tightly around yourself. For 1 minute, or until you make an attack or cast a spell, you become invisible (as the *greater invisibility* spell) and entirely inaudible — you cannot be detected by any sense that relies on sound, vibration, tremor, or echolocation. While wrapped, you are functionally hidden from all non-visual forms of detection, and even creatures with truesight or *see invisibility* cannot perceive you by visual means unless they also have some means of seeing through the cloak's unique acoustic-optical properties (DM's discretion — the cloak is not magical darkness or standard illusion).

**First Strike.** While hidden under the effects of Absolute Quiet, the first attack you make is an automatic critical hit. This property ends immediately after that attack resolves, and the Absolute Quiet effect ends at the same moment.

---

## Balance Notes
The passive acoustic immunity is a permanent *pass without trace* for sound (but not sight), invaluable for infiltration. Absolute Quiet effectively grants *greater invisibility* (a 4th-level spell, concentration) without concentration, with the added benefit of sound nullification and an automatic critical on the first attack. The auto-crit is a rogue's dream — pair with Sneak Attack for devastating opening strikes. At 2,500,000 (lower-mid very rare), it's priced below *cloak of invisibility* (legendary, ~10,000,000) because the invisibility is only 1/long rest for 1 minute rather than at-will. The auto-crit and sound-null properties make it a specialized infiltration/assassination tool. The truesight bypass clause is intentionally ambiguous to allow DM discretion while preventing the cloak from being an absolute trump card.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Quiet Shroud:** Rare. Acoustic Void passive only (no Absolute Quiet, no *silence* immunity, no First Strike). Cost: 250,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Silence:** Legendary. Absolute Quiet usable 3/long rest, lasts 10 minutes, First Strike applies to the first three attacks made from hiding while cloaked. Cost: 12,000,000 soul stones.
