# Cathedral Chime Shield
*Armor (shield), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A circular shield of pale, milky glass veined with silver — like frozen smoke captured in crystal. When struck, it does not clang like metal or crack like wood; it chimes. A single, pure, cathedral-filling note that lingers in the air for three full seconds before fading, and in that moment everything nearby flinches. Found mounted on the Bell Tower wall beside a massive bronze bell that had not been rung in living memory — the shield was perfectly in tune with it, and when the bell finally fell silent, the shield alone remembered its note.

## Properties

**+1 Shield.** While holding this shield, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. This bonus is in addition to the shield's normal bonus to AC.

**Deafening Chime.** When a creature hits you with a melee attack while you are holding this shield, the shield rings with an intensely loud, pure chime. The attacker and any number of creatures of your choice within 5 feet of you must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 15). On a failure, a creature takes 2d4 thunder damage and is deafened until the end of its next turn. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not deafened. The chime is audible out to 300 feet.

## Balance Notes
The Cathedral Chime Shield is a strong defensive/thorns hybrid. A *+1 shield* alone is an uncommon item (~30,000). Adding the chime effect — which deals modest damage (avg 5 thunder) and inflicts deafened on potentially multiple targets every time you're hit in melee — pushes it firmly into rare territory. Compared to a *shield of missile attraction* (rare, attunement, ~250,000), this is more offensively oriented but limited to melee triggers. The deafened condition is particularly valuable against spellcasters (blocks verbal components for spells with verbal-only components that don't have other component options). Priced at 300,000, it's appropriate for a rare defensive item with an automatic retaliatory rider.
