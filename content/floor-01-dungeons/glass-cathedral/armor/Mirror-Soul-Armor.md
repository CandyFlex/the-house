# Mirror-Soul Armor
*Armor (plate), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Full plate armor whose every surface is a mirror — not mirror-polished steel, but actual glass mirrors set into an unbreakable crystalline matrix. The breastplate reflects whoever stands before you; the pauldrons reflect the ceiling; the gauntlets reflect the hands of anyone who reaches toward you. Found standing upright in the center of the Confessional of Echoes, empty but posed like a guardian — the last keeper of the Cathedral had stepped out of it and never stepped back in. When a creature meets its own reflection in this armor, it does not see the face it expects. The mirrors show not what the creature looks like, but what it *is* — its sins and virtues rendered in distorted, expressionist light: a murderer sees a murderer's face staring back; a coward sees wide, terrified eyes; a hero sees something that makes them stand a little taller. Most cannot bear the sight.

## Properties

**+2 Armor.** You have a +2 bonus to AC while wearing this armor.

**Soul-Reflection.** The mirrors in this armor do not reflect light — they reflect the inner self of any creature that looks upon them. You are immune to the frightened condition while wearing this armor, as the mirrors catch fear before it can reach your mind and reflect it back outward as nothing more than a distorted image, harmless and contained.

**Dreadful Mirror.** When a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to force it to confront its own reflection in the armor's surface. The creature must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 17). On a failure, it is overcome by what it sees — it becomes frightened of you for 1 minute. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. On a success, the reflection still wounds it psychically: the creature takes 2d6 psychic damage but is not frightened.

A creature that does not have a sense of self (such as most constructs, mindless undead, and some aberrations) is unaffected by this property — the mirrors find nothing to reflect.

## Balance Notes
A +2 plate armor alone would be a very rare item (~2,500,000+). Adding fear immunity (comparable to a *ring of mind shielding*'s psychic protection but narrower) and a reaction-based fear/psychic damage retaliatory effect pushes it firmly into the upper very rare tier. The Dreadful Mirror is effectively a single-target, melee-only, reaction-speed *fear* spell with a damage rider on successful saves — potent against melee brutes but limited to 5-foot range and one trigger per round. At 3,500,000, it competes with *+3 armor* (legendary) in effective AC for heavy armor users, trading a point of AC for fear immunity and a powerful retaliation effect. Particularly devastating on paladins and fighters who expect to be in melee and can leverage the fear condition for control.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Reflective Half-Plate:** Rare. +1 half plate, immune to frightened, Dreadful Mirror deals 1d6 psychic on a failed save with no frightened rider. Cost: 350,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Soul-Mirror:** Legendary. +3 plate, Dreadful Mirror has no range limit (any creature that hits you with an attack), WIS DC 19, and creatures frightened this way take 2d6 psychic damage at the start of each turn they remain frightened. Cost: 12,000,000 soul stones.
