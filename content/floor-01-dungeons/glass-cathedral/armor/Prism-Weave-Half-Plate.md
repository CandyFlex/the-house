# Prism-Weave Half-Plate
*Armor (half plate), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Half-plate armor assembled from hundreds of overlapping glass scales, each a different hue — ruby, sapphire, emerald, amethyst, citrine, diamond-clear. The scales shift and ripple with a life of their own, reordering themselves in response to the wearer's will. Found draped across the altar of the Prism Apse, where it had been laid as an offering by a paladin who walked into the light and never walked back out. When sunlight strikes the armor, the entire Prism Apse floods with dancing color — the armor is never the same shade twice, a kaleidoscope made solid.

## Properties

**+1 Armor.** You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor.

**Prismatic Shift.** As a bonus action, you can shift the armor's dominant hue, attuning its protective glass to a specific wavelength of light. You can choose one of the following hues. The effect lasts until you use this property again — you can change hues only once per turn.

- **Crimson (Red).** You gain resistance to fire damage.
- **Cerulean (Blue).** You gain resistance to cold damage.
- **Verdant (Green).** You gain resistance to poison damage, and you have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned.
- **Violet.** You gain resistance to psychic damage.
- **Diamond (Clear).** You do not gain a resistance. Instead, the armor's +1 bonus becomes a +2 bonus to AC. Light passes through the armor unaltered, and you gain advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made while in bright light as your form becomes harder to visually track.

## Balance Notes
The Prism-Weave is the rare counterpart to *armor of resistance* (rare, flat resistance to one type, no attunement) but trades the permanent resistance for flexibility — you can adapt to threats round by round. The Clear mode (+2 total AC, Stealth advantage in bright light) gives it a unique offensive/defensive utility flip. At 350,000, it's priced above standard *+1 armor* (rare, ~250,000) because the adaptive resistance covers four common damage types, and the Clear mode provides both AC and situational Stealth. The once-per-turn limitation prevents mid-reaction resistance swapping, keeping it fair at the table.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Dyed Lamellar:** Uncommon. +1 scale mail, only two hues (Red: fire resist, Blue: cold resist). Cost: 50,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Full Prism Plate:** Very Rare. +2 plate armor, five hues plus a sixth — Obsidian (Black): necrotic and radiant resistance. Can change hue as a reaction when targeted by a spell. Cost: 3,500,000 soul stones.
