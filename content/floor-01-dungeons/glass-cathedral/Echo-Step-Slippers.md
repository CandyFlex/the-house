# Echo-Step Slippers
*Wondrous item (boots), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Slippers of pale, glass-smooth leather that catch the light like polished bone. The soles are etched with a mosaic pattern — the same mirror-tile design that covers the Nave's floor, reproduced in miniature. Found on the feet of a long-dead penitent kneeling in the center of the Nave's mirrored mosaic, forever frozen mid-step. When you walk in them, your footsteps are unnervingly silent — but something walks behind you, following the path you just left, a ghost of glass and sound that unravels when touched.

## Properties

**Silent Echoes.** When you move on your turn, you leave a silent, shimmering afterimage in each 5-foot square you exit. An afterimage looks like a translucent, frozen copy of you mid-stride. Afterimages last until the end of your next turn. You can create afterimages only once per turn.

**Disorienting Resonance.** When a creature enters a square occupied by one of your afterimages for the first time on its turn, the afterimage unravels in a burst of psychic feedback drawn from all the voices trapped in the Cathedral's glass. The creature takes 1d4 psychic damage, and the afterimage is destroyed. A creature that cannot see (such as one affected by the blinded condition) is unaffected by this damage, as it relies on the disorienting visual echo.

---

## Balance Notes
A battlefield control item that incentivizes movement and positioning. At only 1d4 damage per trigger, it's not a primary damage source — it's area denial and soft punishment for enemies pursuing you. Comparable to *boots of striding and springing* (uncommon, attunement, 25,000) but swaps mobility for minor control. The 1/turn limitation and small damage die keep it in uncommon territory. Priced at 40,000 (upper uncommon) because the automatic no-action damage has consistent value across an adventuring day, unlike limited-use items. Particularly strong on monks and rogues with high movement speeds and bonus action disengage.

---

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Whisper Slippers:** Common. No damage — afterimages grant advantage on Stealth checks made against creatures that have seen one in the past round, as they cannot tell which image is real. Cost: 15,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Resonance Walkers:** Rare. 2d4 psychic damage, afterimages last until the end of your *next* next turn (2 rounds), and a creature that ends its turn in an afterimage's square must make a WIS save or be deafened for 1 round. Cost: 275,000 soul stones.
