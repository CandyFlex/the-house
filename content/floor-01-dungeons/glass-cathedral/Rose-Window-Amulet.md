# Rose Window Amulet
*Wondrous item (amulet), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A palm-sized silver roundel framing a miniature stained-glass rose window — concentric rings of ruby, topaz, emerald, sapphire, and amethyst petals radiating from a diamond-clear center. The glass is impossibly fine, each petal no wider than a fingernail, cut with the precision of the Cathedral's own master glazier. Found hanging from a chain in the Rose Window's central oculus, where it had been catching and diffusing dawn light for so long that it no longer needs an external source — it glows on its own, a tiny eternal sunrise. When held, the amulet pulses with a warmth that feels like standing in a sunbeam on a cold day.

## Properties

**Radiant Hum.** While wearing this amulet, you shed dim light in a 10-foot radius. The light's color can be any hue you choose, and you can change it as a bonus action.

**Rose Window Benedictions.** The amulet has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing it, you can expend charges to cast the following spells from the amulet, requiring no material components and without the need for concentration (the amulet itself maintains the spell's focus):

- **1 charge:** *bless* (1st-level version, up to three creatures)
- **2 charges:** *beacon of hope*
- **3 charges:** *aura of vitality* (appears as a 30-foot aura of gently pulsing prismatic light)

When you cast *bless* from the amulet, the blessed creatures' eyes briefly flash the color of the light you currently shed. When you cast *aura of vitality*, the healing pulse appears as a cascade of falling colored light motes — like the Rose Window dismantled into rain.

---

## Balance Notes
The defining feature is concentration-free casting of three staple support spells, which is enormously powerful. A *necklace of prayer beads* (rare, ~250,000) provides one-shot spell casts without concentration but with random bead selection. This amulet gives guaranteed spell access with recharging charges, concentration-free. The tradeoff: *aura of vitality* costs 3 of 5 charges (you can only afford one per day, leaving 2 for bless), and *beacon of hope* is situational. Priced at 350,000 (upper-mid rare) because the concentration economy break on *bless* alone justifies a premium — a party-wide +1d4 to attacks and saves without occupying the cleric's concentration slot is a fight-winning advantage. Comparable to a *staff of healing* (rare, attunement, ~300,000) but more versatile at lower charge efficiency.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Pane Amulet:** Uncommon. 3 charges, regain 1d3 at dawn. Only *bless* (1 charge) available, no concentration. No glow. Cost: 45,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Rose Reliquary:** Very Rare. 8 charges, regain 1d6+2. Adds *death ward* (4 charges) and *greater restoration* (5 charges). All still concentration-free. Cost: 3,000,000 soul stones.
