# Prismatic Focus
*Wondrous item (arcane focus), rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

---

## Description
A crystal of pure prismatic glass, cut into an elongated octagonal prism roughly the length of a human hand. White light entering one end fractures and exits the other as a fan of seven distinct colors, each perfectly separated. Found on a workbench in the Prism Apse, surrounded by notes in a dead language — diagrams of light rays being bent, split, and weaponized. A wizard had spent decades here learning to turn prayer-light into artillery. The focus hums faintly when held, not with sound but with the vibration of light itself, a frequency that exists somewhere between color and music. When you cast through it, your spells don't merely strike — they arrive in the color of your choosing, as if the Cathedral itself has tinted them with stained-glass purpose.

## Properties

**+1 Focus.** You gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls made while using this arcane focus. This bonus does not apply to spell save DC.

**Chromatic Refraction.** When you cast a spell that deals damage, you can change the spell's damage type to one of the following: radiant, fire, cold, lightning, acid, or poison. You must choose the new damage type when you cast the spell. This does not change any other aspect of the spell (area, range, saving throw, etc.). You can use this property at will.

**Spectral Overload.** Once per long rest, when you cast a spell whose damage type you change using Chromatic Refraction, the spell is infused with excess prismatic energy. The spell deals an additional 1d6 damage of the newly chosen type to each creature it damages. You must decide to use this property before you roll damage.

## Balance Notes
A *+1 arcane focus* alone is roughly an uncommon item (~25,000). The at-will damage type change is comparable to the Transmuted Spell metamagic (1 sorcery point per use) but unlimited, making it competitive with the Metamagic Adept feat in value. Stacking with *elemental adept* lets casters bypass resistances consistently. The 1/long rest +1d6 is modest — equivalent to a single *hex* proc per day. At 275,000, it's priced between a *wand of the war mage +1* (uncommon, ~25,000) and a *wand of the war mage +2* (rare, ~250,000), with the added versatility of type-changing taking the place of the +2 to attack. Best on evocation wizards, sorcerers, and warlocks who face varied resistances.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Tinted Crystal:** Uncommon. No +1 bonus, Chromatic Refraction only (no Spectral Overload), and you can only change to fire, cold, or lightning. Cost: 40,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Prism:** Very Rare. +1 to spell attacks and +1 to spell save DC, all seven Chromatic Refraction types, Spectral Overload adds +2d6 and can be used twice per long rest. Cost: 2,500,000 soul stones.
