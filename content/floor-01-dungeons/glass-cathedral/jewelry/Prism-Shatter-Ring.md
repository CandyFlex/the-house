# Prism-Shatter Ring
*Ring, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A band of twisted silver cradling a single prism of pale, cracked glass — cracked not from damage but from the pressure of the light trapped inside it, which has been trying to escape since before the Cathedral was built. The prism pulses faintly in a slow, rhythmic beat, like a second heart on your finger, and each pulse sends a different color racing through the fractures — red, then orange, then yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet, and occasionally a flash of clear that seems to erase whatever it touches. Found in the Prism Apse, set into the altar's keystone, where it had been focusing the Cathedral's collected light for millennia. To wear it is to carry a captive rainbow on your hand, and when you release it — when you finally let the light out — it comes screaming like broken glass riding a sunbeam.

## Properties

**Prismatic Cataclysm.** Once per long rest, as an action, you can release the captive light within the ring. The ring unleashes a 60-foot cone of prismatic glass shards — a storm of razor-edged light that tears through everything in its path. Each creature in the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 17). On a failed save, a creature takes 8d6 slashing damage from the glass shards and is struck by one random color effect. On a success, it takes half the slashing damage and suffers no color effect.

Roll a d8 to determine which color effect applies to each creature that failed its save:

| d8 | Color | Effect |
|----|-------|--------|
| 1 | **Red** | The creature takes an additional 2d6 fire damage as the shards ignite on impact. |
| 2 | **Orange** | The creature takes an additional 2d6 acid damage as the shards dissolve into caustic light. |
| 3 | **Yellow** | The creature takes an additional 2d6 lightning damage and cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn. |
| 4 | **Green** | The creature takes an additional 2d6 poison damage and must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn. |
| 5 | **Blue** | The creature takes an additional 2d6 cold damage and its speed is halved until the end of its next turn. |
| 6 | **Indigo** | The creature is restrained by crystalline bands of light. It can use an action to make a DC 17 Strength check, breaking free on a success. |
| 7 | **Violet** | The creature is blinded. It can make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. |
| 8 | **Clear** | The creature becomes invisible for 1 round — but unwillingly. The invisibility is disorienting: the creature has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn. The effect ends early if the creature attacks or casts a spell. |

---

## Balance Notes
A very rare single-use-per-day AoE ring. The base damage (8d6 slashing, avg 28) is equivalent to a 4th-level *cone of cold* in raw numbers, but the DEX save (easier to pass than CON) and the random rider effect give it versatility. The color effect table makes each use feel unique and keeps the DM engaged. Compared to a *ring of spell storing* (rare, ~250,000) which can hold a 5th-level spell, this ring only does one thing but does it with more panache. At 3,000,000, it's priced comparably to a *wand of fireballs* (rare, ~300,000) extrapolated to very rare burst potential — it does more damage in one massive cone, but only once per day. The random rider prevents it from being min-maxed (you can't guarantee restrained or blinded). The Clear result (invisibility) is a whimsical wildcard that can be tactically beneficial or detrimental depending on positioning.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Shattered Prism Ring:** Rare. 1/long rest, 30-ft cone, 4d6 slashing, d6 color table (no Indigo, Violet, or Clear). Cost: 300,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Prism Cataclysm:** Legendary. 2/long rest, 90-ft cone, 10d6 slashing + 3d6 rider damage, color table includes an additional "Prismatic Echo" effect that repeats the color effect at the start of the creature's next turn. Cost: 12,000,000 soul stones.
