# Mirror-Kin Ring
*Ring, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A band of silver so highly polished it appears liquid, set with a flat disc of obsidian-black glass. When you look into the glass, you do not see your own face — you see a mirror-reversed version of yourself staring back with subtle, unsettling differences: hair parted the other way, weapon in the opposite hand. Found in the Confessional of Echoes, resting on the penitent's bench, where someone long ago confessed to being two people at once. Once per rest, the ring pulls that other-you through the glass, a perfect mirror-double that takes the blow meant for you and shatters into a thousand cutting accusations.

## Properties

**Shattering Double.** As a reaction when you are targeted by a ranged attack or a spell that targets only you (not an area effect), you can cause a glassy duplicate of yourself to emerge from the ring's disc and appear in an unoccupied space of your choice within 5 feet of you. The duplicate is a perfect mirror image — it looks exactly like you but mirrored left-to-right. The triggering attack or spell must then be resolved, but there is a 50% chance it strikes the duplicate instead: roll a d20; on an 11 or higher, the attack or spell targets the duplicate. On a 10 or lower, it targets you as normal.

Whether struck or not, the duplicate shatters after the attack or spell is resolved, spraying glass shards in all directions. Each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the duplicate's space must make a Dexterity saving throw. The save DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d6 piercing damage; on a success, it takes half damage.

Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

---

## Balance Notes
A defensive reaction ring that blends *mirror image*, the Lucky feat's attack-redirect logic, and a minor damage rider. The 50% chance (d20 roll 11+) is worse than the 67-75% miss chance of a fully-stacked *mirror image* but costs no action and works against both weapon attacks and single-target spells. At 1/short rest, it's a clutch defensive tool. The 1d6 AoE shatter is a minor bonus (avg 3.5 damage). Priced at 250,000 — comparable to a *ring of protection* (rare, 250,000, +1 AC/saves) but trading the constant passive for a powerful once-per-rest reactive defense. Balanced by the randomness (50% gamble) and the short-rest cooldown.

---

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Glass Doubles Ring:** Uncommon. Same effect but 1/long rest, no shatter damage, and the duplicate cannot redirect spells — only ranged weapon attacks. Cost: 35,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Hall-of-Mirrors Ring:** Very Rare. 2/short rest, the duplicate has a 75% chance (d20: 6+ to redirect), and the shatter deals 2d6 piercing + 1d6 psychic. Cost: 2,000,000 soul stones.
