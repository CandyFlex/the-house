# Cathedral-Keeper's Staff
*Weapon (quarterstaff), very rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

---

## Description
A six-foot rod of pale, resonant crystal wrapped in silver wire at the grip — the original tuning fork used by the Cathedral's first Keeper to calibrate the entire structure's harmonic frequency. One end is blunt, worn from being struck against the floor thousands of times; the other tapers to a rounded point that hums at a different note depending on which direction it points. Found in the Bell Tower's highest chamber, held in the skeletal hand of the first Keeper, who had died conducting a symphony that would keep the Cathedral standing for eternity. When held, the staff vibrates in sympathy with every sound in the building — footsteps register as faint pulses, shouts as sharp jabs, and the ambient resonance of the glass itself as a steady, reassuring thrum. The keeper who holds it does not command the Cathedral so much as negotiate with it, and the staff is the only language the glass understands.

## Properties

**+2 Weapon.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Resonance Resistance.** While holding this staff, you have resistance to thunder damage. The staff absorbs the energy of sound-based attacks, vibrating in harmonic counterpoint rather than breaking.

**Cathedral Harmonics.** The staff has 10 charges and regains 1d8 + 2 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding it, you can use an action to expend the required number of charges to cast one of the following spells from the staff (spell save DC 17, spell attack bonus +9):

- **1 charge:** *shatter* (2nd-level version)
- **2 charges:** *silence*
- **3 charges:** *clairvoyance* (the sensor appears as a floating crystalline eye of translucent glass)
- **4 charges:** *resonating echo* — functions as *destructive wave* (5th-level) but the damage types are thunder and radiant only (your choice of one or equal split), and the saving throw is Constitution
- **5 charges:** *wall of force* (appears as a shimmering, resonant glass barrier — transparent but humming audibly, and any creature that touches the wall takes 1d4 thunder damage from the vibration)

If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff shatters into a burst of deafening sound: each creature within 30 feet of you must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 6d8 thunder damage on a failure or half as much on a success. The staff is destroyed.

## Balance Notes
A very rare spellcaster staff comparable to a *staff of power* (very rare, ~4,000,000). Both are +2 quarterstaffs with 10 charges, but the spell lists diverge significantly: this trades *magic missile*, *fireball*, *lightning bolt*, etc. for thunder/resonance-themed spells capped by *wall of force*. The *wall of force* inclusion (normally a 5th-level spell, 5 charges) is extremely powerful and accounts for much of the 4,000,000 valuation. Thunder resistance adds defensive utility. The shatter-on-1 risk is standard for charged staves. Slightly more utility-focused than the *staff of power* (which leans combat), the Cathedral-Keeper's Staff rewards battlefield control and siege-breaking.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Acolyte's Staff:** Rare. +1 quarterstaff, 6 charges (regain 1d4+2), only *shatter* (1), *silence* (2), and *clairvoyance* (3). No thunder resistance. Cost: 350,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — High Keeper's Staff:** Legendary. +3 quarterstaff, 15 charges (regain 1d10+5), adds *glass storm* (6 charges, custom 6th-level spell — 40-ft radius of flying glass shards, DEX save or 8d8 slashing + 4d8 thunder), and the shatter-on-1 deals 10d8 thunder in 60-ft radius instead. Cost: 15,000,000 soul stones.
