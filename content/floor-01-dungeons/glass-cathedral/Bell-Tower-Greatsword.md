# Bell-Tower Greatsword
*Weapon (greatsword), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A greatsword whose blade was cast from the Bell Tower's own bronze bell — melted down and reforged into a slab of ringing metal five feet long, veined with crystalline inlays that carry vibration like nerves carry pain. The blade never stops humming; rest it point-down on stone, and the stone begins to sing. Found driven into the Bell Tower's floor like a stake through a heart, the metal still warm from the last time it was rung. Swinging it is like swinging a church bell on a chain — slow, ponderous, inevitable. And every time it strikes, the entire Cathedral knows. The peal rolls outward in concentric shockwaves, and for seconds afterward, anyone standing too close to where the blow landed feels their teeth rattle and their ears ring with the lingering aftershock.

## Properties

**+2 Weapon.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Thunderous Peal.** Each time you hit a creature with this weapon, the impact creates a thunderous peal audible out to 300 feet. The peal does not deal immediate damage beyond the weapon's normal attack — but it lingers. At the start of your next turn (or at the start of the struck creature's next turn, whichever comes first), the lingering vibration erupts: each creature of your choice within 10 feet of the target you struck (including the target itself, if it is still within 10 feet) takes 1d8 thunder damage. If you have struck the same target multiple times before your next turn, the peals do not stack — a creature within overlapping 10-foot zones takes the 1d8 damage only once.

**Toll the Bell.** Once per long rest, as an action, you can slam the blade point-first into the ground. The impact creates a 30-foot radius sphere of deafening, earth-shaking resonance centered on the blade, which lasts for 1 minute or until you use an action to pull the blade free (ending the effect early). For the duration, the blade remains embedded and you cannot make attacks with it, but the area pulses with destructive sound. At the start of each of your turns, each creature in the area must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 17). On a failure, a creature takes 3d6 thunder damage and is deafened until the start of your next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and is not deafened. You are immune to the deafening effect of this property, and you have resistance to thunder damage while within the area.

## Balance Notes
A very rare weapon with two distinct thunder-themed effects. The delayed 1d8 AoE on every hit provides consistent bonus damage (avg 4.5 per hit) but requires enemies to remain clustered — tactically interesting and not guaranteed. The Toll the Bell ability is a 1/long rest battlefield control nuke: 3d6 (avg 10.5) per round for up to 10 rounds in a 30-ft radius, but at the cost of disarming yourself for the duration (no greatsword attacks). You can end it early to regain your weapon. At 3,000,000, it's priced alongside other very rare weapons like the *frost brand* (very rare, ~2,000,000) and *nine lives stealer* (very rare, ~3,000,000). The attunement cost and the self-disarm during Toll the Bell keep it from being an auto-pick over a simpler +3 weapon.
