# Stained-Soul Lens
*Wondrous item (eye lens), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A single oval monocle of stained glass no thicker than a pane of ice, rimmed in tarnished brass. The glass is sectioned into microscopic panes — each the width of a spider's thread — tinted in every color that has ever passed through the Rose Window at dawn. Found resting on the sill of the Rose Window itself, still warm from the morning light. When worn over one eye, the world resolves into color-coded truth — souls cannot hide from the spectrum.

## Properties

**Soul-Sight.** While wearing this lens over one eye, you see the true moral and ethical alignment of any creature you look at as a faint, colored aura surrounding their form. Creatures of good alignment glow with warm tones (gold, amber, rose); evil creatures radiate cool, bruised colors (violet-black, sickly green, cold blue). Lawful creatures appear with structured, geometric auras; chaotic creatures flicker with shifting, fluid auras. This functions as simultaneous *detect evil and good* and *detect law and chaos* for any creature within your line of sight, but the effect is entirely visual — you must see the creature, and the auras do not penetrate total cover or magical invisibility.

**Stained Light.** Once per long rest, as an action, you can cast *color spray* at 2nd level from the lens. The spray erupts as a 15-foot cone of kaleidoscopic light — every color the Rose Window has ever known. The spell's save DC is equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier (your choice when you attune).

---

## Balance Notes
A utility-focused uncommon item. The at-will alignment detection is powerful for social/investigative play but purely visual (requires line of sight, blocked by cover/invisibility), which prevents it from trivializing hidden threats. The 1/long rest *color spray* at 2nd level (6d10 hit points of blinding) provides a modest combat escape option. At 30,000 soul stones, it's priced similarly to a *lantern of revealing* (30,000) — a detection item of comparable tier. Stronger than *gem of brightness* (25,000) in detection but weaker in combat.

---

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Tinted Lens:** Common. Only the alignment-detection property (no *color spray*). Cost: 10,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Rose-Window Lens:** Rare. Alignment detection extends to 60 ft, and *color spray* can be cast 2/long rest at 3rd level. Additionally, 1/long rest you can cast *see invisibility*. Cost: 275,000 soul stones.
