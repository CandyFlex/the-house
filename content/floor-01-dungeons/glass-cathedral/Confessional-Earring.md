# Confessional Earring
*Wondrous item (earring), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A single earring of spun glass — a droplet of frozen sound, caught mid-vibration and shaped into a teardrop. It is always cold against the earlobe, no matter how warm the room, and if you hold perfectly still you can hear the faintest whisper inside it: a murmur of overlapping voices too distant and too numerous to distinguish. Found hanging from the grille inside the Confessional of Echoes, where it had absorbed every lie and every truth spoken through the lattice for untold centuries. The earring cannot repeat what it has heard — but it knows the difference between the two, and it vibrates ever so slightly when someone nearby is trying to deceive you.

## Properties

**Lie-Resonance.** When a creature within 30 feet of you speaks a deliberate falsehood, the earring vibrates subtly — a sensation only you can feel. You know that a lie was spoken somewhere within range, but you do not know who spoke it, what was said, or what the truth would be. The earring detects intentional deception only; it does not detect statements the speaker genuinely believes to be true, even if they are factually incorrect, nor does it detect omissions, half-truths, or unspoken deceptions.

**Cathedral's Confession.** Once per long rest, as an action, you can pose a single question to the empty air. The accumulated whispers of every voice ever heard within the Glass Cathedral coalesce into a response — a single word of truth, spoken in a chorus of crystalline voices directly into your mind. This functions as the *commune* spell with one question, but without the ritual casting, without the 25% cumulative failure chance, and without contacting a deity. The Cathedral itself answers — which means its knowledge is limited to what has been spoken, whispered, sung, or confessed within its walls. Questions about events, places, or people with no connection to the Cathedral's history may receive no answer.

## Balance Notes
A social/investigation item. The passive lie detection is significantly weaker than *zone of truth* (2nd-level, save-every-round, reveals the liar) or the *ring of truth telling* (homebrew territory) — you know a lie was told nearby but nothing more, which prevents it from trivializing social encounters. The 1/long rest *commune*-equivalent is powerful but scope-limited — it can only answer about things connected to the Cathedral's history. At 200,000 (low rare), it's priced below combat rares because its value is heavily campaign- and DM-dependent. A DM running intrigue-heavy play will find this item far more valuable than the price suggests; a combat-focused table may never use it. Comparable to a *medallion of thoughts* (uncommon, ~25,000) with a heavy thematic upgrade.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Whisper-Earring:** Uncommon. Only the passive lie detection (no *commune*), range reduced to 15 feet. Cost: 30,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Oracle's Earring:** Very Rare. 3/long rest Cathedral's Confession (one question each), the passive detection reveals which creature lied if it's within line of sight, and 1/long rest you can cast *legend lore* using the earring. Cost: 2,500,000 soul stones.
