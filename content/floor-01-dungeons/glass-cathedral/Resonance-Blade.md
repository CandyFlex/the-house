# Resonance Blade
*Weapon (longsword), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A longsword whose blade is forged from resonant crystal rather than steel — translucent, faintly blue-white, humming at a frequency just above the threshold of hearing. The edge is ground so fine that it vibrates visibly when held still. Found set into the Bell Tower's central pillar, where centuries of ringing had tempered it with sympathetic vibration. With each strike against the same foe, the blade's hum deepens, building from a whisper to a roar — by the third blow, the air around it shimmers with standing waves, and the sound alone can crack bone before the edge even lands.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Escalating Resonance.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, the blade's harmonic frequency attunes to that creature. Each subsequent time you hit the same creature consecutively, the resonance intensifies: the second consecutive hit deals an extra 1d4 thunder damage, the third deals an extra 2d4 thunder damage, and the fourth and any subsequent consecutive hits deal an extra 3d4 thunder damage. The resonance resets (returning to no bonus damage) if you attack a different target, miss with an attack, or if you go a full round without attacking that creature. The blade emits an audible hum that grows louder with each tier of resonance — at 3d4, the hum is clearly audible within 60 feet.

## Balance Notes
Compared to a *+2 weapon* (rare, no attunement, flat +2 to hit/damage), the Resonance Blade is only +1 to hit but can reach +1d4–+3d4 bonus damage on sustained single-target focus. At the 3d4 cap (average +7.5), it significantly outdamages a +2 weapon, but only against a single target and only after three consecutive hits — a demanding requirement in practice. The attunement requirement and reset-on-miss clause prevent it from being an always-on damage boost. Priced at 250,000 (mid-rare), it's between a *flame tongue* (rare, 2d6 fire always, ~300,000) and a standard *+2 weapon* (~200,000). Rewards patient, accurate martials.
