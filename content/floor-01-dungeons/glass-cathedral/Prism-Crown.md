# Prism Crown
*Wondrous item (crown), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A circlet of shifting, living stained glass — each pane a different color, constantly rearranging itself in a slow, silent kaleidoscope around your head. The glass panes are separated by hair-thin lead lines that glow faintly, as if lit from within by the same light that passes through the Rose Window at noon. Found resting on the altar in the heart of the Prism Apse, where it had been waiting for a head to crown since the Cathedral was built. Wearing it, the world snaps into perfect clarity — illusions peel away like dead skin, invisible creatures stand revealed in their true colors, and when you cast a spell the crown reaches out with tendrils of colored light and pulls your magic in two directions at once, duplicating the effect as naturally as a prism splits a sunbeam.

## Properties

**Prismatic Truesight.** While wearing the crown, you have truesight out to a range of 30 feet. Within this radius, you see things as they truly are — invisible creatures and objects are visible to you, you automatically detect visual illusions and succeed on saving throws against them, you perceive the original form of shapechangers and creatures transformed by magic, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane. The truesight is manifested as a faint prismatic halo that overlays your vision — creatures you perceive with truesight appear limned in shifting rainbow light perceptible only to you.

**Chromatic Splitting.** When you cast a spell of 1st level or higher that targets a single creature, the crown splits the magic — prism-like — allowing it to target one additional creature. The additional target must be within 30 feet of the original target and must be a creature the spell is otherwise capable of targeting (for example, a spell that targets "a creature you can see" can only split to another creature you can see within 30 feet of the first). The spell is duplicated exactly — same level, same damage, same effects — against both targets, using a single concentration if the spell requires it. You can use this property a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

**Coronal Flare.** Once per long rest, as an action, you can cast *prismatic spray* (7th-level) from the crown. The spell's saving throw DC is 17. The spray erupts from the crown itself, manifesting as a 60-foot cone of multicolored light rays — each ray a different pane of the crown detaching and firing outward.

## Balance Notes
An exceptionally powerful very rare item for casters. Truesight at 30 ft replicates a 6th-level *true seeing* spell (but shorter range) passively — this alone is worth 1,500,000+ on a wondrous item. Chromatic Splitting is a no-sorcery-point-cost Twinned Spell usable PB times per long rest — the Metamagic Adept feat gives 2 sorcery points (2 twinned 1st-level spells) as a feat, while this gives up to 6 uses at high levels for no resource cost. Coronal Flare adds a free 7th-level spell once per day. At 3,500,000, it's priced among the upper tier of very rare caster items, comparable to a *staff of power* (very rare, ~4,000,000) but more focused on spell augmentation than raw blasting. The spell-splitting limitation (must be single-target, within 30 ft) prevents it from doubling *disintegrate* across a room.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Diadem of Refraction:** Rare. Truesight 15 ft, Chromatic Splitting 1/long rest, no Coronal Flare. Cost: 350,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Crown of Colors:** Legendary. Truesight 60 ft, Chromatic Splitting at will (no usage limit), Coronal Flare can be cast 2/long rest, and 1/long rest *prismatic wall*. Cost: 20,000,000 soul stones.
