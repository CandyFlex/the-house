# Harmonic Bow
*Weapon (longbow), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A longbow of pale, resonant glasswood — the limbs carved from a single crystalline branch that grew through the Choir Loft's vaulted ceiling, never touching soil. Where a bowstring should be, there is only a taut line of visible vibration: a standing wave of pure sound, humming at middle C. Found in the Choir Loft, propped against the conductor's podium, as if the choirmaster had stepped away mid-rehearsal and never returned. Drawing the "string" back creates no tension in the arm — only an ascending note that deepens until release, at which point the bow fires not arrows but bolts of compressed, visible sound: shimmering projectiles that scream across the battlefield and detonate on impact in a deafening crack.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Sonic Bolts.** The Harmonic Bow does not require physical ammunition. When you make a ranged attack with it, you fire a bolt of compressed sound. Make a ranged spell attack using your Dexterity modifier (proficiency bonus applies if you are proficient with longbows). On a hit, the target takes 1d8 thunder damage (instead of the weapon's normal piercing damage). The bow has a normal range of 150 feet and a long range of 600 feet. Attacks made with the bow at long range do not have disadvantage in areas of *silence*, as the sound bolts simply fail to form — the attack automatically misses.

**Harmonic Cascade.** Once per short rest, when you take the Attack action, you can fire a Harmonic Cascade shot instead of one of your attacks. The cascade fires three sonic bolts simultaneously — make a separate attack roll against up to three creatures you can see within range (no additional action cost). Each bolt that hits deals half the normal thunder damage (round down), but the impact creates a shockwave that pushes the target 10 feet directly away from you. The cascade consumes no ammunition beyond the single attack it replaces.

## Balance Notes
A ranged weapon for DEX characters that switches damage to thunder (less commonly resisted than piercing) and does not consume ammunition — a logistical benefit. The Cascade (1/short rest, 3 attacks at half damage) is effectively a multi-target *scorching ray* tuned down: normal hits deal 1d8+1 (avg 5.5), three half-damage hits deal ~2.75 each for ~8.25 total, comparable to two normal shots but spread across three targets with forced movement. At 300,000, it's priced between a *+2 longbow* (rare, ~250,000) and an *oathbow* (very rare) — the lack of ammunition requirement and the push utility offset the Cascade's limited uses and half-damage penalty. Strong on any DEX martial, particularly rangers and fighters who benefit from forced movement.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Whisper Bow:** Uncommon. +1 longbow, fires silent physical arrows that make no sound. 1/long rest: fire a shot that deals 1d6 bonus thunder damage on hit and the target cannot speak for 1 round. Cost: 50,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Resonator:** Very Rare. +2 longbow, 1d10 thunder per shot, Cascade fires 4 bolts at full damage with 15-ft push, and 2/short rest. Cost: 3,000,000 soul stones.
