# Silence-Keeper's Mantle
*Wondrous item (cloak), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A cloak woven from whisper-thin glass fibers — each strand finer than silk, translucent and cool to the touch. The fibers are spun so tightly that sound cannot pass between them; draped over your shoulders, the mantle swallows the noise of your own movements as efficiently as the Cathedral swallows a whispered prayer. Found folded neatly on the keeper's stool in the Confessional of Echoes, where the same person had sat for centuries, listening — never speaking. When you wear it, you understand why the keeper never spoke: the mantle whispers back, carrying the words of everyone who ever confessed within these glass walls, and if you listen closely, you can pick out a single voice and send your own words chasing after it across impossible distances.

## Properties

**Silent Step.** You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks that rely on moving silently, but only in environments that are naturally quiet or silent. In environments with ambient noise (wind, rain, crowd chatter, running water), the advantage does not apply — the mantle's noise-canceling properties are most effective when there is the least sound to cancel.

**Vow of Silence.** Once per long rest, as an action, you can cast *silence* (2nd-level) centered on yourself. The area of silence is immobile — it remains centered on the point where you cast it, even if you move. The spell otherwise functions as normal (20-foot radius sphere, no sound within, creatures are deafened, immune to thunder damage, verbal components impossible).

**Confessional Whisper.** While wearing the mantle, you can whisper a message of up to 25 words. The message travels as a crystalline echo through the ethereal resonance of the Glass Cathedral, reaching a single creature of your choice that you have met before, anywhere within 1 mile. The creature hears the whisper as if you were standing directly behind them, speaking in a voice of chiming glass — but they cannot reply. This property can be used at will, but only once per minute (the whispers need time to find their way through the Cathedral's infinite echoes).

## Balance Notes
A utility-heavy rare item with three distinct properties. The conditional Stealth advantage is weaker than a *cloak of elvenkind* (uncommon, ~25,000) but stacks with it in the right environment. The immobile *silence* is a tactical tool — great for locking down a room but you can't drag it with you, unlike the spell's normal mobile AOE. The one-way whisper is a thematic *sending* with shorter range, no reply, and no component cost. At 200,000 (lower-mid rare), it's priced between a *cloak of elvenkind* and a *cloak of the bat* (rare, 250,000). Best for scouts, infiltrators, and party faces in political/social campaigns.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Novice's Mantle:** Uncommon. Only the Silent Step property (no *silence*, no whisper). Cost: 30,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Arch-Keeper's Mantle:** Very Rare. All properties, plus the whisper becomes two-way (target can reply with up to 25 words), range increases to 10 miles, and the *silence* can be cast 2/long rest. Cost: 2,200,000 soul stones.
