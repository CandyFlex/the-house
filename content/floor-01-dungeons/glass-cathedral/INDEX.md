## Design Notes

All 20 items feature genuinely distinct mechanics — no two items share the same activation method, condition, and damage profile. The set is evenly split between light/color/prism-themed items and sound/resonance/echo/silence-themed items (10 each).

Soul stone costs are calibrated within the specified ranges:
- **Uncommon:** 25,000–40,000 (range: 15K–50K)
- **Rare:** 200,000–350,000 (range: 150K–500K)
- **Very Rare:** 2,500,000–4,000,000 (range: 1.5M–4.5M)

Every item includes: full YAML frontmatter, painterly description prose, image prompt, Properties section, Mechanics Block, Balance Notes, Variants (lower and higher rarity), and D&D Beyond Homebrew HTML block.
