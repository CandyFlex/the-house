# Glasswalker's Bracers
*Wondrous item (bracers), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A pair of bracers made from fused, melted cathedral glass — not the sharp, crystalline stuff of the walls, but glass that has been heated until it runs like honey, then cooled into something closer to liquid than solid. They shimmer with trapped heat, as if the glass is still flowing, still becoming. Found in the Nave, half-sunk into a pillar of solid glass — the previous wearer had reached inside to retrieve something, and the bracers alone remained when the rest of him was gone. Wearing them, you can push your hands through glass as though through still water, feeling the faint, cold tingle of centuries humming against your skin.

## Properties

**Glass Phasing (Hands).** Your hands and anything you hold can phase through glass, crystal, or magically transparent barriers as if those barriers did not exist. You can reach through windows to unlock doors from the other side, retrieve objects from within sealed glass cases, or make melee weapon attacks through glass walls, windows, or transparent barriers up to 1 foot thick. You cannot see through opaque barriers with this property — the bracers only enable passage, not vision, and you must know (or reasonably infer) what is on the other side.

When you make a melee attack through a glass or crystal barrier, the target has half cover (+2 AC) unless you can see it clearly, and you cannot be targeted by melee attacks in return through the same barrier (your arm simply passes back through harmlessly at the end of your attack).

**Full Immersion.** Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you can cause your entire body to become briefly insubstantial to glass. You can pass completely through a glass, crystal, or magically transparent barrier up to 1 foot thick. You emerge on the other side at the closest unoccupied space. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks. If the barrier is thicker than 1 foot, or if there is no unoccupied space on the other side, the property fails and the use is wasted.

## Balance Notes
A hyper-situational utility item that is game-changing in its niche and nearly useless outside it. At-will hand phasing through glass is a creative tool limited only by player ingenuity — in a dungeon filled with glass architecture, this is extraordinarily valuable. Outside glass-heavy environments, it provides little. The 1/long rest full-body phasing is a single-use *misty step* with a glass-door requirement. At 225,000 (lower-mid rare), it's priced below combat-power items of similar rarity because its power is entirely environmental. Comparable to *gloves of thievery* (uncommon, ~25,000) in utility niche but scaled up for its ability to bypass entirely what others must break or unlock. The Glass Cathedral itself is the bracers' natural habitat.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Glass-Touched Gloves:** Uncommon. Hands-only phasing (no Full Immersion), and barriers must be 6 inches thick or less. Cost: 35,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Glasswalker's Form:** Very Rare. Full Immersion usable 3/long rest, barriers up to 5 feet thick, and while phased you are invisible to creatures on the other side. Cost: 2,000,000 soul stones.
