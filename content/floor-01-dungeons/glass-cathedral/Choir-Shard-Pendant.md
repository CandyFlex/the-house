# Choir-Shard Pendant
*Wondrous item (necklace), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A teardrop of frozen glass no larger than a thumbprint, suspended from a whisper-thin silver chain. Trapped inside is a single perfect note — the last chord sung by the Cathedral's choir before the silence fell. Found in the Choir Loft, still warm from the echo of a hymn that hasn't been sung in centuries. When the pendant is near a voice, the glass ripples faintly, hungry for sound.

## Properties

**Silence the Voice.** As a reaction when a hostile creature within 30 feet of you speaks or produces a non-magical sound, you can force that creature to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature is silenced — it cannot speak, produce verbal components for spells, or make any intentional sound — until the end of its next turn. A creature that cannot be magically silenced (such as a creature under a *silence* spell or that is naturally mute) is unaffected. Once you use this property twice, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

---

## Balance Notes
Compared to *silence* (2nd-level spell, 20-ft radius, 10 minutes, concentration), this is a reaction-speed single-target 1-round silence with only two uses per rest. The action economy advantage is offset by the very short duration, single-target limitation, and low DC. At 35,000 soul stones, it sits in the upper-mid uncommon range — comparable to a *wand of magic detection* (30,000) but more combat-relevant. Primarily useful against spellcasters reliant on verbal components.

---

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Echo Shard:** Same mechanics but only 1 use per long rest. Cost: 18,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Chorister's Pendant:** Rare. 3 uses per short rest, DC 15, and the creature also takes 2d6 psychic damage on a failed save from the shattering feedback of its own stolen voice. Cost: 200,000 soul stones.
