# Shatterglass Dagger
*Weapon (dagger), uncommon*

---

## Description
A blade of pure cathedral glass — impossibly sharp, without hilt or crossguard, tapering from a needle-point to a grip wrapped in pale leather. When the light hits it right, you can see the reflection of the Nave's vaulted ceiling trapped forever in the glass, a ghost-image of soaring arches and fractured windows. Found embedded in the mirrored floor mosaic of the Nave, where it had fallen from a shattered saint's hand. The edge sings faintly when drawn — a single, sustained note just below human hearing.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Glass-Shatter Strike.** When you score a critical hit with this dagger, the blade erupts in a burst of razor-edged shards. The target takes an additional 2d4 slashing damage as the glass explodes outward, and the dagger is destroyed — it ceases to exist until the start of your next turn, during which time you cannot use it to make attacks or benefit from its +1 bonus. At the start of your next turn, the dagger reforms in your hand (or at your feet if your hand is occupied), whole and undamaged, its song restored.

## Balance Notes
A +1 weapon with a moderate crit-fishing bonus. The +2d4 on crit (average +5) is slightly better than the standard Brutal Critical effect but comes with a real cost — you lose the dagger for the remainder of the round (no opportunity attacks, no off-hand bonus action attacks, no Extra Attack follow-ups). Compared to a basic *+1 dagger* (uncommon, ~20,000), the 5,000 premium is fair for an effect that fires only 5% of the time and carries a tactical downside. Sits at the low end of uncommon weapon pricing.

## Variants
- **Lower Rarity — Cracked Dagger:** Common. +1 to attack rolls only (no damage bonus), same shatter mechanic but only +1d4 on crit. Cost: 12,000 soul stones.
- **Higher Rarity — Cathedral Shardblade:** Rare. +2 dagger, +3d4 on crit, and the shattering creates a 5-ft burst affecting all adjacent creatures. Cost: 250,000 soul stones.
