# Sporewalker's Cloak
*Wondrous item (cloak), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A hooded cloak woven from the fibrous caps of giant depth-mushrooms, dyed in shifting bands of purple and teal that catch light like a spilled slick. The fabric is soft and slightly damp, and it smells faintly of petrichor and sweet rot. When danger draws close, the cloak exhales — a thin veil of spores that hangs in the air like a second skin.

## Properties

**Spore-Ward.** While wearing this cloak, you have resistance to poison damage and advantage on saving throws against being poisoned.

**Choking Escape (1/Short Rest).** When you take damage from a creature within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to release a dense cloud of fungal spores into your space. Until the start of your next turn, your space and each space within 5 feet of you is heavily obscured. You can immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks as part of this reaction.

## Balance Notes
Poison resistance and advantage on poisoned saving throws is a solid defensive package at uncommon — compare to the *periapt of proof against poison* (rare), which grants immunity. Choking Escape provides a narrow-trigger defensive disengage with a small obscurement zone; the trigger (taking damage from an adjacent creature) and 1/short-rest limit keep it from overshadowing similar escape options. The obscurement fades quickly at the start of your next turn, preventing abuse as persistent cover.

---

## Variants
**Sporewalker's Shroud (Rare).** Increase Choking Escape to 2/short rest; the spore cloud lasts for 1 minute (as *fog cloud*, no concentration) and you can move up to your full speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Poison resistance becomes poison immunity.
