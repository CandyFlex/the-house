# Sporepuff Slippers
*Wondrous item (boots), uncommon*

---

## Description
Slippers woven from puffball mycelium, pale grey and impossibly soft, the texture of a mushroom cap turned inside out. They exhale when you land — a soft *paff* of luminous spores that hangs in the air for a heartbeat before drifting away. Children in the settlements near the Fungal Depths trade stories of a mycelial cobbler who knits these from the dreams of sleeping spore-beasts. The shoes are perpetually dusted with a fine powder that never quite brushes off and glows faint purple in moonlight.

## Properties

**Puff Landing.** When you fall, you can use your reaction to release a burst of spores from the slippers that cushions your descent. You take no falling damage from falls of 30 feet or less, and falling damage from greater heights is reduced by 3d6. The spores create a 10-foot-radius cloud of lightly obscured luminescent powder that lasts until the end of your next turn.

**Spore-Glide.** Your jump distance increases by 5 feet. Additionally, when you make a running long jump, you leave a faint trail of dimly glowing spores along your arc — creatures have disadvantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to track you by scent until the start of your next turn.

**Stomp Cloud (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can stomp your foot to release a dense burst of spores in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on yourself. The area becomes lightly obscured until the end of your next turn. Each creature of your choice in the area when the cloud appears gains advantage on the next Dexterity (Stealth) check it makes before the end of your next turn, as the spores cling and mute sounds.

## Balance Notes
Three properties, none attunement-gated, all utility-focused. Puff Landing is a narrow but genuinely useful fall-save — 30ft immunity covers most dungeon pits and combat knockbacks, while 3d6 reduction for greater falls is a nice cushion that won't let a character survive terminal velocity. The 10ft spore cloud is a minor obscurement ribbon. Spore-Glide's jump bonus is small but stacks with the *jump* spell, and the anti-tracking ribbon is flavorful. Stomp Cloud requires an action (high cost) and produces only light obscurement, so it's a setup tool, not a combat escape. No attunement makes this a swap-in utility item — perfect for the "fun uncommon" brief.

## Variants
**Greater Sporepuff Slippers (Rare, attunement).** Puff Landing negates all falling damage and the spore cloud lasts 1 minute (as *fog cloud* but luminescent and non-magical). Stomp Cloud recharges on a bonus action and creates heavy obscurement. The slippers grant you a climbing speed equal to your walking speed on fungal surfaces. Cost: 180,000 soul stones.
