# Marrow-Mesh Gambeson
*Armor (studded leather), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A suit of studded leather whose "studs" are actually calcified fungal nodules harvested from the marrow-cap — a mushroom that leaches minerals from the bones of creatures who die too close to it. The leather underlayer is woven from living mycelial threads, soft and warm against the skin. When damaged, the threads knit themselves back together with a wet, fibrous sound, like roots growing in fast-forward. The armor breathes — literally. If you press your ear to it, you can hear a slow, patient exhale, as if something inside is resting but not dead.

## Properties

**+1 Armor.** You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor.

**Living Fibers.** When you take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage while wearing this armor, the living mycelial threads absorb and redistribute the impact, reducing the damage by 3. This reduction applies after resistances.

**Fungal Exoskeleton (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can command the armor's mycelial threads to grow outward, encasing you in a temporary exoskeleton of hardened fungal chitin. You gain 15 temporary hit points and a +1 bonus to AC for 1 minute. When the effect ends, the armor becomes dormant — you lose the Living Fibers damage reduction until you finish a short or long rest, as the threads slowly regenerate.

## Balance Notes
The Living Fibers passive provides a permanent -3 to physical damage, which is effectively a very mild *heavy armor master* feat baked into magic armor. 3 damage reduction is meaningful at low levels and against multi-attacking hordes but minor against single heavy hits — it won't break encounters. The Fungal Exoskeleton is the real draw: 15 temp HP +1 AC for 1 minute as an action is comparable to a 2nd-level *false life* plus a mini *shield of faith* — solidly rare. The tradeoff (losing DR 3 after the exoskeleton fades) adds a tactical decision: emergency defense now versus sustained mitigation later. This is ideally suited for frontline skirmishers (Rogues, Rangers, Dex Fighters) who want medium durability but high mobility. Compare to *glamoured studded leather* (rare, +1, cosmetic) — the Gambeson offers significantly more combat utility for the same slot.
