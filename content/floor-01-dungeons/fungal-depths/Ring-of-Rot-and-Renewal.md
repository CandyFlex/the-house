# Ring of Rot and Renewal
*Ring, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A ring of woven fungal fibres lacquered to the hardness of polished bone, set with a cabochon of preserved mycelium that shifts color from pale white to deep violet depending on the nearest living thing's vitality. When death occurs nearby, the ring drinks—the cabochon flushes deep violet, then slowly fades as it feeds the energy into the wearer. The process is silent, odorless, and profoundly unsettling to those who can sense it.

## Properties

**Feed on the Fallen.** When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you dies, you can use your reaction to draw vitality from its decay. You gain temporary hit points equal to 1d10 + your proficiency bonus. Constructs and undead do not trigger this property.

**Speaking Through the Rot (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you touch the corpse of a humanoid or beast that has been dead no longer than 10 days and speak the ring's command word. You can cast the *speak with dead* spell without expending a spell slot or requiring material components. When you do, the corpse answers not with its own voice but through a chorus of fungal filaments that animate its throat—the sound is wet, rustling, and hollow.

**Warden Against Rot.** You have advantage on saving throws against disease. The ring vibrates faintly when you come within 5 feet of a source of harmful spores, molds, or fungal hazards.

## Balance Notes
Three properties: an unlimited-use reaction to gain temporary HP on nearby creature death (compare to the warlock's *dark one's blessing*, which grants CHA mod + warlock level on kill), *speak with dead* 1/long rest with an extended 10-day window (3rd-level spell, slightly better than baseline), and disease resistance with fungal hazard detection (thematic ribbon). The reaction THP has no usage cap but requires a visible death within 30 feet—the DM controls the supply. Against hordes of weak enemies this is very strong; against a single boss it yields nothing until the fight is over. This swinginess is appropriate for Rare. Watch for synergy with summoner builds that can manufacture allied creature deaths.

---

## Variants
**Uncommon — Lesser Ring of Rot.** Remove the Speaking Through the Rot property entirely. Feed on the Fallen grants temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus only (no 1d10 roll). Warden Against Rot remains unchanged. Cost: 55,000 soul stones.
