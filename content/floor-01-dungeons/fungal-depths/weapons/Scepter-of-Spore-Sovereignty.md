# Scepter of Spore Sovereignty
*Weapon (mace), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A mace whose head is a dense, petrified mushroom cap — the size of a closed fist — mounted on a shaft of spiraling chitin-bark. The cap's surface is pocked with tiny pores, each one exhaling a thread-fine wisp of golden spore, so faint you'd miss it if you weren't looking. The spores taste of honey and rot at the back of your throat. When the scepter strikes, the pores open wide and the victim inhales something they will spend the rest of their short life trying to cough out. The Sovereign did not rule through fear. It ruled because everything that breathed the throne room's air belonged to it. This scepter is a small piece of that air, sealed in a weapon.

## Properties

**+2 Weapon.** You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Sovereign's Infection.** When you hit a creature with this mace, the attack deals an additional 1d8 poison damage. Once per turn when you hit a creature, you can force it to make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature is infected with sovereign spores for 1 minute. While infected, the creature takes 1d6 poison damage at the start of each of its turns. Additionally, you can use a bonus action on your subsequent turns to compel the infected creature to use its reaction to make one melee attack against a creature of your choice within its reach. The infected creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Monarch's Decree (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can raise the scepter and release a wave of sovereign authority. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you that can see or hear you must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by you for 1 minute. While charmed in this way, a creature regards you as its fungal sovereign, cannot take reactions, and has its speed halved. A charmed creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Spore-Kissed.** You have resistance to poison damage while holding this scepter. Additionally, you can communicate simple concepts telepathically with any creature currently infected by the Sovereign's Infection property, out to a range of 60 feet.

## Balance Notes
This item delivers battlefield control through a melee weapon chassis — a design that forces the wielder to stay in the fight to reap the benefits. At Very Rare, it offers: a +2 weapon with +1d8 poison (strong but gated behind the most-resisted damage type), a once-per-turn infection that deals ongoing damage and lets you compel attacks (powerful but costs your bonus action and the target's reaction, and ends on a save), and a 1/long rest mass-charm that shuts down reactions and slows — comparable to a 4th or 5th level spell. The infection mechanic creates a compounding threat: the longer you hit something, the more poisoned and controlled it becomes. However, the reliance on CON saves (which high-CR creatures ace) and poison damage (commonly resisted or immune) keeps it from dominating every encounter. The passive poison resistance is a defensive ribbon that fits thematically. Compare to *dwarven thrower* (very rare, +3 warhammer, ranged, +1d8/2d8) — the Scepter trades raw damage for control.
