# Sporewood Longbow
*Weapon (longbow), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A longbow carved from the petrified stalk of a depth-cap mushroom, its limbs streaked with veins of dried bioluminescent sap that still pulse faint green when drawn. The bowstring is a braided fungal sinew that hums a wet, organic note on release. Arrows loosed from this bow sprout minute fungal barbs mid-flight—they burrow on impact and bloom into choking spores.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Spore-Barbed Shot.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, the attack deals an additional 1d4 poison damage.

**Bloom on Impact.** When you score a critical hit with this weapon, a 10-foot-radius cloud of fungal spores erupts centered on the target. The cloud is heavily obscured and lasts until the start of your next turn. The target must also succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of your next turn.

**Sporecloud Arrow (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can fire an arrow that transforms mid-flight into a dense spore mass. Choose a point within the weapon's range. A 20-foot-radius sphere of thick, choking spores appears centered on that point, replicating the effects of the *stinking cloud* spell (spell save DC 15), except the cloud is purple-green and pulses with faint bioluminescence. The cloud lasts for 1 minute, until a wind of moderate or greater speed disperses it, or until you dismiss it as a bonus action.

## Balance Notes
Four properties at Rare: +1 weapon, +1d4 poison on hit (modest—many creatures resist or are immune to poison), a critical-hit spore cloud with poison (a 5% excitement spike), and a 1/long rest *stinking cloud* (3rd-level spell). A *flame tongue longbow* would be Rare with +2d6 fire and no +X bonus; Sporewood trades raw damage for control and debuff. Poison damage is intentionally weaker than fire to justify the additional utility properties. Watch for synergy with classes that expand critical-hit range (Champion Fighter, Hexblade's Curse).

---

## Variants
**Uncommon — Lesser Sporewood Longbow.** Remove the Sporecloud Arrow property and reduce the save DC to 13. The +1d4 poison and crit-spore-cloud remain, making it a solid Uncommon weapon with a splash of control. Cost: 35,000 soul stones.
