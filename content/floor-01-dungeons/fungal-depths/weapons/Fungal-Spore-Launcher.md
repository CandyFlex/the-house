# Fungal Spore-Launcher
*Weapon (light crossbow), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A light crossbow whose stock, limbs, and string have been entirely replaced by fungal matter — the stock is compressed shelf-mycelium, the limbs are curved chitin-bark, and the string is a taut cord of braided hyphae that hums faintly when drawn. It does not fire bolts. Instead, a cluster of pale nodules grows along the underside of the stock, each one a tight-packed sporepod. When you pull the string, a pod detaches, stretches, and hardens mid-flight into a barbed spore-bolt that bursts on impact. The launcher is warm to the touch, and if you hold it too long in silence, you can hear it breathing.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Living Magazine.** The launcher generates its own ammunition — a cluster of sporepods grows along the stock. It can fire up to 20 spore-bolts before the pods must regenerate, which occurs over the course of a short rest. You do not need to supply or load ammunition.

**Sporecloud Impact.** When you hit a creature with a spore-bolt, the bolt bursts into a 5-foot-radius cloud of fungal spores centered on the target. The cloud is lightly obscured and lasts until the start of your next turn. A creature that starts its turn in the cloud must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the start of its next turn.

**Sporepod Burst (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can fire an oversized sporepod that detonates on impact. Choose a point within the weapon's normal range. Each creature in a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 poison damage and 2d6 acid damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. The area becomes heavily obscured by thick fungal spores until the end of your next turn.

## Balance Notes
Three properties at Rare: a +1 weapon, a passive on-hit spore cloud with a weak save (DC 13), and a once-per-short-rest AoE that deals respectable mixed damage with a strong control rider. The living magazine is technically a fourth property but is effectively a quality-of-life feature (no ammo tracking for 20 shots) — compare to a +1 crossbow with unlimited ammo, which would be uncommon. The on-hit spore cloud is a minor debuff (DC 13 is low against mid-tier enemies) but provides free light obscurement for allies. The Sporepod Burst is the showcase — 4d6+2d6 (avg 21) in a 15ft radius is comparable to a *fireball* at 4th level (9d6, avg 31.5) but split across a commonly-resisted damage type (poison) plus acid, and the heavy obscurement lets allies reposition. This item shines for poison-immune parties or characters who want to control sightlines while dealing damage.

## Variants
**Greater Spore-Launcher (Very Rare).** +2 weapon. Sporecloud Impact deals 1d4 poison damage to creatures that start their turn in the cloud (no save). Sporepod Burst recharges on a 5-6 roll at the start of your turn and its damage increases to 6d6 poison + 3d6 acid. The magazine capacity increases to 30. Cost: 2,500,000 soul stones.
