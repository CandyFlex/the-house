# Fungi-Touched Staff
*Staff (quarterstaff), rare (requires attunement by a druid, sorcerer, warlock, or wizard)*

---

## Description
A quarterstaff of twisted, petrified mycelium—dense as hardwood, warm to the touch, and covered in a fine network of dormant fungal threads that twitch when magic passes through them. The staff's crown is a half-open mushroom cap that glows with slow, tidal pulses of violet light. When planted in damp earth, the staff drinks, and the cap opens further. It was grown, not carved—a gift from the Depths to a surface-dweller who proved worth keeping.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff.

**Fungal Focus.** This staff can be used as a spellcasting focus. While holding it, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls for spells that deal poison damage.

**Spells.** While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend charges to cast one of the following spells (spell save DC 15): *entangle* (1 charge—the plants are fungal tendrils), *stinking cloud* (2 charges—manifests as thick purple spores), or *blight* (3 charges—manifests as rapid fungal decay). The spells require no material components.

**Depth Nourishment.** If the staff is planted in natural soil or fungal substrate during a long rest, it regains 1 additional charge at dawn (for a total of 1d4+2 that day). If planted in the Fungal Depths specifically, it regains all expended charges.

## Balance Notes
A Rare staff for druids, sorcerers, warlocks, and wizards. Provides a +1 weapon (Uncommon), +1 to poison spell attack rolls (half a *wand of the war mage*), and a 5-charge system for *entangle* (1st), *stinking cloud* (3rd), and *blight* (4th). The Depth Nourishment ribbon encourages roleplay and location-based decisions without significantly altering combat balance. Compare to *staff of the woodlands* (Rare, druid-only, +2 weapon, at-will *pass without trace*, 10 charges for six spells)—Fungi-Touched has wider class access but fewer and more modest spells. Watch for synergy with poison-focused subclass features (e.g., Way of Mercy Monk multiclass).

---

## Variants
**Very Rare — Greater Fungi-Touched Staff.** Increase charges to 10 (regain 1d6+2 at dawn), raise the save DC to 17, and add *cloudkill* to the spell list (5 charges—manifests as a rolling wave of violet-and-green spores). The +1 bonus to poison spell attack rolls increases to +2. Cost: 950,000 soul stones.
