# Rot-Tender's Sickle
*Weapon (sickle), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A sickle whose blade is a single curved tooth of petrified shelf-fungus, sharpened to an edge that glistens faintly green. The handle is wrapped in pale root-leather and pulses with a warmth that does not belong to anything dead — it drinks from the dying and gives to the living. The Depths' rot-tenders were the Sovereign's field surgeons, harvesting rot to fuel renewal. They believed that nothing truly dies in the fungal caverns; it only changes shape. This sickle is the tool that makes that belief true.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Harvester's Toll.** When you hit a creature with this sickle, the attack deals an additional 1d4 necrotic damage. The sickle's blade drinks the essence released, storing it as a charge. The sickle can hold up to 3 charges. Charges are lost when you finish a long rest.

**Reap and Sow.** As an action, you can expend stored charges to produce one of the following effects:
- **1 Charge:** Cast *cure wounds* at 1st level.
- **2 Charges:** Cast *cure wounds* at 2nd level, or cast *healing word* at 1st level as a bonus action.
- **3 Charges:** Cast *cure wounds* at 3rd level, or touch a creature that has died within the last minute and grant it advantage on its next death saving throw if it is restored to life within the next hour.

No material components are required for these spells, and they appear as fungal filaments knitting flesh closed.

**Wilt-Kindness (1/Short Rest).** When a creature within 30 feet of you is reduced to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to grant them 1 hit point instead. That creature gains this hit point immediately but falls to 0 hit points after 1 minute if it has not received any other healing. Once you use this property, you cannot use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

## Balance Notes
This item converts damage dealt into healing — a design that rewards aggression with support capability. At an average of 2.5 necrotic per hit and a cap of 3 charges, a character needs about 3 successful attacks to fully charge, which takes roughly 2–3 rounds in combat. The healing output (1st–3rd level *cure wounds*) is limited by the charge cap and the fact that charges reset on a long rest (no hoarding). The 1/short rest Wilt-Kindness is strong — it's essentially a delayed *spare the dying* that buys 1 minute of borrowed time — but it's gated by the reaction cost, proximity, and the fact that the creature still falls after a minute without follow-up healing. The sickle's base damage is low (1d4+1+mod +1d4 necrotic, comparable to a shortsword), keeping the attack balanced. This item makes the wielder an off-healer who must stay in melee to generate value, which is a compelling play pattern.

## Variants
**Rot-Tender's Greater Sickle (Very Rare).** Increase the bonus to +2. Increase the necrotic damage to 1d6. Charge cap increases to 5. Wilt-Kindness recharges on a short rest and grants 1d8 temporary HP instead of 1 HP. Cost: 2,200,000 soul stones.
