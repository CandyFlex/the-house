# Crown of the Spore Sovereign
*Wondrous item (head), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A circlet grown rather than forged — interlocking shelf fungi bleached white and hardened to ivory, a single glowing violet bloom at the brow. The bloom never closes, never wilts, and turns to face anyone who addresses the wearer, as though listening. When the crown's power wakes fully, the bloom sheds, and the spores rise. The Depths acknowledge only one sovereign at a time. The crown chooses whom to crown, and it has never chosen the same person twice.

## Properties

**Sovereign of Rot.** You can cast the *speak with plants* spell at will, except the spell affects only fungal organisms — mushrooms, molds, mycelia, and fungal creatures such as violet fungi, shriekers, and gas spores. When you make a Charisma check to interact with a fungal creature, you have advantage.

**Spore Cataclysm (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can cast the *cloudkill* spell (spell save DC 17) without expending a spell slot or requiring material components. The cloud manifests as a dense, churning mass of purple-green spores shot through with veins of golden light. While the cloud is active, you can use a bonus action on each of your turns to move it up to 10 feet in any direction, as long as the new center point is within 120 feet of you.

**Sovereign Spore Aura (1/Short Rest).** As a bonus action, you can awaken the crown's bloom. For 1 minute, the bloom sheds a torrent of toxic spores in a 10-foot-radius aura centered on you. When you use this bonus action, and as a bonus action on each of your subsequent turns, each creature of your choice within the aura must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 2d6 poison damage. The bloom glows intensely during this time, shedding bright light in a 20-foot radius. You can suppress or resume the aura as a bonus action, but the 1-minute duration continues to expire while suppressed. Once this property has been used, it cannot be used again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Symbiotic Protection.** While wearing this crown, you are immune to the effects of any fungal hazard or trap in the Fungal Depths. Fungi, mycelial growths, and spore-based hazards in any environment do not treat you as hostile and will not voluntarily harm you unless you attack them first. This protection extends to any allies within 10 feet of you while you are conscious.

## Balance Notes
This item earns its Very Rare rarity through a 5th-level spell once per day (*cloudkill* with bonus action movement freedom), a 1/short rest persistent damage aura, and stacked utility. Compare to a *staff of power* (very rare, also grants a 5th-level spell plus multiple lower-tier effects) — this crown is narrower in scope but offers a coherent fungal-sovereign playstyle. The Sovereign Spore Aura rewards melee-range aggression and can deal up to 20d6 poison damage across a full minute, but the poison damage type and CON saves heavily constrain its practical output against fiends, undead, and constructs. *Cloudkill* with bonus action drift gives exceptional zone control — DMs should ensure encounters feature dynamic terrain rather than choke points the party can lock down indefinitely. The Symbiotic Protection's ally aura (10 ft) encourages the wearer to stay near the frontline even without heavy armor, creating a high-risk/high-reward melee caster identity. The at-will fungal *speak with plants* is largely a flavor ribbon but can short-circuit fungal-themed exploration encounters — lean into the roleplay rather than blocking it.

## Variants

**Circlet of the Spore Courtier (Rare).** Remove Spore Cataclysm. Sovereign Spore Aura becomes 1/Long Rest instead of 1/Short Rest, and the aura damage is reduced to 1d6 poison. Symbiotic Protection no longer extends to allies. Reduce cost to 1,200,000 soul stones.
