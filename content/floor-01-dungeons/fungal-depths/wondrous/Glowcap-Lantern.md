# Glowcap Lantern
*Wondrous item (held), uncommon*

---

## Description
A lantern whose glass panes are not glass at all but the cured caps of glowcap mushrooms, stretched thin and fitted into a frame of petrified mycelium. The light within comes from a fist-sized fungal node that pulses gently, never hot, never dimming — a violet-green glow that paints everything it touches in eerie, living color. Fungal prospectors carry these instead of torches; the depths do not permit fire, but they permit growth.

## Properties

**Bioluminescent Light.** While open, the lantern sheds bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. The light is a soft purple-green and produces no heat. Closing the shutter (no action required) reduces the light to dim light in a 5-foot radius. The lantern's light never extinguishes naturally and requires no fuel. It ceases to glow only if the fungal node inside is destroyed (AC 10, 5 hit points) or if the lantern is submerged in acid.

**Sporelight Burst (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can open the lantern fully and release a burst of adhesive spores. Each creature of your choice within a 20-foot cone originating from the lantern must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or be outlined in purple-green light for 1 minute. While outlined in this way, the creature sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius, can't benefit from being invisible, and attack rolls against it have advantage. This effect replicates the *faerie fire* spell without requiring concentration or components.

## Balance Notes
The eternal, heatless light is a common-tier utility ribbon — functional but not powerful. The real value is a once-per-long-rest *faerie fire* (1st-level spell) without concentration or components at a modest DC 13. Compare to the *driftglobe* (uncommon), which offers *light* at-will plus *daylight* 1/day. No attunement keeps this accessible as a party utility item. The low save DC is appropriate for the rarity and lack of attunement cost.

---

## Variants
**Greater Glowcap Lantern (Rare).** Bright light extends to 60 feet (dim 60 feet). Sporelight Burst becomes 2/long rest, the cone widens to 30 feet, and the save DC increases to 15. The fungal node has AC 13 and 15 hit points.
