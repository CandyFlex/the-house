# Bloom of the Wilt-King
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A single flower that grows from a fist-sized bulb of black, glassy mycelium. The flower is the color of a fresh bruise — deep purple at the petals' base fading to a sickly yellow at the tips — and it opens and closes in a rhythm that has nothing to do with day or night. It opens when something dies nearby. When a creature breathes its last within the Bloom's reach, the petals spread wide and a single strand of golden pollen drifts from the dying body into the flower's throat, and the bulb pulses once — a heartbeat in reverse. The Wilt-King was what the Depths called the Sovereign before it took the throne: the thing that ruled the rot, the monarch of what comes after. This Bloom is a fragment of that older, hungrier crown. It does not create life. It borrows from death and pays back with interest.

## Properties

**Harvest the Passing.** The Bloom feeds on death. When a creature that is not a construct or undead dies within 30 feet of you while you are holding or wearing the Bloom, the bulb gains 1 charge. The Bloom can hold a maximum of 7 charges. At dawn, its charges reset to 1 if it has fewer than 1 charge. Charges are visible as faint golden motes swirling inside the black bulb.

**Spend Life, Buy Life.** As an action, you can expend the Bloom's charges to produce one of the following effects:

- **3 Charges:** Cast *revivify*. The spell requires no material components. The revived creature's skin bears faint bioluminescent fungal veins for 24 hours — harmless, but unmistakable.
- **2 Charges:** Cast *blight* at 4th level (save DC 17). The spell's appearance is fungal — the target's flesh erupts with dark, spore-weeping blooms.
- **1 Charge:** As a reaction when a creature within 30 feet of you is reduced to 0 hit points, you can grant that creature 1 hit point instead. The creature gains this hit point immediately but falls to 0 hit points after 1 minute if it has not received any other healing. The creature is also immune to the damage that triggered this reaction for the next minute from the same source.
- **5 Charges:** As an action, cast *death ward* on yourself. The spell requires no material components. The ward appears as a thin, iridescent fungal membrane that sheathes your body and fades to invisibility.

**Wilting Presence.** While holding or wearing the Bloom, you have resistance to necrotic damage. Additionally, Tiny plants and fungi within 10 feet of you wilt and blacken within seconds — harmless, but deeply unsettling to druids.

## Balance Notes
The Bloom of the Wilt-King is a necromantic support item that rewards proximity to combat death. At Very Rare, it competes with items like *rod of resurrection* (legendary, 5/day *heal*/*revivify* with no charge system) and *staff of healing* (rare, 10 charges for healing spells). The Bloom's charge system is unique: you must be within 30 feet of dying creatures to fuel it, creating tension between safety and power. The 1-charge death-prevention reaction is the signature — it's essentially a delayed *spare the dying* that buys 1 minute of borrowed time, and the immunity to the triggering damage source for that minute is a powerful anti-spike tool. The *revivify* at 3 charges is gated behind three creature deaths, which most fights won't provide — so you'll typically enter combat with charges you've accumulated. The 1-charge floor at dawn ensures you always have at least the reaction ready. The *blight* option gives the Bloom offensive utility so it's not purely a heal-bot item. The necrotic resistance is strong but narrow, and the wilting ribbon is pure flavor. This item fundamentally changes how the wielder positions: closer to dying enemies, further from safety.

## Variants
**Bloom of the Death-Throne (Legendary).** Charge cap increases to 12. New effects: 7 charges — cast *resurrection* (requires a full charge cycle of 7 creature deaths, no material components); 4 charges — cast *circle of death* (DC 19). Charges reset to 3 at dawn instead of 1. Necrotic resistance becomes necrotic immunity. Cost: 15,000,000 soul stones.
