# Seedling Satchel
*Wondrous item (belt), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A leather belt pouch that is not leather but cured shelf-fungus — soft, warm, and perpetually damp. Inside, a bed of compressed mycelium cradles a cluster of thumb-sized fungal seedlings, each one a different color and texture. The seedlings wriggle faintly when you reach for them, like grubs trying to burrow deeper. At dawn, the satchel exhales — a soft, humid puff — and new seedlings push through the mycelial bed, ready for picking. The Depths' spore-gardeners wore these into battle as much as into the gardens, because in the dark, a pocket full of problems is the best kind of shield.

## Properties

**Living Crop.** The satchel produces 1d4 fungal seedlings at dawn (maximum of 4 seedlings in the satchel at any time). Each seedling can be thrown (range 20/60 feet) as an action or planted in an adjacent space. The seedlings come in four varieties — when you draw one, roll a d4 to determine which you've grabbed:

- **1 — Puffball.** The seedling bursts on impact into a 10-foot-radius cloud of thick white spores. The area is heavily obscured until the end of your next turn. Creatures inside cannot make opportunity attacks.
- **2 — Stinkcap.** The seedling detonates into a 15-foot-radius cloud of choking yellow spores. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. A poisoned creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
- **3 — Glowspore.** The seedling releases a spray of luminescent spores in a 20-foot-radius sphere. The area is filled with bright light for 1 hour and dim light for an additional hour. Invisible creatures and objects in the area are visibly outlined in glowing spore-dust for 1 minute.
- **4 — Griproot.** The seedling erupts into a 10-foot-radius area of thick, ropy fungal tendrils. The area becomes difficult terrain, and any creature that starts its turn in the area or enters it for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be restrained until the start of its next turn. The tendrils wither after 1 minute.

**Spore Surge (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can shake the satchel vigorously to force-growth 1d4 seedlings instantly. These seedlings survive for 1 minute before wilting — use them or lose them.

## Balance Notes
The Seedling Satchel is a bag of tricks with a fungal flavor — four distinct grenade effects with a random element. The randomness (d4 per draw) is the balancing factor: you can't guarantee you'll get the Puffball for an escape or the Griproot for crowd control. At up to 4 seedlings per day, it delivers roughly 4 first-to-third-level spell effects, which fits Rare when you factor in the unpredictability. The 1/short rest Spore Surge adds tactical flexibility — force-grow in combat when you need options immediately, but the 1-minute shelf life forces immediate use. Compare to *necklace of fireballs* (rare, 3–6 *fireball* casts, no randomness) — the Satchel offers weaker but more versatile effects and self-replenishes daily. The belt slot is often uncontested, making this a strong utility pick for any character who wants battlefield control options without spell slots.

## Variants
**Spore-Gardener's Satchel (Very Rare).** Seedling production increases to 2d4 (max 8). Spore Surge produces 2d4 seedlings. New seedling type: **Necrocrown** (on d6 roll of 5-6 combined): the seedling releases spores in a 15-foot radius, forcing a DC 17 CON save — on failure, 4d8 necrotic damage and the creature's speed is halved for 1 minute. Cost: 2,200,000 soul stones.
