# Chitin-Bark Carapace
*Armor (breastplate), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A breastplate crafted from the fused chitin of giant depth-beetles and plates of petrified fungal bark, lacquered to a dark iridescent sheen. Tiny pores dot the surface—dormant, waiting. When struck, the plates contract reflexively and the pores exhale, releasing a burst of choking spores that punish the attacker. The armor is lighter than steel but harder than bone, and it never stops smelling faintly of the cavern floor after rain.

## Properties

**+1 Armor.** You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks, this magic version does not.

**Fungal Resistance.** While wearing this armor, you have resistance to poison damage.

**Spore Burst (1/Short Rest).** When a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to release a burst of choking spores from the armor. The attacker must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn. A creature that succeeds on this save is immune to your Spore Burst for 24 hours.

**Depth Adaptation.** While wearing this armor, you can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light. This vision is monochromatic, rendering everything in shades of violet and green.

## Balance Notes
Four properties: +1 AC with no stealth penalty (Uncommon chassis), poison resistance, a 1/short rest reaction to poison an attacker, and thematic darkvision (ribbon). The +1 breastplate alone is Uncommon; layering poison resistance, a reaction debuff, and low-light adaptation pushes it firmly into Rare. Compare to *dragon scale mail* (Very Rare, +1, elemental resistance, advantage on frightful saves vs. dragons, dragon detection)—Chitin-Bark is substantially weaker, fitting Rare comfortably. DMs should note that the poison condition on Spore Burst ends at the attacker's next turn, keeping it manageable.

---

## Variants
**Uncommon — Lesser Carapace.** Remove the Spore Burst and Depth Adaptation properties. The armor still grants +1 AC and poison resistance—a solid defensive piece for a lower-tier party. Cost: 40,000 soul stones.
