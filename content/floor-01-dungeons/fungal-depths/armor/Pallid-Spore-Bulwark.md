# Pallid Spore Bulwark
*Armor (shield), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A broad shield grown from a single pallid shelf-fungus, its surface pale as bone and faintly convex, lined with concentric rings like tree rings — each one a century of the Sovereign's reign. The face is covered in a fine, powdery bloom that never smears, never thins, and never stops smelling faintly of cold cellars and forgotten fruit. When struck, the shield exhales — a dry, choking puff of pallid spores that hang in the air like a ghost's breath. The Sovereign's chamberlains carried these shields not to fight, but to remind visitors that even in the throne room, they were breathing the Sovereign's air.

## Properties

**+1 Shield.** You have a +1 bonus to AC while holding this shield.

**Spore Retaliation.** When a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to force the attacker to make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature is poisoned until the end of its next turn and takes 2d6 poison damage. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not poisoned. Once this property is used, it cannot be used again until the start of your next turn.

**Bulwark Bloom (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can plant the shield in the ground. It expands rapidly into a curving wall of pale fungal growth up to 15 feet long, 10 feet high, and 1 foot thick. The wall provides half cover to creatures behind it. The area in front of the wall (a 15-foot cone extending 10 feet from the wall's face) becomes difficult terrain as pale mycelial tendrils writhe across the ground. The wall lasts for 1 minute or until you use a bonus action to collapse it back into shield form. If the wall drops to 0 hit points (AC 13, 30 HP, vulnerable to fire, immune to poison and psychic), it is destroyed and the shield cannot be used again until you finish a long rest.

## Balance Notes
A +1 shield is effectively an uncommon item by itself. Spore Retaliation adds a strong reactive control tool — at-will (1/round) forced save against poisoned plus damage — which is comparable to the *shield of missile attraction* curse but inverted (you punish attackers instead of drawing fire). The poison condition is strong but gated behind a CON save, which most frontline bruisers pass easily. Bulwark Bloom is the showcase: battlefield control that creates cover for allies and difficult terrain for enemies. It's flexible and tactical, lasting a full minute, and the risk of destruction (fire vulnerability, 30 HP, loss until long rest) prevents spamming it recklessly. This shield rewards aggressive frontliners who want to push into melee and punish anyone who hits them back. Compare to *arrow-catching shield* (rare, +2 vs ranged) — the Bulwark offers more versatile defensive utility.

## Variants
**Sovereign's Bulwark (Very Rare).** +2 shield. Spore Retaliation deals 3d6 poison damage and the poisoned condition lasts for 1 minute (repeat save at end of each turn). Bulwark Bloom creates a 30-foot wall and the difficult terrain extends 20 feet. The wall has AC 15 and 60 HP, resistance to bludgeoning and piercing. Cost: 2,600,000 soul stones.
