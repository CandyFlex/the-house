# Root-Grip Greaves
*Wondrous item (boots), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Greaves of hardened chitin-bark and pale root-leather that encase the wearer's legs from knee to sole. Thin mycelial filaments extend from the soles, threading into the ground with every step — not enough to slow you, just enough to know the earth beneath you. When you stand your ground, the filaments thicken and anchor, weaving into soil, stone, or flesh with equal indifference. The Sovereign's royal guard wore these when they stood watch over the throne chamber for centuries without moving, becoming indistinguishable from the fungal pillars around them. Some say the guards are still there — the greaves still rooted, the bodies long since consumed.

## Properties

**Fungal Stride.** Your walking speed increases by 10 feet. Additionally, difficult terrain created by plants, fungus, mud, or rotting organic matter does not cost you extra movement.

**Root Anchor (1/Short Rest).** As a bonus action, you can command the greaves' filaments to anchor you to the ground. Until the start of your next turn, your speed becomes 0, you cannot be moved against your will by any spell or effect, you have advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws against effects that would knock you prone or push you, and you gain a +2 bonus to AC. You can end this effect early as a bonus action.

**Mycelial Grip.** You have advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to avoid being moved, knocked prone, or grappled while you are standing on dirt, stone, wood, or fungal surfaces.

## Balance Notes
Three properties with distinct roles: Fungal Stride is pure mobility (the +10ft speed matches *boots of speed* without the bonus action activation but with half the bonus, and the terrain ignore is a nice fungal-flavored ribbon). Root Anchor is the signature — a bonus action self-anchor that trades all mobility for defensive buffs. The immovability + +2 AC makes this a "plant your feet and hold the line" tool ideal for tanks holding chokepoints. The short-rest recharge limits it to roughly once per fight. Mycelial Grip is a passive defense against forced-movement effects, which are common at higher levels. Together, these make the wearer exceptionally hard to displace when they choose to stand firm, but the speed-0 penalty on Root Anchor creates a meaningful tradeoff — you can't reposition if the fight moves away from you. Compare to *boots of striding and springing* (uncommon) and *dwarven plate* (very rare, immovable passive) — the Greaves sit comfortably at rare as an active ability with a cost.

## Variants
**Deep-Root Greaves (Very Rare).** Root Anchor lasts for 1 minute (no longer ends at start of next turn) and the AC bonus increases to +3. Fungal Stride grants a climbing speed equal to your walking speed on any surface covered in fungus or rot. Cost: 1,800,000 soul stones.
