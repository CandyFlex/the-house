# Heart of the Mycelium
*Wondrous item (amulet), very rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
An amulet whose centerpiece is a preserved fungal heart — the core node of the colossal mushroom that rules the Depths' heart chamber, cut from the stalk by the first delver to survive reaching it. It is the size of a child's fist, warm and faintly spongy, and it beats. Not in rhythm with any living pulse, but in a slow, patient cadence like water dripping into a cavern pool centuries deep. The filaments that extend from it into the chain are alive, threading into the wearer's skin at the collarbone — painless, permanent while attuned, and hungry.

## Properties

**Mycelial Impervious.** You have immunity to poison damage and to the poisoned condition. Additionally, you can breathe normally in any environment — including underwater, in vacuum, and within the area of a *stinking cloud* or similar effect. The amulet provides the oxygen your body needs via the fungal filaments embedded in your skin.

**Rise, Spore-Thrall (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can target the corpse of a humanoid or beast that has been dead no longer than 1 hour. The corpse rises as a spore-thrall under your control. A spore-thrall uses the statistics of a **zombie** with the following changes: its slam attack deals an additional 1d6 poison damage, it has immunity to poison damage and the poisoned condition, it has vulnerability to fire damage, and fungal growths across its body shed dim light in a 10-foot radius. The spore-thrall obeys your verbal commands (no action required). It remains animated for 24 hours, until it drops to 0 hit points, or until you use this property again. You can control only one spore-thrall at a time.

**Spore-Step (1/Short Rest).** As a bonus action, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. When you do, you leave a 10-foot-radius cloud of thick fungal spores in the space you departed. The cloud is heavily obscured and lasts until the start of your next turn. A creature that enters the cloud for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the start of its next turn.

**Mycelial Network.** While wearing this amulet, you can communicate telepathically with any creature you can see that is under the effect of a poison, disease, or fungal condition you created, out to a range of 120 feet. You don't need to share a language, though the creature must be able to understand at least one language. Additionally, you can sense the approximate location of any spore-thrall you have animated within 1 mile of you.

## Balance Notes
This item sits at Very Rare due to its five distinct properties, each offering meaningful combat or exploration utility. The closest official equivalent is the *periapt of proof against poison* (rare, poison immunity only) layered with a *ring of evasion*-tier mobility tool and a minion-creation ability comparable to a 3rd-level *animate dead*. The breath-in-any-environment property is a strong ribbon that bypasses environmental challenges (underwater, vacuum, gas traps) — DMs should account for this in exploration design. The spore-step teleport plus poison cloud creates a potent hit-and-run loop that synergizes with Mycelial Network for battlefield communication — rogues and monks gain extraordinary repositioning power here. Watch for players stacking the spore-thrall with other permanent minions; the one-thrall limit prevents army-building but a necromancer multiclass could still combine it with *animate dead*. This item fundamentally changes playstyle from passive defense to an aggressive, minion-controlling fungal skirmisher.

## Variants

**Heart of the Sporeling (Rare).** Retain Mycelial Impervious and Rise, Spore-Thrall. Remove Spore-Step and Mycelial Network. The spore-thrall loses its bonus 1d6 poison damage on slam attacks. Reduce cost to 800,000 soul stones.
