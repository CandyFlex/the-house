# Mycelial Messenger
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

---

## Description
A tiny mushroom — cap no wider than a thumbnail — that grows from a compressed puck of living mycelium the size of a coin. When held to your lips, the cap quivers, its gills parting like an ear waiting to listen. Speak into it, and the cap closes tight. Place it on the ground, and it detaches from its puck and scuttles away on threadlike legs of pale mycelium, vanishing into the earth. Hours later, it returns — or doesn't, if the message found no one to receive it. The Depths' spore-shamans used these to coordinate harvests across miles of cavern. The Sovereign used them to whisper to its children in distant pockets of the dark.

## Properties

**Whisper and Scatter.** As an action, you can speak a message of up to 25 words into the mushroom. Name a creature you have met before that is on the same plane of existence as you. Place the mushroom on the ground, and it detaches and burrows into the earth, traveling at a rate of 1 mile per 10 minutes along the mycelial threads that permeate the ground. When it reaches the named creature, the mushroom surfaces nearby and repeats your message in your voice, then immediately begins its return journey. If the creature is more than 10 miles away, is on another plane, or does not exist, the mushroom returns after 2 hours with its cap sealed shut. Once used, the mushroom cannot be used again until the next dawn.

**Many Mouths.** You can have up to three Mycelial Messengers. The mushrooms recognize each other — if you hold one, you know the approximate direction and distance (within 100 feet) of any others you own that are on the same plane.

- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Attunement:** No
- **Charges:** 1/dawn (per mushroom)
- **Save DC:** —
- **Spell Equivalent:** *Sending* (3rd level), but slower (10 min/mile), one-way only, and limited to 25 words and 10-mile range. Comparable to *animal messenger* (2nd level).
- **Damage/Effect:** 25-word message delivery up to 10 miles at 1 mile/10 min; max 3 mushrooms; directional sense between owned messengers

---

## Balance Notes
The Mycelial Messenger is essentially a slower, grounded version of *sending* with a shorter range (10 vs. unlimited miles) and a delay based on distance. It's useful for intra-dungeon or local-area communication, especially for parties that split up. The 10-mile cap and 10-minutes-per-mile travel time make it impractical for long-distance emergencies, preventing it from replacing *sending* spells. Three messengers allow a small communication network — one with base camp, one with the scout, and one spare — which is powerful for coordinated infiltration but limited by the one-use-per-day restriction. No attunement means anyone can carry one, making it an excellent party-wide utility item. Compare to *sending stones* (uncommon, unlimited uses but paired and short messages) — the Messenger trades frequency for flexibility (anyone you've met).

## Variants
**Mycelial Spore-Net (Rare, attunement).** A master mushroom that can receive messages from up to 6 Mycelial Messengers simultaneously. As an action, you can broadcast a reply to all messengers whose caps are open. The master mushroom also lets you cast *clairvoyance* once per long rest by sensing through any messenger within 1 mile. Cost: 200,000 soul stones.
