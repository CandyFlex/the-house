# Mycelial Net
*Weapon (net), uncommon*

---

## Description
A net woven from pale, rubbery fungal strands that still twitch faintly when held. The weave is alive — or as alive as the Depths allow. When it strikes a target, needle-thin hyphae burrow into armor seams and flesh, anchoring the net with a grip that tightens the more the victim struggles. If left in darkness after use, the net drinks moisture from the air and knits its own wounds, whole again by the time its wielder catches their breath.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls made with this magic net. The net's escape DC increases to 13 (from the standard 10).

**Mycelial Anchor.** When you hit a Large or smaller creature with this net, fungal filaments burrow into the target. The net's escape DC increases by an additional 2 (to 15) for as long as the creature remains restrained by it. In addition, a creature restrained by the net has disadvantage on checks made to escape from it.

**Spore Regrowth.** If the net is destroyed (whether by slashing damage or otherwise), it regrows during a short rest as long as at least one strand remains. If the net is completely incinerated or dissolved in acid, it is destroyed permanently.

## Balance Notes
Nets are inherently weak weapons — attack rolls always have disadvantage unless the wielder has Crossbow Expert or Sharpshooter, and the standard escape DC of 10 is trivial. The +1 bonus offsets the disadvantage tax slightly, while the enhanced escape DC (15 with the anchor) makes this a genuinely threatening control option at low tiers without being inescapable. The Spore Regrowth property solves the net's biggest practical problem: by RAW, a net is destroyed on a single use, making non-magical nets prohibitively expensive consumables. No attunement is appropriate — this is a utility weapon, not a character-defining item.

---

## Variants
**Greater Mycelial Net (Rare).** Becomes a +2 net. Base escape DC increases to 15, and Mycelial Anchor increases it to 17. Spore Regrowth completes in 1 minute instead of a short rest. The net gains resistance to slashing damage.
