# Decayer's Gauntlets
*Wondrous item (gloves), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Gloves sewn from the caps of wilt-cap mushrooms—a species that blooms only on corpses. The leather is grey-brown and soft, perpetually cool and faintly damp. When the wearer touches living flesh, the gloves excrete a thin film of necrotizing enzymes that blacken and blister on contact. The Depths' spore-shamans crafted these for a purpose: what the cavern wants dead, these gloves ensure stays dead.

## Properties

**Caustic Touch.** When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike or a melee weapon attack, the attack deals an additional 1d6 poison damage.

**Spore Choke (Proficiency Bonus/Long Rest).** When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can use a bonus action to release a concentrated burst of spores into the wound. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of your next turn. You can use this property a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

**Wilt.** When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack while wearing these gloves, the creature's body rapidly decomposes—flesh blackens, softens, and collapses into a fine grey powder over the course of 1 minute. This does not affect the creature's equipment or prevent effects that can restore the creature to life.

## Balance Notes
Three properties: +1d6 poison damage to all melee attacks (unarmed strikes and weapon attacks alike), proficiency-bonus-scaling uses of Spore Choke per long rest, and the Wilt flavor ribbon. A +1d6 elemental rider on a single weapon would be Uncommon; making it universal across all melee attacks justifies Rare. Spore Choke grants 2–6 poison attempts per day and scales naturally with character level—strong but constrained by both the poison damage type (commonly resisted) and the poisoned condition immunity common among high-CR creatures. Wilt is purely narrative with no mechanical combat advantage.

---

## Variants
**Uncommon — Lesser Decayer's Gloves.** Reduce the Caustic Touch damage to 1d4 poison and remove the Spore Choke property entirely. Wilt remains as a flavorful ribbon. A solid early-game boost for melee characters. Cost: 45,000 soul stones.
