# Glass Cathedral

*Hex 7. Hard (15 pts). Three room nodes → Boss. Active daily.*

---

## Brief

The Glass Cathedral is an impossible structure — a cathedral built entirely of crystalline glass, resonant and alive. Every sound echoes forever in harmonic layers. Light fractures through prismatic panes into cascading color. The floor is a mirrored mosaic; the ceiling, a kaleidoscope. But the glass is sharp. It sings. It remembers the voices of everyone who ever spoke within it, and it answers back.

The Cathedral was Caesar's chapel — not for worship, but for confession. He brought prisoners here, made them speak their sins and secrets, and the glass recorded everything. Now abandoned, the Cathedral has gone feral — the accumulated whispers have become a chorus, a fractured consciousness that speaks in a thousand voices at once.

Style ideology: Beauty as archive. The Cathedral is the memory of a tyrant's justice — every confession, every plea, every scream preserved in crystalline resonance. It is a library written in sound and light, and it is very, very lonely.

---

## Public Façade

A shimmering palace of glass visible from miles away on a clear day — a cathedral whose spires catch the sun and splinter it into rainbows. The entrance is a massive rose window set into the ground at the center of a stone plaza — stepping onto the window causes it to become transparent, and you fall through into the Nave below. The glass does not break; it passes through you like light through a prism, depositing you gently on the mirrored floor. Those who watch from outside see you vanish in a cascade of color.

Daily cycle. Resets at vespers (sunset). Weekly phase-lock: open only on days with clear skies — clouds shroud the cathedral in opaque grey and the portal will not activate.

## Admin Sub-Entrance

A maintenance access: the Bell Tower's service stair. A narrow spiral staircase hidden behind a false stained-glass panel in the southern transept — visible only to those who carry an item from the Cathedral's pool (the glass recognizes its own). Climb 200 steps to reach the Interstitial Layer — a narrow walkway suspended between the Cathedral's outer glass skin and the inner sanctum. From here, you see the rooms below through colored panes: the Nave in blue, the Choir Loft in gold, the Confessional in deep violet, the Prism Apse in shifting rainbow. The walkway is silent — sound does not travel here. Your footsteps make no noise. The only thing you hear is the layer's own resonance: a faint, constant note, like a distant choir holding a single chord for eternity.

**Admin challenges:**
- The interstitial silence is oppressive. After 20 minutes, make a DC 14 WIS save or gain the deafened condition (psychosomatic) for 1 hour.
- The walkway has gaps — sections of missing glass where the panes have shattered. Crossing requires DC 13 Acrobatics or fall into the room below.
- **Corruption note:** The whispers are starting to bleed through. At random intervals (every 10 minutes, 50% chance), the walkway fills with a fragment of a confession — a voice speaking a secret, a plea, a scream. DC 14 WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute.

## Room Nodes

### Node 1 — The Nave of Echoes

**Public experience:** The Nave is the Cathedral's main hall — a vast space of mirrored floors and glass columns. Every footstep rings and returns a hundredfold. Visibility is perfect but sound is chaos — creatures navigating the Nave have disadvantage on Perception checks that rely on hearing. Three Glass Choirists (use banshee stats, replace wail with: Cacophony — all creatures in 30ft take 3d6 thunder and must make CON save or be deafened for 1 minute) drift through the columns, singing in cracked-glass harmony. They are the preserved voices of Caesar's choir, forever rehearsing a hymn to a man who never listened.

**Room challenge:** Combat in overwhelming noise. The Choirists' song stacks — if two sing simultaneously in overlapping areas, creatures make saves at disadvantage. The mirrored floor makes hiding impossible (no cover, no concealment).

**Admin options:**
- From the walkway: you can adjust the Nave's acoustic dampening. Dial it to 0% (silence) — all sound-based attacks have their damage halved. Dial it to 100% — all thunder damage doubles, but the Choirists become vulnerable to thunder.
- Maintenance hatch: drops you into the organ loft above the Nave, out of the Choirists' patrol zones.

### Node 2 — The Choir Loft

**Public experience:** A crescent-shaped balcony overlooking the Nave, lined with glass organ pipes that play themselves when anyone enters. The pipes are actually Resonant Glassworms — glass-like constructs that extrude from the pipes and attack with concentrated sonic blasts. Stats: use glasswork golem stats (from crystal/glass constructs), 4 worms, each attacks with a 30ft line of sonic energy (1d8 thunder, DEX save negates). The worms are vulnerable to bludgeoning and immune to thunder.

**Room challenge:** The loft is a sniping gallery — the worms attack from the pipes, and the balcony's transparent glass railing provides no cover. The organ music intensifies each round — starting at round 3, all DEX saves in the room are made at disadvantage due to the floor vibration.

**Admin options:**
- The organ's power source is a series of glass resonance crystals above the loft. Disabling them from the walkway (DC 14 Arcana) silences the organ and stuns the worms for 2 rounds.
- A maintenance rope drops from the walkway to the organist's seat, bypassing the worm ambush.

### Node 3 — The Confessional of Echoes

**Public experience:** Three glass confession booths in a semicircle, each large enough for one creature. When a creature enters a booth and closes the glass door, the booth activates: the creature must make a DC 14 CHA save or be compelled to speak a secret they have never told anyone. If they save, they take 2d6 psychic damage from the pressure. If they fail, they speak the secret aloud — and the Cathedral records it. The secret echoes throughout the dungeon, audible to every other creature inside. The booths cannot be opened from the outside once a creature is inside (DC 18 STR to break the glass door). After confessing, the creature is released.

**Room challenge:** This is a trap/puzzle room. Delvers must pass through to reach the next area, but one of the three booths is the true exit — the other two are dead ends that teleport the confessor back to the Nave. Discovering which is real requires a DC 15 Investigation check (the real booth has a hairline crack in its glass). If anyone confesses, their secret is broadcast to all — and the Boss knows it too.

**Admin options:**
- The booths' recording function can be toggled off from the walkway (DC 12 Arcana) — secrets are not broadcast.
- The wrong booths' teleportation can be redirected to the admin walkway instead of the Nave, allowing you to extract trapped allies.

## Boss Chamber — The Prism Apse

**Public experience:** The Cathedral's holiest space: a semicircular chamber behind the altar, where a single massive prism hangs suspended from the ceiling by chains of woven light. Below the prism stands the **Chorus-Above**, a being of pure collected voice — not a creature with a body, but a column of swirling, colored light that speaks in thousands of overlapping whispers. The Chorus-Above is the Cathedral's accumulated confessions given form. It knows every secret ever confessed here. Stats: CR 13. Uses an upscaled banshee as base, with lair actions. Attacks: Voice of Thousands — 60ft cone, 6d6 psychic + 6d6 thunder, CON half (DC 17). Shattered Secret — touch attack, target must make WIS save or be paralyzed for 1 minute as their mind is filled with the confessions of everyone who ever spoke in the Cathedral. Prismatic Rebuke — reaction, when hit by an attack, the Chorus projects a burst of colored light, attacker makes DEX save or is blinded for 1 round. The Chorus-Above is immune to psychic and thunder, resistant to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing from nonmagical attacks, vulnerable to radiant.

**Boss Challenge:** The Chorus whispers constantly — all creatures in the chamber have disadvantage on WIS and CHA saves. The Chorus knows every secret confessed in this room (DM should prepare 3–4 secrets relevant to the party to whisper as psychological warfare).

**Lair Actions (Initiative 20):**
- The prism reflects — a beam of concentrated light targets one creature (ranged spell attack, +10, 4d8 radiant, blinds for 1 round).
- The glass shards on the floor rise — all creatures make DEX save or take 2d8 slashing from swirling glass.
- A resonant chord — all creatures make CON save or be pushed 20ft away from the Chorus.

**Admin Override:**
- The Prism is the Cathedral's core. Shattering it would destroy the dungeon but also release every stored confession into the floor — an irreversible data breach. Instead, the prism has a "Forgiveness Protocol" — speak a true absolution (DM judgment: must genuinely forgive someone who wronged you) and the Chorus will pause for 1 minute, allowing safe passage.
- From the apse balcony (admin-only): you can adjust: whisper aggression (Chorus damage dice scaling), confession playback (whether secrets echo throughout dungeon), loot pool weighting.
