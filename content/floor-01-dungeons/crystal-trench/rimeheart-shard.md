# Rimeheart Shard
*Wondrous item (spellcasting focus), very rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

---

## Description
A hand-sized shard of geode heart-crystal, the core fragment left behind when the larger Geode Heart greatsword was carved. Where the greatsword is a weapon of war, the Rimeheart Shard is a tool of deep magic — it does not strike; it channels. The shard hovers just above the palm when held, suspended by its own cold, and the air around it crystallizes into tiny frostflakes that orbit like a personal winter. It was found in the Heart Chamber alongside the greatsword, as though the geode had deliberately split itself — one half for the warrior, one half for the sage.

## Properties

**Cryo-Focus +2.** While holding or carrying this shard, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of spells you cast that deal cold damage. The shard can be used as a spellcasting focus.

**Rimeheart Spells.** While the shard is on your person, you can cast the following spells without expending a spell slot or requiring material components (spell save DC 17): *cone of cold* (1/long rest) and *wall of ice* (1/long rest). The spells manifest as eruptions of blue-white crystalline shards.

**Geode Absorption.** While holding this shard, you have resistance to cold damage. Additionally, when you take cold damage, you can use your reaction to have the shard absorb some of the energy. The cold damage you take is reduced by an amount equal to 1d10 + your spellcasting ability modifier. Once this reaction has been used, it cannot be used again until you finish a short or long rest.

## Balance Notes
Four properties at Very Rare for spellcasters. The +2 spell attack/DC for cold spells is comparable to a *rod of the pact keeper +2* (rare) but narrower (cold spells only) — the restriction justifies the extra power budget. *Cone of cold* (5th level) 1/long rest and *wall of ice* (6th level) 1/long rest — two spells at Very Rare is appropriate (compare to *staff of frost*, very rare, which gets *cone of cold*, *ice storm*, *wall of ice*, and *fog cloud* via charges). Cold resistance (passive) plus a reaction damage absorption (1/short rest, ~11–15 damage reduction) provides a defensive layer that synergizes with the Trench's frozen origin. The +2 cold DC is the most potent property — a level 11 wizard with 20 INT and this shard has DC 19 on cold spells.

---

## Variants
**Legendary: Heart of the Trench.** Increase the focus bonus to +3, add *investiture of ice* 1/long rest to the Rimeheart Spells property, and the Geode Absorption reaction reduces cold damage by 2d10 + your spellcasting ability modifier.
