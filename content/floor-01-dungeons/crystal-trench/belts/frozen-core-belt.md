# Frozen Core Belt
*Wondrous item (belt), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A belt woven from pale crystal fibers that glow faintly at the buckle — a polished orb of geode core, the size of a small fist, set in a silver bezel. When worn, it tightens against the gut with a cold that goes inward rather than outward, settling somewhere behind the navel like a chip of winter lodged in the soul. The dwarves who first mined the Trench called it a "second spine" — it does not warm you, it makes the cold irrelevant.

## Properties

**Glacial Constitution.** While wearing this belt, your Constitution score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22.

**Rime Exhalation (1/Short Rest).** As an action, you can exhale a 15-foot cone of freezing breath. Each creature in that area must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Cold Endurance.** You are naturally adapted to cold climates, as described in chapter 5 of the Dungeon Master's Guide. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against effects that would reduce your speed due to cold or ice.

## Balance Notes
Three properties at Rare: +2 CON (max 22) is the headline — compare to *belt of dwarvenkind* (rare, +2 CON max 20, plus darkvision/poison package). Frozen Core Belt gives a higher CON cap (+22) but trades the dwarf package for a 1/short rest 15-ft cone breath (4d6, ~14 avg — modest AoE) and cold adaptation. The breath weapon is weaker than a *wand of fireballs* (rare, *fireball* 3/day at 8d6) but recharges on short rest. The CON bonus makes this attractive to any frontline class, and the max 22 means barbarians and fighters gain even more from this item than casters.

## Variants
**Uncommon Variant (Frostheart Sash).** +1 CON (max 20), Rime Exhalation reduced to 3d6 (DC 13), no Cold Endurance. Cost: 80,000 soul stones.

**Very Rare Variant (Glacial Spine Girdle).** +2 CON (max 24), Rime Exhalation increases to 6d6 (DC 17) and becomes a 30-ft cone. Gain resistance to cold damage. Cost: 1,000,000 soul stones.
