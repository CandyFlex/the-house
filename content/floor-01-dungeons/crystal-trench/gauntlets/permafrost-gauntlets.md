# Permafrost Gauntlets
*Wondrous item (gloves), rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Gauntlets forged from segmented geode plates over a lining of pale blue leather, each knuckle capped with a faceted crystal that gleams like compacted snow. When the wearer's fist connects, frost blooms across the target in an audible crackle. The gauntlets are always cold to the touch and leave a rime-print on anything they grasp for more than a breath.

## Properties

**Frostbite Strike.** When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack or an unarmed strike, the attack deals an additional 1d6 cold damage.

**Freezing Grasp.** When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can use a bonus action to force frost to encase the creature's lower body. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be grappled by the ice (escape DC 15). While grappled in this way, the creature's speed is 0. You can only have one creature grappled by this effect at a time. If you use Freezing Grasp on a second creature, the ice on the first creature shatters, freeing it. The ice melts after 1 minute if not shattered earlier.

## Balance Notes
Two properties — an always-on +1d6 cold damage rider makes this competitive with uncommon weapons that get +1d6 elemental damage but require a specific weapon. The Freezing Grasp bonus action grapple is the tactical layer — it requires a hit first (resource gate), uses a bonus action, and only holds one target. Compare to *flame tongue* (rare, +2d6 fire, no grapple). Permafrost Gauntlets trade 1d6 raw damage for a control option and the flexibility of applying to any melee attack (including unarmed and off-hand weapons). The unlimited-use grapple with no saving throw to maintain once applied is strong — DMs should be mindful that this can lock down a single non-Huge+ creature indefinitely with repeated hits.

## Variants
**Uncommon Variant (Frost-Kissed Bracers).** Reduce Frostbite Strike to +1d4 cold damage and remove Freezing Grasp. Instead, the wearer can cast *frostbite* (XGtE) as a bonus action once per short rest (DC 13). Cost: 60,000 soul stones.

**Very Rare Variant (Diamond-Coil Gauntlets).** Frostbite Strike increases to +2d6 cold damage. Freezing Grasp affects creatures up to Huge size and the escape DC increases to 17. The wearer also gains resistance to cold damage. Cost: 900,000 soul stones.
