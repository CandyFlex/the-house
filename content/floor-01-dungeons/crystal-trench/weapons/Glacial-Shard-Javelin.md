# Glacial Shard Javelin
*Weapon (javelin), uncommon*

---

## Description
A javelin carved from a single spire of geode crystal, slender and wickedly sharp, with a point that tapers to a translucent needle. It hums faintly when thrown, a shard of ice forming in the wielder's empty hand before the javelin has even struck its target. Prospectors believe it was once the bolt of a trap in the Trench's deepest corridor, enchanted to arm itself endlessly.

## Properties

**+1 Weapon.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Crystal Recall.** This javelin returns to your hand immediately after you make a ranged attack with it.

**Frostbite Halt.** When you hit a creature with this javelin, frost spreads across its limbs. The target's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. This reduction does not stack with itself.

## Balance Notes
Three properties is high for an uncommon weapon, but each is modest: +1, returning (a ribbon on a thrown weapon), and a 10ft speed reduction for one round (like *ray of frost* without the damage). No attunement required, which is correct for a +1 weapon with only minor riders. Compare to a standard *+1 weapon* (uncommon) — the extras are sidegrades. The speed reduction requires a hit and doesn't stack, preventing lockdown strategies.

## Variants
- **Rare — Glacial Spire:** +2 weapon. Frostbite Halt speed reduction increases to 20 feet. Additionally, when the javelin returns to your hand after a ranged attack, you can choose to have it leave a line of rime in the 5-foot-wide path it traveled; that path becomes difficult terrain for creatures other than you until the start of your next turn. Cost: ~250,000 soul stones.
