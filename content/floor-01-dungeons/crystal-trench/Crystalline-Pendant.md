# Crystalline Pendant
*Wondrous item (necklace), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A thumb-sized geode crystal suspended on a thin silver chain, shaped like a teardrop that caught the moment of freezing. The crystal glows faintly when danger draws near and warms against the skin when the cold around it grows hungry. Miners who ventured too deep into the Trench wore pendants like these — most were found still glowing on the bodies of those who did not return, the cold having taken everything but the crystal's warning.

## Properties

**Frostbound Mercy.** While wearing this pendant in a cold environment, the first time you are reduced to 0 hit points after finishing a long rest, you immediately stabilize. This property recharges at dawn.

**Rime Shell (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can speak the pendant's command word to surround yourself in a shell of crystalline frost. You gain 10 temporary hit points for 1 hour. While you have these temporary hit points, a creature that hits you with a melee attack while within 5 feet of you takes 10 cold damage. This replicates the effects of the *armor of agathys* spell cast at 2nd level, requiring no spell slot or components.

**Frost Sense.** The pendant grows noticeably warm when you are within 60 feet of a source of extreme natural cold, such as a white dragon's lair, a region of supernatural freezing, or a hazard that deals 2d6 or more cold damage per round. This property does not reveal invisible or hidden sources.

## Balance Notes
Three properties: a conditional auto-stabilize (narrow, once per day), a 2nd-level *armor of agathys* 1/long rest (appropriate for uncommon — 10 temp HP and 10 retaliation damage is modest), and a flavorful detection ribbon. The stabilize is cold-environment-gated, preventing it from being a universal safety net. Compare to *periapt of wound closure* (uncommon, always stabilize + double hit dice healing) — Frostbound Mercy is strictly worse (conditional + 1/day), making room for the other properties. The item requires attunement, which is correct given its layered defensive utility.

## Variants
- **Rare — Crystalline Heart:** Rime Shell now casts *armor of agathys* at 4th level (20 temporary hit points, 20 cold retaliation damage). Frostbound Mercy no longer requires a cold environment — it always functions (still once per long rest). Cost: ~280,000 soul stones.
