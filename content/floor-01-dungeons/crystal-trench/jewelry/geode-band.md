# Geode Band
*Ring, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A band of polished geode crystal that fits the finger perfectly — too perfectly, as if it chose rather than was chosen. When its power stirs, frost spreads from the ring across the knuckle and retreats just as quickly. It is one of a set — prospectors have found three such bands in the Trench, each in a different chamber, none bearing the same owner's bones nearby.

## Properties

**Frost Ray.** While wearing this ring, you can cast the *ray of frost* cantrip at will. The spell's spellcasting ability is Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (your choice when you attune to the ring). The ray manifests as a needle of blue-white crystal light.

**Chilling Rebuke.** When you deal cold damage to a creature, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the next attack roll that creature makes before the end of its next turn. The creature's limbs stiffen visibly as frost creeps across them.

**Geode Storm (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can cast the *sleet storm* spell (spell save DC 15) without expending a spell slot or requiring components. The sleet manifests as a sudden eruption of swirling crystalline shards rather than conventional ice.

## Balance Notes
Three properties: at-will *ray of frost* (cantrip — common-tier normally, but the scaling makes it useful at any level), a reaction debuff (imposes disadvantage on one attack roll after cold damage, which triggers off the ring's own *ray of frost* for self-synergy), and 1/long rest *sleet storm* (3rd-level spell at Rare is standard — compare to *wand of fireballs*, rare, which gets *fireball* 3/day). Only 1/long rest *sleet storm* is conservative; the reaction rider and at-will cantrip fill the power budget. The reaction competes with *shield*, *counterspell*, and opportunity attacks, so it carries real opportunity cost even though it has no per-rest limit.

---

## Variants
**Uncommon Variant (Glacial Band).** Remove *sleet storm*. The ring still grants *ray of frost* at will and Chilling Rebuke, but the save DC for any cold effects you create with the ring is 13 instead of 15. Cost: 45,000 soul stones.

**Very Rare Variant (Heart of the Geode).** Upgrade *sleet storm* to *ice storm* (cast at 5th level) once per long rest. Add: when you cast a spell that deals cold damage while wearing the ring, you can reroll any 1s on the damage dice (must use the new roll). Save DC increases to 16. Cost: 750,000 soul stones.
