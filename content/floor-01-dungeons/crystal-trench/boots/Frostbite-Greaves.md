# Frostbite Greaves
*Wondrous item (boots), uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
A pair of knee-high boots carved from interlocking plates of blue-white geode crystal, lined with frost-ferret fur the color of new snow. The soles are etched with a pattern of branching rime that reforms every few seconds, shedding frost with each step. They are never warm, but the cold does not bite — it simply waits, ready to be left behind.

## Properties

**Glacial Traction.** You can move across icy surfaces without needing to make Dexterity saving throws to avoid falling prone, and ice or snow is not difficult terrain for you.

**Rime Trail.** When you move across ice, snow, or a surface covered in frost, you can choose to leave a trail of slick rime in the spaces you leave. Each 5-foot square you exit becomes difficult terrain for creatures other than you until the end of your next turn. Once you use this property, you cannot do so again until the start of your next turn.

## Balance Notes
Two modest passives: ice traction (compare to *slippers of spider climbing*, uncommon) and a limited difficult-terrain control effect. The trail doesn't deal damage and only lasts one round per use, making it a tactical positioning tool. The ice-surface requirement keeps it situational. No attunement would make it an automatic pick for any party entering a frozen dungeon; attunement ensures the wearer commits the slot.

---

## Variants
- **Rare — Frostbite Walkers:** Glacial Traction now applies to all surfaces (you ignore nonmagical difficult terrain and do not expend extra movement for it). Rime Trail deals 1d4 cold damage to any creature other than you that enters a rime-covered square for the first time on a turn. Cost: ~150,000 soul stones.
