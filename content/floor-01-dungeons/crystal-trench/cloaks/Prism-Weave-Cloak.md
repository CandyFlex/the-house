# Prism Weave Cloak
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

---

## Description
Woven from hair-thin crystalline fibers harvested deep in the Crystal Trench, this cloak catches and splinters every scrap of light that touches it. In brightness it shimmers with a pale blue-white iridescence, and in shadow it drinks the dark until the wearer is little more than a ripple in still air. The fibers are brittle to the touch yet impossibly flexible — the Trench's strange gift, a frozen thing that learned to bend. Those who walk the Trench in silence know that the crystals watch, and this cloak teaches them to watch back.

## Properties

**Refractive Camouflage.** The cloak's crystalline fibers bend light around you. While wearing this cloak in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

**Spell Deflection.** When you are targeted by a ranged spell attack, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll as the cloak's facets scatter the incoming magic into a brief spray of harmless refracted light. Once you use this property, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Mirror Fracture (1/Long Rest).** As an action, you can cast *mirror image* without expending a spell slot. The illusory duplicates appear as refracted crystalline versions of yourself — three faceted silhouettes that shift and shimmer as light passes through them.

## Balance Notes
Three properties, each serving a different defensive layer:

1. **Refractive Camouflage** — a conditional Stealth boost. Narrower than *cloak of elvenkind* (uncommon, advantage on all Stealth checks + enemy Perception disadvantage) but costs no attunement slot penalty since this item already requires attunement. The dim-light/darkness restriction makes it thematic — crystal doesn't bend light it can't catch.
2. **Spell Deflection** — a defensive reaction against ranged spell attacks, 1/short rest. Stronger than it looks because it works against any ranged spell attack (not just a particular school or level), but the once-per-rest limit prevents it from becoming a permanent shield. Gives the wearer a meaningful tactical choice each fight.
3. **Mirror Fracture** — a 2nd-level spell 1/long rest is well within Rare budget, and the crystalline flavor ties it to the item's identity.

The cloak is defense-heavy but not passive — the wearer must decide when to use the reaction and when to pop mirror image. Compare to *cloak of displacement* (rare, imposes disadvantage on all attack rolls against you until you take damage, resets each turn) — the Prism Weave Cloak offers more variety but less raw defensive uptime. It rewards active play.

## Variants
- **Uncommon — Glint Cloak:** Refractive Camouflage only. No Spell Deflection, no Mirror Fracture. Does not require attunement. Cost: ~30,000 soul stones.
- **Very Rare — Prism Weave Mantle:** All Rare properties. Spell Deflection recharges on a short rest (2 uses). Mirror Fracture 2/long rest. Additionally, as a bonus action 1/long rest, you can cause the cloak to flare with blinding refracted light — each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the start of your next turn. Cost: ~1,500,000 soul stones.
