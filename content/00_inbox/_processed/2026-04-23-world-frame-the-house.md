# World Frame — The House

The House is the world. Not a building inside a world, not a dungeon, 
not a pocket dimension — the world itself. All reality that has ever 
been observed manifests from within The House. There is no external 
universe that contains it. There is no sky above it or earth below it 
that belongs to something else. The House is the total frame of 
existence, and it is mesophysical in nature — operating at the layer 
between the metaphysical and the physical, where intention and matter 
are not yet fully separate.

The House has seven floors, organized by the colors of the visible 
spectrum: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet — ROYGBIV. 
Each floor is a complete reality. Floor 1 is Red, Floor 2 is Orange, 
Floor 3 is Yellow, Floor 4 is Green, Floor 5 is Blue, Floor 6 is 
Indigo, Floor 7 is Violet. Color is the primary organizing language of 
The House. When you say "the red faction" you mean a faction of Floor 
1. When you say "an indigo scholar" you mean someone tied to Floor 6. 
Colors are not decorative — they are structural. Each floor carries 
its color as thematic weight, archetype, and cosmological signature.

The floors are adjacent, not hierarchical. They connect sequentially — 
1 connects to 2, 2 to 3, and so on — but no floor ranks above another. 
There is no "higher" or "lower" in any meaningful sense. Floor 1 is 
the entrance when someone enters The House from outside, but internally, 
every floor is its own center. Scale within each floor is 
non-Euclidean and intentional. A floor may contain what appears to be 
a continent, a planet, a galaxy, or an entire multiverse. These are 
not contradictions. They are features of the frame. Do not flag 
apparent scale inconsistencies — they are part of how The House works.

No external space to The House has ever been observed. No explorer has 
found an edge. No cosmology has mapped something outside. References 
to "outside" within The House are treated as internal manifestations — 
psychological, spiritual, or metaphorical — unless explicitly 
identified as a cosmological question worth investigating. The House 
does not have an outside. It simply is.
