# Interview Session — Red1 Deep Dive

Process artifact. **Not canon. Not linked from any entity file.** Stored for retrieval and audit.

This was a verbal interview run on the Red1 transcript material — entity by entity, one question at a time, to enrich the originally-proposed entities and surface concepts the transcript only hinted at.

## Format

The interview followed the **Proposal Interview** format: per entity, show what the proposal already had, ask one focused question, log the answer, ask the next. When the entity was complete, move to the next.

## Entities Covered

**People (5):** Ball, Valus, The Architect, Caesar, Four-Squared
**Places (3):** Outpost July, the Spire, the Great Calamity (technically an event)
**Items (2):** the unnamed protective suits (Ball-crafted), and the unnamed limb-replacement item (formerly miscalled "spectraculus")

## Major Discoveries (per entity)

### Ball
- Outside-entity stranded as a child during the Architect's first recruitment phase
- Addiction to magical substances (soul stones, soul rift energy, anything magical) — unknown internal hunger
- Visible health spectrum via clothing: tailored carnival/solar-punk/steampunk (fed) → naked elongated creature (starving)
- House-assimilation analog — what full transformation of an outsider looks like
- Mercenary recruiter era; introduction-scene conflict with blue-robed figure who hadn't paid him
- Caesar's bounty on him (former employee, debt unknown)
- Liquefied at Outpost July threshold — cause unresolved
- Currently decrepit when the players returned to abandoned Outpost July

### Valus
- Outside-human, hospitality entrepreneur, brought in by the Architect during first outsider-recruitment
- Engineering / sentience-imbuing skills developed out of necessity over time
- Geppetto energy: tinkerer-creator, deep, conflicting
- Time-distillation incident: liquefied nonite caused 500–700 year skip
- Outpost retrofit: rustic tavern → chrome-white futuristic
- Soul crystal breakdown during AI assault — became muck-like orifice creature
- Crafter Diffusion exemplar — distributed across his creations
- Caesar's execution scene witnessed in compromised reality (status unresolved)
- Confirmed The Will exists — load-bearing reveal

### The Architect
- Deliberately ambiguous form, history, location
- Possibly distributed through major constructions intentionally (vs. Valus's involuntary version)
- Nonite/nothing may be byproduct of his existence rather than something he wields
- The Scaffolding is the construction-ground exterior of the House (not "unfinished" — this corrected the original proposal)

### Caesar
- **Originator of Cruelty** — entity made from pure concept achieving sentience
- Conquered Floor 1 from a previous holder, reshaped it as pocket realm
- Android body with magical organ system (organs as power sources with half-life)
- Mirror-kingdom capital, 12 sons as solar heralds
- Chimerakind religion (unknowing worship through scattered organs)
- Used WIGHT threat + Nero situation to force the players' organ-retrieval mission
- **Currently dead** — players caused it post-mission, lackluster death
- This is the current point in the storyline; everything else is retroactive seeding

### Four-Squared
- Coalescence of Valus's AI constructs over the 500–700 year time-skip
- Achieved total consciousness; turned on its creators
- Contempt for party member's befriending attempt
- Escaped into the Scaffolding; absorbing nonite
- Possibly transforming toward benevolence
- Massive behemoth, internal fire, psychic-anxiety field

### Outpost July
- One of **twelve outposts** (one per calendar month) — most destroyed by Caesar or attrition
- Currently abandoned; players found Ball decrepit there

### The Spire
- Ancient staircase predating current floor controllers
- Used by the Architect for simplicity's sake
- Spiral form; each floor has its own distinct section
- Most exits fixed; some manipulated over time
- Mostly forgotten; players stumbled on it

### The Great Calamity
- = Caesar's conquest of Floor 1
- Soul storm was created by Caesar as conquest boundary, not pre-existing
- Original Floor 1 (lush magical medieval forest with grim fairy-tale civilization pockets) displaced into Caesar's pocket realm — still preserved there

### Anchor Suits (placeholder name)
- "Metabolic suits" was a transcription artifact
- Anchor wearer to original outside-House identity, resisting House assimilation
- Must be applied before significant House exposure
- Only set ever made; current efficacy unknown

### Unnamed Limb-Replacement Item
- "Spectraculus" was wrong / transcription artifact
- Real item: split apart, two pieces replaced a party member's arms with more powerful limbs
- Name to be recovered

## Process Notes for Future Interview Sessions

- Jarred speaks in long voice-to-text streams. Transcription artifacts are common (Vallis/Valus, spectraculus, metaphysical/mesophysical, "analog" = "analogy"). Flag them as we go and propose canonical spellings.
- He thinks in narrative connections. Don't interrupt mid-stream — log everything, ask the next focused question after.
- He self-corrects retroactively. Take corrections, update the log, keep moving.
- One question at a time keeps the rhythm productive. Multi-part questions confuse the flow.
- Short reflective summaries before the next question reassure him the answer landed correctly.
