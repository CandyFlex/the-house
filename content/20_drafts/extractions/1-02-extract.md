## New Entities

Four major towers, multiple halls, practice courts, a gallery, the Chamber of Appointment (null-magic zone), a courtyard, and an undercroft.

- **Storm Entities** — Demons of the storm, morphing shades, high rack demons. All hostile.

- **Nero** — RC is deeply alarmed by his presence. Nero is pre-registered to an unknown entity/book — his entry shows up in green, cryptic language RC cannot read. RC warns IO not to give Nero any position in the hierarchy.
