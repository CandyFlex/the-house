# Pass 2 — Extract: Red1

## New Entities

### People

- **Ball** — chaotic chipmunk, blue-robed when first seen as humanoid figure arguing with the chipmunk; the chipmunk is the actual entity (the blue-robed figure was ejected by Ball). Tied to a "wanted poster" in Outpost July, suggesting fugitive status. Originally a young child brought into the House, given the "go recruit" job by Valus to get him out of Outpost July. Manufactured the metabolic suits onto the players' flesh. Liquefied himself at the threshold of Outpost July.
- **Valus** (multiple transcription spellings: Valus, Valis, Vallis, ballast, VAL — see Ambiguities) — large humanoid shadow, 8–20ft scaling form, ~10ft default outline, 4–6 long tentacles, wispy older-man's voice. Former wealthy human: hospitality manager and owner of a hotel/amusement area for children before being brought into the House by the architect. Tasked by the architect to build Outpost July as the magistrate / interface for arrivals. Immaculate crafter — built a 120-ft arachnid mount with demiplane storage. Saw Floor 1 in its earlier (pre-calamity) state.
- **The Architect** — creator of the House (per Valus's account). Brought Valus in. Created the Scaffolding sub-realm. Opened Outpost July to the outside. Has not been described or seen further; Valus speaks vaguely of intent. Created/originated nonite use.
- **Caesar** — current ruler of the Red floor. "Cruel robotic being." Sits/lies in the middle of the desolate radial — i.e., the center point of Floor 1's giant radial clock. Speculated by Valus to be the figure who caused the Great Calamity that transformed Floor 1.
- **The Robot / Four-squared** — large monolithic AI / mythological-looking ancient sentient robotic creature. Formed when one of four powered automatons absorbed the others. Numbered 1–3 with "4" eating the others' power, hence "four squared" symbology. ~100–200ft tall, rivals Valus's giant shadow form.
- **The Reaper Soul** — first encounter on Red floor. Spectral soul lingering powerfully enough to manifest a small graveyard mirror-verse demiplane. Attacked the players when approached. Killed.
- **The Nefarious Captors (unnamed group)** — encountered in the side-demiplane adjacent to Floor 1 where the players first awoke. Attempted to capture/experiment on/enslave them. Identity and faction unknown.

### Places

- **Outpost July** — Valus's establishment on Floor 1. Steampunk-arched exterior on a 300ft wall facing the radial cliff. Interior is non-Euclidean: tavern + arcade facade, "wondrously built," vending machines, automatons running everywhere, tables/banners, six tiered amusement areas including a courtyard / dueling sport space. Houses both lodging and combat-sport reward economies.
- **Floor 1's "Newer Form"** (the current state of Floor 1 — to be folded into the existing `floor-01-red.md` rather than its own entity) — barren wasteland, large crater-like radial peninsula, ringed by craterous arched mountains. Topography is itself a giant radial clock made of the floor's own stone/terrain — secret, unmarked by etching, recognizable only to those who knew the prior form. "Transmorphic" — terrain has mirror-finish patches. Liquid present is mercury-style, undrinkable, toxic. Sky is always lit, overcast, with on average 3 suns and at most 6 visible. Volatile: many people brought in die during arrival; their souls shatter into the substrate, opening rifts that become small pocket mirror-verses.
- **The Side Demiplane (Adjacent to Floor 1)** — where players first awoke, before reaching Floor 1 proper. Site of the captor encounter and Ball's intervention. Not yet named in source.
- **The Reaper's Mirror-Verse** — small graveyard demiplane, accessed through a mirage-portal from Floor 1. Now empty (reaper killed). Pattern-example of the soul-shatter rift mechanic.
- **The Spire** — large staircase that runs through and connects all seven floors. Privileged or back-end transit. Allows certain people access to doors "where they're not supposed to be."
- **The Scaffolding** — sub-realm originally created by the architect and "left." Overcast gray light, obsidian-black landscape, abysmal/glowy. Building blocks of nonite throughout. Accessed by the players during the four-squared encounter as part of an escape attempt.
- **The Arena Vacuum** (event-place, may belong to Outpost July's courtyard) — a magical capsule mechanism that vacuum-isolates a fight from Outpost July, creating a temporal bubble. Triggered the time-skip during the four-squared formation.

### Items / Crafted

- **Metabolic Suits** — manufactured by Ball onto the players' flesh from nothing. Sink into and protect the wearer; allow them to remain on the floor / inside the House. Look similar to Ball's own clothing. Function: bodily protection in the House.
- **The Arachnid Mount** — 120ft-long arachnid built/crafted by Valus. Functions as mount, building, and demiplane-holding container; transports the party.
- **Spectraculus / "Spraculus"** (unclear name) — a "high-level reward" obtained by winning in the courtyard duel. Power amplifier. Absorbed by an automaton, triggering its power escalation into the four-squared chain.

### Magic / Mechanics / Cosmology

- **Nonite** — substance that solidifies "nothing." Etymology: "non" (nothing) + "-ite" (mineral/manual suffix; speaker explains "nothing in this world is something"). Real-world analog: gray matter / black matter / dark energy. Only beings of exceptionally high power can take "raw nothing" or "suspended nothing" and consolidate it into nonite. Properties: amplifier / battery / fundamental building block. Reality-breaking when wielded by sufficient skill. Originally used by the architect; primary construction material of the Scaffolding.
- **Soul-Shatter Rift Mechanic** — when people die during arrival on Floor 1, their soul/person shatters into the floor's makeup, causing rifts/portals. The strongest of these become small pocket mirror-verses housing the lingering soul.
- **Demiplane Adjacency** — the House contains the inner world; demiplanes adjacent to floors exist; the House also creates internal sub-realms beyond the natural floor sequence (the Spire, the Scaffolding being the prominent named ones).
- **Soul Source Constraint** — no new souls are created; the House works only with existing ones. Inhabitants must "go out and gather" from external reality to bring more in. Speaker notes this has "enslaved-type" connotations.
- **Floor 1 as Entryway** — recognized across all floors and even by archetypes / "highest power beings" as the canonical entrance to the House. Sub-entrances exist for other beings.
- **Cross-Floor Bridging (door-conjuring)** — Valus, in last-ditch defense against four-squared, gathered all his power to open a gate to another floor. Manifested as a "mid-century-looking office door, the color purple" (→ Floor 7).

### Events

- **The Great Calamity** — ancient event ("millennia upon millennia ago"). Transformed Floor 1 from its original form into the current desolate radial-clock wasteland. Coincides with young Ball's first venture outside Outpost July. Valus presumed Ball dead for ages afterward. Caesar is suspected but not confirmed as the cause.
- **Players' Arrival** — woke in adjacent demiplane → captured by nefarious group → ejected to Floor 1 by Ball after the blue-robed captor refused to pay → received metabolic suits → traveled to mountain face → descended cliff → reached Outpost July.
- **Reaper Encounter** — at the mirage-portal graveyard demiplane. Players defeated the reaper, took items.
- **The Courtyard Duel + Time Skip** — players won the spectraculus in the dueling courtyard. The arena's vacuum-capsule mechanism isolated the fight from Outpost July, creating a temporal vacuum. Outside the bubble, hundreds of years passed. Valus had upgraded everything in their absence and feared them lost.
- **The Robot Ascension (Four-Squared)** — automatons absorbed the spectraculus's power, then absorbed each other; one consumed all and became "four squared" — the robot.
- **The Scaffolding Excursion** — players bridged into the Scaffolding sub-realm during the four-squared escape attempt.
- **The Purple Door Bridge** — Valus, near defeat, expended his power to manifest a Floor 7 portal as a mid-century office door. (Transcript ends mid-action.)
