## Summary
Protective garments crafted by [[ball]] from consumed magical components and applied directly to the players' flesh on first arrival. Anchor the wearer to their original outside-House identity, resisting the House's gradual assimilation of outsiders. Applied immediately to prevent the erosion process from taking hold. Only set ever made. Current efficacy uncertain.

## Established
- **How made**: Ball consumed magical components from the House and applied them directly to the players — crafted from inside out, not from external material.
- **Application**: Sink into the flesh and become absorbed — not removable garments. Invisible once applied.
- **Function**: Anchor the wearer's identity to their original outside-House self. Resist the House's transformative erosion — the same process that turned Ball into a chipmunk over millennia, that the soul storm absorbs victims into.
- **Application window**: Must be applied before significant House exposure. The longer someone is unprotected inside the House, the deeper the erosion.
- **Uniqueness**: Only ever made for this party. Ball crafted them on the spot. No known prior or subsequent instances.
- **Current efficacy**: Unknown. The players have since been significantly affected by things inside the House.
- **Style**: Resemble Ball's own clothing in aesthetic.
